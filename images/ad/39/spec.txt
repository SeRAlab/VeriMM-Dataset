Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer with an enable signal. The module selects one of the four input lines (A, B, C, or D) based on the 2-bit selection derived from inputs B and C. If the enable signal (EN) is active, the output (Y) reflects the selected input; otherwise, it outputs a LOW signal. This functionality allows controlled data routing based on the selection and enables an additional functionality to disable the output altogether when necessary.
module top_module(
    Y,
    A,
    B,
    C,
    D,
    EN
);
