Design a simple SR-latch using Verilog. The SR-latch should have two inputs, S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs, Q and Qn (not Q). The output Q should be set to 1 when S is high and R is low, and reset to 0 when R is high and S is low. When both S and R are low, the output should retain its previous state. When both S and R are high, the state is considered invalid.
