
Design an Interrupt Controller that handles multiple interrupt requests. The controller should have the following functionality:
- It accepts 4 interrupt requests (irq[3:0]) and generates an interrupt acknowledge signal (ack).
- The controller should prioritize the highest priority interrupt (irq[3] has the highest priority, irq[0] has the lowest).
- The ack signal should be high for one clock cycle when an interrupt is processed.
- The controller should also have a reset signal that clears the state.
