Design a circuit that operates as a synchronous D flip-flop with additional control signals. The output Q is updated on the rising edge of the clock (CLK) based on the state of the input D, alongside control signals SCD and SCE. When SCD is high, the flip-flop captures the value of D; when SCE is high, it resets Q to LOW. The circuit incorporates a reset signal (RESET_B), which forces Q to LOW when asserted, ensuring proper initialization under reset conditions. The design includes delayed versions of input signals to manage timing appropriately in a synchronous environment.

    Q      ,
    CLK    ,
    D      ,

);
