
Design a checksum module in Verilog that takes an 8-bit input data and a 1-bit control signal. The control signal indicates whether to compute the checksum (1) or to validate the checksum (0). The module should output a 1-bit valid signal indicating whether the computed checksum matches the provided checksum. The computed checksum shall be the bitwise XOR of all bits of the input data. The module should also have a reset signal to initialize the state.
