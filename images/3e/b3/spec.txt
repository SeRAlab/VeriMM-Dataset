
Design a 16-QAM modulator in Verilog that takes a 4-bit input representing two symbols (I and Q) and produces a corresponding 2-bit output (representing the amplitude of each symbol). The modulator should operate on a clock signal and include a reset signal that initializes the output to zero. The amplitude mapping is as follows:
- 00 -> (1, 1)
- 01 -> (1, -1)
- 10 -> (-1, 1)
- 11 -> (-1, -1)
