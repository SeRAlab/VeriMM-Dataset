
Design a QAM modulator in Verilog that takes in 4 bits of input data and generates a corresponding 16-QAM signal. The output signal should be a pair of 4-bit signals representing the I (in-phase) and Q (quadrature) components of the QAM signal. The mapping of the 4-bit input to the (I, Q) output pairs should be predefined according to standard 16-QAM mapping.
