
Design a Fractional-N Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) in Verilog that generates a specific output frequency based on a given reference frequency and a fractional division factor. The system should include a Phase Frequency Detector (PFD), a charge pump, a loop filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO). The output frequency should be calculated using the formula:
Output Frequency = Reference Frequency * (N + F)
where N is the integer division factor and F is the fractional division factor.
