Design a one-bit wide, 4-to-1 multiplexer. The circuit selects one of the four input signals based on the 2-bit selector (sel) value. When sel equals 00, the output is equal to in[0]; when sel equals 01, the output is in[1]; for sel equal to 10, the output is in[2]; and when sel equals 11, the output is in[3].
module top_module(input wire [3:0] in, input wire [1:0] sel, output reg out);
