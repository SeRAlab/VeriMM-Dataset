

You are tasked with designing a verilog module for a simple combinational circuit that takes in two inputs A and B, and outputs the bitwise AND of the two inputs. The module should have the following interface:

Inputs:
- A: a single bit input
- B: a single bit input

Outputs:
- Y: a single bit output representing the bitwise AND of A and B

You should name your module `and_gate`.
