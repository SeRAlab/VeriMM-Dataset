The module takes a clock input (clk) and an active-low reset (rstn), and processes a single-bit input (cin) through a series of registers to generate an output (cout). The output will be high only when the input ‘cin’ transitions from low to high, specifically after three consecutive clock cycles of high, indicating a specific edge condition. This design effectively implements a low-to-high edge detector with a delay mechanism.
module top_module(
input clk,
