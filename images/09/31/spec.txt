Design a SRT (Sweeney, Robertson, and Tocher) divider in Verilog that takes a 16-bit input dividend and a 16-bit input divisor. The quotient should be a 16-bit output, and the remainder should be a 16-bit output. The module should have a clock and reset signal, and the division should complete in a finite number of cycles, producing the quotient and remainder at the end of the process.
