The module updates its internal state based on clock signals and a clock enable input. It shifts pixel data and decrements the x-coordinate until it reaches zero, indicating the sprite is active. Upon receiving load signals, it updates specific registers with new values from the input. The output includes the current pixel state, upper color bits, active status, and other relevant data to facilitate sprite rendering at specific coordinates on the screen.
