Design a butterfly unit for complex number addition. The module takes two complex numbers represented by their real and imaginary components (x_real, x_imag, y_real, y_imag) as 2-bit inputs and outputs their sum as a new complex number (z_real, z_imag). The real and imaginary parts of the output are computed by simply adding the respective real and imaginary components of the input numbers.
module top_module(
  input [1:0] x_real,
  input [1:0] x_imag,
  input [1:0] y_real,
  input [1:0] y_imag,
  output [1:0] z_real,
  output [1:0] z_imag
);
