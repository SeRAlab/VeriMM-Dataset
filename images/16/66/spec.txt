Design a Verilog module that implements the point addition operation on an elliptic curve defined over a finite field. The inputs to the module will be the x and y coordinates of two points P1(x1, y1) and P2(x2, y2) on the curve, along with a signal to initiate the addition operation. The output will be the x and y coordinates of the resulting point R(x3, y3) after the addition. Ensure to handle the case when P1 and P2 are the same point correctly, which involves using the tangent to the curve.
