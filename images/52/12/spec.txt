When PWRDN is asserted, both outputs are driven low, effectively disabling the oscillator. If PWRDN is deasserted, the clock outputs toggle every 18.518 time units, creating a square wave oscillation for both outputs. The parameters allow for future customization of power management and clock division, although they are not utilized in this specific implementation.
