Design a 16-bit shift register with parallel load capability. The shift register should have the following inputs: a 16-bit data input (data_in), a load signal (load), a shift signal (shift), and a clock signal (clk). When the load signal is high, the data_in should be loaded into the register. When the shift signal is high, the data in the register should be shifted to the right by one position, with the least significant bit (LSB) shifting out. The most significant bit (MSB) should be filled with zero after shifting.
