Design a 4-bit ripple carry adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs, a and b. When the enable signal (en) is active, the module outputs the computed sum; otherwise, it outputs zero. The module also provides a carry output that indicates if there is a carry from the most significant bit during addition, but this output is valid only when the enable signal is active. If en is low, both the sum and carry outputs are driven to their inactive states.
module top_module(
    a,
    b,
    en,
    sum,
    carry
);
