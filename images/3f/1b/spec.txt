Design a combinational logic circuit that performs point addition on an elliptic curve defined over a finite field. The inputs are coordinates (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) of two points on the curve, and the output is the coordinates (x3, y3) of the resulting point after addition. The curve used is defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b, with a = 0 and b = 7 (Weierstrass form). The field is defined by a prime p.
