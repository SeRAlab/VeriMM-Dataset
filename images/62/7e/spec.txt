Develop a Verilog module that represents a 9-bit LFSR (Linear Feedback Shift Register) with an asynchronous positive edge triggered reset and a synchronous enable signal. The LFSR should shift right and use XOR feedback.
(1) reset: Resets the LFSR to all ones.
(2) enable: Enables the shift operation.
(3) q: The current content of the LFSR.
