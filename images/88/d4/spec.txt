Upon reset, the LEDs are initialized to light up the most significant bit. Depending on the direction input, the LEDs will shift left or right on each `clk_bps` pulse, wrapping around when reaching the end of the LED range. If the LEDs reach the least significant bit while shifting right, they reset to the initial position; similarly, when shifting left and reaching the most significant bit, they reset to the least significant bit.
