Design a priority arbiter that resolves requests from four different sources (req0, req1, req2, req3) based on fixed priority. The arbiter should output the grant signal (grant) to indicate which source has been granted access to a shared resource. The priority order is req0 > req1 > req2 > req3. The arbiter should also have a clock signal (clk) and a reset signal (rst) to manage its operation.
