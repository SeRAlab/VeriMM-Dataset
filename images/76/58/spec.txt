Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 4-bit inputs (sum, cout, opB, or opA) based on a 2-bit selection signal (dsp_sel). The module continuously updates internal registers to hold the values of the input operands and the sum, while the output is determined by the value of dsp_sel: when dsp_sel is 00, output the lower 4 bits of sum; when 01, output the cout; when 10, output opB; and when 11, output opA. This design facilitates the selection of different data paths for further processing based on external signals.
