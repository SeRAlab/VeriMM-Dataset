Develop a comparison circuit that checks if two 8-bit inputs, a and b, are equal. It outputs a HIGH signal on `out` when the inputs are equal, and a LOW signal on `out_bar`. Conversely, `out_bar` will output a HIGH signal when the inputs are not equal, effectively providing both the result of the equality check and its negation.
module top_module(
    input [7:0] a,
    input [7:0] b,
    output out,
    output out_bar);
