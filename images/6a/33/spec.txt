Implement a comparator module that compares two inputs, A and B, and outputs the comparison results through three output signals: GTO for greater than, LTO for less than, and EQO for equal. If A is greater than B, GTO is asserted, while LTO and EQO are deasserted. If A is less than B, LTO is asserted, and GTO and EQO are deasserted. If A is equal to B, the module mirrors the states of the input flags (GTI, LTI, EQI) to the output EQO, GTO, and LTO, respectively.
module top_module(input A,input B,input GTI,input LTI,input EQI,output reg GTO,output reg LTO,output reg EQO);
