Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control input:
- 000: ADD
- 001: SUBTRACT
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
- 101: NAND
- 110: NOR
- 111: NOT (only on the first operand, second operand is ignored)
The result should be an 8-bit output, and an overflow flag should be generated for ADD and SUBTRACT operations.
