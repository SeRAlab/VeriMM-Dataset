Design a 2-bit shift register with asynchronous reset. The shift register should have two inputs: 'data_in' for input data and 'shift' signal to trigger the shifting operation. When 'shift' is high, the register should shift the bits to the right, and 'data_in' should be loaded into the leftmost bit. If 'reset' is high, the register should reset to '00'. The output of the register should be available as 'data_out'.
