The circuit can shift data left or right by two bits, as well as rotate the bits in either direction. The output is updated on the rising edge of a clock signal using a two-stage pipeline to enhance timing performance. This module effectively allows flexible manipulation of 4-bit input data for applications requiring bit-level adjustments.

    input [3:0] data,
