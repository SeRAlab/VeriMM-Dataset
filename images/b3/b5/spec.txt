Design a circuit that performs simultaneous multiplication of two pairs of 8-bit inputs. For the first pair, inputs a0 and b0 are multiplied to produce a 16-bit output z0, while the second pair, inputs a1 and b1, are multiplied to yield a 16-bit output z1. Both multiplication operations are triggered on the rising edge of the clock signal.
module top_module(input wire clk,input wire [ 7:0] a0,input wire [ 7:0] b0,output reg [15:0] z0,input wire [ 7:0] a1,input wire [ 7:0] b1,output reg [15:0] z1);
