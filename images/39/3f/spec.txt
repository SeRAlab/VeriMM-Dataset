
Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the fan speed based on temperature readings. The system should have a 2-bit input representing temperature levels (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high, and 11 for very high) and a 2-bit output representing fan speed (00 for off, 01 for low, 10 for medium, 11 for high). The fan speed should change only on the rising edge of a clock signal.
