This module facilitates communication between a microcontroller (or similar master device) and a VGA display, handling data transfer, address selection, and acknowledgments. The design includes toggling between these planes based on control signals and manages data flow while adhering to the Wishbone protocol for synchronous operation. The module incorporates acknowledgment signals to confirm successful transactions and ensures robustness through a reset state.
