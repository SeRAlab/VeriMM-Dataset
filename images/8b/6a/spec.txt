Design a negative-edge triggered D flip-flop with active-low clear functionality. The flip-flop captures the input data 'd' on the falling edge of the clock signal 'clk'. If the clear signal 'clr' is asserted low, the output 'q' is reset to LOW regardless of the clock state. If the clear signal is inactive, the output follows the input 'd' on the next falling clock edge.
