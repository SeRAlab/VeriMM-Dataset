
Design a fan speed control logic in Verilog. The fan has three speeds: low, medium, and high. The control logic should take a 2-bit input representing the desired speed (00 for off, 01 for low, 10 for medium, 11 for high) and a clock signal. The output should indicate the current speed of the fan. If the input changes, the fan speed should transition to the new speed on the next clock edge.
