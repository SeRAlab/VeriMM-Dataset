Design a power gating controller that controls the power domain of a device based on a power enable signal. The controller should take a 3-bit input representing the current state of the device (000 for off, 111 for fully powered) and a 1-bit power enable signal. The output should be a 3-bit power state that indicates the power level of the device, where the device can be powered down to a minimum state when the power enable signal is low.
