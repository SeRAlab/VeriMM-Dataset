Design a 4-bit comparator circuit that evaluates two 4-bit inputs, A and B. The module outputs three signals: EQ (equal), GT (greater than), and LT (less than). The EQ output is high when A is equal to B, the GT output is high when A is greater than B, and the LT output is high when A is less than B, allowing for simultaneous comparisons of the inputs.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output reg EQ,
    output reg GT,
    output reg LT
);
