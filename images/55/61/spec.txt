Design a 4-bit shift register that captures a serial input on the rising edge of the clock and outputs the parallel data on the falling edge. The register is reset to zero when the reset signal is asserted. Each clock cycle, the current contents of the register are shifted left by one position, and the new serial input is placed in the least significant bit. The output reflects the state of the register at the falling edge of the clock.
