Design a traffic light controller that cycles through green, yellow, and red light states based on a clock input. It uses a counter to manage timing, with the green light on for 10 seconds, yellow for 2 seconds, and red for 8 seconds. The system outputs the appropriate light signal while resetting the counter after one complete cycle.
module top_module(
  input clk,
  output reg green,
  output reg yellow,
  output reg red
);
