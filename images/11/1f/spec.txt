Design a 16-bit CRC generator that takes a 16-bit input data and generates a 16-bit CRC code. The CRC is based on a polynomial defined by the generator polynomial 0xA001 (which corresponds to the polynomial x^16 + x^15 + x^2 + 1). The circuit should be capable of processing data in a serial manner, where each bit of data is fed into the generator one at a time. The CRC should be updated on each bit and be available at the output after all bits have been processed.
