
Design a 4-bit SRAM cell that can store a 4-bit data word. The SRAM cell should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Inputs: `data_in` (4 bits), `write_enable` (1 bit), and `clk` (clock signal).
- Outputs: `data_out` (4 bits).
The cell should write the `data_in` to storage when `write_enable` is high and maintain the stored data on `data_out` when `write_enable` is low.
