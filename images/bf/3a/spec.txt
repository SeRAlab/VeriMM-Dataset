Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 72-bit input signals (x0, x1, x2, x3) based on a 2-bit selection input (sel) and outputs the selected signal (y). When sel is 00, output x0; 01 outputs x1; 10 outputs x2; and 11 outputs x3. This module enables the routing of a 72-bit data signal based on the selection criteria.
