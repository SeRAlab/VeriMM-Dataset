Design a GPIO controller that has the following functionality:
1. It has a 4-bit output (GPIO_OUT) and an input (GPIO_IN).
2. A control signal (ENABLE) that, when high, allows the GPIO_OUT to follow the GPIO_IN value.
3. When ENABLE is low, GPIO_OUT should hold its last value.
4. A reset signal (RST) that resets GPIO_OUT to 4'b0000 when asserted.
