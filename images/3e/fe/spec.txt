
Design a LIFO stack in Verilog. The stack should support the following operations:
1. Push: To add an item to the top of the stack.
2. Pop: To remove the item from the top of the stack.
3. Peek: To view the item on the top of the stack without removing it.
4. IsEmpty: To check if the stack is empty.
5. IsFull: To check if the stack is full.

The stack should have a fixed size defined by a parameter. The push and pop operations should be controlled by a clock and a reset signal. The stack should be able to hold 8-bit data.
