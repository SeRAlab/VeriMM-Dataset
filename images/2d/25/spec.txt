
Create a Verilog module that implements a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller should take a clock signal, a reset signal, and a voltage level as inputs. Based on the voltage level, the controller should adjust the frequency output to different levels (low, medium, high) and maintain a state representing the current frequency level. The frequency states should be encoded as 2 bits: 00 for low, 01 for medium, and 10 for high. Upon reset, the frequency level should be set to low.
