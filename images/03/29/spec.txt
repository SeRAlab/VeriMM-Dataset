Design a carrier frequency recovery circuit that uses a phase-locked loop (PLL). The circuit should have an input for the received signal and an output for the recovered carrier frequency signal. The PLL should include a phase detector, low-pass filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO). The phase detector will compare the input signal with the feedback from the VCO, and the output will be used to adjust the VCO frequency to lock onto the carrier frequency of the input signal.
