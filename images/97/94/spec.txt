Implement a game over detection circuit that sets the output signal `gameover` high when the positions of two entities (e.g., a player and a goal) on a 2D plane, defined by their x and y coordinates, match. The module checks the equality of the x and y coordinates of the two entities at each rising edge of the CLOCK_50 signal, indicating a clock cycle in the game logic. If both positions are the same, the game over signal is asserted; otherwise, it is deasserted.
