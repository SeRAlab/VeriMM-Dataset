Design a sound signal multiplier-accumulator capable of processing audio input data at a specified volume. The module takes a clock signal and inputs for volume (`vol_in`, 6 bits) and data (`dat_in`, 8 bits). It features control signals to load new data and clear the accumulated sum. The operation includes shifting and accumulating audio samples, managing an internal register to hold the current sum (`sum_out`, 16 bits), while providing a ready signal to indicate when it is finished processing. The design integrates a simple control mechanism via a counter to facilitate serial operations, and it uses combinatorial logic for managing the audio accumulation and carrying.
module top_module(

	clock,   

	vol_in,  
	dat_in,  

	load,    
	clr_sum, 

	ready,   
	sum_out  
);
