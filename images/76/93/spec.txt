Design a 3-bit shift register that can operate in two modes controlled by the 'shift' input signal. When 'shift' is high, the register shifts its contents to the left and inserts the input 'd' as the new least significant bit (LSB). When 'shift' is low, it shifts the content to the right while the most significant bit (MSB) retains its value, and the input 'd' is inserted as the new MSB. The output 'q' reflects the value of the MSB of the register at any clock edge.
module top_module( input clk, input d, input shift, output q );
