Design a clock gating mechanism using a transparent latch. The module outputs ENCLK, which is effectively the gated clock signal. When EN is asserted and TE is set, the latch allows the clock signal (CLK) to pass through as ENCLK. If EN is low, ENCLK follows the CLK signal. This design helps reduce power consumption in digital circuits by selectively enabling the clock signal based on the enable (EN) and test enable (TE) inputs.
