
Design a 64-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode input:
- 000: Add
- 001: Subtract
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
- 101: Shift Left
- 110: Shift Right
- 111: Compare (set output to 1 if A < B, else 0)

The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs (A and B) and produce a 64-bit output along with a 1-bit carry/borrow flag.
