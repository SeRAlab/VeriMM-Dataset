
Design a simple SATA controller state machine that manages the states of data transfer. The SATA controller should have three states: IDLE, TRANSFER, and DONE. The controller will transition from IDLE to TRANSFER when a 'start' signal is high. It will remain in the TRANSFER state while a 'busy' signal is high. Once 'busy' is low, it will transition to the DONE state. The controller will return to IDLE when a 'reset' signal is activated.
