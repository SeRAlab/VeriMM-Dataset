Design a circuit that performs an AND operation on two input signals, in1 and in2, under the control of an enable signal, en. The output, out, reflects the result of the AND operation only when en is high; otherwise, it becomes LOW. Additionally, the circuit provides an output signal, out_valid, that indicates whether the output is valid based on the state of the enable signal.
module top_module(
    in1,
    in2,
    en,
    out,
    out_valid
);
