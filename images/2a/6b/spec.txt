
Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor that predicts whether a branch will be taken or not based on the previous two branch outcomes. The predictor should have a 2-bit saturating counter for branch prediction. The states of the counter are as follows:
- 00: Strongly Not Taken
- 01: Weakly Not Taken
- 10: Weakly Taken
- 11: Strongly Taken

The branch predictor should take two inputs: the current branch outcome (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and a clock signal to update the counter. The output should be the predicted outcome based on the current state of the counter.
