Develop a circuit interface for a crystal oscillator that generates a clock signal and an enable signal. The module produces a square wave clock output (`clk`) at a specified frequency, determined by the `freq` parameter, and a duty cycle defined by the `duty_cycle` parameter. The `en` output signals high when the clock signal is in the high state and low otherwise. The internal logic utilizes a counter to accurately toggle the clock signal every half of the clock period, ensuring the desired frequency and duty cycle are maintained, facilitating synchronization for other components in a digital system.
module top_module(
  output reg clk,
  output reg en
);
