
Design a simple audio mixer that takes two audio signals (each 8-bit wide) and a control signal to either mix the two audio signals or output one of the signals directly. If the control signal is '1', the output should be the sum of the two signals, while if the control signal is '0', the output should be the first audio signal. Ensure that the output is limited to 8 bits (saturate to 255 if the sum exceeds 255).
