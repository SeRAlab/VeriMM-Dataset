Design a calculator module that performs multiplication and decoding of two 4-bit signed inputs, A and B. The module computes the signed product of A and B, producing an 8-bit result. It then decodes the sign bit of the multiplication result into a 3-bit output, which contributes to the final output. The combined output is displayed on an 8-bit LED indicator. The design implements a signed multiplier for arithmetic operations and a simple decoder to interpret the sign, ensuring that the results are visually represented by the LEDs in an inverted format.
module top_module(
    input signed [3:0] A,
    input signed [3:0] B,
    output [7:0] LEDS
);
