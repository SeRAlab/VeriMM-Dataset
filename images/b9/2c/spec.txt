Design a synchronous register module that captures and outputs two separate signals, `foo` and `bar`, on the rising edge of the clock. It updates `foo` with `foo_in` when `foo_valid` is high and updates `bar` with `bar_in` when `bar_valid` is high.
module top_module(input wire clk,
   output reg foo, bar,
   input wire foo_valid, foo_in,
   input wire bar_valid, bar_in
   );
