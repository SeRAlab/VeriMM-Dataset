Implement a Verilog module for point addition on an elliptic curve defined over a finite field. The inputs to the module should be the coordinates of two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) on the elliptic curve, and the output should be the coordinates (x3, y3) of the resulting point after addition. You can assume the elliptic curve is defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b, and the points are valid on this curve. The operation should follow the rules of projective coordinates to avoid division.
