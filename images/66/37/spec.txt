The module operates synchronously on a clock signal, utilizing a finite state machine (FSM) for managing the multiplication process. It initializes the product based on the least significant bit of B, then iteratively shifts A and B while adjusting the intermediate product based on the current state and the value of the least significant bit of B, until all bits of B are processed. The final output Y reflects the computed product.
module top_module(
    input signed [31:0] A,

    output signed [63:0] Y
