
Design an SR flip-flop with asynchronous reset using Verilog. The flip-flop should have inputs S (set), R (reset), and Clock (clk). The output should be Q and its complement Qn. The flip-flop should set Q to 1 when S is high and R is low, reset Q to 0 when R is high and S is low, and maintain its state otherwise. Implement the asynchronous reset feature, where R should take precedence over S when both are high.
