Develop a circuit that performs an operation combining XNOR logic and addition. The circuit takes two 8-bit inputs, x and y, computes their XNOR (bitwise complement equivalent) to produce an 8-bit result. This result is then concatenated with a 6-bit input, int_s0, to create a 14-bit operand. The lower 7 bits of this operand are added to the upper 7 bits (from the same 14-bit operand), producing a single output bit, s0, indicating the sum's carry-out, and a 7-bit output, cout, representing the lower bits of the sum. This functionality is suitable for applications needing combined logical and arithmetic operations.
module top_module(x, y, int_s0, s0, cout);
