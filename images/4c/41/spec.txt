Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs various arithmetic and logical operations based on a control input. The result of the operation is output as `data_o`, and the ALU can also set a zero output signal, `Zero_o`, based on the result. The design allows for flexibility in performing different computations as required by a larger digital system, such as a processor.
module top_module(
    data1_i,
    data2_i,
    ALUCtrl_i,
    data_o,
