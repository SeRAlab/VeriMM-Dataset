
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple ECC point addition operation. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) on the elliptic curve and produce the resulting point (x3, y3) after performing the addition. Assume the curve is defined over a finite field, and the inputs are represented in binary format. The output should also be in binary format. Include necessary control signals for the operation.
