Design a Verilog module that implements a simple audio mixer. The mixer takes two audio signals (A and B) as 8-bit inputs and a control signal (control) that determines how the two signals are mixed. If control is 0, the output (Y) should be the sum of A and B, and if control is 1, the output should be the difference (A - B). The output should be clamped to fit within 8 bits (0 to 255) to prevent overflow.
