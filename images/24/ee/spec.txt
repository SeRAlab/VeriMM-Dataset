Design a 64-bit ALU that can perform basic arithmetic and logical operations including addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, bitwise OR, and bitwise XOR based on a 3-bit operation selector. The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs and output a 64-bit result. The operation selector works as follows: 
000 - Addition
001 - Subtraction
010 - Bitwise AND
011 - Bitwise OR
100 - Bitwise XOR
The ALU also has a zero flag output that indicates if the result is zero.
