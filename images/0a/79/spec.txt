
You are tasked with designing a simple digital temperature sensor interface in Verilog. The sensor outputs a 5-bit binary value that represents temperature in degrees Celsius from 0 to 31. Your design should convert this binary value into a 3-bit output value that indicates whether the temperature is Low (0), Medium (1), or High (2). Define the thresholds as follows:
- Low: 0 to 10 degrees
- Medium: 11 to 20 degrees
- High: 21 to 31 degrees
The output should be valid whenever the input is stable.
