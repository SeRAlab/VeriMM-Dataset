
Design a hazard detection circuit that identifies data hazards in a pipelined CPU. The circuit should take three inputs: `current_instruction`, `next_instruction`, and `writeback_instruction`. The output should indicate whether a hazard is present by outputting a signal `hazard_detected`. A hazard is detected if:
1. The `current_instruction` and `next_instruction` are both trying to write to the same register.
2. The `writeback_instruction` is trying to write to the same register as the `current_instruction` before it is read.
