
Design a Fractional-N PLL with a phase detector, a loop filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO). The PLL should accept a reference input frequency and a fractional value to generate a desired output frequency. The phase detector will produce a control signal based on the phase difference between the reference signal and the VCO output. Implement the logic to maintain the desired frequency by adjusting the VCO based on the control signal. Ensure that the design handles fractional division correctly.
