Design a PCIe packet checker that verifies the integrity of a PCIe packet. The module should check for the validity of the packet based on the following criteria:
1. The packet must start with a predefined header (e.g., a specific bit pattern).
2. The packet length must not exceed a specified maximum length.
3. The checksum of the packet must match a calculated value.
The module has one input for the packet data, a clock signal, a reset signal, and an output signal indicating whether the packet is valid.
