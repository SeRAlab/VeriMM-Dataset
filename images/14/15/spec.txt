Design a simple audio compressor module that reduces the dynamic range of audio signals. The module should take a 16-bit audio sample as input and output a compressed 16-bit audio sample. The compression ratio can be controlled by a 2-bit selector input. The module should include the following functionalities:
- Selector input: 00 for no compression, 01 for mild compression, 10 for medium compression, and 11 for high compression.
- The module should perform the compression in a clocked manner, updating the output on a rising clock edge.
