The output is divided into two 8-bit outputs: `out_hi` outputs the higher 8 bits of the Gray code, while `out_lo` outputs the lower 8 bits. The Gray code is computed using the formula that involves XORing the count value with its right-shifted version, ensuring that only one bit changes at a time in successive counts.
