Design a gated D flip-flop with enable (EN) and toggle enable (TE) inputs. The flip-flop captures the clock input (CLK) on its rising edge when the enable signal (EN) is high and toggle enable (TE) is low, setting the output (Q) to 1. If enable is not asserted or toggle enable is active, the output resets to 0. Additionally, when the toggle enable (TE) input is asserted, it allows the output (Q) to directly follow the clock signal, creating a transparent latch condition. This design is useful for controlling data flow in synchronous systems based on specific control signals.
