Design a 3-bit up/down counter that increments or decrements its count based on the `up_down` control signal. When `up_down` is high, the counter counts up from 000 to 111, wrapping around back to 000 after reaching 111. Conversely, when `up_down` is low, the counter counts down from 111 to 000, wrapping back to 111 after reaching 000. The counter resets to 000 upon receiving a reset signal.
module top_module(
  input clk,
  input reset,
  input up_down,
  output reg [2:0] count
);
