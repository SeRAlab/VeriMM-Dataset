Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that accepts four input signals (IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4) and selects one of them as the output (Q) based on the two selection signals (S0 and S1). When both selection signals are LOW (0), IN1 is chosen; if S0 is LOW and S1 is HIGH (1), IN2 is selected; if S0 is HIGH and S1 is LOW, IN3 is output; and if both selection signals are HIGH, IN4 is the result. The module includes power supply inputs (VDD, VSS) for operation.
module top_module(input IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4, output Q, input S0, S1, VDD, VSS);
