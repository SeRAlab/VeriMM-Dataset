Design a timing control module that outputs a bunch signal (`bunch_strb`) based on specific strobe inputs and a clock signal. The module counts "bunches" of samples and produces a strobe signal when certain conditions involving start and end timing are met, particularly influenced by sample spacing. It tracks a counter to manage the accumulation of "bunches" based on the inputs `no_bunches` and `no_samples`. When `store_strb` is active, it increments a sample index, and upon reaching configured bounds defined by input strobe signals, it toggles the output signal accordingly. This design is intended for applications requiring precise timing control in sample processing or data acquisition systems.
