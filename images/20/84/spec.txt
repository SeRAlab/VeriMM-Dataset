Design a finite state machine (FSM) that implements a simplified version of the AES encryption process. The FSM should take a 128-bit input data block and a 128-bit key, and output a 128-bit encrypted data block. The AES encryption process consists of several rounds, and for simplicity, you can implement only 2 rounds of the AES algorithm. The FSM should transition through states for key expansion, initial round, and the two rounds of encryption, producing the output at the end of the second round.
