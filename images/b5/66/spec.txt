Design a 4-bit shift register with load and shift capabilities. On the rising edge of the clock, if the load signal is asserted, the register will load the input values; otherwise, if the shift signal is asserted, it shifts the current register contents to the left by one position while filling the least significant bit with zero. The current value of the register is continuously available at the output.
