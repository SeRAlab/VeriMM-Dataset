Design a circuit that interfaces with a 16-bit switch input to drive a seven-segment display and LEDs. The lower 8 bits of the switch input (sw[7:0]) are inverted and mapped to the segments of a seven-segment display (ca through cg) and a decimal point (dp). The upper 8 bits of the switch input (sw[15:8]) are inverted and output to the display's active low anode lines (an). Additionally, the entire switch input is directly output to a 16-bit LED driver (led), effectively reflecting the status of the switches.
module top_module(sw, ca, cb, cc, cd, ce, cf, cg, dp, an, led);
