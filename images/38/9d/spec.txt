Design a 16-to-4 priority encoder in Verilog. The encoder should take a 16-bit input and output a 4-bit binary representation of the highest priority active input. If no inputs are active, the output should be 4'b1111 (indicating 'no input'). The inputs are labeled as `in[15:0]`, where `in[0]` has the highest priority and `in[15]` has the lowest priority.
