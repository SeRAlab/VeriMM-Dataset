
You need to create a temperature sensor circuit that takes a 5-bit binary input representing temperature in degree Celsius and outputs a 3-bit binary code indicating the category of the temperature:
- 000 for cold (0-15°C)
- 001 for warm (16-25°C)
- 010 for hot (26-35°C)
- 011 for very hot (36-45°C)
- 100 for extreme hot (46°C and above)
