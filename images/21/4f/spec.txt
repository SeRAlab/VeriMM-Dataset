Design an SRT (Sweeney, Robertson, and Tocher) divider that takes two binary numbers as input: a 4-bit dividend and a 4-bit divisor, and produces a 4-bit quotient and a 4-bit remainder. The SRT divider should perform the division based on the SRT algorithm, which utilizes subtraction and shifting to compute the quotient and the remainder. The output should be valid when a start signal is asserted.
