Design a Fan Speed Controller using a Verilog module. The controller should take an input representing the temperature level (in degrees Celsius), and based on this input, it will control the fan speed in three levels: 
- Low (00): for temperatures 0-20 degrees
- Medium (01): for temperatures 21-30 degrees
- High (10): for temperatures above 30 degrees
The output will be a 2-bit code representing the fan speed.
