    up_down_counter

None

Counter Logic:
The counter increments or decrements its value based on the up_down signal.
Synchronous Clear Logic:
The counter is reset to zero on the rising edge of the clock when the clr signal is asserted.

Output Assignments:
The count output is assigned the value of the counter register.
