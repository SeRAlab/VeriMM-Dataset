
Design a 64-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode input:
- `000`: Addition
- `001`: Subtraction
- `010`: Bitwise AND
- `011`: Bitwise OR
- `100`: Bitwise XOR
- `101`: Shift Left
- `110`: Shift Right
- `111`: Set on Less Than (SLT)

The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs and produce a 64-bit output based on the selected operation. Additionally, the ALU should include a carry-out signal for addition and subtraction operations.
