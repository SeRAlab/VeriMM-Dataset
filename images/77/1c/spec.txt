Design a circuit that inverts the upper nibble (bits 15 to 12) of a 16-bit input when the enable signal is triggered on the positive edge. The lower nibble (bits 11 to 0) remains unchanged. The output includes a valid flag that indicates when the output data is valid, which is asserted when the data has been processed and reset on the negative edge of the enable signal.
module top_module(
  input [15:0] data_in,
  input enable,
  output reg [15:0] data_out,
  output reg valid
);
