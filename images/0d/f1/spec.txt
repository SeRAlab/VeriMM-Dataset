Design a simple Verilog module that implements a single-layer perceptron for a neural network. The perceptron should take two 1-bit inputs (x1, x2) and produce a 1-bit output (y) based on weights w1 and w2, which are fixed values of 1 and -1 respectively. The perceptron also needs to have a bias input (b). The activation function should be a simple step function, where if the weighted sum (w1*x1 + w2*x2 + b) is greater than or equal to zero, the output (y) is 1; otherwise, it is 0. The bias input will be 0 for simplicity.
