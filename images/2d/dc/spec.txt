Design a Verilog module for an SR latch using NAND gates. The SR latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn). The behavior should be as follows: when S is high and R is low, Q should be high; when S is low and R is high, Q should be low; when both S and R are low, the previous state should be maintained; when both S and R are high, the output is undefined (but for simulation, you can assume it remains in the previous state).
