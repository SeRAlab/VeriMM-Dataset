Design a control signal multiplexer that outputs three sets of signals: EX_signal_o, MEM_signal_o, and WB_signal_o. The selection of these outputs is governed by two control inputs: stall_select_i and flush_select_i. If either stall_select_i or flush_select_i is asserted (i.e., set to high), the module outputs undefined states for all signals, indicating a stall or flush condition. Otherwise, it simply forwards the corresponding input signals (EX_signal_i, MEM_signal_i, and WB_signal_i) directly to their respective outputs. This design is useful in contexts such as pipeline control in microprocessors, where managing the flow of signals during stalled or flushed states is critical.

    MEM_signal_o   ,
    WB_signal_o   
);
