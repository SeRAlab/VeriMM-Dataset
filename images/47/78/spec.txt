Design a digital frequency counter that processes a clock input (clk) and a signal input (sig) to measure the frequency of the input signal. The module maintains a 14-bit counter to track the elapsed clock cycles and a 7-bit counter for the signal. Upon detecting a rising edge on the signal input, it calculates the frequency by dividing a predefined constant by the number of clock cycles counted since the last signal pulse, provided that the change in clock counts exceeds a defined minimum threshold. The output, f, represents the computed frequency based on these conditions, and the counter resets appropriately after each signal edge detection.
