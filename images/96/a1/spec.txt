Design a 4-bit wide shift register with parallel load capability. When the load signal is asserted, the register takes its input from the parallel_in bus, loading all bits simultaneously. Otherwise, the register shifts its contents to the left on each clock cycle, with the serial input (shift_in) being introduced at the least significant bit. The module outputs the current state of the shift register through parallel_out and provides the least significant bit of the register as serial_out.
module top_module(clk, load, parallel_in, shift_in, parallel_out, serial_out);
