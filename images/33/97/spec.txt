Design a 1-bit SRAM cell in Verilog that can store a single bit of data. The SRAM cell should have the following inputs: a write enable signal (we), a data input (d), a wordline signal (wl), and an output signal (q). The SRAM cell should have two cross-coupled inverters to maintain the state and should change its state based on the inputs when the wordline is high and write enable is active.
