Design a synchronous 8-bit counter with a programmable load feature and carry output. It operates on the rising edge of the clock and is reset when the reset signal is active. The counter increments on each clock cycle when both clock enable and a separate job clock enable signal are asserted. When the load signal is activated, the counter resets to zero unless the job clock enable signal is simultaneously active, in which case it loads a specific value of one. Additionally, the counter generates a carry signal when it reaches a specified modulus value, indicating an overflow condition for further cascading in digital applications.

  clk, rst, clk_ena,
  
  clk_ena_job,  
  
  load,  
  
  carry,  
  
  
  dfload,  
  
  dout  
);
