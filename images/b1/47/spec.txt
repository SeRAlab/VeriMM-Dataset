Design a 3-bit shift register that captures a single input data bit `d` on the rising edge of the clock `clk` and shifts the existing data through the three-bit output `q`. The least significant bit of the output is directly assigned the input `d`, while the other bits store the previous values from the output, enabling a simple serial-in, parallel-out configuration.
