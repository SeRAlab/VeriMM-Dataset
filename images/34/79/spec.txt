
Design a Verilog module that performs Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (QAM) for a given input of 4 bits. The module should take two 4-bit inputs representing in-phase (I) and quadrature (Q) components and output a 2-bit QAM symbol. The QAM symbol should be determined by mapping the I and Q values to a corresponding QAM constellation point. You need to create a mapping for 16-QAM.
