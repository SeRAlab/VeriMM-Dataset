Design a 4-bit shift register module with a resetting capability. The register stores a 4-bit value, shifting its contents to the left on every clock cycle when the shift signal is asserted. The most recent input data is captured at the least significant bit during a shift operation. If the reset signal is activated, the entire register is cleared to zero. The output provides the most significant bit of the register for external use.
