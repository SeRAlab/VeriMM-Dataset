This Verilog module implements a logic circuit that utilizes a 2-input OR gate configured as a dual-input (O22) cell, which produces an output signal X based on the logical OR operation of inputs A1 and A2, as well as B1 and B2. It also replicates the value of X to output Y. The module includes power and ground connections (VPWR, VGND, VPB, VNB) typical for standard-cell designs in digital integrated circuits, ensuring proper functionality within a larger system. The design purpose is to facilitate a conditional logic output through the OR operation while providing redundancy with the output connection to Y.

    X   ,

    A2  ,

    VPWR,
    VGND,

    Y
);
