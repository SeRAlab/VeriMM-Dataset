Design a digital circuit that generates the output Y based on the inputs A1, A2, A3, and B1, as well as power and ground signals VPWR, VGND, VPB, and VNB. The output Y is HIGH if all A inputs are HIGH (A1, A2, A3), or if VPWR is HIGH, or if VPB is HIGH while VNB is LOW. This output is prevented from being HIGH if B1 is HIGH, VGND is HIGH, or if VPB is LOW while VNB is HIGH. The circuit effectively combines logic operations involving multiple inputs and power management signals to control the output state.
module top_module(
    Y   ,
    A1  ,
    A2  ,
    A3  ,
    B1  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND,
    VPB ,
    VNB
);
