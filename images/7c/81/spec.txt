Design a signal synchronization and touch detection module. The module synchronizes multiple input signals (reset and several pad inputs) to a clock signal, ensuring that changes in these signals are aligned with the clock's rising edge. It outputs a synchronized reset signal and individual synchronized outputs for each pad input. Additionally, it produces a combined output indicating if any of the pad inputs are active (touched), by performing a logical OR operation on the synchronized pad signals.
module top_module(input clock,input reset,input c_pad,input d_pad,input e_pad,input f_pad,input g_pad,output reg reset_sync,output reg c_pad_sync,output reg d_pad_sync,output reg e_pad_sync,output reg f_pad_sync,output reg g_pad_sync,output pad_touched);
