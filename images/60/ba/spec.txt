Design a binary-coded decimal (BCD) adder that adds two 4-bit BCD numbers, A and B, along with a carry-in (Cin). The circuit computes the sum of A, B, and Cin, and if the resulting sum exceeds the BCD limit (9 in decimal, or 01001 in binary), it corrects the sum by adding 6 (0110 in binary) to ensure valid BCD output. The module outputs a 4-bit sum (Sum) and a carry-out (Cout) to indicate if the BCD addition resulted in an overflow.
module top_module(output reg [3: 0] Sum, output reg Cout, input [3 : 0] A, B, input Cin);
