It includes load functionality, allowing a specific value (`load_val`) to be loaded into the counter when the `load` signal is asserted. The counter resets to zero when the `reset` signal is activated. The counting operation occurs with each positive edge of the clock (`clk`). When `up_down` is high, the counter increments; when low, it decrements.
