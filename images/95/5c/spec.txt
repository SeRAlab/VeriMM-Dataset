For sel=01, it performs a bitwise AND with the mask 1100, effectively clearing the lower two bits. With sel=10, it executes a bitwise OR with the mask 0011, setting the lower two bits to 1 regardless of the input. Lastly, when sel=11, it applies a bitwise XOR with the mask 1010.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] in,
    input [1:0] sel,
    output reg [3:0] out
);
