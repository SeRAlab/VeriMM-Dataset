Design a 4-bit up/down counter that increments or decrements its value based on control signals. The counter resets to zero when the reset signal is low. When the reset is high, if both the enable signal and the up_en signal are active, the counter will count upwards by one. Conversely, if the enable signal is active and the down_en signal is high, the counter will count downwards by one.
