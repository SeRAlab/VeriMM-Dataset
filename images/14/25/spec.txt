Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (opcode):  
- 000: AND  
- 001: OR  
- 010: XOR  
- 011: ADD  
- 100: SUB  
The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and output an 8-bit result. Additionally, there should be a carry-out output for the ADD and SUB operations.
