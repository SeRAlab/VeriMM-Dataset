Depending on the value of S, Y will reflect the value of one of the four inputs: when S is 00, Y outputs D[0]; when S is 01, Y outputs D[1]; when S is 10, Y outputs D[2]; and when S is 11, Y outputs D[3].

	input  [3:0] D,
