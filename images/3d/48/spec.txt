A 24-bit Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) that takes a 24-bit input value and converts it to an analog voltage level. The voltage level can be calculated using the formula: V_out = (input_value / 2^24) * V_ref, where V_ref is the reference voltage. The DAC will output a 24-bit value representing the voltage level in digital form (scaled by a factor for precision).
