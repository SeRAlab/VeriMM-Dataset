
Design a non-restoring divider in Verilog. The divider should take a 16-bit dividend and a 16-bit divisor as inputs and produce a 16-bit quotient and a 16-bit remainder as outputs. The division process should be implemented as a sequential logic circuit, which means it should use a clock signal to synchronize the division steps. The design should iteratively perform the subtraction and shifting operations required for division.
