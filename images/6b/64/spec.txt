Design an 8-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) capable of performing various arithmetic and logical operations. The unit accepts two 8-bit inputs, A and B, a 3-bit operation code (OP) for selecting the desired operation, and control inputs for two's complement negation (NEG), carry-in (C_IN), and a data input (D_IN). Depending on the operation code, the ALU can perform operations such as addition (with optional negation and carry-in), bitwise logical AND, OR, and XOR, as well as shift operations (left and right) and rotate operations. It also generates carry-out (C_OUT) and overflow (V_OUT) flags for specific operations, ensuring that the outputs reflect the results and any status indicators relevant to the arithmetic operations carried out.
