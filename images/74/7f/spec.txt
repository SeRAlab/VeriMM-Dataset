Design a one-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer. The output Y is determined by the selector input SEL; when SEL is 0, the output Y takes the value of input A, and when SEL is 1, Y takes the value of input B. The module also includes power and ground inputs (VPWR, VGND) along with substrate connections (VPB, VNB) for proper functioning in a larger integrated circuit environment.
module top_module(
    Y   ,
    A   ,
    B   ,
    SEL ,
    VPWR,
    VGND,
    VPB ,
    VNB
);
