Design a 4-bit synchronous up-counter with a reset and enable feature. The counter increments on the rising edge of the clock when the enable signal is active. If the reset signal is asserted, the counter resets to zero. The counter wraps around back to zero after reaching a maximum value of 9 (1001 in binary).
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input reset,
    input enable,
    output reg [3:0] count_out
);
