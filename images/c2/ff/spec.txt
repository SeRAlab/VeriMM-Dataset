

You are tasked with designing a clock gating circuit that will be used in a larger system. The circuit should take in three inputs: a clock signal (`CLK`), an enable signal (`EN`), and a test enable signal (`TE`). The circuit should output an enable clock signal (`ENCLK`), which should be high only when both the `EN` and `TE` signals are high.

Design a Verilog module that implements this circuit. Your module should be named `clock_gate` and should have the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- `CLK`: a 1-bit clock signal
- `EN`: a 1-bit enable signal
- `TE`: a 1-bit test enable signal

Outputs:
- `ENCLK`: a 1-bit enable clock signal

Assume that all inputs will be synchronous to the same clock and that the `ENCLK` output should also be synchronous to this clock.

Your module should be self-contained and should not require any additional modules or libraries.
