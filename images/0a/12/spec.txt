Design a 4-to-2 binary encoder in Verilog. The encoder should have four input lines (I0, I1, I2, I3) and two output lines (Y1, Y0) along with an enable signal (EN). When EN is high, the encoder should output the binary representation of the highest priority input that is high. If no inputs are high and EN is high, the output should be 2'b00. If EN is low, both outputs should be 2'bxx (undefined).
