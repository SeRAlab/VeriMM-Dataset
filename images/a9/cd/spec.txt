Design a Multiply-Accumulate (MAC) unit that computes the product of two 16-bit signed inputs, A and B, and adds or subtracts a carry-in value based on a conditional compilation flag. The result of the multiplication is stored in a 32-bit signed register, and upon each clock cycle, the output P is updated with the sum or difference of the multiplication result and the carry-in. The circuit resets the outputs to zero when the reset signal is low. The design therefore combines multiplication and accumulation functionality, providing flexibility to either add or subtract based on the defined compilation condition.
