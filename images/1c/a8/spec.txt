Design an 8-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift capabilities. The shift register should have the following inputs: `clk` (clock), `load` (load signal), `shift` (shift signal), and an 8-bit input `data`. The output should be the current state of the shift register. When the `load` signal is high, the `data` input should be loaded into the shift register. When the `shift` signal is high, the contents of the shift register should shift right by one position, and a '0' should be inserted at the MSB (most significant bit) position. If both `load` and `shift` are low, the shift register should maintain its current state.
