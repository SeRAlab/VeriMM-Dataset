Design a full adder circuit that computes the sum of three input bits: A, B, and a carry-in (CI). The module outputs the sum (SUM) as a single bit and the carry-out (COUT), indicating whether there is a carry to the next significant bit. The design accounts for power and ground connections (VPWR and VGND) typically used in integrated circuit (IC) implementations.
module top_module(
  input wire A, B, CI, VPWR, VGND,
  output wire SUM, COUT
);
