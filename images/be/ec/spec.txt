Design a vending machine controller that manages candy and change dispensing. The module takes a 50 MHz clock input and allows for the insertion of coins (5, 10, or 25 cents) and a button to initiate candy dispensing. Upon receiving a reset signal, it initializes its internal counters for candy and change. When the button is pressed, and enough money has been inserted, the machine dispenses candy and calculates the appropriate change based on the inserted amount minus the candy price. The outputs indicate whether candy has been dispensed and the amount of change returned.
module top_module(CLOCK_50, COIN, BUTTON, RESET, CANDY_DISPENSE, CHANGE_DISPENSE);
