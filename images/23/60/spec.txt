Design a simple branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should take a 1-bit input representing the actual outcome of a branch (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and a 1-bit input representing the predicted outcome. The predictor should update its prediction based on the actual outcome. The internal state will be represented by a 2-bit counter which has four states: Strongly Not Taken, Weakly Not Taken, Weakly Taken, and Strongly Taken. The output will be the predicted outcome.
