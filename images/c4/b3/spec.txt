

You are tasked with designing a module that takes in a 4-bit binary input and outputs its two's complement. The module should be able to handle both positive and negative numbers. 

The two's complement of a binary number is obtained by inverting all the bits (changing 0s to 1s and 1s to 0s) and adding 1 to the result. For example, the two's complement of 0101 is 1011.

Your module should have the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- `in` (4-bit binary number)

Outputs:
- `out` (4-bit two's complement binary number)

Your module should be named `twos_complement`.
