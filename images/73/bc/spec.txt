Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four single-bit input lines (in_0, in_1, in_2, in_3) based on a 2-bit selection signal (sel). When sel is set to '00', the output will be in_0; '01' will choose in_1; '10' selects in_2; and '11' selects in_3. The module efficiently combines two 2-to-1 multiplexers to route the appropriate input to the output signal (out).
module top_module(

    in_0,
    in_1,
    in_2,
    in_3,
    sel,
    out
    );
