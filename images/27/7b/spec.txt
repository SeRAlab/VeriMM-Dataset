Design a 2-input logistic regression unit that takes two 1-bit binary inputs (x1 and x2) and produces a 1-bit binary output (y) based on the formula: y = 1 / (1 + exp(-W1*x1 - W2*x2)), where W1 and W2 are the weights (for simplicity, let W1 = 1 and W2 = 1). The output y should be rounded to the nearest integer (0 or 1).
