Design a Verilog module that implements a SRT (Sweeney, Robertson, and Tocher) divider. The divider should take two inputs: a numerator and a denominator, both of which are 8-bit binary numbers. The output should be the quotient (also 8 bits) and the remainder (8 bits). The module should handle cases where the denominator is zero by outputting a specific error code (for example, outputting 255 for both quotient and remainder in such cases).
