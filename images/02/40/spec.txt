Design a 2-channel DMA (Direct Memory Access) controller that can handle two independent data transfer requests. The DMA controller should have the following signals: `req1`, `req2` (request signals from two channels), `ack1`, `ack2` (acknowledge signals for the two channels), `clk` (clock), and `reset` (to reset the controller). When `req1` is active, the controller should acknowledge `ack1` after one clock cycle, and similarly for `req2` with `ack2`. If both requests are active at the same time, prioritize `req1` over `req2`. If the reset signal is active, both `ack1` and `ack2` should be deactivated.
