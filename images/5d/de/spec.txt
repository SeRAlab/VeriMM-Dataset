If the most significant bit (bit 15) of the input is 1, the output will be filled with ones in the upper 16 bits; if it is 0, the upper 16 bits will be filled with zeros, effectively preserving the sign of the original value.
module top_module(
  input [15:0] in,
  output reg [31:0] out
);
