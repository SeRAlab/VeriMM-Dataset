Design a thermal management system that controls the speed of a fan based on the input temperature. The module takes a temperature input and outputs a fan speed that varies linearly between a minimum and maximum speed, corresponding to a specified temperature range. If the temperature is below the minimum threshold (20°C), the fan operates at its minimum speed (0%). If the temperature exceeds the maximum threshold (80°C), the fan runs at full speed (100%). For temperatures within this range, the fan speed is calculated proportionally to the temperature to maintain effective cooling.
module top_module(
  input temp,
  output fan_speed
);
