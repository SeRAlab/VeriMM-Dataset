Design a three-input logic circuit that implements a composite AND gate functionality. The circuit takes two direct inputs (A1 and A2) and one inverted input (B1_N). It first generates the logical NOT of B1_N, then computes the AND of A1 and A2, and finally produces the output Y by performing a second AND operation between the result of the first AND and the inverted B1_N. The output Y will be high only when both A1 and A2 are high, and B1_N is low.
module top_module(
    input A1,
    input A2,
    input B1_N,
    output Y
);
