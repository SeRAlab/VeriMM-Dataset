    parallel_to_serial

On every rising edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n), the serial data is updated.

If the load signal is high, the parallel data (parallel_data) is loaded into a shift register.
Otherwise, the shift register shifts right by 1 bit and the least significant bit is output as serial data (serial_out).
