
Design an SR flip-flop in Verilog. The flip-flop should have two inputs, S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs, Q and Qn (the inverse of Q). The flip-flop should behave according to the following truth table:
- When S = 1 and R = 0, Q should be set to 1.
- When S = 0 and R = 1, Q should be reset to 0.
- When S = 0 and R = 0, Q should retain its previous value.
- When S = 1 and R = 1, the output is considered a forbidden state.
