
Create a 4-point FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) module in Verilog that takes 4 complex input samples and computes the 4-point FFT. The inputs will be two 4-bit real parts and two 4-bit imaginary parts for each of the 4 complex numbers. The outputs should be similarly structured, with the real and imaginary parts of the transformed complex numbers. You need to handle the FFT stages and utilize a sequential logic approach to manage the calculations and state transitions.
