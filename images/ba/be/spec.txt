Design a clock gating mechanism with a D flip-flop that generates an enable clock signal (ENCLK) based on input conditions. The module takes a clock signal (CLK), an enable signal (EN), and a test enable signal (TE) as inputs. When both EN and TE are asserted, the clock signal is passed through to a gated register; otherwise, the gated clock is set to LOW. The output of the D flip-flop captures the gated clock, which is then assigned to ENCLK. This design is useful for power management in digital circuits by selectively enabling the clock signal only under specific conditions.
