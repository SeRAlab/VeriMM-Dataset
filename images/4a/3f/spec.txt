It supports addition and subtraction (using 2's complement for subtraction), as well as bitwise AND and OR operations. The output includes the operation result and a set of flags: the sign flag, zero flag, carry-out flag, and overflow flag. The module efficiently handles both unsigned and signed operations and provides condition codes that are useful for subsequent decision-making in control logic.
