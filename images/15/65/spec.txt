Design a 64-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (opcode):  
0: Addition  
1: Subtraction  
2: Bitwise AND  
3: Bitwise OR  
4: Bitwise XOR  
5: Logical Shift Left  
6: Logical Shift Right  
7: Set on Less Than  
The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs and output a 64-bit result based on the selected operation. The design should also include an output for a carry flag, which indicates whether there was a carry out from the most significant bit during addition or subtraction operations.
