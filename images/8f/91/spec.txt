Design a D-type flip-flop with asynchronous set and reset capabilities. The module captures the value of the input D on the rising edge of the CLK signal and outputs the stored value at Q. The Q_N output provides the inverse of Q. The flip-flop also includes active-low control signals for asynchronous set (SET_B) and reset (RESET_B), allowing the output to be set to high or reset to low independently of the clock.

    Q      ,
    Q_N    ,
