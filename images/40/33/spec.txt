Design a 2-channel DMA controller that manages two data transfer requests. The controller will have the following inputs: a clock signal (clk), a reset signal (reset), two request signals (req1 and req2), and two acknowledge signals (ack1 and ack2) from the two channels. The output will be a busy signal to indicate when the DMA controller is processing a request. The controller should prioritize req1 over req2 if both requests are active simultaneously. On reset, the busy signal should be low.
