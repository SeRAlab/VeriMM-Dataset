The module takes four binary inputs (x, y, z, w), and based on the combination of these inputs, it activates one of the 16 output lines (d) if the enable input (e) is asserted. When e is low, all outputs are inactive; when e is high, the corresponding output line for the binary value represented by the inputs x, y, z, and w is driven high, effectively decoding the 4-bit input into a 16-line output.
