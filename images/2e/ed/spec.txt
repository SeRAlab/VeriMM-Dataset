
Design a Verilog module that implements a simplified version of the Black-Scholes model to calculate the theoretical price of a European call option based on the following inputs:
- S: Current stock price (fixed point representation, 16 bits)
- K: Strike price (fixed point representation, 16 bits)
- T: Time to expiration in years (fixed point representation, 8 bits)
- r: Risk-free interest rate (fixed point representation, 8 bits)
- sigma: Volatility of the stock (fixed point representation, 8 bits)

The output of the module should be the calculated option price (fixed point representation, 16 bits). Use a combination of combinational logic to approximate the calculations needed for the Black-Scholes formula.
