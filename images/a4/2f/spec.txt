Design a 4-bit right circular shift register that initializes to the value `0001` and rotates its bits on every positive clock edge, starting after an initial delay. The first output, D1, corresponds to the least significant bit (LSB) of the register, while outputs D2, D3, and D4 correspond to the other bits of the register. Output D5 is a constant high signal. The module effectively creates a sequential operation where the bits are shifted right upon activation.
