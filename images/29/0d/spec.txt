Design a simple branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor takes a 1-bit input (taken/nottaken) and predicts whether a branch is taken or not. The counter should be updated based on the actual outcome, and the prediction should be outputted. The states of the counter should be represented as follows: 00 - Strongly Nottaken, 01 - Weakly Nottaken, 10 - Weakly Taken, 11 - Strongly Taken.
