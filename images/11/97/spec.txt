Create a Verilog module that implements a simple FFT butterfly operation. The module should take two complex inputs (each represented by two 8-bit values for real and imaginary parts) and produce two complex outputs. The operation will use the formula:  
Output1 = Input1 + (W * Input2)  
Output2 = Input1 - (W * Input2)  
where W is the complex unity root of unity for FFT. The design should allow for parameterization of the bit-width of the inputs.
