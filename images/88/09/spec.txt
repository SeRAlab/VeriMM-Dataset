Design a serial sender that transmits an 8-bit input (din) serially, accompanied by a parity bit. Upon the assertion of the strobe signal, the sender loads the shift register with the 8-bit data from din followed by its parity bit (even parity calculated via the XOR operation on the input). On each clock cycle (clk), the sender outputs the most significant bit (MSB) of the shift register (dout) and shifts the register to prepare for the next bit transmission, effectively serializing the data over time.
