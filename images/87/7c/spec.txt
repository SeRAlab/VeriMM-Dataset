Design a circuit that functions as a five-input logic system which utilizes a combination of AND and OR gates. Specifically, the circuit first computes the AND of input1 and input2, as well as the AND of input3 and input4, and then further ANDs that result with input5. The final output is determined by OR-ing the result of the first AND operation with the output of the second AND operation. This results in an output that is HIGH when certain combinations of the five inputs are active.
module top_module(
    input input1,
    input input2,
    input input3,
    input input4,
    input input5,
    output output1
);
