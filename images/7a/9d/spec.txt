Design a circuit to detect the rising and falling edges of a digital signal. On each clock cycle, it captures the current state of the input signal and compares it to the previous state. The output 'rise' is asserted high when a transition from low to high occurs (indicating a rising edge), while the output 'fall' is asserted high when a transition from high to low occurs (indicating a falling edge). The module also includes an asynchronous reset input to initialize the internal state.
module top_module(input rst,input clk,input sig,output rise,output fall);
