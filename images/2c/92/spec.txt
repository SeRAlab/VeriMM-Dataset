Design a Verilog module that simulates a simple WiFi signal strength indicator. The module takes a 4-bit input representing the signal strength (from 0 to 15) and outputs a 3-bit value that categorizes the strength into three levels: weak (0), medium (1), and strong (2). The mapping should be as follows: 0-4 -> weak, 5-10 -> medium, 11-15 -> strong.
