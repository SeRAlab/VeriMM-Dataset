Design a combinational circuit that implements a simple Support Vector Machine (SVM) decision function. The circuit should take three inputs: two feature values (x1 and x2) and a weight vector (w1 and w2), and produce a single output that indicates whether the input point falls above or below the decision boundary defined by the equation: w1 * x1 + w2 * x2 + b > 0. Assume b = 0 for simplification.
