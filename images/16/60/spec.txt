Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using 1-bit saturating counters. The branch predictor should predict whether a branch will be taken (1) or not taken (0) based on the previous history of the branches. The predictor should have two states: strongly taken (11), weakly taken (10), weakly not taken (01), and strongly not taken (00). On every clock cycle, the predictor should update its state based on the actual outcome of the branch (taken or not taken).
