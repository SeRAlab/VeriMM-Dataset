When `rst_n` is LOW, the counter initializes to either a full count of 63 (binary 111111) if `dir` is HIGH, or to 0 if `dir` is LOW. When the reset is not active, the counter increments by 1 with `dir` LOW and decrements by 1 with `dir` HIGH, providing basic counting functionality in both directions.

    input clock,

    );
