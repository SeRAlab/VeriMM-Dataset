Design a 3-bit shift register combined with a selection mechanism. The register captures a new data input (d) on the rising edge of a clock signal (clk) and shifts the existing bits to the left. After three clock cycles, the module evaluates the 3-bit value held in the register and uses it to control a multiplexer that outputs one of the three bits based on a selection condition. Specifically, when certain patterns are detected in the shift register, the output (q) depends on the significant bit or the lesser bits according to the defined selection logic.
