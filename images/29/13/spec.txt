Design a fixed-point multiplier in Verilog that takes two 8-bit signed fixed-point numbers (Q1.7 format, where 1 bit is for the integer part and 7 bits are for the fractional part) as inputs and produces a 16-bit signed fixed-point result (Q1.15 format). The multiplication should handle overflow by saturating the output to the maximum or minimum value representable in Q1.15 format.
