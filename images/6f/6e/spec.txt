Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer with 4-bit wide inputs. The module takes a 2-bit selector input, Sel, to choose one of the four 4-bit inputs (S0, S1, S2, S3) and routes the selected input to the 4-bit output, out. When Sel is '00', S0 is selected; when '01', S1 is chosen; '10' selects S2; and '11' selects S3.
module top_module(Sel, S0, S1, S2, S3, out);
