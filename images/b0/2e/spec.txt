The module implements a clock generator that outputs a single-bit clock signal, `clk_out`, at a reduced frequency determined by the parameter `CLK_CYCLES`. When the `resetn` input is low, the module initializes the internal counter and sets `clk_out` to low. On every positive edge of the input clock, it counts down from `CLK_CYCLES - 1` to zero, toggling `clk_out` to high when the counter reaches zero and resetting the counter thereafter. Thus, the module effectively generates a divided clock signal, providing a square wave output with a period defined by the value of `CLK_CYCLES`. The additional formal verification section is included for testing purposes, ensuring the integrity of the counter and the relationship with `clk_out` under various reset conditions.
