Design a Verilog module that performs LU decomposition on a 2x2 matrix represented as four inputs (a, b, c, d) where:
- a, b represent the first row of the matrix,
- c, d represent the second row of the matrix.
The output should be two matrices L and U represented by the variables l11, l21, u11, u12, u21, u22. The decomposition should satisfy the equation A = LU, where A is the original matrix.
