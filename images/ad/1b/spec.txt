Design a decoder for operation selection that determines two control signals based on a 2-bit input. The module generates output signals to enable either an addition/subtraction operation or a CORDIC operation based on the 2-bit `operation` input. Specifically, when `operation` is 01, it activates the addition/subtraction mode, and when `operation` is 11, it activates the CORDIC mode, while for all other values, both modes are disabled.
module top_module(
input wire [1:0] operation,
output reg op_mod_add_subt,
output reg op_mod_cordic
);
