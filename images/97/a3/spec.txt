The division factor can be set to values ranging from 2 to 9 and is controlled by a 3-bit selection vector. The module includes a reset functionality, which initializes the internal counter and divisor. The counter increments with each clock cycle and toggles the output signals whenever it reaches the divisor value, effectively producing a divided clock output. The signals CDIV1 and CDIVX have been assigned static initial values of zero, indicating they are not actively driven and will require further implementation for correct functionality.
