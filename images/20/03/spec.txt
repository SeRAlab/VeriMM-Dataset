Design a 3x3 Gaussian blur filter in Verilog that takes an 8-bit grayscale pixel as input along with its neighboring pixels and outputs a blurred pixel value. The blur effect is calculated using a Gaussian kernel. The weights of the Gaussian kernel can be approximated for simplicity as follows: 

| 1 |  2 |  1 |
|---|----|----|
| 2 |  4 |  2 |
| 1 |  2 |  1 |

The output pixel value is calculated by summing the weighted pixel values and dividing by the total weight (16).
