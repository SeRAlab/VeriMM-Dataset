Design a SR flip-flop using Verilog. The flip-flop has two inputs: S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs: Q and Q_not. The output Q should be high when S is high and R is low, and Q should be low when R is high and S is low. If both S and R are low, the flip-flop retains its previous state. If both S and R are high, it is an invalid state.
