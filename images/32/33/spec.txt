Design a simple 2-bit Branch Predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should take a 1-bit input indicating whether the branch was taken (1) or not taken (0). The output should be the current prediction (1 for taken, 0 for not taken). The states of the counter are: 00 (strongly not taken), 01 (weakly not taken), 10 (weakly taken), and 11 (strongly taken). The counter increments when the branch is taken and decrements when it is not taken, but it should not go below 00 or above 11.
