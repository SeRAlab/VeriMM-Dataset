Design a 4-bit binary counter that increments its value on each positive clock edge. When a reset signal is activated, the counter resets to zero, and the `max_count_reached` flag is cleared. The counter wraps around to zero when it reaches its maximum count of 15 (4'b1111) and sets the `max_count_reached` flag high to indicate that the maximum count has been achieved.

    input clk,
    input rst,
    output reg [3:0] count,
    output reg max_count_reached
);
