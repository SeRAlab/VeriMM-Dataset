
You have been tasked with designing a digital circuit for a tap controller using Verilog. The tap controller should have two inputs, VPWR and VGND, and one output. The VPWR input represents the power supply voltage, while the VGND input represents the ground voltage. The output of the tap controller should be a signal that controls the flow of water from a tap.

Design a Verilog module named "tap_controller" that implements the tap controller. The tap controller should have the following behavior:
- When VPWR is high and VGND is low, the tap should be closed and the output signal should be low.
- When VPWR is low and VGND is high, the tap should be open and the output signal should be high.
- When both VPWR and VGND are high or low, the tap controller should be in an undefined state and the output signal can be either high or low.

Your Verilog module should be self-contained and should not rely on any external modules. Your module should be no more than 200 lines of code.
