Design a module that operates as a power management switch, controlling the supply voltage based on the SLEEP_B signal. When SLEEP_B is high (active), the module provides power (1) to the output while keeping ground signals low (0). Conversely, when SLEEP_B is low (inactive), it turns off the supply and pulls ground high. The output X directly reflects input A regardless of the sleep status, thus maintaining data integrity while managing power consumption.
module top_module(
    input  A      ,
    output X      ,
    input  SLEEP_B
);
