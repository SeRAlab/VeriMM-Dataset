Implement a sound processing module that takes four 8-bit audio channel inputs and their corresponding frequency settings. It normalizes the channels based on the given frequencies, setting the output of a channel to zero if its frequency is either 0 or 1. The module then right-shifts each normalized channel by 2 bits (effectively dividing by 4) and sums them together to produce an 8-bit output signal. This design is intended for applications requiring audio mixing or processing with specific handling of input conditions.
