Design a 32-bit wide register that supports clock gating. The register captures the input data (DATA) on the rising edge of the gated clock (gated_clk), which is asserted when the enable signal (EN) is high and the test enable signal (TE) is low. If TE is high or EN is low, the clock is effectively disabled, preventing data from being latched into the output (Q). This design helps in reducing power consumption by selectively enabling the clock based on the control signals.
