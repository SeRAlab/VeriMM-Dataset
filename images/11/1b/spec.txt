Design a Fan Speed Control System that changes the fan speed based on the temperature sensor input. The system has three states: Low, Medium, and High, corresponding to temperature ranges. The fan speed will increase based on the temperature input. The temperature input is 4 bits, where 0000 represents 0°C and 1111 represents 15°C. The fan speed states are as follows:
- 0°C to 5°C: Low speed
- 6°C to 10°C: Medium speed
- 11°C to 15°C: High speed
The system should have a reset signal to return to Low speed.
