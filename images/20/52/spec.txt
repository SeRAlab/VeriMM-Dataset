
Create a Verilog module that performs bilinear interpolation for a given 2x2 pixel block. The inputs will be the intensities of the four pixels (I00, I01, I10, I11) and the fractional coordinates (x_frac, y_frac) ranging from 0 to 1 (for the purpose of this problem, represent them as 4-bit values). The output will be the interpolated pixel intensity.
