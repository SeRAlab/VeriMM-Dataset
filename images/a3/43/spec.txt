Design a 2-to-4 line decoder with an active chip select (CS) input. When CS is high, the enable output is set to all zeros. When CS is low and the combination of inputs A1 and A0 specifies a binary value from 00 to 11, the corresponding one of the four output lines will be activated (set to high). Each state energizes a unique output line—0001 for A0A1 = 00, 0010 for 01, 0100 for 10, and 1000 for 11—effectively enabling one of four outputs based on the address inputs while allowing for the decoder’s activation to be controlled by the CS signal.
