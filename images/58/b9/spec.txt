Design a circuit that combines a transition detector and a rotator to process a 32-bit input based on specific control signals. The module first detects a transition in the most significant bit of the input. When a transition is detected, the module can perform either a left or right shift operation on the input depending on the `ena` control signals. If `ena` is `00`, the input is left shifted by two bits; if `ena` is `01`, it is right shifted by two bits. The processed (rotated) output can then be conditionally loaded into the output based on the `load` signal. Additionally, the final output is computed by adding segments of the original and rotated inputs based on the state of the `ena` signal. This design effectively integrates shift and arithmetic operations in response to detected transitions.
