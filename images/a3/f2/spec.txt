This module implements a paddle control mechanism for a graphical application, such as a game. It maintains the paddle's vertical position at the center or bottom of the screen based on the 'middle' input, while adjusting its horizontal position ('paddle_x') in response to rotary events. When enabled, it updates the paddle's position either to the right or left based on the 'rotary_right' signal, ensuring the paddle stays within defined screen boundaries, taking into account its size ('radius') and speed. The paddle's position is reset to the center of the screen when the 'reset' signal is activated, providing an organized control system for interactive gameplay.
