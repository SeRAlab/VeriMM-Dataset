Design a simple finite state machine (FSM) that represents a basic SLAM algorithm. The FSM will have three states: IDLE, MAPPING, and LOCALIZING. The FSM transitions from IDLE to MAPPING when a 'start' signal is high, transitions from MAPPING to LOCALIZING when a 'map_done' signal is high, and transitions back to IDLE when a 'reset' signal is high. The FSM should also output the current state as a 2-bit binary value.
