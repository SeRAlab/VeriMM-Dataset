

You have been asked to design a module that will take in two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and output their sum (8-bit output). You are to implement this design as a Verilog module named "adder". The module should have the following ports:

- A (input, 8-bit)
- B (input, 8-bit)
- sum (output, 8-bit)

You must use only the following Verilog operators: "+", "-", ">", "<", ">=", "<=", "==", "!=", "&&", "||", and "!". You must also ensure that your design is synthesizable.
