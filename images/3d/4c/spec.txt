
Design a Verilog module for a Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filter that takes a 4-bit input sample and produces a 4-bit output sample. The FIR filter coefficients are hardcoded as follows: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5} which means that the filter will take the current sample and the previous four samples into account. The output should be the sum of the products of the input samples and the coefficients.
