
Design a 2nd-order Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) filter in Verilog. The filter should implement the following difference equation:
y[n] = b0*x[n] + b1*x[n-1] + b2*x[n-2] - a1*y[n-1] - a2*y[n-2]
where x[n] is the input, y[n] is the output, and b0, b1, b2, a1, and a2 are filter coefficients. The design should accommodate fixed-point arithmetic and allow simulation in a Verilog testbench.
