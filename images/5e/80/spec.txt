Design a digital logic gate that produces a HIGH output, X, based on specific input conditions. The output is asserted HIGH when any of the following conditions are met: A1 is HIGH, A2 is LOW, A3 is HIGH; A1 is LOW, A2 is HIGH, A3 is LOW; A1 is HIGH, A2 is HIGH, A3 is LOW; or B1 is HIGH while C1 is LOW; or when the power supply conditions (VPWR high and VGND low, along with VPB high and VNB low) are satisfied. The output is LOW for the combination of A1, A2, A3 being LOW, HIGH, LOW respectively, or when B1 is LOW and C1 is HIGH. The circuit effectively implements a multi-condition control gate suitable for digital logic applications.
module top_module(
    X   ,
    A1  ,
    A2  ,
    A3  ,
    B1  ,
    C1  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND,
    VPB ,
    VNB
);
