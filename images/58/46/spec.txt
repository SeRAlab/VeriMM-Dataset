Design a circuit that performs both addition and subtraction of two 4-bit numbers, A and B, controlled by a single input S. When S is 0, the module outputs the sum of A and B on the Sum output and a carry-out signal on Cout. When S is 1, it computes the difference (A - B) and provides the result on the Difference output. The design uses additional bits for overflow detection, facilitating proper arithmetic operations within the constraints of 4-bit representation.
module top_module(
    A,
    B,
    S,
    Sum,
    Difference,
    Cout
);
