Using the Booth's algorithm, this module shifts and adds the partial products based on the value of the least significant bit of the B register, while maintaining a count of iterations. It includes a reset signal to initialize the output and internal registers, and operates synchronously on the rising edge of a clock signal. The final product is output as an 8-bit result.
