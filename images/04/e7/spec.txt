
Design a combinational logic circuit in Verilog that computes the value of a polynomial given by the equation:
P(x) = a3*x^3 + a2*x^2 + a1*x + a0
where a3, a2, a1, and a0 are the coefficients of the polynomial and x is the input variable. The circuit should take 4 coefficients and a single input x, and output the result of the polynomial evaluation.
