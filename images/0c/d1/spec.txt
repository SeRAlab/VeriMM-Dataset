Design a simple WiFi signal strength decoder that takes a 5-bit input representing the signal strength and outputs a 3-bit value indicating the signal quality. The output will be: "000" for very weak (0-5), "001" for weak (6-10), "010" for fair (11-15), "011" for good (16-20), "100" for very good (21-25), and "101" for excellent (26-31).
