Design a 2x2 LU decomposition circuit. The circuit should take a 2x2 matrix represented by four input bits (a, b, c, d) and output the lower triangular matrix L (l11, l21) and the upper triangular matrix U (u11, u12, u22) in a 2x2 representation. The decomposition should satisfy the conditions:
L * U = A, where L is a lower triangular matrix and U is an upper triangular matrix.
