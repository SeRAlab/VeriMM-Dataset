
Create a simple SPI controller in Verilog that interfaces with a master device. The controller should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Input: clk (clock signal), reset (active high reset), mosi (Master Out Slave In), miso (Master In Slave Out), ss (Slave Select, active low).
- Output: data_out (data received from the master), data_ready (asserted high when data is available).
The SPI controller should sample the MOSI line on the rising edge of the clock when SS is low, and shift the received bits into a register. When 8 bits are received, the data_ready output should be asserted high for one clock cycle.
