Design a circuit that processes a 100-bit input vector to generate three outputs: `out_and`, `out_or`, and `out_xor`. The `out_and` output is the logical AND of all bits in the input vector, indicating if all bits are HIGH; `out_or` is the logical OR of all bits, reflecting if at least one bit is HIGH; and `out_xor` is the logical XOR of all bits, providing a single bit that signifies the parity of the input bits (even or odd count of HIGH bits).
module top_module( 
    input [99:0] in,
    output out_and,
    output out_or,
    output out_xor 
);
