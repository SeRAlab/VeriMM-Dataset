Design a Parallel-in Serial-out (PISO) shift register that takes a parallel input of 8 bits, shifts it out serially on a clock edge, and has a load control signal to load the parallel data. The output should be one bit at a time, with the first bit coming out after the first clock pulse following a load, and the rest of the bits shifting out on subsequent clock pulses. The design should also include a reset functionality to initialize the register.
