Design a Verilog module that converts RGB color values to YUV color space. The RGB values will be provided as 8-bit inputs (R, G, B) and the output will be the YUV values as 8-bit outputs (Y, U, V). The formulas for conversion are as follows:  
Y = 0.299*R + 0.587*G + 0.114*B  
U = -0.14713*R - 0.28886*G + 0.436*B  
V = 0.615*R - 0.51499*G - 0.10001*B  
Assume proper rounding for the output values, ensuring they are represented as 8-bit values.
