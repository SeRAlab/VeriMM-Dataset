
Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that can perform left and right shifts based on a 2-bit control signal. The module should have the following inputs:
- 4-bit input data (data_in)
- 2-bit control signal (shift_dir) where 
  - '00' = no shift 
  - '01' = left shift by 1 
  - '10' = left shift by 2 
  - '11' = right shift by 1
The output should be a 4-bit shifted data (data_out).
