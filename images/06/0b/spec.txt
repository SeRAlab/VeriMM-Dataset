Design a power gating circuit that controls the power supply to a block based on a control signal. The circuit should have two inputs: 'power_on' (1-bit) and 'block_input' (4-bit). The output 'block_output' (4-bit) should either pass 'block_input' when 'power_on' is high or output a known state (e.g., '0000') when 'power_on' is low.
