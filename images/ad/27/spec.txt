Design a Verilog module based on the following description. Assume signals are positive clock/clk edge triggered unless otherwise stated.
Create a circuit that takes an input signal w and produces an output signal z. The input w is connected to an OR gate, a NOR gate, and an AND gate. Each gate's output is connected to a D flip-flop. The outputs of the flip-flops are then fed into a three-input NAND gate to produce the output z. The second input of the OR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output, the second input of the NOR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's output, and the second input of the AND gate is its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output.
