Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simple heuristic function for an A* search algorithm. The circuit should take three inputs: `g_cost` (the cost to reach the current node), `h_cost` (the heuristic estimate to reach the goal node), and `f_cost` (the total cost, which is `g_cost + h_cost`). The circuit should output `f_cost` as a 4-bit value.
