Design a simple RSA encryption module that takes a 16-bit plaintext input and a 16-bit public key as inputs and produces a 16-bit ciphertext as output. The encryption operation is defined as ciphertext = (plaintext^e) mod n, where e is the public exponent and n is the modulo. For simplicity, you can take e as 3 and n as a fixed value (e.g., 65537).
