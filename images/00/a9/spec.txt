Design a JK latch using Verilog. The JK latch should have inputs J, K, and clock, and an output Q which represents the current state of the latch. The behavior of the JK latch is defined as follows: When J and K are both low, the output Q remains unchanged; when J is high and K is low, Q is set to high; when J is low and K is high, Q is reset to low; when both J and K are high, the output Q toggles its state on the rising edge of the clock.
