
Design a fan speed controller that can control the speed of a fan based on a 3-bit input representing the desired speed (0 to 7). The fan has three operational states: OFF (0 speed), LOW, MEDIUM, and HIGH, corresponding to 1, 2, and 3 speeds respectively. The fan speed should change on the positive edge of a clock signal. If an invalid speed (greater than 3) is provided, the fan should remain in its last state.
