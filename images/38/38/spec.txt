
Design a 32-bit ALU that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: AND
- 001: OR
- 010: ADD
- 011: SUBTRACT
- 100: NOR
- 101: NAND
- 110: XOR
- 111: XNOR

The ALU takes two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and produces a 32-bit output (result) along with a zero flag that indicates whether the result is zero.
