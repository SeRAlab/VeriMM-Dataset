
Design a 32-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (opcode):
- 000: AND
- 001: OR
- 010: ADD
- 011: SUBTRACT
- 100: NOR
- 101: NAND
- 110: XOR
- 111: XNOR

The ALU will take two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and output a 32-bit result. Additionally, it should have a carry-out flag for addition and subtraction operations to indicate overflow.
