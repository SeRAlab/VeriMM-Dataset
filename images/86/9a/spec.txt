Develop a multi-bit, 4-to-1 multiplexer capable of selecting one of four n-bit wide inputs (a, b, c, d) based on a 2-bit selection signal (sel). The output will be the chosen input corresponding to the value of sel, with cases defined for each selection ensuring one of the inputs is forwarded to the output. If an invalid selection occurs, the output defaults to zero.
