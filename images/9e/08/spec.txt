Design a circuit that performs either addition or subtraction on two n-bit input values, inA and inB, based on the sub control signal. When sub is low (0), the module outputs the sum of inA and inB; when sub is high (1), it outputs the difference of inA and inB. The parameter n can be adjusted to define the bit-width of the inputs and the output.
