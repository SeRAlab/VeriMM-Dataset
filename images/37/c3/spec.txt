
Create a Verilog module for a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller should take input signals for the desired frequency and voltage, and based on these inputs, it will select a specific operational state. The states will be encoded as a 2-bit output representing different combinations of frequency and voltage settings (00: Low Frequency/Low Voltage, 01: Low Frequency/High Voltage, 10: High Frequency/Low Voltage, 11: High Frequency/High Voltage). The module should include a clock and reset signal.
