Implement a simple DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency based on a given performance level input. The controller takes a 2-bit performance level signal (`perf_level`) and outputs corresponding voltage (`voltage`) and frequency (`frequency`) settings. The following mappings are defined:  
- `00`: voltage = 1V, frequency = 1GHz  
- `01`: voltage = 1.2V, frequency = 1.2GHz  
- `10`: voltage = 1.4V, frequency = 1.5GHz  
- `11`: voltage = 1.8V, frequency = 2GHz  
The output values should be updated on the rising edge of a clock signal.
