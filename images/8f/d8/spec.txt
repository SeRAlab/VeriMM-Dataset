Design a module that performs binary-coded decimal (BCD) multiplication of two 8-bit hexadecimal inputs. It outputs the product in both hexadecimal format and as a BCD representation. The module takes two 8-bit inputs, A_hex and B_hex, computes their product, and separates the result into BCD digits for the decimal representation in OP_dec, which consists of 4 BCD digits (representing thousands, hundreds, tens, and units). Additionally, it extracts and converts the inputs A_hex and B_hex into their respective BCD formats, providing outputs A_dec and B_dec. The output OP_hex directly represents the hexadecimal product of A_hex and B_hex.
module top_module(A_hex,B_hex,OP_hex,OP_dec,A_dec,B_dec);
