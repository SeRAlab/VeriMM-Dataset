Design a circuit that performs addition of two 8-bit inputs, `data_in1` and `data_in2`. The output, `data_out`, will either be the result of the addition or the bitwise negation of that result, depending on the state of the control signal `ctrl`. When `ctrl` is high (1), the circuit outputs the bitwise NOT of the sum; when `ctrl` is low (0), it outputs the regular sum.
module top_module(
  input [7:0] data_in1,
  input [7:0] data_in2,
  input ctrl,
  output reg [7:0] data_out
);
