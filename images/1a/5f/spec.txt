
Create an SR latch using Verilog. The SR latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn). When S is high and R is low, Q should be set to high. When R is high and S is low, Q should be reset to low. When both S and R are low, the latch should maintain its previous state. When both S and R are high, it should be considered an invalid state.
