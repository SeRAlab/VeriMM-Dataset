
Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the fan speed based on temperature readings. The system should operate in three modes: Low, Medium, and High speed based on the given temperature thresholds. The fan speed is determined by a 2-bit control input. The temperature input is a 4-bit value representing the temperature in degrees Celsius. The system should transition to the appropriate fan speed based on the logic below:
- If the temperature is less than 20, the fan operates at Low speed (00).
- If the temperature is between 20 and 30, the fan operates at Medium speed (01).
- If the temperature is above 30, the fan operates at High speed (10).
If the control input is 11, the fan should turn off.
