Design a position counter module that tracks two position values (`pos1` and `pos2`) based on clock signals and sensor inputs. The module increments both positions by one whenever the sensor detects a positive edge (transition from LOW to HIGH) and allows for resetting each position individually through the `clear` signals. Additionally, it can decrement `pos2` by a specified `distance` if the `subtract` control signal is asserted. The module initializes both position counters to zero at startup and maintains their values across clock cycles, effectively serving as a dual-purpose position tracker with clear and decrement capabilities.
module top_module(input wire clk,output reg [15:0] pos1,output reg [15:0] pos2,input wire sensor,input wire [1:0] clear,input wire subtract,input wire [15:0] distance);
