Design a testbench for a binary multiplier unit (BMU) that drives two control signals, cx0 and cx1, as inputs and observes a 2-bit output, bm. The testbench systematically applies various combinations of cx0 and cx1, simulating a sequence of input values over time while capturing the output for analysis. Additionally, it incorporates shared memory commands to log the activity of the BMU during the simulation, facilitating debugging and verification of the multiplier's functionality.
module top_module(
    input cx0,
    input cx1,
    output reg [1:0] bm
);
