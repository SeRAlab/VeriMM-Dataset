Design a simple Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) circuit that takes a 4-bit input and produces a 4-bit output. The output should be the average of the two pairs of input bits for the low-pass filter and the difference of the two pairs of input bits for the high-pass filter. The input bits are divided into two pairs: (a1, a0) and (b1, b0), and the resulting output should be a concatenation of the low-pass and high-pass outputs.
