
Design a simple PID controller in Verilog. The PID controller should take the current error signal as input and output the control signal which is the combination of proportional, integral, and derivative actions. The proportional gain (Kp), integral gain (Ki), and derivative gain (Kd) are constants that need to be defined. Assume the integral is calculated over a fixed number of clock cycles and that the output is a fixed-width signal. The controller should have a reset functionality that clears the integral and derivative terms.
