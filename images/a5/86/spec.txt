Design a 4-bit up counter that increments its output value on the rising edge of a gated clock signal. The gating of the clock signal is controlled by two inputs: an enable signal (EN) that allows counting to occur and a test enable signal (TE) that likely affects the latch that controls the clock. When the counter is enabled (EN=1), it increments the output (ENCLK) with each rising edge of the gated clock. If not enabled, the counter holds its value.
