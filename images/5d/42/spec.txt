The module takes a 4-bit input and a 2-bit shift command, where '00' indicates a logical left shift, '01' indicates a logical right shift, '10' represents an arithmetic left shift, and '11' dictates an arithmetic right shift. The output reflects the shifted value, allowing for both directional and type-based manipulation of the input data. The design includes an intermediate stage to hold the shifted result before being updated on the clock's rising edge.
