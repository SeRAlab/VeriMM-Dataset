Implement the Verilog module based on the following description. Design a finite state machine to control a parking gate system. The system has three states: Closed, Opening, and Open. It accepts two inputs: sensor (which detects a car) and timer (which triggers the gate to close after a timeout). When a car is detected (sensor=1), the gate moves from Closed to Opening and then to Open. After a timeout (timer=1), the gate moves from Open to Closing and finally back to Closed. Use an asynchronous reset to initialize the gate to Closed state.
