Design a 16-bit arithmetic and logic unit (ALU) that performs various operations based on a 3-bit function selector (ALU_FUNC). The ALU supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division (with remainder), and bitwise operations (AND and OR). It also includes an overflow detection mechanism, setting the OF_detect output high if an overflow occurs during addition or if a negative result is produced in subtraction. The result of the operations is output as a 32-bit wide value, with the upper 16 bits cleared to zero for non-overflowing operations, except in the case of multiplication and division where they are effectively treated as combined results.
