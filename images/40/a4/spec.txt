Design a 16-bit shift register that can shift left or right based on a control signal. The shift register should have an input data line (d_in) and a control signal (shift_dir) to determine the direction of the shift (0 for left, 1 for right). The output should be a 16-bit data line (d_out). The shift register should also have a clock (clk) and a reset (rst) signal to initialize the register to 0.
