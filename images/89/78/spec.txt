Design a circuit that performs 8-bit addition or subtraction based on a control signal. The module takes two 8-bit inputs, Data_A and Data_B, and an operation mode signal (op_mode). When op_mode is set to a specific value, it outputs the sum of Data_A and Data_B along with a carry-out signal; if op_mode is set to the opposite value, it outputs the difference. The final output, Data_S, is a 9-bit wide result to accommodate the carry-out from the addition. This design is effectively a configurable arithmetic unit capable of performing both addition and subtraction operations.
