
Design a digital phase shifter circuit that takes a clock signal and a control signal. The phase shifter should have the ability to shift the output signal by 0, 90, 180, or 270 degrees based on the control signal. The control signal will be a 2-bit input where '00' means no shift, '01' means a 90-degree shift, '10' means a 180-degree shift, and '11' means a 270-degree shift.
