
Design a simple Bluetooth module status checker. The input will be a 2-bit status signal to indicate the state of the Bluetooth module: 
00 - Off, 
01 - Connecting, 
10 - Connected, 
11 - Error. 
The output will be a 3-bit encoded output indicating the status: 
000 - Off, 
001 - Connecting, 
010 - Connected, 
111 - Error.
