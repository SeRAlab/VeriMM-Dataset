

You are tasked with designing a module that takes in two inputs, `data` and `enable`, and produces an output `out` according to the following specifications:
- When `enable` is low, `out` should be 0.
- When `enable` transitions from low to high, `out` should take on the value of `data` at that moment and hold that value until `enable` goes low again.
- When `enable` is high, `out` should hold its previous value.

Design a module that implements this behavior within 200 lines of Verilog code.
