
Create a simple 2-bit Branch Predictor. The predictor should use a 2-bit saturating counter to predict the branch outcome (taken or not taken). The states will be:
00 - strongly not taken
01 - weakly not taken
10 - weakly taken
11 - strongly taken

The input will be a branch signal (1 for taken, 0 for not taken) and the output will be the prediction (1 for taken, 0 for not taken). The state will be updated based on the actual branch outcome.
