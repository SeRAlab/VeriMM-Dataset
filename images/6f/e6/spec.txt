Design a 4-bit up/down counter that counts based on control signals. When enabled (EN), the counter increments from 0 to 15, and when it reaches 15 and EN is active, it wraps around to 0. If the toggle enable (TE) signal is high, the counter decrements from 15 to 0, and wraps around to 15. The counting operation is synchronized with the clock signal (CLK), and the output is updated on the positive edge of an enabled clock signal (ENCLK), which is controlled by an external latch.
