Output ports:

Implementation:
The module includes a 4-bit register, data_out. The register shifts left or right based on the dir signal on the positive edge of the clock signal (posedge clk). If the load signal is high, the register is loaded with data_in. If the reset signal is high, the register is reset to zero.
