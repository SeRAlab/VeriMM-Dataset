Design a serializer-deserializer (SerDes) module that manages the serial transmission and reception of 16-bit data over a clock signal. The module captures the higher byte of input data on each clock cycle, while it maintains the transmission of the control signals for the low and high byte based on the data's evenness. It outputs the received data by XORing the incoming data with the error signal if an error is present. The module preserves the clock signal during transmission, ensuring synchronized operation between the transmitting and receiving ends.
