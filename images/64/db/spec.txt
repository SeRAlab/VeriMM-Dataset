This module implements a speed control multiplexer that selects between two different control signals to determine the speed and polarity outputs. It takes as inputs a direct control rate and polarity, along with a send packet rate and polarity, along with a selection signal. When the selection signal (sendPacketSel) is asserted, the module outputs the send packet's rate and polarity; otherwise, it outputs the direct control rate and polarity. This design is useful in applications where different speed configurations are required based on operational modes.
module top_module(directCtrlRate, directCtrlPol, sendPacketRate, sendPacketPol, sendPacketSel, fullSpeedRate, fullSpeedPol);
