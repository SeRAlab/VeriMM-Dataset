Design a finite state machine (FSM) controller that manages four states: Initial, Load, Read, and Increment. This module responds to specific input signals—Clock, IncrementData, LoadData, and Reset—to transition between states. In the Initial state, both outputs AddOne and WriteOrRead are LOW. In the Load state, WriteOrRead is HIGH and AddOne remains LOW for loading operations. During the Read state, both outputs are LOW. In the Increment state, AddOne is HIGH, indicating an increment operation, while WriteOrRead is also HIGH for writing data. The state transitions occur on the rising edge of the Clock or upon a Reset. The controller effectively orchestrates the behavior of a data operation that can either increment a value or load data based on external commands.
