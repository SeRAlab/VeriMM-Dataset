
Create a 5-stage pipelined multiplier that takes two 8-bit inputs and produces a 16-bit output. The stages are as follows: 
1. Fetch: Read input values.
2. Decode: Decode the operands.
3. Execute: Perform multiplication.
4. Memory: Store the intermediate result.
5. Writeback: Output the final result.
Ensure that the multiplier can handle multiple sets of inputs in succession using a valid signal to indicate when inputs are valid.
