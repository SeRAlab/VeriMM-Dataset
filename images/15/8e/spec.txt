
Design a simple Run-length encoding (RLE) circuit that encodes a sequence of 5 bits. The input is a 5-bit binary number that can have sequences of 0s and 1s. Your task is to output the run-length encoded representation of the input. The output should consist of the number of consecutive bits followed by the bit itself. If the input is '00011', the output should be '3 0 2 1', indicating there are three 0s followed by two 1s.
