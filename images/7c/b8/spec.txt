Implement a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 32-bit input signals (a0, a1, a2, a3) based on a 6-bit address input (addr). The selection is determined by the specific values of addr, where addr values of 6'b110000, 6'b110001, 6'b110010, and 6'b110011 correspond to the outputs of a0, a1, a2, and a3 respectively. The selected input is then provided as the 32-bit output (y).
