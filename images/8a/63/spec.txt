The module performs two operations based on a 2-bit select signal (sel): when sel is 00, it outputs the two's complement of the lower two bits of in_data; when sel is 01, it performs a left shift on in_data, effectively discarding the least significant bit. For any other values of sel, the output defaults to 00.
