Design a 4-bit ripple carry adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, along with a carry-in bit. The module outputs a 4-bit sum and a carry-out signal. The adder utilizes a combinational approach to propagate carries through the addition process, allowing for the correct computation of the resulting sum and carry for any input combination.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] a, b,
    input carry_in,
    output [3:0] sum,
    output carry_out
);
