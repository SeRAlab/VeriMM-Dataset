The ALU supports bitwise AND, OR, addition, subtraction, comparison (less than), and bitwise negation of OR and AND operations. The output of the ALU is provided on a 32-bit output bus (OUT), and the module also includes a ZERO indicator output that signals when the OUT is zero, thereby helping to detect the result of operations.
module top_module(input [3:0] CTL, 
			  input [31:0] A, 
			  input [31:0] B, 
			  output reg [31:0] OUT, 
			  output ZERO
