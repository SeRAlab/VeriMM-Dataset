Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer with two selection inputs (sel0 and sel1) and four data inputs (in0, in1, in2, in3). The output (out) will be determined by the combination of the selection lines: when sel0 and sel1 select a specific input, that corresponding input is forwarded to the output. When sel0=0 and sel1=0, in0 is selected; sel0=0 and sel1=1 selects in1; sel0=1 and sel1=0 selects in2; and sel0=1 and sel1=1 selects in3. This circuit effectively routes one of the four inputs to the output based on the binary value of the selection lines.
module top_module(in0, in1, in2, in3, sel0, sel1, out);
