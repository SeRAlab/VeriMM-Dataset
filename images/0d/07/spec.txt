Design a FIFO (First-In-First-Out) queue with a depth of 4. The FIFO should have inputs for `data_in`, a `write` signal to indicate when data is being written, a `read` signal to indicate when data is being read, and an output `data_out` for the data being read from the FIFO. The FIFO should also include a `full` signal that indicates when the FIFO is full and an `empty` signal that indicates when the FIFO is empty.
