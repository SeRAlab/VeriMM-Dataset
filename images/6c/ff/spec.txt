This module increments the PC by 4 on each clock cycle to facilitate the normal execution flow, but can also handle branching. When a branch signal is activated, the module calculates a new PC address based on a branch address input, effectively directing the flow to a different sequence of instructions. The design incorporates a four-stage pipeline to propagate the current PC value through various stages, ensuring synchronized updates and precise management of instruction addresses.
