Design a circuit that performs a two-part addition of two 9-bit inputs. The lower 5 bits of both inputs are added together to create a 6-bit intermediate result (`temp1`), while the upper 5 bits are also added together to produce another 6-bit intermediate result (`temp2`). Finally, the output is a 10-bit wide result (`res`), where the most significant 5 bits consist of the upper part of the addition results (from `temp2`, using only bits 1 to 5), and the least significant 5 bits are directly taken from the lower addition result (`temp1`).
module top_module(
    input [8:0] in1,
    input [8:0] in2,
    output [9:0] res
    );
