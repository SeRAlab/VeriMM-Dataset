The circuit first inverts the input signals and creates a new set of four combined signals that are based on specific pairs of original and inverted input bits. It then sums the results of these combinations and produces a single output signal indicating whether the sum meets or exceeds the threshold of four. The output will be high if the condition is satisfied, serving effectively as a simple threshold detector.

    input [7:0] input_signals,
