Design a circuit that detects edges on an input signal (rx_int) based on a clock signal. This module generates three output signals: pos_rx_int indicates a positive edge (transition from LOW to HIGH), neg_rx_int indicates a negative edge (transition from HIGH to LOW), and doub_rx_int detects if there is a transition (change) in the signal. The circuit utilizes a shift register-like structure to capture the current and two previous states of the input signal, allowing it to effectively determine the edge conditions. The reset signal (rst_n) initializes the registers to LOW when asserted.
module top_module(input clk,input rst_n,input rx_int,output pos_rx_int,output neg_rx_int,output doub_rx_int);
