Design a 4-bit barrel shifter capable of performing variable shifts on a 4-bit input based on a 2-bit select signal. The module shifts the input A either to the right by 1 position, to the left by 1 position, or to the left by 2 positions, depending on the provided shift value, with the option to produce no shift at all when the shift value is zero.

    input [3:0] A,
    input [1:0] shift,
    output reg [3:0] out
);
