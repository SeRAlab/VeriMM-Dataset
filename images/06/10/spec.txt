Design a Parallel-in Parallel-out (PIPO) shift register in Verilog. The shift register should have a width of 5 bits, enabling loading of data in parallel and shifting out data in parallel. The design should include a clock input, a load enable signal, and a reset signal. When load enable is high, the data should be loaded into the shift register. On each clock edge, if load is not active, the data should shift out.
