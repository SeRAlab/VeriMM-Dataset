A fixed-priority arbiter for 4 requestors is to be designed. The arbiter should grant access to the requestors based on a predefined priority. Requestors R0 (highest priority), R1, R2, and R3 (lowest priority) will send requests. The arbiter should cycle through the requestors and grant access to the highest priority requestor that is currently requesting. When a request is granted, the arbiter will output which requestor is granted access.
