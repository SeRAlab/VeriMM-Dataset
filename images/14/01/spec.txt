Design a 32-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) in Verilog. The LFSR should have a polynomial characteristic defined by the feedback taps at positions [32, 22, 23, 17, 16, 15, 14, 13]. The LFSR should be able to be reset to a known state, and it should output the current value of the LFSR at every clock cycle.
