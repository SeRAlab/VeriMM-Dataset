Implement a restoring divider that takes a 10-bit numerator and a 10-bit denominator as inputs and outputs a 10-bit quotient and a 10-bit remainder. The restoring division algorithm involves repeated subtraction of the divisor from the current dividend. The division should be performed in a clocked manner, where each clock cycle represents one iteration of the division process. The circuit should complete the division in a fixed number of cycles, for example, 10 cycles, regardless of the inputs.
