It translates the address used by the slave into the correct format for the master, applying a specified address translation mask when necessary. The module reads slave signals such as address, enable, read/write commands, and data, then generates appropriate master signals, ensuring that data is modified based on the address being accessed. It also propagates wait states between master and slave to manage data transfer flow.
