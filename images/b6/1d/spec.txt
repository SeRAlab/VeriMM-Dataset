Design a D flip-flop with asynchronous set and reset functionalities. The module captures the value of the input signal D on the falling edge of the clock (CLK_N) while providing active-low asynchronous set (SET_B) and reset (RESET_B) inputs. When RESET_B is de-asserted (logic LOW), the output Q is reset to LOW, while asserting SET_B (logic LOW) forces the output to HIGH regardless of the clock state. The outputs Q and its complement Q_N are provided, with the clock and reset signals being processed through delays to ensure proper synchronization.
