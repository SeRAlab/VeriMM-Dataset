Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that takes a 4-bit input and a 2-bit shift amount to perform a circular right shift operation on the input data. The output will reflect the data input shifted based on the specified shift amount, where a shift amount of 00 results in no shift, 01 shifts the bits by one position to the right, and 10 shifts them by two positions to the right, wrapping the bits around. The design effectively allows for flexible bit manipulation in digital circuits.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] data_in,
    input [1:0] shift_amount,
    output [3:0] data_out
);
