Design a Verilog SPI master controller that can send and receive data using SPI protocol. The master should have the following inputs and outputs:
Inputs: 
- clk: Clock signal
- reset: Active high reset
- mosi: Master Out Slave In (data from master to slave)
- ss: Slave Select (active low)
- miso: Master In Slave Out (data from slave to master)
Outputs:
- sclk: Serial Clock
- mosi_out: Data to slave
- state: Current state of the SPI transaction (idle, sending, receiving)
