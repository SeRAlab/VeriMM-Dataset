Develop a clock rate divider that reduces the frequency of a given input clock signal by a factor of 25 million. When the reset signal is low, the cycle counter resets to zero. Upon each positive edge of the input clock, the counter decrements from 25 million until it reaches zero, at which point the output signal, downClock, toggles to indicate a divided clock cycle. This allows for generation of a slower clock signal suitable for lower frequency applications.
