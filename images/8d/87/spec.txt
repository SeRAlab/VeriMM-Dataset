Design a module that performs addition or subtraction of two signed 43-bit inputs, a_V and b_V, based on a control signal 'add'. When 'add' is asserted, the circuit outputs the sum of the two inputs; when 'add' is deasserted, it outputs the difference. The output, ap_return, is a signed 44-bit result, and the module always indicates readiness with the ap_ready signal set high.
module top_module(
        ap_ready,
        a_V,
        b_V,
        add,
        ap_return
);
