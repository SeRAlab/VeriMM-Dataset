The multiplexer selects between inputs A and B based on the select signal S, outputting the chosen input to OUT. When the SLEEP_B input is LOW, the circuit enters sleep mode, forcing the OUT to be LOW regardless of the selected input, effectively disabling output.

    A,
    B,
