
Design a phase detector that detects the phase difference between two input signals, `signal_A` and `signal_B`. The phase detector should output a 2-bit signal `phase_out` indicating the phase difference:
00 - no phase difference 
01 - signal_A leads signal_B by 90 degrees 
10 - signal_A lags signal_B by 90 degrees 
11 - invalid phase difference (when both signals are high or low).
