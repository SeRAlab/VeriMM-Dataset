Design a 4-bit adder-subtractor that supports both addition and subtraction operations based on the input control signal `Sub`. When `Sub` is low, the circuit performs a standard addition of inputs A and B, along with a carry-in, Cin. When `Sub` is high, it computes the 2's complement of B and then adds it to A and Cin, effectively performing subtraction. The final output includes a 4-bit sum and a carry-out signal, Cout, which indicates if there was an overflow in the addition/subtraction operation.
module top_module(input [3:0] A, B, input Cin, Sub, output [3:0] Sum, Cout);
