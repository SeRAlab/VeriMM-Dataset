Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 3-bit wide input signals (in1, in2, in3, in4) based on a 2-bit selection input (s1). When s1 is 11, the output (m1out) will be equal to in1; when s1 is 10, it will be in2; when s1 is 01, it will be in3; and for any other value of s1 (including 00), it will default to in4.
