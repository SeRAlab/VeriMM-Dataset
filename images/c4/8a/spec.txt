

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that implements a 4-bit adder with carry-in and carry-out. The module should have the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- `a`: a 4-bit input signal
- `b`: a 4-bit input signal
- `cin`: a single-bit carry-in signal

Outputs:
- `sum`: a 4-bit output signal representing the sum of `a` and `b`
- `cout`: a single-bit carry-out signal

Your module should be named `adder4`. You may use any verilog operators or constructs that you deem necessary for the implementation. Your module should be synthesizable and should not use any non-synthesizable constructs.
