
Design a hazard detection and resolution unit that detects potential hazards in a 2-input combinational logic circuit. The circuit should take two inputs (A and B) and generate an output (Y) based on the logic function Y = A & B. The circuit should also implement hazard detection by monitoring when inputs change rapidly, which could lead to glitches in the output. If a hazard is detected, the output should be held stable for one clock cycle.
