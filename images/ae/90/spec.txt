Design a 3-to-4 multiplexer that selects one of three input signals (a, b, or c) based on a 2-bit control signal (ctrl). Depending on the value of ctrl, one or more of the four outputs (w, x, y, z) will reflect the active input signal. The outputs are configured such that at least one output will always be high corresponding to the selected input, effectively allowing a multi-output representation of the selected input signal.
module top_module(
    input a, b, c,
    input [1:0] ctrl,
    output w, x, y, z
);
