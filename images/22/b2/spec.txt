
Design a combinational logic circuit to perform LU decomposition of a 2x2 matrix. Given a 2x2 matrix represented as a 2-bit wide input (a, b, c, d), the circuit should compute the L and U matrices such that:
L = | 1     0   |
    | c/d   1   |

U = | a   b   |
    | 0   d - (c * b / d) |

The output should be in the form of two 4-bit wide outputs for L and U matrices.
