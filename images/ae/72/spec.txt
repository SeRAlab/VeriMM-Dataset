Implement a set-reset (SR) flip-flop that operates on the positive edge of a clock signal. The flip-flop asynchronously resets its output to LOW when the reset signal is high. It sets the output to HIGH when the set signal is high and maintains its current state when both set and reset signals are low. This design is commonly used for storing a single bit of data in digital circuits.
