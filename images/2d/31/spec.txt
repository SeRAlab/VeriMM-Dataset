Design a branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The branch predictor should predict whether a branch will be taken or not based on the previous history of branch outcomes. The counter has four states: 00 (Strongly Not Taken), 01 (Weakly Not Taken), 10 (Weakly Taken), 11 (Strongly Taken). The design should take in a branch outcome (1 for taken, 0 for not taken) and update the counter accordingly. The output should be the prediction of the next branch outcome based on the current state of the counter.
