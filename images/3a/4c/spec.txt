
Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) value comparator. The comparator will take two 16-bit input values (ADC1 and ADC2) and output a 2-bit result indicating whether ADC1 is less than, equal to, or greater than ADC2. The output should be:
00 - ADC1 < ADC2
01 - ADC1 = ADC2
10 - ADC1 > ADC2
