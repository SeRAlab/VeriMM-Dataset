Design a synchronous 8-bit adder with an input carry bit. On each rising edge of the clock, it captures two 8-bit inputs (x and y) and a carry-in signal, computes their sum including the carry, and outputs both the 8-bit sum and a carry-out flag indicating whether there was a carry from the most significant bit.
module top_module(input clk,input [7:0] x,input [7:0] y,input carry_in,output carry_output_bit,output [7:0] sum);
