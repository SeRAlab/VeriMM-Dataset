Design a module that implements a simple combinational circuit with four outputs based on the inputs A1, A2, B1, and VPWR. The module produces the following outputs: X is the AND of A1 and A2, Y is the OR of A1 and A2, Z is the XOR of A1 and A2, and W is the AND of B1 with the power supply VPWR. This design effectively combines various logical operations, categorizing it as a multi-output logic gate module.
module top_module(
    input A1,
    input A2,
    input B1,
    input VPWR,
    output X,
    output Y,
    output Z,
    output W
);
