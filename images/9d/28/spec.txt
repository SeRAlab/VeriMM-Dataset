Design a basic arithmetic calculator that performs four types of operations—addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division—on two signed 8-bit operands based on a 2-bit opcode. The module is triggered by a clock signal, with a reset capability to initialize the results. When not in reset, it calculates the result according to the specified opcode and asserts a valid signal to indicate that the result is ready.
