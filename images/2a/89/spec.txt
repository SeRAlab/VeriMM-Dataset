Design a fixed-priority arbiter that grants access to one of four requesters (R0, R1, R2, R3) based on their priority. The arbiter should have an input for requests and an output for grants. The highest priority is given to R0, followed by R1, R2, and R3. If multiple requests are asserted at the same time, the arbiter should grant access to the highest priority requester. The arbiter should also have a clock and reset signal.
