This module serves as a testbench for a half-adder circuit designated as HAX1. It simulates the behavior of a half-adder, which takes two single-bit binary inputs (A and B) and produces two outputs: the sum (YS) and the carry (YC). The testbench sequentially applies combinations of the inputs (00, 01, 10, and 11) and verifies the outputs against expected values. The functionality checks ensure that when both inputs are zero, the carry and sum are both zero; when one input is one, the carry remains zero while the sum becomes one; and when both inputs are one, the carry is one and the sum is zero, thereby confirming the correct operation of the half-adder logic.
