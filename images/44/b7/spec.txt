Design a sign-extension circuit that converts a 3-bit immediate value into an 8-bit extended value. When the most significant bit (MSB) of the 3-bit input is 1, the upper 5 bits of the output are filled with ones to preserve the negative value in a signed representation. Conversely, if the MSB is 0, the upper 5 bits are filled with zeros, indicating a positive value. The lower 3 bits of the output directly reflect the input immediate value.
module top_module(input [2:0] immediate,
              output reg[7:0] extended
    );
