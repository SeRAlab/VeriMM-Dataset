Design a 2-bit binary adder. The module takes two 2-bit binary numbers, A (composed of A1 and A0) and B (composed of B1 and B0), as inputs and produces a 3-bit output representing the sum (S0, S1 for the sum bits and S2 for the carry-out). The first output bit (S0) sums the least significant bits with no carry, the second output bit (S1) accounts for any carry from the addition of the least significant bits, and the third output bit (S2) indicates the carry resulting from the addition of the most significant bits.
module top_module(
    input A0,
    input A1,
    input B0,
    input B1,
    output S0,
    output S1,
    output S2
    );
