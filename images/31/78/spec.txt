
Design a 4-bit DRAM cell that can store a single 4-bit value. The cell should have the following inputs: `data_in` (4 bits), `we` (write enable), and `clk` (clock). The output should be `data_out` (4 bits), which outputs the stored value when `we` is low and captures `data_in` when `we` is high on the rising edge of `clk`.
