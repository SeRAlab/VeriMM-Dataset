Design a 32-bit wide adder that computes the sum of two 32-bit input values, `a` and `b`, along with an additional carry-in bit. The module outputs a 32-bit sum and a carry-out flag. The carry-out indicates whether there is an overflow in the addition operation beyond the 32 bits.
module top_module(input [31 : 0] a,input [31 : 0] b,input carry_in,output wire [31 : 0] sum,output wire carry_out);
