Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode:
- 000: ADD
- 001: SUB
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
- 101: SLT (Set Less Than)
- 110: NOR
- 111: NAND

The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs A and B, a 3-bit opcode as input, and output a 32-bit result. Additionally, provide a zero flag (Z) indicating if the result is zero.
