Design a 5-tap Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filter in Verilog. The filter should take a 5-bit input signal and produce a 5-bit output signal. The coefficients for the filter are fixed as follows: {1, 1, 1, 1, 1}. The output should be the sum of the input samples multiplied by their respective coefficients. The input will be provided in a 5-sample sequence, and the output should be calculated after all samples have been provided.
