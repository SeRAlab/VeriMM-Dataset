The module captures the input signal in a series of registers with each clock cycle, enabling three separate operations: a bitwise AND of the least significant bit of the first register, the second bit of the second register, and the third bit of the third register; a bitwise OR using the same bit selections; and a bitwise XOR from the same bits. The results of these operations are output as distinct signals: `out_and`, `out_or`, and `out_xor`.
