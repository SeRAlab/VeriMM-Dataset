
Design a fixed-priority arbiter that grants access to one of several requestors based on their priority. The arbiter should have three requestors (R0, R1, R2) with R0 having the highest priority and R2 having the lowest. When a request is granted, the arbiter should output the index of the granted requestor. If multiple requestors request access simultaneously, the arbiter should grant access to the highest priority requestor. The arbiter should also have a clock and reset signal, where on reset, no requestor is granted access.
