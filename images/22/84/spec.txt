Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal: 
000 - Addition,
001 - Subtraction,
010 - AND,
011 - OR,
100 - XOR,
101 - NOT (Unary operation where A is treated as A and all bits are inverted),
110 - Increment,
111 - Decrement. 
The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs A and B, and produce an 8-bit output Y.
