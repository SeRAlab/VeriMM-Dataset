Design a 4-bit shift register with load and shift operations. When the load signal is asserted, the register will take in 4 bits of data, provided reset is not active; otherwise, it resets to zero. Upon asserting the shift signal, the current data will shift left by one bit, introducing a zero on the least significant bit side, while still resetting to zero when the reset signal is active. Additionally, the module determines if there is an overflow by checking if the most significant bit (MSB) is set, and outputs the current state of the shift register on the data_out port.
module top_module(data_in, load, shift, reset, data_out, overflow);
