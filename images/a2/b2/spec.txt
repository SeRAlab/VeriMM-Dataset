This module implements a round constant generator for the AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) encryption algorithm, producing a 32-bit output based on a clock signal and a load control signal. When the load signal (`kld`) is asserted, the output is initialized to the first round constant (0x01_00_00_00). On subsequent clock edges, the module generates the next round constant by incrementing a counter (`rcnt`) and calling the `frcon` function, which maps the counter value to its corresponding AES round constant. The round constants generated follow the sequence defined by the AES specification, facilitating proper key expansion needed for encryption operations.
