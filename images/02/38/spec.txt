
Design a LIFO (Last In First Out) stack in Verilog. The stack should support the following operations:
1. `push`: to add an element to the top of the stack.
2. `pop`: to remove the element from the top of the stack.
3. `top`: to retrieve the element at the top of the stack without removing it.
4. `is_empty`: to check if the stack is empty.
The stack should be of a fixed size defined by a parameter, and you need to handle underflow and overflow conditions as well.
