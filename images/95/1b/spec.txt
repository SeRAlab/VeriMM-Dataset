Design a set-reset (SR) latch using two inputs, S (set) and R (reset), and two outputs, Q and Qbar. When the S input is asserted, the latch sets the output Q to high, while Qbar becomes low. Conversely, when the R input is asserted, it resets Q to low and sets Qbar high. The latch retains its state when both inputs are inactive, creating a simple bistable memory element.
