Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (op_code): 
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
The ALU has two 8-bit inputs A and B, and an 8-bit output Result. Additionally, there should be a zero flag output that indicates if the Result is zero.
