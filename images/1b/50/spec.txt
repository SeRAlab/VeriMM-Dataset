
Design a 1-bit DRAM cell that can store a single bit of data. The cell should have three main operations: 
1. `write` - to store a bit in the cell.
2. `read` - to retrieve the bit stored in the cell.
3. `refresh` - to reset the cell to avoid data loss, ensuring the stored bit remains valid.
The cell should have an input signal for data, a clock signal to synchronize the operations, and control signals for write, read, and refresh operations.
