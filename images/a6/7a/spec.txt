Design a dual-register bank that stores two 16-bit inputs, entrada_e1 and entrada_e2. On a rising clock edge, if a reset signal is activated, both registers are cleared to zero. If the enable write signal, hab_escrita, is asserted, the inputs are written to the registers. After that, the module outputs the values in descending order, with e1 always being the greater and e2 the lesser of the two register values. This design effectively functions as a comparison and reordering mechanism for two 16-bit data inputs.
