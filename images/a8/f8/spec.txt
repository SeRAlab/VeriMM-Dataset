Design a frequency counter that measures the frequency of an input signal (`freqin`) over a specified period defined by `SecondCount`. The module uses a clock signal (`clk`) to synchronize operations: it increments a counter on the falling edge of `freqin` when not stopped, and resets the counter when a second has elapsed. When the elapsed time reaches `SecondCount`, it calculates the frequency by multiplying the counter value by two (to account for the counter incrementing on the falling edges) and outputs this value as a 24-bit wide signal. Additionally, the counter can be reset, allowing it to start fresh measurements after its output is updated.
module top_module(input clk,input freqin,output [23:0] frequency);
