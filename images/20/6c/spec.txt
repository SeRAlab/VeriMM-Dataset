Design a 4-way round-robin arbiter in Verilog. The arbiter should have 4 request inputs (req[3:0]) and 4 grant outputs (grant[3:0]), with a single clock and reset signal. The arbiter should follow a round-robin policy, granting access to one of the requesters based on the last granted output. If a request is made, it should grant to the next requester in the round-robin order.
