Design a 3-input AND gate with an enable control. The module computes the bitwise AND of three 3-bit wide inputs (a, b, and c) to produce a 3-bit intermediate result. When the enable signal (en) is asserted (high), the output (y) will be the AND of the three bits of the intermediate result. If en is not asserted (low), the output will always be LOW. This effectively allows the function to operate as a gated AND circuit.
module top_module(
    input [2:0] a, b, c,
    input en,
    output y
);
