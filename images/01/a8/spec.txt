Design a 1-bit SRAM cell in Verilog that includes a write and read functionality. The SRAM should have three inputs: `data` (1-bit), `we` (write enable, 1-bit), and `clk` (clock, 1-bit). The output should be `q` (1-bit), representing the stored data. When `we` is high, the `data` input should be written to the SRAM cell on the rising edge of `clk`. When `we` is low, the output `q` should reflect the stored value.
