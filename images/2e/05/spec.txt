Design a 3-input Support Vector Machine (SVM) decision function in Verilog. The function should take three 1-bit inputs (x1, x2, x3) and produce a single 1-bit output (y). The decision function outputs '1' if the weighted sum of inputs exceeds a threshold of '1', where the weights for x1, x2, and x3 are 1, 2, and 1 respectively.
