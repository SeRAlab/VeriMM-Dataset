Design a restoring divider circuit that computes the quotient and remainder of a 4-bit dividend and a 4-bit divisor. The circuit utilizes a state machine to handle the division process, systematically loading the dividend and divisor, performing shifts, and subtracting to calculate the quotient and remainder. The quotient and the lower 4 bits of the remainder are output once the division is complete, allowing for integer division with restoration.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] dividend,
    input [3:0] divisor,
    output reg [3:0] quotient,
    output reg [3:0] remainder
);
