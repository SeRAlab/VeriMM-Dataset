Design a JK latch using Verilog. The JK latch has two inputs J and K, a clock input (CLK), and two outputs Q and Qn. The latch should store the previous state when CLK is low and update the outputs based on the inputs J and K when CLK goes high. The behavior should follow these rules:  
1. If J=K=0, Q remains unchanged.  
2. If J=0 and K=1, Q resets to 0.  
3. If J=1 and K=0, Q sets to 1.  
4. If J=K=1, Q toggles its current state.
