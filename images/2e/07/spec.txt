Implement a 16-bit DRAM cell with read and write functionality. The DRAM cell should have the following ports: `input [15:0] data`, `input clk`, `input we` (write enable), `input addr [3:0]` (address), and `output reg [15:0] q`. The DRAM cell should store data on the rising edge of the clock when `we` is high and provide the stored data on `q` when `we` is low.
