Design an SR-latch using Verilog. The latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn) representing the state of the latch. The latch should maintain its state until it receives a new input. If both S and R are low, the latch should maintain its previous state. If S is high and R is low, Q should be high, and Qn should be low. If S is low and R is high, Q should be low, and Qn should be high. If both S and R are high, this condition should be avoided.
