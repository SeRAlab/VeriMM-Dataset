Design a synchronous SRAM module with 1800 addressable 256-bit wide registers. The module supports simultaneous read and write operations controlled by a clock signal and a write enable (WE) signal. When WE is asserted, data from the WriteBus is stored in the Register at the specified WriteAddress on the rising edge of the clock. The ReadBus1 and ReadBus2 outputs provide access to the data from the registers specified by ReadAddress1 and ReadAddress2 respectively, allowing for concurrent read operations.
module top_module(clock, WE, WriteAddress, ReadAddress1, ReadAddress2, WriteBus, ReadBus1, ReadBus2);
