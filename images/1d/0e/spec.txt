Design a 2-bit branch predictor with the following states: 
- 00: Strongly not taken
- 01: Weakly not taken
- 10: Weakly taken
- 11: Strongly taken

The predictor should take two inputs: 
1. clk (clock)
2. branch_out (1 for taken and 0 for not taken)

The predictor output should be the predicted state (2 bits) based on the current state and the branch outcome. The state transitions should follow these rules:
- From 00: 
  - 0 -> 00 (stay)
  - 1 -> 01 (to weakly not taken)
- From 01:
  - 0 -> 00 (to strongly not taken)
  - 1 -> 10 (to weakly taken)
- From 10:
  - 0 -> 01 (to weakly not taken)
  - 1 -> 11 (to strongly taken)
- From 11:
  - 0 -> 10 (to weakly taken)
  - 1 -> 11 (stay)
