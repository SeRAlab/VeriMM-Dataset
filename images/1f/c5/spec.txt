
Design a simple Kalman filter for sensor fusion in Verilog. The filter should take two inputs: `sensor1` and `sensor2`, which represent the measurements from two different sensors. The filter will produce an output `estimated_value` which is the fused estimate of the state based on the measurements and a predefined state transition and measurement model. The Kalman filter will have an initial state estimate and covariance, and will update the estimates with each clock cycle using the Kalman equations.
