Design a clock gating circuit that enables or disables the flow of a clock signal based on two control signals, EN (enable) and TE (test enabled). When both EN and TE are high, the output ENCLK generates a clock signal; otherwise, it outputs low, effectively preventing the clock from propagating. This design is useful for reducing dynamic power consumption in digital circuits by gating the clock to portions of the circuit that are not in active use.
module top_module(
  input CLK,
  input EN,
  input TE,
  output ENCLK
);
