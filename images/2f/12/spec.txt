Design a JK flip-flop in Verilog that has the following characteristics:
- Inputs: J, K (1-bit each), CLK (1-bit, clock signal), and CLR (1-bit, clear signal).
- Output: Q (1-bit, current state) and Qn (1-bit, inverted current state).
- The flip-flop should reset to 0 when CLR is high.
- On the rising edge of CLK, if J and K are both low, Q remains unchanged. If J is high and K is low, Q is set to 1. If J is low and K is high, Q is set to 0. If both J and K are high, Q toggles its previous state.
