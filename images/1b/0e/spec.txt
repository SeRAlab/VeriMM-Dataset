Design a 64-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
- 101: Left Shift
- 110: Right Shift
- 111: Set on Less Than (SLT)

The ALU should have two 64-bit inputs (A and B), a 3-bit control input (ALUOp), and a 64-bit output (result).
