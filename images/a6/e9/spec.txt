Design a digital circuit that calculates the remainder of the division of two n-bit wide unsigned integers, where n is set to 8 bits. The module takes two inputs: a numerator and a denominator. If the denominator is zero, the module outputs a remainder of zero to avoid division by zero. If the numerator is less than the denominator, the remainder is simply the numerator. Otherwise, it computes the quotient by dividing the numerator by the denominator, multiplies this quotient by the denominator to find the dividend, and then calculates the remainder by subtracting this dividend from the numerator.
module top_module(
  input [n-1:0] numerator,
  input [n-1:0] denominator,
  output reg [n-1:0] remainder // Changed 'output' to 'output reg'
);
