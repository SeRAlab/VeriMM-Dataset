Design a VGA timing generator that produces horizontal and vertical synchronization signals along with counters for pixel coordinates. The module increments the horizontal counter (`hcount`) at each clock pulse, resetting after reaching 1343 to indicate the end of a line. The vertical counter (`vcount`) increments when the horizontal counter resets, and it resets after reaching 805 to signify the end of a frame. The `hsync` and `vsync` outputs generate proper synchronization signals based on the defined timing intervals, while `at_display_area` indicates whether the current pixel is within the display region (1023x767 pixels), enabling efficient control of video signal output for a VGA interface.
