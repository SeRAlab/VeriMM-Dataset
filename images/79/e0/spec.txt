Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer with an enable signal. The module selects one of the four 4-bit wide input signals (in0, in1, in2, in3) based on a 2-bit select line (sel). If the enable signal (en) is high, the output (out) will reflect the selected input; if en is low, the output will be forced to zero, effectively disabling the multiplexer.

    in0,
    in1,
    in2,
    in3,
    sel,
    en,
    out
