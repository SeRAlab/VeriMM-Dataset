Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog that grants access to one of four requestors based on a fixed priority. The arbiter should have four request inputs (req0, req1, req2, req3) and one grant output (grant). The priority order is: req0 > req1 > req2 > req3. If multiple requests are active, only the highest priority request should receive the grant signal. The grant signal should remain asserted until the requestor de-asserts its request.
