Develop a 4-bit barrel shifter that performs various shifting operations on a 4-bit input D based on a 2-bit select signal S. The module supports four modes: no shift (S=00), left shift (S=01) where the least significant bit is replaced by a zero, right shift (S=10) where the most significant bit is replaced by a zero, and circular shift (S=11) that rotates the bits to the right while maintaining their positions. The output Q reflects the transformed value of D according to the selected operation.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] D,
    input [1:0] S,
    output reg [3:0] Q
);
