Design an 8-bit AES encryption module that performs a single round of AES encryption. The module should take an 8-bit plaintext input and a 8-bit key input, and produce an 8-bit ciphertext output. The module should include the SubBytes, ShiftRows, and AddRoundKey transformations. Assume that the S-box is predefined, and the ShiftRows transformation is simplified for 8 bits. The encryption should be triggered on a positive clock edge.
