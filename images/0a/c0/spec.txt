Design a 2-bit Convolutional Encoder with a code rate of 1/2. The encoder should take a 2-bit input and produce 4 output bits based on the following generator polynomials: G1 = [1, 0, 1] and G2 = [1, 1, 1]. The output bits will be produced on the rising edge of the clock, and the encoder should have a reset signal to initialize its state.
