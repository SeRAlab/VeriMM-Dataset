

You are tasked with designing a module that takes in five inputs and produces one output. The inputs consist of two single-bit values, A1 and A2, and two two-bit values, B1 and B2, and one single-bit value, C1. The output, Y, should be a single-bit value. You are to design a module that will implement the following logic:

- If A1 is high, Y should be high.
- If A1 is low and A2 is high, Y should be low.
- If A1 is low, A2 is low, and B1 is greater than B2, Y should be high.
- If A1 is low, A2 is low, and B1 is less than B2, Y should be low.
- If A1 is low, A2 is low, and B1 is equal to B2, and C1 is high, Y should be high.
- If A1 is low, A2 is low, and B1 is equal to B2, and C1 is low, Y should be low.

Your module should be named "logic_gate" and should take in the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- A1 (single-bit)
- A2 (single-bit)
- B1 (two-bit)
- B2 (two-bit)
- C1 (single-bit)

Outputs:
- Y (single-bit)

You should use Verilog to implement this module.
