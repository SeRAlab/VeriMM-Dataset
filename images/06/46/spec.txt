Implement a Run-Length Encoding (RLE) encoder in Verilog. The encoder should take a stream of binary data (1's and 0's) as input and output the run-length encoded data. The input will be a 1-bit signal received at every clock cycle, and the output will consist of two signals: a count of the number of consecutive bits (run length) and the value of the bit itself. The length of the run should be limited to a maximum of 15 (to fit in 4 bits), and when a new bit is detected, the previous run should be output immediately.
