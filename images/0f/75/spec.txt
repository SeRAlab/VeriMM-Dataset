
Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency of a digital system based on the workload. The controller has a clock input, a workload signal (workload), and an output frequency signal (freq_out). The frequency output can take three levels based on the workload:
- Low frequency (0) when workload is 0
- Medium frequency (1) when workload is 1
- High frequency (2) when workload is 2 or more
The controller should change the frequency output on each rising edge of the clock.
