Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a processor based on the load. The controller should have the following inputs: `load` (a 4-bit value representing the percentage load), `clk` (the clock signal), and `reset` (active high reset signal). The outputs should be `voltage` (a 2-bit value representing the voltage level) and `frequency` (a 2-bit value representing the frequency level). The mapping of load to voltage and frequency is as follows:
- Load 0-25%: Voltage = 00, Frequency = 00
- Load 26-50%: Voltage = 01, Frequency = 01
- Load 51-75%: Voltage = 10, Frequency = 10
- Load 76-100%: Voltage = 11, Frequency = 11
