Design a JK latch using Verilog. The JK latch should have two inputs: J and K, and one output Q. The latch should toggle its output based on the values of J and K inputs on the rising edge of the clock signal. If J and K are both high, the output should toggle. If J is high and K is low, the output should set to 1. If J is low and K is high, the output should reset to 0. If both J and K are low, the output should retain its previous state.
