
Design a Verilog module that implements the RSA encryption algorithm's modular exponentiation operation. The module should take three inputs: the base (a), the exponent (e), and the modulus (n). It should output the result of (a^e) mod n. The exponentiation should be performed using the method of exponentiation by squaring. The inputs are 8 bits wide, and the output must be 8 bits wide as well. The computation should be performed in a sequential manner with a clock signal.
