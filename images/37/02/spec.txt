Design a 16-bit DRAM cell that can store and retrieve a 16-bit value. The DRAM cell has the following inputs and outputs:
- Inputs: 
  - `write_enable` (1 bit): signal to enable writing to the cell.
  - `data_in` (16 bits): data to be written into the cell.
  - `clk` (1 bit): clock signal.
- Outputs:
  - `data_out` (16 bits): data read from the cell.

The behavior of the DRAM cell is as follows:
- When `write_enable` is high, on the rising edge of `clk`, the `data_in` value should be stored in the cell.
- When `write_enable` is low, on the rising edge of `clk`, the current value in the cell should be output to `data_out`.
