Design an SR latch using Verilog. The latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn). The outputs should change based on the following conditions: when S is high and R is low, Q should be high. When R is high and S is low, Q should be low. When both S and R are low, the latch maintains its state. When both S and R are high, it should enter an invalid state which we will define as Q and Qn both being high.
