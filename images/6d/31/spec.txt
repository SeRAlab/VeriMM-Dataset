Design a barrel shifter that performs variable bit-wise shifting of an 8-bit input, `operand_a`, based on a control input, `shift`. The shifting operation can either shift left or right depending on the bits in the `shift` input, with an option to round the shifted value based on the `round` signal. The circuit outputs the resulting shifted value as `result` and generates a carry output indicating the overflow of the most significant bit during the operation.
module top_module(
    result,
    carry,
    operand_a,
    shift,
    round
);
