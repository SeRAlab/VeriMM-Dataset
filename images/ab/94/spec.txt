Design a 2-to-1 multiplexer that selects between two 32-bit wide inputs based on the enable signal, En. When En is high (1), the output, Out, will reflect the value of In1; when En is low (0), Out will take the value of In2.
module top_module(
    input En,
    input [31:0] In1,
    input [31:0] In2,
    output [31:0] Out
);
