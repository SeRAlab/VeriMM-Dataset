Design an NVMe controller status register that captures the state of the controller, including its ready status, error status, and operational mode. The controller has a 3-bit operational mode (00 for idle, 01 for active, 10 for maintenance) and a ready signal that indicates if the controller is prepared to process commands. The register should be updated on the rising edge of the clock signal. Additionally, when an error occurs, the error status should be set, and the operational mode should revert to idle.
