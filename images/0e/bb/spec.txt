
Design a Fractional-N PLL (Phase-Locked Loop) that generates a frequency output based on a fractional division ratio. The PLL should accept an input frequency (`f_in`), a fractional division ratio (`N`), and generate a frequency output (`f_out`). The output frequency should be calculated as `f_out = f_in / N`. The PLL should handle both integer and fractional values of `N` and maintain synchronization.
