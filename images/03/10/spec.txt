
Design a Restoring Divider in Verilog that takes two inputs: a dividend and a divisor. The output should be the quotient and the remainder. The division operation should be performed using a restoring division algorithm. The inputs will be 8 bits for both the dividend and the divisor. The output should provide 8 bits for the quotient and 8 bits for the remainder. The division should be performed over multiple clock cycles, and the state of the divider should be retained between cycles.
