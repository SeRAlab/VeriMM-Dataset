This module implements a GMII (Gigabit Media Independent Interface) converter that facilitates communication between a Media Access Control (MAC) layer and a physical layer device (PHY). It synchronizes signals for transmission and reception, passing relevant signals such as transmit and receive data, clock, and error signals between the MAC and PHY interfaces. Specifically, it drives the PHY's transmit signals from the MAC inputs, relays receive signals back to the MAC, and maintains proper clock signals to ensure reliable data transfer at a 125 MHz frequency. The design ensures proper handling of control signals like error reporting and data valid indications, allowing for seamless integration in high-speed networking applications.
