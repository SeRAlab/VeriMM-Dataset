Design a JK-latch in Verilog. The latch should have two inputs, J and K, and an output Q. The latch should toggle its output based on the values of J and K on the rising edge of a clock signal. If J and K are both low, the output should hold its previous state. If J is high and K is low, the output should set to 1. If J is low and K is high, the output should reset to 0. If both J and K are high, the output should toggle.
