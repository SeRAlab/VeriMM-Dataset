It takes two 32-bit inputs (read_data_1 and read_data_2) and outputs a 32-bit result (Alu_Result) corresponding to the selected operation, which includes addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, bitwise OR, logical NOR, left shift, right shift, and comparison for less than. Additionally, the module generates a 2-bit Zero output that indicates whether the result of the operation is zero.
