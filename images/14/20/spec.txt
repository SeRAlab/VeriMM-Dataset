Design a 16-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that performs addition, subtraction, and bitwise AND operations based on a 2-bit control signal. The inputs are two 16-bit data values (A and B) and a control signal (Ctrl). The output should be a 16-bit result based on the operation specified by Ctrl: 00 for addition, 01 for subtraction, and 10 for bitwise AND.
