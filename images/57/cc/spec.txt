The design utilizes three 2-input AND gates to combine the inputs: the first two gates process A1 and A2, and B1 and C1, respectively, while the third AND gate combines the results of the first two gates to generate the final output.
module top_module(
    Y ,
    A1,
    A2,
    B1,
    C1
);
