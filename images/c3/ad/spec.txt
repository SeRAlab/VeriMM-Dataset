

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that takes in two 4-bit unsigned binary numbers as inputs, `a` and `b`, and outputs the sum of these two numbers, `sum`, as a 5-bit unsigned binary number. The module should also output a `carry_out` signal indicating whether there is a carry-out from the most significant bit of the sum.

Your module should be named `add_4bit`.

You should assume that the inputs are valid, and no overflow or underflow occurs.
