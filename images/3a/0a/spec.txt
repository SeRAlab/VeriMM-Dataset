Design an LFSR (Linear Feedback Shift Register) that generates a 5-bit pseudo-random sequence based on a given feedback polynomial. The feedback polynomial for this LFSR is defined as x^5 + x^4 + 1. The LFSR should take a 5-bit seed input and produce a 5-bit output on each clock cycle. The output should be the state of the LFSR after shifting and applying the feedback.
