
Design a 64-bit Data Encryption Standard (DES) encryption module in Verilog. The module should take a 64-bit input plaintext and a 64-bit key, and produce a 64-bit ciphertext as output. The DES algorithm consists of various steps including initial permutation, 16 rounds of processing, and final permutation. For simplification, you may implement only a single round of DES for this problem, which will include the key expansion and round function without full DES details.
