Design a circuit that performs bitwise addition of two 8-bit inputs, in1 and in2. The module allows for signed addition by utilizing a control input, signed_ctrl, which extends the sign of in2 while adding it to in1. The output is the lower 8 bits of the resulting sum, accommodating both unsigned and signed inputs based on the control signal.
module top_module(
  input [7:0] in1,
  input [7:0] in2,
  input signed_ctrl,
  output [7:0] out
);
