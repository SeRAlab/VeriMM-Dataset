Design a 4-bit magnitude comparator that outputs three flags: EQ, GT, and LT. The EQ flag indicates equality of inputs A and B, the GT flag is high if A is greater than B, and the LT flag is high if A is less than B. The outputs are determined continuously based on the values of A and B.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output reg EQ,
    output reg GT,
    output reg LT
);
