

You are tasked with designing a module that takes in two 8-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, and outputs the maximum value between them. If `a` is greater than or equal to `b`, the output should be `a`. Otherwise, the output should be `b`. 

Design a module that can perform this operation in Verilog. Your module should be self-contained and should not require any external modules or functions. Your module should be no more than 200 lines of Verilog code.
