Design a temperature decoder that classifies a 4-bit temperature input into three distinct output states: TempLeve, TempNormal, and TempGrave. The outputs reflect different temperature ranges based on the binary representation of the input. TempNormal activates when the input indicates a medium temperature, TempLeve signifies a low temperature condition, while TempGrave denotes a high or critical temperature. This module effectively simplifies the representation of temperature states for appropriate system responses.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] temp,
    output TempLeve,
    output TempNormal,
    output TempGrave
    );
