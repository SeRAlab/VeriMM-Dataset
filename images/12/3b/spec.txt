
Design a simple Bluetooth connection state machine that manages the connection states of a Bluetooth device. The device should have three states: IDLE, CONNECTING, and CONNECTED. The device starts in the IDLE state. On receiving a "start" signal, the machine transitions from IDLE to CONNECTING. After a fixed number of clock cycles (e.g., 5), the machine transitions from CONNECTING to CONNECTED. A "reset" signal allows the device to return to the IDLE state from any state.
