Design a digital circuit that implements a 100-input Binary Decision Diagram (BDD) for logical operations. It computes the logical AND, OR, and XOR of a 100-bit input vector and outputs the most significant bit (MSB) of each operation. The MSB of the AND operation is provided as `out_and`, the MSB of the OR operation as `out_or`, and the MSB of the XOR operation as `out_xor`.
module top_module( 
    input [99:0] in,
    output out_and,
    output out_or,
    output out_xor 
);
