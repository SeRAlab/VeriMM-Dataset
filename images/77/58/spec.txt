On the positive edge of the clock signal or the active-high reset signal, the module updates the output signal 'switch_out'. If the reset signal is high, 'switch_out' is set to 0. If the reset signal is low and the counter reaches the threshold, 'switch_out' is updated to the value of 'switch_in'.
