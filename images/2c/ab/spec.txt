Design a combinational logic circuit that evaluates a polynomial of the form P(x) = a_n * x^n + a_(n-1) * x^(n-1) + ... + a_1 * x + a_0, where the coefficients a_i are provided as inputs and x is the variable. The circuit should take an input value for x and output the evaluated polynomial result. The inputs for coefficients and x will be 8 bits each, and the output should be 16 bits to accommodate possible overflow.
