
Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the operating voltage and frequency based on the input workload. The controller has two inputs: `workload` (a 4-bit value representing workload levels) and `mode` (a 1-bit value that indicates if the system is in 'high performance' or 'low power' mode). The output consists of two signals: `frequency` (a 3-bit value) and `voltage` (a 2-bit value). The mapping is as follows:
- `workload` (0000 to 1111): 0 to 15
- In 'high performance' mode, the frequency should be:
  - Frequency = workload + 2 (0-7 maps to 2-9)
- In 'low power' mode, the frequency should be:
  - Frequency = workload (0-7 maps to 0-7)
- The voltage output follows:
  - Voltage = workload[3:2] (upper 2 bits of workload)
