Design a voice activity detection (VAD) circuit that identifies the presence of speech based on audio input. The module computes the energy of the incoming audio signal and compares it to a predefined threshold. When the computed energy exceeds the threshold, it outputs a signal indicating that speech has been detected. The circuit accumulates the energy over time using a register, thus allowing for detection that can adapt to varying signal conditions.
