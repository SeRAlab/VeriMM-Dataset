Design a reset management module that controls various reset signals based on multiple input conditions. The module generates a master reset signal (`mb_reset`) that is asserted only when all four inputs (external reset, auxiliary reset, debug reset, and clock lock status) are active. Additionally, it outputs individual reset signals for bus structure and peripheral components, as well as active-low resets for interconnects and peripherals, all driven by the external reset input. This design ensures that the system and its components are properly reset under specified conditions, promoting reliable operation during initialization and fault recovery.
