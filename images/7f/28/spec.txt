Design a programmable up/down counter that operates on an 8-bit width. The counter increments when the `up_down` signal is high, resetting to zero upon reaching a predefined maximum count of 10. Conversely, when `up_down` is low, it decrements and wraps around to 10 when it goes below zero. The counter can be cleared to zero with the `clear` signal. The current count value is outputted through the `qd` signal.
module top_module(clk, clear, up_down, qd);
