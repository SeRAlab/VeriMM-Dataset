Design a finite state machine (FSM) in Verilog that implements the point addition operation on an elliptic curve over a finite field. The inputs to the FSM are two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) representing the coordinates of the points to be added. The output of the FSM should be the result of the point addition (x3, y3). The FSM should handle the cases of point addition, point doubling, and point at infinity.
