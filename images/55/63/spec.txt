Design a D latch circuit that captures the input data (D) when the enable signal (EN) is asserted high. The output (Q) will hold the value of D while EN remains high, and the complementary output (Q_bar) will provide the inverted value of Q. When EN is low, the outputs maintain their last state, thus effectively storing the input data until the enable signal allows new data to be captured.

  input D,
