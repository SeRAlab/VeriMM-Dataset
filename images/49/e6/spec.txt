Develop a VGA pixel driver that outputs pixel data based on the horizontal sync signal and a double-speed clock. The module takes 24-bit pixel data and uses a boolean signal to switch between two pixel values. On the rising edge of the double-speed pixel clock, the module detects the transition of the horizontal sync signal to toggle between two sets of pixel outputs: it outputs the lower 12 bits when pixel_ab is high and the upper 12 bits when pixel_ab is low, effectively managing pixel data for display synchronization.
