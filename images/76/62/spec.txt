When SET_B is low, the output Q is immediately set high, regardless of the clock state. On each clock edge, if not set, the flip-flop captures the value of D and holds it at the output Q. Additional delay signals are incorporated to ensure proper timing of the inputs, contributing to robust operation in synchronous digital systems.

    output reg Q,
    input CLK,
