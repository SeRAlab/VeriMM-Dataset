
Design a 16-bit shift register that can shift left or right based on a control signal. The shift register should have a parallel load capability, allowing it to load a 16-bit input when a load enable signal is active. The control signals are as follows:
- shift_left: Shift the register content one bit to the left.
- shift_right: Shift the register content one bit to the right.
- load: Load the input data into the register.
- clk: Clock signal for synchronization.
- rst: Asynchronous reset to clear the register.
