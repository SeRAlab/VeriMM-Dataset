Design a synchronous 16-bit binary adder with an additional carry input. This module computes the sum of two 16-bit inputs, A and B, along with a carry-in signal (Cin), and outputs the 16-bit result (Sum) along with the carry-out signal (Cout). It resets to zero when the reset signal (rst_n) is low and updates the sum on the rising edge of the clock. The carry-out signal indicates whether there was an overflow from the addition of A, B, and Cin.
