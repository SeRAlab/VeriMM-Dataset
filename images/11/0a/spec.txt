Implement a fixed-priority arbiter that grants access to one of the multiple requesters based on their priority. The arbiter should have 4 request signals (req[3:0]) and one grant signal (grant[3:0]). The highest priority is assigned to req[0], followed by req[1], req[2], and req[3]. The output grant should be active for one clock cycle in accordance with the highest priority request that is currently active.
