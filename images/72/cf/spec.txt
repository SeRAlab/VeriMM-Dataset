Design a 4-bit up/down counter that increments or decrements its output based on control signals. The counter will increase its value by 1 when the `up` signal is high and the `en` (enable) signal is triggered. Conversely, it will decrease by 1 when the `up` signal is low and `en` is triggered. The counter can be reset to zero asynchronously when the `rst` (reset) signal is activated.
module top_module(
    input up,
    input en,
    input rst,
    output reg [3:0] out
);
