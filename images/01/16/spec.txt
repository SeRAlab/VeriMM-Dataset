
Design a SRT (Sweeney, Robertson, and Tocher) divider in Verilog. The divider should take two inputs: a dividend (dividend) and a divisor (divisor), and produce a quotient (quotient) and a remainder (remainder). The divider should work with 8-bit signed integers and should be able to handle cases where the divisor is zero by producing a specific output (e.g., setting the quotient to zero and the remainder to the dividend).
