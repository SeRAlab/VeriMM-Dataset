Design a Verilog module that takes a 5-bit binary input representing a portfolio allocation and outputs a 5-bit optimized allocation based on a simple optimization rule. The optimization rule is that the output allocation should not exceed a total of 15 (out of a possible 31). If the input exceeds this total, the output should be adjusted down to fit the limit while maintaining proportional allocation. If the total is 15 or less, the output should simply match the input.
