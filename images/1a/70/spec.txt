
Design a Verilog module for a Low Dropout Regulator (LDO) that takes a reference voltage (Vref), input voltage (Vin), and a control signal (enable) as inputs and outputs a regulated output voltage (Vout). The output voltage should be Vout = Vref when Vin is greater than or equal to Vref and the control signal is high. If the control signal is low, Vout should be 0. If Vin is less than Vref and the control signal is high, Vout should also be 0.
