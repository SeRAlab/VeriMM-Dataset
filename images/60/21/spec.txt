Design a 1-to-4 demultiplexer that routes a single input signal (d_in) to one of four outputs (d_out_0, d_out_1, d_out_2, d_out_3) based on a 2-bit selector input (d_sel). When d_sel is 00, the input is directed to d_out_0; when d_sel is 01, to d_out_1; when d_sel is 10, to d_out_2; and when d_sel is 11, to d_out_3. All outputs are initially set to LOW unless selected, allowing for effective signal distribution based on the selector.
module top_module(
    d_in,     
    d_sel,    
    d_out_0,  
    d_out_1,  
    d_out_2,  
    d_out_3   
);
