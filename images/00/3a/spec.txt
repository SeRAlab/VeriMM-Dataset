Design a 4-point FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) circuit. The circuit should take 4 complex inputs represented as 8-bit real and imaginary components and output 4 complex results, also represented as 8-bit real and imaginary components. The inputs are denoted as (a0, b0), (a1, b1), (a2, b2), (a3, b3) where each 'a' and 'b' represent the real and imaginary parts respectively.
