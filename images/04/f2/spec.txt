Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature readings. The system will have a temperature input and three output signals representing low, medium, and high speeds. The fan speed will be determined based on the following conditions:
- If the temperature is below 20 degrees, the fan runs at low speed.
- If the temperature is between 20 and 30 degrees, the fan runs at medium speed.
- If the temperature is above 30 degrees, the fan runs at high speed.
The system should have a clock and reset input for synchronous operation.
