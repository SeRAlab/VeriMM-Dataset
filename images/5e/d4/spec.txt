Design a logic circuit that detects specific combinations of four input digits (c3, c2, c1, c0) to generate a single output. The output 'o' will be HIGH when any of the following conditions are satisfied: all inputs are LOW, only the least significant input (c0) is HIGH, the last three inputs (c2, c1, c0) are all HIGH, or when the most significant and second to most significant inputs (c3, c2) are HIGH while the two least significant inputs (c1, c0) are LOW. This module can function as a decoder for specific state recognition or as part of an indicator circuit.
module top_module(input c3, c2, c1, c0, output o);
