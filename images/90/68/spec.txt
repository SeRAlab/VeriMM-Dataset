Design a 4-bit binary adder that takes two 4-bit inputs, X and Y, and produces a 5-bit output, S, which represents their sum. The output S can accommodate the maximum possible result of the addition, which is 15 + 15 = 30, thus requiring an extra bit for overflow. The module updates the output S whenever there is a change in either input.
