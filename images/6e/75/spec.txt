Design a 4-bit adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs, A and B. The sum is output as a 4-bit value, Z, but if the computed sum exceeds the maximum value representable by 4 bits (which is 15), Z is clamped to 15 (i.e., outputting 1111). This design effectively handles overflow conditions by capping the output at the maximum allowable value for the given bit-width.
