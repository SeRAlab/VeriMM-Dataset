Design a 2-to-1 demultiplexer that routes one of three 16-bit input signals (`reg1_a_in`, `reg2_a_in`, or `reg3_a_in`) to a single output (`a_bus_out`) based on the 2-bit selection signal (`selec`). When `selec` is `01`, it outputs `reg1_a_in`; when `selec` is `10`, it outputs `reg2_a_in`; and when `selec` is `11`, it outputs `reg3_a_in`, effectively allowing the selection of one of the three input registers to be sent to the output.
