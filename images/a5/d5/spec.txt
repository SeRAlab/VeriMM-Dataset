Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that shifts the input data left or right based on a 2-bit control signal. The control signals are defined such that: '00' performs a left logical shift by one bit (inserting a 0 at the least significant bit), '01' performs a right logical shift by one bit (inserting a 0 at the most significant bit), '10' performs a circular left shift (moving the most significant bit to the least significant position), and '11' performs a circular right shift (moving the least significant bit to the most significant position). The output reflects the shifted result of the input data.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] data_in,
    input [1:0] control,
    output [3:0] data_out
);
