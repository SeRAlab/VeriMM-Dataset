Design a Verilog module that implements a simple elliptic curve point addition operation on a finite field defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b. The inputs to the module will be two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) on the curve, and the output will be the result of adding these two points (x3, y3). The values of a and b will be constants defined within the code. Ensure the module handles the special cases of point addition, such as the identity point and when the points are identical.
