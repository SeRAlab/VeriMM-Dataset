Design a clock gating circuit that controls the clock signal based on the enable (EN) and test enable (TE) signals. The module generates an output signal, ENCLK, which is effectively the clock signal (CLK) when EN is asserted (high) and TE is also asserted. When TE is deasserted, ENCLK outputs a LOW regardless of the clock signal. Additionally, the circuit includes a reset feature that sets ENCLK to LOW when activated, ensuring proper initialization and safety during power-up.
module top_module(CLK, EN, TE, reset, ENCLK);
