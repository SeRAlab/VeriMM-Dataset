Design a 4-tap Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filter in Verilog. The filter should take a 4-bit input and produce a 4-bit output. The filter coefficients are fixed and should be defined as [1, 2, 3, 4]. The output is calculated as the sum of the products of the input samples and the filter coefficients. Assume that the input samples are provided in a serial fashion over four clock cycles.
