Design a SPI (Serial Peripheral Interface) master controller that can transmit and receive data from a slave device. The controller should support four modes of operation, defined by the clock polarity (CPOL) and clock phase (CPHA). It should be able to select a slave device and initiate a communication sequence, handling the clock generation, data transmission, and reception. The controller should also include an enable signal to control the start and stop of the SPI communication.
