Design an LFSR (Linear Feedback Shift Register) in Verilog that generates a pseudo-random sequence of bits. The LFSR should have 5 bits and tap positions at 5 and 4 (feedback from bit 5 and bit 4). The output of the LFSR should be generated on the rising edge of the clock. The initial state of the LFSR should be a non-zero value provided as an input.
