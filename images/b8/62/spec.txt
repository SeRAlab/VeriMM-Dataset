Design a module that performs a byte swapping and shifting operation on a 16-bit input. The module takes a 16-bit input and swaps its two bytes, then shifts the resulting value to the right by 8 bits. Additionally, it computes the parity of the shifted byte (taking the XOR of all bits), which is output as a separate signal. Finally, the output is arranged such that the lower byte consists of the original lower byte and the upper byte consists of the original upper byte, effectively maintaining the swapped byte positions.
module top_module(
    input wire [15:0] in,
    output wire [15:0] out,
    output wire parity
);
