Design a simple PCIe endpoint controller that manages read requests. The controller should accept incoming read requests and generate the appropriate response after a fixed delay. The controller has the following signals: `req` (input), `addr` (input), `data_out` (output), and `ack` (output). The `ack` signal should be asserted after a delay of 4 clock cycles when a read request is received. The `data_out` should be equal to the address received for the read request.
