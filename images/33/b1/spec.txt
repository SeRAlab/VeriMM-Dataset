
Create a Verilog module for a 64-point FFT butterfly unit. The module should take two complex inputs (real and imaginary parts), and compute the butterfly operation, which is defined as:
- output_real = input1_real + twiddle_factor_real * input2_real - twiddle_factor_imag * input2_imag
- output_imag = input1_imag + twiddle_factor_real * input2_imag + twiddle_factor_imag * input2_real
The twiddle factor for the FFT can be pre-calculated or passed as an input to the module.
