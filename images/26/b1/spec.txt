Design a simple UART controller that transmits 8-bit data serially at a predefined baud rate. The controller should have the following inputs: a clock signal, a start signal to begin transmission, an 8-bit data input, and an output that represents the transmitted serial data (1-bit at a time). The transmission should start with a low start bit, followed by the 8 data bits, and end with a high stop bit. The module should also include a baud rate generator to control the timing of the bits being sent.
