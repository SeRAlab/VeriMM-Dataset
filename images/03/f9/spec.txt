Design a combinational logic circuit that converts RGB color values to YUV color space. The RGB inputs will be 8 bits each for R, G, and B, and the output Y, U, and V will also be 8 bits each. The conversion formulas are as follows:
Y = 0.299 * R + 0.587 * G + 0.114 * B
U = -0.14713 * R - 0.28886 * G + 0.436 * B + 128
V = 0.615 * R - 0.51498 * G - 0.10001 * B + 128
Ensure that the outputs are rounded to the nearest integer. Implement this in Verilog code.
