Design a 1D Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) module that takes a 4-bit input and generates 4-bit approximation and detail coefficients as output. The DWT operation will use a simple Haar wavelet transformation defined as follows:
- Approximation Coefficient (c0) = (a0 + a1) / 2
- Detail Coefficient (d0) = (a0 - a1) / 2
- Approximation Coefficient (c1) = (a2 + a3) / 2
- Detail Coefficient (d1) = (a2 - a3) / 2
Where a0, a1, a2, and a3 are the 4-bit input values.
