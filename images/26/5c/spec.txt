
Design a simple Kalman filter for sensor fusion in Verilog. The filter will combine two sensor inputs (sensor_a and sensor_b) to estimate the state (estimated_state) and the uncertainty (estimated_uncertainty). The inputs are 8-bit values representing the measurements, and the filter will update its state and uncertainty based on fixed gain values. You will need to define the filter parameters, including the process noise covariance and measurement noise covariance.
