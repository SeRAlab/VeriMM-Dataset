Upon activation, it transitions through states—starting from "Idle," moving to "Hold" where it increments a millisecond counter until the specified delay (DELAY_MS) is reached, and then transitioning to a "Done" state. The output (DELAY_FIN) indicates the completion of the delay when the enable signal is active and the delay has elapsed, allowing for subsequent system actions to be triggered based on this timing function.
