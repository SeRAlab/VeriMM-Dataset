Design an SR latch using Verilog. The SR latch should have two inputs, S (set) and R (reset), and two outputs, Q and Qn (not Q). The outputs should hold their state until the inputs change. When S is high and R is low, Q should be set to high. When S is low and R is high, Q should be reset to low. Both S and R being high is an invalid state and should be avoided. The latch should remember its state between input changes.
