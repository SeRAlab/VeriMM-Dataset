Design a 4-bit binary counter that increments its count on each rising edge of the clock input (clk). When the reset (rst_n) is asserted low, the counter resets to zero. The counter wraps around to zero after reaching its maximum value of 15 (4'b1111), ensuring continuous counting in a cyclic manner.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input rst_n,
    output reg [3:0] count
