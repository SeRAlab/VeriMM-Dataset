Develop a 4-bit circular shift register with serial input. On each positive edge of the clock, if the shift signal is high, the contents of the shift register are rotated right, preserving the bit in the most significant position. If the shift signal is low, the least significant bit of the shift register is replaced with the incoming data input. The output is the least significant bit (LSB) of the shift register.
