Design a Verilog module that implements a simple RRT-like operation, which takes two 5-bit inputs representing coordinates (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) and produces a 5-bit output that represents a midpoint coordinate. The midpoint should be calculated as:  
mid_x = (x1 + x2) / 2  
mid_y = (y1 + y2) / 2  
The output should be a concatenation of mid_x and mid_y, thus the output will be a 10-bit value.
