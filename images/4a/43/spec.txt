Design a UART (Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter) module for serial communication, enabling data transmission and reception of 16-bit wide data. The module includes input control signals for read/write operations and a clock signal, while supporting a baud rate of 115200. It implements a transmit shift register to serialize data for output via the TX pin, and a receive shift register to capture incoming serial data from the RX pin. The design utilizes counters to manage bit timing and ensures proper synchronization with the clock. The output port indicates the status of the receive and transmit operations through a data output signal, providing state information based on control signal inputs.
