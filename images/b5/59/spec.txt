Design a traffic light control finite state machine (FSM) that cycles through three states: RED, GREEN, and YELLOW. The FSM operates based on a clock signal and can reset to the RED state upon receiving a reset signal. In the RED state, the red light is activated while the yellow and green lights are turned off; in the GREEN state, the green light is activated; and in the YELLOW state, the yellow light is activated. The state transitions occur in the order of RED to GREEN, GREEN to YELLOW, and YELLOW back to RED, ensuring proper signaling for vehicle traffic control.
module top_module(
  input clk,
  input reset,
  output reg red,
  output reg yellow,
  output reg green
);
