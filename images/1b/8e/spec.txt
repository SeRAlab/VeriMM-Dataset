Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simple switching regulator controller. The circuit should have three inputs: `Vin` (input voltage), `Vref` (reference voltage), and `Iload` (load current). The output `Vout` (output voltage) should be high when `Vin` is greater than `Vref` and `Iload` is within a specified range (0 to 10). Otherwise, the output should be low.
