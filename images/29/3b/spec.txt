
Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit opcode:
- 0000: AND
- 0001: OR
- 0010: XOR
- 0011: ADD
- 0100: SUB
- 0101: SLT (Set Less Than, outputs 1 if A < B, otherwise 0)
- 0110: NAND
- 0111: NOR
- 1000: XNOR
- 1001: SRA (Shift Right Arithmetic)
- 1010: SLL (Shift Left Logical)
- 1011: SRL (Shift Right Logical)
- 1100: NOT (only A)
- 1101: PASS A
- 1110: PASS B
- 1111: NOP (No Operation)

The ALU should also produce a zero flag (Z) that indicates whether the result is zero.
The inputs to the ALU are two 32-bit operands (A, B) and a 4-bit opcode. The output is a 32-bit result and the zero flag.
