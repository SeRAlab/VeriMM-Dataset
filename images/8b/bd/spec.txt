Design a scan chain interface for integrated circuit testing and debugging. This module manages various test configurations and controls through inputs such as scan mode, global shift enable, and test disable signals. It determines whether to use short or long scan chains based on the specified conditions and controls outputs for scan chain select signals and memory write operations, ensuring proper functionality during testing scenarios. Furthermore, it includes mechanisms to disable mux driving and manipulate memory bypass settings, facilitating effective test mode activation and ensuring accurate data capture during scan operations.
