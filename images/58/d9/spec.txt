Implement a 32-bit carry-select adder (CSA) that computes the sum of two 32-bit inputs, A and B. The adder is divided into two segments: the upper 16 bits and the lower 16 bits. Carry outputs are managed using an additional wire to facilitate the carry propagation from the higher order to lower order bits, ensuring efficient addition. The final output consists of a 32-bit sum (S) and a carry-out (C32) that indicates any overflow from the most significant bit.

    input [31:0] A,
    input [31:0] B,
    output [31:0] S,
    output C32
);
