Design a double comparator circuit that compares two 2-bit binary numbers, represented by the inputs (x0, x1) and (y0, y1). The comparator takes external signals indicating previous equality (eqin), greater-than (gtin), and less-than (ltin) comparisons, and generates updated comparison results: eqout for equality, gtout for greater-than, and ltout for less-than. The eqout signal is asserted when both bits of x are equal to those of y. The gtout signal is asserted if x is greater than y based on the current inputs or previous greater-than results, while ltout indicates that x is less than y based on the current or previous comparisons.
