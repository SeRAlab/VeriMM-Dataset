Create a JK latch using Verilog. The latch should have inputs J, K, and clock, and it should output Q and Q_not. The behavior should be such that when J and K are both low, the output remains the same; when J is high and K is low, the output should set; when J is low and K is high, the output should reset; and when both J and K are high, the output should toggle on the clock edge.
