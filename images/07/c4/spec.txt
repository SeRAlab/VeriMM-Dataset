Design a 16-bit ALU that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode input:  
000: ADD  
001: SUB  
010: AND  
011: OR  
100: XOR  
101: NOT (only for the first operand)  
110: NAND  
111: NOR  
The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs and produce a 16-bit output based on the operation specified by the opcode.
