
Design a Restoring Divider in Verilog. The divider should take two inputs: a dividend (multiples of 4 bits) and a divisor (4 bits). The output should be the quotient (4 bits) and a remainder (4 bits). The division process should be clocked, and the quotient and remainder should be updated on the rising edge of a clock signal. The dividing operation should follow the restoring division algorithm.
