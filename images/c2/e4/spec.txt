

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that implements a 4-bit binary adder with carry-in and carry-out. The module should have the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- `A`: a 4-bit binary number
- `B`: a 4-bit binary number
- `Cin`: a single bit representing the carry-in

Outputs:
- `Sum`: a 4-bit binary number representing the sum of `A` and `B`
- `Cout`: a single bit representing the carry-out

You may use any standard verilog operators and modules, but you may not use any pre-built adder modules.
