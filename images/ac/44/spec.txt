Design a circuit that performs a signed multiplication of two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produces a 15-bit output, result, after right-shifting the product by 8 bits. The multiplication operation is triggered on the rising edge of the clock when a start signal is asserted, with the ability to reset the output and the done flag when the reset signal is active. The done output indicates that the multiplication operation has been completed successfully.
module top_module(input signed [7:0] A,B,
                   input clk,reset,start,
                   output reg done,
                   output reg signed [14:0] result);
