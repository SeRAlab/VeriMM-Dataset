
Design a 4-input linear regression calculator in Verilog. The circuit should compute the weighted sum of the four inputs (x1, x2, x3, x4) with given weights (w1, w2, w3, w4). The output should be the result of the linear regression equation: y = w1*x1 + w2*x2 + w3*x3 + w4*x4, where the weights are constants defined in the module.
