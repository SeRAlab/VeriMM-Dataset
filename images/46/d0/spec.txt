Design a buffer copier module that facilitates the transfer of data between two virtual RAM (VRAM) buffers during specific periods defined by a vertical blanking signal. The module utilizes an 8-bit bi-directional data bus and two outputs to control read and write operations to the back and front VRAM, respectively. It initiates the copying process upon receiving a copy enable signal when the vertical blanking signal is asserted. During this operation, a counter tracks the number of cycles (limited to 4800) while transferring data from the back VRAM to the front VRAM. Once the copy is completed or if conditions are not met, it resets the control signals, ensuring proper management of the copy process and data flow.
module top_module(input wire clk,input wire vblank,output reg front_vram_wr_low,output reg back_vram_rd_low,output reg copy_in_progress,input wire [7:0] copy_enable,inout wire [7:0] front_vram_data,inout wire [7:0] back_vram_data,output wire [12:0] front_vram_addr,output wire [12:0] back_vram_addr);
