Design a 4-bit magnitude comparator that compares two 4-bit inputs, A and B. It outputs three flags: EQ (equal), GT (greater than), and LT (less than). The EQ output is high when A is equal to B, GT is high when A is greater than B, and LT is high when A is less than B, providing a complete comparison of the two values.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output reg EQ,   // Changed from wire to reg
    output reg GT,   // Changed from wire to reg
    output reg LT    // Changed from wire to reg
);
