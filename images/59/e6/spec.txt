Design a module for decoding Manchester-encoded data using frequency shift keying (FSK). The module processes incoming signals to reconstruct a 45-bit decoded output from the FSK signal, utilizing a control input for manual reset. It tracks received bits and stores every second FSK bit into the decoded output while toggling between odd and even bit reception until all bits are collected or a manual reset is activated, ultimately signaling completion with a 'done' output.
module top_module(input sqwv,input fsk_out_trigger,input fsk_out,input manual,output reg [44:0] decoded,output reg done);
