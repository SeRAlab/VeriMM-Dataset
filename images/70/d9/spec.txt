Design a unit step signal generator that produces a single pulse output on the `step` output. The circuit is activated by a rising edge of the clock signal (`clk`). When the `rst` input is high, the `step` output is reset to LOW, and a flag is set to allow the next signal detection. Upon receiving a HIGH `signal` while the flag is active (indicating that the pulse has not yet been generated), the `step` output goes HIGH for one clock cycle, and the flag is then set to prevent further triggering until the next reset.
