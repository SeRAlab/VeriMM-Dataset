Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the speed of a fan based on the temperature input. The system has three speed levels: low, medium, and high. The fan speed should change according to temperature thresholds: 
- If the temperature is less than 20 degrees, the fan should operate at low speed (00).
- If the temperature is between 20 and 30 degrees, the fan should operate at medium speed (01).
- If the temperature is greater than or equal to 30 degrees, the fan should operate at high speed (10).
The system should also have a reset function that sets the fan speed to low when activated.
