Design a combinational circuit that implements a simple SRT (Sweeney, Robertson, and Tocher) divider. The circuit should take two inputs: a dividend (numerator) and a divisor (denominator), and produce a quotient and a remainder as outputs. The inputs should be 8 bits wide, and the outputs should also be 8 bits wide. The circuit should handle division by zero by setting the quotient to zero and the remainder to the dividend.
