Design a simple switching regulator control circuit that takes a 3-bit binary input representing the desired output voltage level and generates a 1-bit output signal indicating whether the output voltage is too high (1) or too low (0). The desired output voltage levels correspond to the following inputs: 000 = 0V, 001 = 1V, 010 = 2V, 011 = 3V, 100 = 4V.
