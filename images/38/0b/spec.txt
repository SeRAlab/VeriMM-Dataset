
Design a 1-bit DRAM cell that can store a single bit and support read and write operations. The DRAM cell should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Inputs: `wordline` (active high), `data_in` (1-bit data to write), `clk` (clock), `write_enable` (active high to allow writing).
- Outputs: `data_out` (1-bit data read from the cell).
The behavior of the DRAM cell should be such that when `write_enable` is high and `wordline` is activated, the `data_in` should be written to the cell on the rising edge of `clk`. When `wordline` is activated and `write_enable` is low, `data_out` should reflect the stored value.
