
Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) that takes a 16-bit binary input and produces a 4-bit output representing the quantization level. The quantization levels are divided into 16 equal parts. If the input is greater than or equal to the maximum voltage level (e.g., 65535 for a 16-bit ADC), the output should be saturated to the maximum output level (i.e., 15).
