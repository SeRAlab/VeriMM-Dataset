
Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage of a processor based on current workload. The controller should take in a workload indicator (a 2-bit binary number) and based on its value, it will output the corresponding frequency setting (3 bits) and the voltage level (2 bits). The frequency and voltage settings are defined as follows:
- Workload 00: Frequency = 000 (low), Voltage = 00 (low)
- Workload 01: Frequency = 001 (medium), Voltage = 01 (medium)
- Workload 10: Frequency = 010 (high), Voltage = 10 (high)
- Workload 11: Frequency = 011 (max), Voltage = 11 (max)
The state should change based on the workload input and should only update on the rising edge of a clock signal.
