Design a fan speed control system that regulates the speed of a fan based on temperature levels. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The system will have an input for temperature and outputs for the fan speed setting. The fan speed should increase with temperature levels. The temperature levels are categorized into three ranges: 0-25°C (Low), 26-50°C (Medium), and 51-75°C (High). The fan speed should change according to the input temperature every clock cycle.
