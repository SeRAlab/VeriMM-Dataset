The output clock will only be enabled if the global enable (global_en) is active in combination with either module enable (module_en) or local enable (local_en), or if the external enable (external_en) is asserted. Additionally, the design incorporates a scan enable signal (pad_yy_icg_scan_en) for testing purposes, allowing for flexible operation in different configurations while ensuring clock gating for power management and efficiency.
module top_module(
  clk_in,
  global_en,
  module_en,
  local_en,
  external_en,
  pad_yy_icg_scan_en,
  clk_out
