
Create a simplified version of the ChaCha20 stream cipher that generates a keystream based on a 32-bit key and 64-bit nonce input. The module should take a 32-bit key (key) and a 64-bit nonce (nonce) as inputs and produce a 32-bit output (keystream) based on a simplified algorithm. The keystream should be generated based on a basic permutation of the key and nonce through a few rounds of a simple transformation.
