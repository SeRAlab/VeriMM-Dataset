Design a dual-edge-triggered flip-flop module that operates with asynchronous set and reset signals, and incorporates a configurable clock input. The output Q holds the state, with Q_N providing the inverted value. The state can be directly manipulated by the data input D when enable (SCE) is activated. If the secondary control input (SCD) is asserted, the state is reset to '0'. The asynchronous active-low set (SET_B) and reset (RESET_B) inputs allow the output to be directly controlled regardless of the clock signal, holding Q high when SET_B is low and resetting Q to low when RESET_B is low.
module top_module(
    Q      ,
    Q_N    ,
    D      ,
    SCD    ,
    SCE    ,
    CLK_N  ,
    SET_B  ,
    RESET_B,
    VPWR   ,
    VGND   ,
    VPB    ,
    VNB
);
