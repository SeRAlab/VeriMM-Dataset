Design a reset controller that has two inputs: a system clock (clk) and an active-low reset signal (reset_n). The controller should output a reset signal (reset_out) that remains high (inactive) when reset_n is high and goes low (active) for two clock cycles whenever reset_n transitions from low to high. The reset_out signal should only go low while the reset_n signal is transitioning from low to high, and it should not go low again until the system has been reset. Additionally, ensure the reset_out signal does not cause any glitches when reset_n is asserted.
