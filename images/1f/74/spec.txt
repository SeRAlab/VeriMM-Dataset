Design a 4-bit Carry-Skip Adder (CSA) in Verilog. The Carry-Skip Adder should take two 4-bit binary inputs A and B, along with a carry-in bit Cin. It should produce a 4-bit sum output S and a carry-out bit Cout. The Carry-Skip Adder utilizes carry generation and propagation logic to speed up the addition operation by skipping the carry in certain conditions.
