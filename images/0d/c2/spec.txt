Design a fan speed control system that takes temperature input and adjusts the fan speed accordingly. The system has three levels of fan speed: low, medium, and high. The temperature input is a 4-bit value. If the temperature is less than 10, the fan runs at low speed (00). If the temperature is between 10 and 20, the fan runs at medium speed (01). If the temperature is greater than 20, the fan runs at high speed (10). The state of the fan speed should be stored in a register that updates on every clock cycle.
