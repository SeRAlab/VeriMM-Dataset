Design a simple 8-bit digital-to-analog converter (DAC) circuit. The module takes an 8-bit input, DAC_in, and on each positive edge of the clock signal (clk), it adds this input to a 9-bit internal register, DAC_Register. The most significant bit of DAC_Register is then output as Audio_out, effectively representing the converted audio signal in a single bit stream. The design allows for the accumulation of audio samples over time, making it suitable for applications that require continuous audio output from a digital signal.
module top_module(input clk,input [7:0] DAC_in,output Audio_out);
