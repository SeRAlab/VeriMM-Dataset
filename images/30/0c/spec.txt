
Create a Bluetooth module controller in Verilog that manages the state of a Bluetooth device. The device can be in one of the following states: IDLE, CONNECTING, CONNECTED, and DISCONNECTED. The state transitions are controlled by the following inputs:
- `start_connection`: triggers the transition from IDLE to CONNECTING.
- `confirm_connection`: transitions from CONNECTING to CONNECTED.
- `disconnect`: transitions from CONNECTED or CONNECTING to DISCONNECTED.
- `reset`: resets the state machine to IDLE.

The state of the Bluetooth module should be represented with a 2-bit value:
- 00: IDLE
- 01: CONNECTING
- 10: CONNECTED
- 11: DISCONNECTED
