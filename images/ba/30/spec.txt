Design a synchronous 4-to-16 decoder with a register-based output. The module captures an address input (4 bits) to select one of 16 predefined 16-bit values. On each rising edge of the clock signal, the selected value from a register feed (out3) is transferred through a pipeline consisting of intermediate registers (out2 and out) to produce the final output. The decoder outputs a zero when an invalid address is specified.
