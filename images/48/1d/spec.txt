The LUT evaluates the two input signals and produces an intermediate ready signal (pre_rdy). This signal is then fed into a flip-flop, which samples the output of the LUT on the rising edge of the clock, holding its value in the flip-flop output (ff_output). Finally, the LUT output is directly assigned to another output signal (lut_output). This design can be used for combinational logic followed by sequential storage.
