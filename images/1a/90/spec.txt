
Design a 16-to-4 priority encoder in Verilog. The encoder should have 16 input lines (I0 to I15) and 4 output lines (Y0 to Y3) representing the binary encoding of the highest priority active input. If no inputs are active, the output should be 4'b0000. The priority is assigned based on the input number (I15 has the highest priority and I0 has the lowest).
