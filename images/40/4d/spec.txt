
Design a 5-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) using Verilog. The LFSR should be configured to have a feedback polynomial of x^5 + x^3 + 1, which corresponds to feedback from bits 5 and 3. Your design should include a clock input, a reset input, and a serial output. The LFSR should initialize to a non-zero state on reset.
