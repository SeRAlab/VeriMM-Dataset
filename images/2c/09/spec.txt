
Design a non-restoring divider in Verilog that takes two inputs: a dividend (numerator) and a divisor (denominator), both represented as 8-bit unsigned integers. The output should be the quotient (result of the division) and the remainder of the division. The non-restoring division algorithm should be implemented, and the division should be performed in a single cycle. The module should include a valid signal to indicate when the division operation is complete.
