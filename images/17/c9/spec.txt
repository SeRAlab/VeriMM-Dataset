Design a simple Ethernet MAC controller state machine that handles the transmission and reception of packets. The MAC controller should have two states: IDLE and TRANSMIT. In the IDLE state, the controller waits for a packet to transmit. When a packet is ready, it transitions to the TRANSMIT state, where it sends the packet. After transmission completes, it should return to the IDLE state. The inputs will be `start_transmit` (to initiate transmission) and `tx_done` (to indicate transmission is complete). The output will be `state` (current state of the MAC controller).
