Develop a one-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer with a control signal (ctrl) to select between two data inputs (D0 and D1). When the control signal is LOW (0), the output (S) reflects the value of D0; when the control signal is HIGH (1), the output reflects the value of D1. The design effectively routes one of the two input signals to the output based on the state of the control signal, enabling conditional data selection in digital circuits.
