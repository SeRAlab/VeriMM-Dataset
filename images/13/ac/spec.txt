Design a simple branch predictor that uses a 2-bit saturating counter for predicting whether a branch will be taken or not. The predictor should have an input for the branch direction (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and will update its state based on the input. Use the state to predict the next branch outcome, with states represented as 00 (strongly not taken), 01 (weakly not taken), 10 (weakly taken), and 11 (strongly taken).
