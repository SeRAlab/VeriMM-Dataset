Design a 4-bit magnitude comparator that compares two 4-bit inputs, `a` and `b`. On each positive clock edge, the comparator captures the values and evaluates their relationship: it outputs a signal indicating whether `a` is greater than `b` (gt) or if they are equal (eq). The design operates in two stages, ensuring proper synchronization during the comparison process.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] a,
    input [3:0] b,
    input clk,
    output reg eq,
    output reg gt
);
