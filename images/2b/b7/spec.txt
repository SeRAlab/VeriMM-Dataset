Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the fan speed based on temperature input. The fan can operate in three modes: OFF, LOW, and HIGH. The system should have the following behavior:
- If the temperature is less than or equal to 20°C, the fan should be OFF.
- If the temperature is greater than 20°C and less than or equal to 30°C, the fan should be set to LOW.
- If the temperature is greater than 30°C, the fan should operate at HIGH.
The controller should operate based on a clock signal and have a reset signal to set the fan to OFF.
