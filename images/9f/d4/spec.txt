Design a 4-bit adder/subtractor circuit. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, as well as a control signal C, which determines whether to perform addition (C=0) or subtraction (C=1). The output O is the 4-bit result of the operation, while CO indicates a carry-out in case of addition. Additionally, OV signals an overflow condition based on the input values and the result. This module combines arithmetic operations with overflow detection in a single integrated design.
module top_module(input [3:0] A, B, C, output [3:0] O, output CO, OV);
