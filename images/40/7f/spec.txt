Design a combinational logic circuit to evaluate a polynomial of degree 4 of the form: 
P(x) = a4*x^4 + a3*x^3 + a2*x^2 + a1*x + a0, 
where a4, a3, a2, a1, a0 are the coefficients and x is the variable. The coefficients and x are 8-bit unsigned integers. The circuit should output the result as a 32-bit unsigned integer.
