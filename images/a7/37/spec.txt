Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (in0, in1, in2, in3) based on the values of two select lines (s0 and s1). The output (out) reflects the chosen input based on the select line configuration: when s1=0 and s0=0, in0 is selected; when s1=0 and s0=1, in1 is selected; when s1=1 and s0=0, in2 is selected; and when both s1 and s0 are 1, in3 is selected. The design includes power and ground connections for proper operation of the AND gates used in the implementation.
module top_module(
    in0,
    in1,
    in2,
    in3,
    s0,
    s1,
    vpwr,
    vgnd,
    out
);
