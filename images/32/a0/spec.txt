Design a 5-stage pipelined arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that can perform addition, subtraction, and bitwise AND operations. The ALU should have inputs for two operands, a 2-bit opcode to select the operation, and outputs for the result and a valid signal. The pipeline stages are: Fetch, Decode, Execute, Memory, and Write-back. The ALU should be able to process multiple operations concurrently, with each stage of the pipeline being clocked.
