
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple neural network with a single-layer perceptron. The perceptron should take three inputs (x1, x2, x3), apply weights (w1, w2, w3) to them, sum the results, and apply a step activation function to produce the output. The weights and inputs should be configurable, and the output should be a single bit indicating whether the weighted sum exceeds a threshold value.
