Design a synchronous FIFO (First-In, First-Out) buffer with a maximum depth of 8 entries. The module accepts a 16-bit input data signal and controls operations for writing to and reading from the memory. It maintains internal pointers for the head and tail of the queue, as well as a count of the current items stored. On clock edges, it allows data to be written when the buffer is not full and read when it is not empty, while also providing status outputs for empty and full conditions. The module resets its state on the reset signal.
