Design a fixed-priority arbiter that selects one of multiple request signals based on their priority. The arbiter should have 4 request inputs (req[3:0]) and one grant output (grant). The priority order is req[3] > req[2] > req[1] > req[0]. When a request is granted, the arbiter should hold that grant until the request is released (active low). The arbiter also has a clock (clk) and reset (rst) input.
