Design a synchronous circuit that captures a one-bit input (IN) into three separate 2-bit wide registers (reg1, reg2, reg3) based on a clock (CLK) signal and an active-low reset (RST). Upon a rising edge of CLK, if RST is not asserted, the most significant bit of reg1 and reg2, and the indexed position of reg3, will be updated to the value of IN. If RST is asserted, the respective bits are initialized to LOW. This design allows for the storage and retention of input states across clock cycles while being resettable.
