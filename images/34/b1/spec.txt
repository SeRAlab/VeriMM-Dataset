Design a simple 8-bit Analog to Digital Converter (ADC) module that takes a 3-bit binary input representing a voltage level (from 0 to 7) and outputs an 8-bit binary representation of the corresponding digital value. The output should be equal to the input value multiplied by a scaling factor of 32 (i.e., for an input of 3, the output should be 96, which is 3 * 32 in binary).
