Design a 2-channel DMA controller that has two request lines (req1 and req2) and two grant lines (gnt1 and gnt2). When a request is made, it should grant access to one of the channels based on priority. The priority is given to channel 1 over channel 2. When both requests are active, the controller should grant access to channel 1. If channel 1's request is not active and channel 2's request is active, it should grant access to channel 2. The output grants should remain active for one clock cycle after the request is received.
