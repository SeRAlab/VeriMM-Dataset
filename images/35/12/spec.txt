Design a Verilog module that implements a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The DVFS controller should take the current temperature and workload as inputs and adjust the voltage and frequency levels accordingly. The output should be the selected voltage and frequency levels based on predefined thresholds for temperature and workload. Use a simple state machine to control the selection logic. The temperature range is 0-100 degrees Celsius, and workload ranges from 0-100%. The voltage levels are 0.8V, 1.0V, and 1.2V, and the frequency levels are 1GHz, 2GHz, and 3GHz.
