Design a 4-bit adder/subtractor circuit with a carry-out signal. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a single-bit selection input, sel. When sel is 0, the module performs addition of A and B, outputting the result as sum and the carry-out as cout. When sel is 1, it effectively computes A minus B by utilizing the two's complement of B. The computed sum and adjusted carry-out accurately reflect the operation selected by sel, enabling both addition and subtraction functionality.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    input sel,
    output [3:0] sum,
    output cout
    );
