
Design a simple SATA controller state machine that can handle three states: IDLE, TRANSMIT, and RECEIVE. The state transitions are as follows:
- From IDLE to TRANSMIT on the signal `start_tx`.
- From IDLE to RECEIVE on the signal `start_rx`.
- From TRANSMIT back to IDLE on the signal `stop_tx`.
- From RECEIVE back to IDLE on the signal `stop_rx`.
The state machine should have a clock input and an asynchronous reset input.
