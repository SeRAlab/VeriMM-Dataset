
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple elliptic curve point addition operation over a finite field. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) on the elliptic curve defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b, where 'a' and 'b' are constants. The output should be the resulting point (x3, y3) after the addition operation. The implementation should follow the common formulas for point addition in elliptic curves.
