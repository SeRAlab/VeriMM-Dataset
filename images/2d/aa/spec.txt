Design a data forwarding unit that detects when there is a data hazard in a pipelined processor. The forwarding unit should have two inputs: the destination register of the instruction currently in the Execute stage (EX_dst) and the source registers of the instruction in the Decode stage (ID_src1 and ID_src2). The forwarding unit should output two signals: ForwardA and ForwardB, indicating whether the data from the EX stage should be forwarded to the ID stage for each of the source registers.
