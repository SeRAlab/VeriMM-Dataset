Design a 2-to-4 line decoder that converts a 2-bit binary input (A and B) into a corresponding activated output line (Y0, Y1, Y2, Y3). Depending on the input combination, one of the four outputs is asserted HIGH (active LOW logic), while the others remain LOW. Specifically, Y0 is HIGH when both inputs are LOW, Y1 is HIGH when A is LOW and B is HIGH, Y2 is HIGH when A is HIGH and B is LOW, and Y3 is HIGH when both inputs are HIGH.
module top_module(
    input A,
    input B,
    output reg Y0,
    output reg Y1,
    output reg Y2,
    output reg Y3
);
