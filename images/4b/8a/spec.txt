Design a tap controller that generates a binary output based on the power supply conditions. The output, `tap_output`, is set to LOW (0) when the power supply (VPWR) is active and the ground (VGND) is inactive, and conversely, it is set to HIGH (1) when the power supply is inactive and the ground is active. This module can be used for power management applications to indicate different states of power supply integrity.
module top_module(
    input VPWR,
    input VGND,
    output reg tap_output
);
