
Design a 2-channel DMA controller with the following specifications:
- The controller has two channels (Channel 0 and Channel 1).
- Each channel has a request signal (req), a grant signal (grant), and an acknowledge signal (ack).
- When a request is made by either channel, the controller grants access to one channel at a time based on a round-robin scheduling algorithm.
- The controller should also handle acknowledge signals to indicate successful data transfer.
- The system should be clocked and resettable.
