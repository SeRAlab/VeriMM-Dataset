Design a circuit that implements a control logic based on two inputs, A and TE, and produces an output Z. The output Z is determined by the state of A and TE as follows: if A is high (1) and TE is low (0), Z outputs the power supply voltage (VPWR); if A is low (0) and TE is high (1), Z outputs the ground voltage (VGND); if both A and TE are high, Z outputs a constant high (1); and if both A and TE are low, Z outputs a constant low (0). This module serves as a level control circuit that can manage power states based on the inputs.
module top_module(
    input A,
    input TE,
    input VPWR,
    input VGND,
    output reg Z
);
