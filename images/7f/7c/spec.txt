Design a circuit that functions as a 4-input AND gate with one of its inputs being a 2-input NAND gate. The module generates an output Y, which is HIGH only when inputs A3, A4, and B1 are all HIGH, and additionally, when inputs A1 and A2 are not both HIGH (ensured by the NAND operation). This design effectively combines the functionality of both a NAND and an AND gate in a single output logic function.
module top_module(
    Y   ,
    A1  ,
    A2  ,
    A3  ,
    A4  ,
    B1  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND,
    VPB ,
    VNB
);
