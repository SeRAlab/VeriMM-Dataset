
Design a simple dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS) controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency based on the load. The input will be a 3-bit load signal (0 to 7), and the output will be a 2-bit frequency setting (00 to 11) and a 2-bit voltage setting (00 to 11). The voltage and frequency settings will be determined based on predefined load thresholds:
- Load 0-1: Frequency 00, Voltage 00
- Load 2-4: Frequency 01, Voltage 01
- Load 5-7: Frequency 10, Voltage 10
