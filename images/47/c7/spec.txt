Otherwise, it checks if the input time unit exceeds or equals 10,000, setting `valid_timeunit` to HIGH if the condition is satisfied and to LOW otherwise. The counter increments with each clock cycle, although its purpose isn't utilized in this design.

  input reset,
  input [31:0] module_to_check_timeunit,
