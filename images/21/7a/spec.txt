Design a simple DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency based on the input voltage level. The controller has a 2-bit input voltage level (00 to 11) and a 2-bit output frequency level, where each input voltage corresponds to a specific frequency level. The mapping is as follows: 00 -> 00 (lowest frequency), 01 -> 01, 10 -> 10, 11 -> 11 (highest frequency). The output frequency should be updated on the rising edge of a clock signal.
