Design a JK flip-flop in Verilog. The JK flip-flop should have inputs J, K, clock (clk), and asynchronous reset (reset). The outputs should be Q and Qn. The behavior should be: Q takes the value of J when K is low, Q takes the complement of Q when J is high and K is high, and Q retains its value when both J and K are low. The reset signal should set Q to 0 asynchronously.
