Design a 2-channel DMA (Direct Memory Access) controller that allows two channels to request data transfer from peripheral devices to memory. The controller should accept requests from the channels, grant access to one channel at a time, handle data transfers, and indicate when the transfers are complete. The controller should have the following inputs and outputs:
Inputs: 
- clk (clock signal)
- rst (reset signal)
- req1 (request from channel 1)
- req2 (request from channel 2)
Outputs:
- ack1 (acknowledge for channel 1)
- ack2 (acknowledge for channel 2)
- busy (indicates if the controller is currently busy with a transfer)
