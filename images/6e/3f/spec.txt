Design a data transfer module for an ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) interface that operates synchronously with a clock input. The module receives control signals and data from a source ADC and transmits these signals, as well as the corresponding data, to a destination ADC. It includes overflow handling through the `src_adc_dovf` and `dst_adc_dovf` signals. The `status` output communicates a fixed RP_ID value, indicating the module’s identity, while enabling seamless data flow and synchronization between the source and destination components.

  clk,

  status,

  src_adc_dsync,
  src_adc_ddata,
  src_adc_dovf,

  dst_adc_dwr,
  dst_adc_dsync,
  dst_adc_ddata,
  dst_adc_dovf
);
