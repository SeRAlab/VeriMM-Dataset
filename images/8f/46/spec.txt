Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs multiple operations based on a 3-bit control input. The ALU supports bitwise AND, OR, addition, subtraction (using two's complement), and comparisons for signed/unsigned less-than, as well as bitwise XOR and NOR operations. The output, `alu_res`, reflects the operation determined by the control signals, while the overflow flag, `of`, indicates arithmetic overflow in addition or subtraction operations.
module top_module(input      [31:0] a, b, 
           input      [2:0]  control, 
           output reg [31:0] alu_res,
           output            of);
