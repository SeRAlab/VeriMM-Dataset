Design a simple Ethernet MAC controller state machine that handles receiving and transmitting data frames. The controller should have three states: IDLE, RECEIVE, and TRANSMIT. The state transitions are defined as follows:  
- From IDLE to RECEIVE when a 'receive' signal is asserted.  
- From RECEIVE to IDLE after the frame is received.  
- From IDLE to TRANSMIT when a 'transmit' signal is asserted.  
- From TRANSMIT to IDLE after the frame is transmitted.  
The output should indicate the current state of the controller.
