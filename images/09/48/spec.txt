Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature readings. The system should have three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The fan's speed should increase with temperature. The temperature is represented as a 4-bit binary number (0000 to 1111), where 0000 corresponds to 0 degrees, and 1111 corresponds to 15 degrees. The speed settings are as follows: 
- 0-4 degrees: Low speed (00)
- 5-9 degrees: Medium speed (01)
- 10-15 degrees: High speed (10)
The fan speed should be stored in a 2-bit register.
