
You are tasked with designing a module that takes in two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and outputs the result of A multiplied by B. The module should be named "multiplier" and should contain only combinational logic. The output should be a 8-bit signal named "result". 

Assume that the inputs are in two's complement format and that the output should also be in two's complement format. The most significant bit of the output should be the sign bit. 

You may use any Verilog operators or constructs that you see fit.
