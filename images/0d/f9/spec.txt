Design a Verilog module that takes a 4-bit input representing the current frequency and outputs a 2-bit value indicating the voltage level required for Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS). The mapping is as follows: 0000 -> 00 (Voltage Level 0), 0001 -> 01 (Voltage Level 1), 0010 -> 01 (Voltage Level 1), 0011 -> 10 (Voltage Level 2), 0100 -> 10 (Voltage Level 2), 0101 -> 11 (Voltage Level 3), 0110 -> 11 (Voltage Level 3), 0111 -> 00 (Voltage Level 0), 1000 -> 00 (Voltage Level 0), 1001 -> 01 (Voltage Level 1), 1010 -> 01 (Voltage Level 1), 1011 -> 10 (Voltage Level 2), 1100 -> 10 (Voltage Level 2), 1101 -> 11 (Voltage Level 3), 1110 -> 11 (Voltage Level 3), 1111 -> 00 (Voltage Level 0).
