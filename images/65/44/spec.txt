The module is clocked and reset-sensitive, where it outputs an 8-bit sum and a carry-out bit (cout). The adder processes the lower 4 bits of the inputs in the first stage and holds them in temporary registers, while the upper 4 bits are stored in additional registers. The final sum is calculated in a subsequent stage by adding the results from the first stage with the upper halves of the inputs, ensuring that the carry-out is handled correctly. This design enables increased throughput by overlapping the addition of lower and upper bits in a pipelined manner.
