Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a processing unit based on the current workload. The controller should have an input signal to indicate the workload level (0-3, where 0 is low and 3 is high) and outputs for voltage and frequency settings. Voltage and frequency settings should be predefined values based on the workload level:  
- Level 0: Voltage = 1.0V, Frequency = 1.0GHz  
- Level 1: Voltage = 1.2V, Frequency = 1.5GHz  
- Level 2: Voltage = 1.5V, Frequency = 2.0GHz  
- Level 3: Voltage = 1.8V, Frequency = 2.5GHz  
The outputs should be updated on a clock edge.
