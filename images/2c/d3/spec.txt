
Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) output comparator in Verilog. This comparator will take two 16-bit inputs, `data_in1` and `data_in2`, and output a 2-bit result indicating their relationship:
- If `data_in1` is greater than `data_in2`, the output should be `2'b01`.
- If `data_in1` is less than `data_in2`, the output should be `2'b10`.
- If `data_in1` is equal to `data_in2`, the output should be `2'b00`.
