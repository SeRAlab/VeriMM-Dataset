Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (in0, in1, in2, in3) based on two selection inputs (sel0 and sel1). The output (out) will reflect the value of the selected input. When sel0 and sel1 are both low, in0 is selected; sel0 low and sel1 high selects in1; sel0 high and sel1 low chooses in2; and when both are high, in3 is chosen. The design includes logic gates to implement the selection and combinational logic necessary for the multiplexing function.
module top_module(out, in0, in1, in2, in3, sel0, sel1);
