Implement a 4-bit Brent-Kung adder. The Brent-Kung adder is a parallel prefix adder that uses a tree of carry computations to achieve faster addition. Your task is to create a Verilog module that takes two 4-bit inputs and produces a 4-bit sum and a carry-out. The inputs are A and B, and the outputs are S (sum) and Cout (carry-out).
