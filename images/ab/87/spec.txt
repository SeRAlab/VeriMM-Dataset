Design a 4-bit up/down counter that increments or decrements its count based on control signals. The counter operates on the positive edge of the clock signal, resetting to zero when the reset signal is low. When the enable signal is active, the counter will increment its value when the up_down signal is high (indicating counting upwards) and decrement when it is low (indicating counting downwards).
module top_module(
    input wire clk,
    input wire rst,
    input wire en,
    input wire up_down,
    output reg [3:0] count
);
