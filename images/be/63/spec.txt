Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (IN0, IN1, IN2, IN3) based on a 2-bit selection signal (S). The output (MO) reflects the value of the selected input, where S=00 corresponds to IN0, S=01 to IN1, S=10 to IN2, and S=11 to IN3. If the selection is invalid, the output defaults to LOW.
module top_module(MO, IN0, IN1, IN2, IN3, S);
