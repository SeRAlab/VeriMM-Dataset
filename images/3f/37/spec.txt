Design a simple interrupt controller in Verilog that has the following functionality:  
- It has 3 interrupt request inputs (irq0, irq1, irq2) and one interrupt acknowledge output (ack).  
- When any of the interrupt requests is asserted (high), the output ack should also be asserted.  
- The ack should reset after one clock cycle.  
- The design should also include a reset input that clears the ack when asserted.
