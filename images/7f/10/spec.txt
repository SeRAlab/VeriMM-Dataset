Design a D-type flip-flop with an asynchronous reset feature. The flip-flop captures the value of input D on the rising edge of the clock signal CLK. When the reset signal RSTB is low, the output Q is set to LOW regardless of the clock, while QN outputs the inverted value of Q. When RSTB is high, the flip-flop follows the input D, allowing for data storage in digital circuits.
