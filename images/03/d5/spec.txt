
Design a Verilog module for an audio mixer that takes two audio signals as inputs and provides a mixed output. The audio signals are represented as 8-bit unsigned integers. The mixer should have a volume control for each input, allowing the user to adjust the contribution of each signal to the final output. The volume control values are also 8-bit unsigned integers, where `0` means no contribution and `255` means full contribution. The mixed output should be clamped to a maximum of `255`.
