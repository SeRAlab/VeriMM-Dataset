Design a 64-bit linear feedback shift register (LFSR) with an additional 4-bit output. The circuit produces a pseudo-random sequence based on the feedback generated by an XOR operation on specific bits of the shift register. On each clock pulse, the most significant bit is replaced with the feedback value, while the output Q takes the two least significant bits of the shift register. The register resets to zero when the active-low reset signal is asserted, ensuring controlled initialization of the LFSR and output.
