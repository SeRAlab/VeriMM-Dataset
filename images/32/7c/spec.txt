
Design a positive edge-triggered SR flip-flop in Verilog. The flip-flop should have two inputs (S for Set, R for Reset) and one output (Q). The flip-flop should follow the characteristic behavior where:
- If S=1 and R=0, Q is set to 1.
- If S=0 and R=1, Q is reset to 0.
- If S=0 and R=0, Q maintains its previous state.
- If S=1 and R=1, the state is undefined (you can choose to keep Q unchanged).
