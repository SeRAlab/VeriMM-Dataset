Design a power gating circuit in Verilog that uses two control signals, `power_en` and `power_gnd`, to control the power supply to a device. The output `device_state` should be set to 1 if both `power_en` is high and `power_gnd` is low, indicating that the device is powered on. If `power_en` is low or `power_gnd` is high, `device_state` should be 0, indicating that the device is powered off.
