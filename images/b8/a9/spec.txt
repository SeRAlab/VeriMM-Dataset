Design a 16-bit wide synchronous parallel-in, parallel-out (PIPO) register. The register loads the input data (din) into the output (dout) on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when the load signal (ld) is asserted. If the clear signal (clr) is asserted, the output is reset to zero. This functionality allows for controlled data storage and retrieval in digital circuits.
