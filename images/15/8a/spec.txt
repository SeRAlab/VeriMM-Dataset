Design a 64-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode input: 
000 - ADD
001 - SUBTRACT
010 - AND
011 - OR
100 - XOR
101 - NAND
110 - NOR
111 - XNOR
The ALU should take two 64-bit input operands and produce a 64-bit output result. The module should also include a valid output signal that indicates when the operation is complete.
