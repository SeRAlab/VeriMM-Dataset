
Design a finite state machine (FSM) that can control a simple traffic light system. The traffic light has three states: Green, Yellow, and Red. The FSM will change states according to the following rules:
1. The light starts in the Green state.
2. It stays in the Green state for 10 time units.
3. It then transitions to the Yellow state, which lasts for 3 time units.
4. Finally, it transitions to the Red state, which lasts for 10 time units.
5. After Red, it goes back to Green and repeats the cycle.
6. The FSM should have an enable signal that allows it to operate; if the enable signal is low, the FSM should hold its current state.
