Additionally, the output SD_CLK is kept in sync and resets to zero on a reset signal (RST). This module effectively takes a higher frequency clock and divides it down to a desired lower frequency clock, which can be useful in various timing applications or in generating controlled clock signals for synchronous circuits.

           input CLK,
           input [7:0] DIVIDER,

       );
