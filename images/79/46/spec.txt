Design a 4-bit binary adder with carry-in and carry-out capabilities. The module takes two 4-bit inputs (A and B) and a carry-in (CIN) to compute their sum (SUM) along with a carry-out (COUT) for cases where the addition exceeds 4 bits. Additionally, it determines overflow (OVF) by checking for specific conditions during the addition, ensuring accurate detection of overflow scenarios based on the inputs.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    input CIN,
    output [3:0] SUM,
    output COUT,
    output OVF
);
