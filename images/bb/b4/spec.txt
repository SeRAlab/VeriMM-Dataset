Design a circuit that computes the color difference between two 16-bit pixel values, `pix1` and `pix2`. The module independently calculates the absolute differences of the red, green, and blue color components, which are extracted from the pixel values. The red difference is determined from the least significant 5 bits, the green difference is calculated from the next 6 bits, and the blue difference is based on the most significant 5 bits. The final output, `diff`, is a 7-bit value representing the total color difference by summing the individual color differences.
module top_module(
    input [15:0] pix1,
    input [15:0] pix2,
    output [6:0] diff
    );
