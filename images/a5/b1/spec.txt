The module supports two independent address lines and write enable signals for each port. When `write_en_port1` or `write_en_port2` is asserted on the rising edge, the corresponding data from `data_in_port1` or `data_in_port2` is stored in the memory location specified by `addr_port1` or `addr_port2`, respectively. The module outputs the data stored at the addresses provided by `addr_port1` and `addr_port2` on `data_out_port1` and `data_out_port2`, enabling concurrent access to the RAM by two ports.

);
