Design a 4-point FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) module in Verilog. The module should take four complex input values represented by pairs of real and imaginary parts, perform the FFT, and output the transformed complex values. The FFT algorithm can be implemented using the Cooley-Tukey algorithm. The inputs are 4 pairs of 8-bit signed integers (for real and imaginary parts), and the outputs should also be 4 pairs of 8-bit signed integers.
