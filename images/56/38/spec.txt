Depending on the 'form' input, it either adds pairs of inputs (A and C, B and D) or a combination of all four inputs for generating outputs Y1 and Y2. The structure employs both hierarchical and generate constructs to facilitate multiple parallel additions, ultimately allowing the selection of the resultant sum outputs through the 'vec' input.
module top_module(form, vec, A, B, C, D, Y1, Y2);
