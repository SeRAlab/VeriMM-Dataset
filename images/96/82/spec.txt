The module generates the carry signals based on the propagate (`p`) and generate (`g`) logic to quickly determine carry outputs for each bit, improving performance over traditional ripple carry adders. The final sum is produced by XORing the inputs and the carry signals, while the output carry, `Cout`, indicates if there is an overflow from the most significant bit.
