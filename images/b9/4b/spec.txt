Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) capable of performing a variety of logical and arithmetic operations based on the 4-bit ALUControl input signal. It supports operations including bitwise AND, OR, addition, left and right shifts, subtraction, and comparison, with the ability to specify a shift amount. The output includes the result of the performed operation (ALUResult) as well as a zero flag that indicates if the result is zero.
