Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller takes an input signal representing workload (in the range of 0 to 15) and adjusts the output clock frequency and voltage level based on predefined workload thresholds. The output frequency and voltage levels are represented as 2-bit and 3-bit values, respectively. The mapping is as follows:
- Workload 0-3: Frequency 00 (low), Voltage 000 (low)
- Workload 4-7: Frequency 01 (medium), Voltage 001 (medium)
- Workload 8-11: Frequency 10 (high), Voltage 010 (high)
- Workload 12-15: Frequency 11 (max), Voltage 011 (max)
