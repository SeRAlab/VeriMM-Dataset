Design a combinational logic circuit that produces an output signal X based on the logical conditions of its inputs. The output X will be HIGH when all three inputs A1, A2, and A3 are HIGH or when both inputs B1 and B2 are HIGH, but only if the power supply signals VPWR and VGND are also active (HIGH). This module effectively acts as a logic gate that combines AND and OR operations with an additional requirement for power conditions.
module top_module(
    X   ,
    A1  ,
    A2  ,
    A3  ,
    B1  ,
    B2  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND
);
