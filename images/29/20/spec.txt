Design a 1-bit SRAM cell that can store a single bit of data. The SRAM cell should have the following inputs: `D` (data input), `WE` (write enable), and `CLK` (clock). The output should be `Q` (stored data output). The SRAM cell should store the data on the rising edge of the clock when `WE` is high, and maintain the stored data when `WE` is low.
