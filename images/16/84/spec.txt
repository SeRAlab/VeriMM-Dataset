
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Elliptic Curve Point Addition operation. The module should take two points on the elliptic curve (P1 and P2) and produce the resulting point (R) as output. The points are represented in homogeneous coordinates (X, Y, Z), where Z is the scaling factor. The elliptic curve equation used is of the form Y^2 = X^3 + aX + b over a finite field.
The inputs are as follows:
- P1_x, P1_y, P1_z (coordinates for the first point)
- P2_x, P2_y, P2_z (coordinates for the second point)

The output will be:
- R_x, R_y, R_z (coordinates of the resultant point after addition)
