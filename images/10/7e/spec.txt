Design a 2nd-order IIR filter with coefficients for a digital signal processing application. The filter should accept input samples and produce output samples based on the difference equation:  
y[n] = b0*x[n] + b1*x[n-1] + b2*x[n-2] - a1*y[n-1] - a2*y[n-2]  
where:  
b0, b1, b2 are the feedforward coefficients,  
a1, a2 are the feedback coefficients.  
The coefficients can be defined as parameters in the Verilog module.
