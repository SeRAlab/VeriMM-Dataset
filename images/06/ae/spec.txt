
Design a 2-bit synchronous up/down counter. The counter should have two inputs: `up` (to count up) and `down` (to count down). The counter should increment on the rising edge of a clock signal when `up` is high and decrement when `down` is high. The counter should wrap around such that it goes from 11 to 00 when counting up and from 00 to 11 when counting down. The counter should be initialized to 00.
