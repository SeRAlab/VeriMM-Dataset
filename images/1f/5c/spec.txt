Design an 8-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on the provided control signal: 
1. Addition (op = 00)
2. Subtraction (op = 01)
3. AND operation (op = 10)
4. OR operation (op = 11)

The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs `a` and `b`, and a 2-bit control signal `op`, and produce an 8-bit output `result` based on the selected operation. The ALU should also have a carry-out output for the addition and subtraction operations.
