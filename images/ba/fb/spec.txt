Design a simple 4-bit calculator capable of performing addition and subtraction. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a control signal, Subtract. When Subtract is low, it outputs the sum of A and B; when Subtract is high, it outputs the difference of A and B. The result is a 4-bit wide output, and an overflow indicator is also provided to signal if the operation has exceeded the maximum representable value.
module top_module(A, B, Subtract, Result, Overflow);
