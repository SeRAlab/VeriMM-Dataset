When enabled, it sends an 8-bit input data along with a parity bit (odd or even) based on the specified mode. The transmission completes when the data and parity bit are sent, indicating a busy status while processing. The output line, `tx`, carries the transmitted data or parity, and the `done` signal indicates the completion of the operation.

                            input wire [7:0] din, 
                            input parity,
                            output busy,
