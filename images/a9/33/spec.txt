When in the PLAY state, if the input signal `din` is low, the FSM transitions to the OVER state and asserts the output `dout` high. In the OVER state, if `din` becomes low, it remains in the OVER state; otherwise, it transitions back to PLAY. An external control input `ci` can force the FSM into the OVER state at any time, while the clock signal `clk` synchronizes state transitions.
