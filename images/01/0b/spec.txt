
Design an 8-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
- 101: Comparison (A < B, output 1 bit)
- 110: Shift Left A by 1
- 111: Shift Right A by 1

The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs A and B and the 3-bit control signal. The output should be an 8-bit result (or 1 bit for comparison).
