Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer with 32-bit wide inputs. Depending on the 2-bit selection signal (`sel`), the module routes one of three 32-bit input signals—`read_reg`, `write_back_data`, or `data_mem_in`—to the output (`out`). Specifically, when `sel` is `00`, `out` receives `read_reg`, when `sel` is `01`, it outputs `write_back_data`, and for any other `sel` value, it passes through `data_mem_in`. This module effectively consolidates multiple data sources into a single output channel based on the selection criteria.
