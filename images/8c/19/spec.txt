The module extracts one of four bytes or one of two half-words determined by the 2-bit ALU output selection. It then extends the selected byte or half-word to generate a 32-bit output based on the 3-bit ExtDM control input. The outputs can represent the original data, right-extended zeroes, or sign-extended values, thus facilitating data formatting for various operations in a processor pipeline.
