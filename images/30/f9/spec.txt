
Design a data forwarding unit for a simple processor. The data forwarding unit should take two inputs: `source1` and `source2`, which represent the data from two different sources (e.g., registers or ALUs). There are also two control signals `forwardA` and `forwardB` that determine whether the data should be forwarded from `source1` or `source2`. The output should be `data_out` which reflects the forwarded data based on the control signals.
