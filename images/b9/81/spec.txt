Design a traffic light controller that manages the operation of a traffic light system based on a clock and reset input. The module cycles through three states: GREEN, YELLOW, and RED, with specified durations for each light. The GREEN light remains active for 9 clock cycles, followed by a transition to YELLOW for 1 cycle, and then to RED for 7 cycles before returning to GREEN. The reset signal initializes the controller to the GREEN state, setting the appropriate output signals for the traffic lights. This design ensures a simple sequential timing mechanism for traffic light control.
module top_module(
  input clk,
  input reset,
  output reg green,
  output reg yellow,
  output reg red
);
