
Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
- 101: Logical left shift
- 110: Logical right shift
- 111: Set on less than (SLT)

The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs and output a 32-bit result, along with a zero flag that indicates if the result is zero.
