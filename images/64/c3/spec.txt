Design a 4-bit ripple carry adder that takes two 4-bit binary inputs (a and b) along with a carry-in (cin) to compute a 4-bit sum (sum) and a carry-out (cout). The module calculates the sum bit by bit, propagating the carry from each less significant bit to the next more significant bit, resulting in the final sum and carry output, allowing for the addition of binary numbers up to 15 + 15 with an additional carry bit.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] a,
  input [3:0] b,
  input cin,
  output [3:0] sum,
  output cout
);
