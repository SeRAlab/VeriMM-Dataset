
You are required to design a simple Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG) using a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR). The LFSR will take an initial seed and produce a sequence of pseudo-random bits. Your task is to create the Verilog module for the CSPRNG that outputs a random bit every clock cycle. The design should also include a reset mechanism to initialize the seed.
