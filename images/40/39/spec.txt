A Voltage-Controlled Oscillator (VCO) that generates a frequency output based on a control voltage input. The frequency output should double or halve based on the value of the control voltage. The control voltage is represented as a 3-bit input: if it is below 4 (000 to 011), the output frequency should be low (0), and if it is 4 or above (100 to 111), the output frequency should be high (1). Implement the VCO behavior in Verilog.
