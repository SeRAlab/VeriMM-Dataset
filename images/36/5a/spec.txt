Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the supply voltage and frequency of a processor based on the current workload. The controller has a clock input, a workload input (in the form of a 3-bit binary number), and outputs the selected voltage level (2 bits) and frequency level (2 bits). The voltage can be set to 00 (0.9V), 01 (1.0V), 10 (1.1V), 11 (1.2V) based on workload levels and the frequency can be set to 00 (500MHz), 01 (1GHz), 10 (1.5GHz), 11 (2GHz) based on the same levels. The controller should change voltage and frequency on the rising edge of the clock based on the workload input.
