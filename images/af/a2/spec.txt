The latch resets asynchronously when the RESET_B signal is low, forcing the output Q to 0 and Q_N to 1. When RESET_B is high and the GATE signal goes high, the current value of D is stored in Q and its complement is driven to Q_N, allowing the output to change only on the rising edge of the GATE signal.
