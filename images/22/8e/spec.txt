
Design a thermal sensor calibration module in Verilog. The module should take a raw temperature value and a calibration factor as inputs, and output a calibrated temperature value. The calibrated temperature is calculated as: 
Calibrated Temperature = Raw Temperature + Calibration Factor.
The inputs are 8-bit unsigned integers, and the output is also an 8-bit unsigned integer. If the calibrated temperature exceeds 255, it should wrap around (i.e., modulo 256).
