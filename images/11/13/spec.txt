Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the frequency and voltage levels based on the input workload. The controller takes a 2-bit workload indicator (`workload`) and outputs the corresponding frequency (`frequency`) and voltage (`voltage`) levels. The mapping is as follows:
- 00: Frequency = 1 GHz, Voltage = 1 V
- 01: Frequency = 2 GHz, Voltage = 1.2 V
- 10: Frequency = 3 GHz, Voltage = 1.5 V
- 11: Frequency = 4 GHz, Voltage = 1.8 V
