Design a D-type flip-flop with asynchronous set and reset capabilities. When the clock signal (CLK) experiences a rising edge, the flip-flop will output the value of input D unless the asynchronous set (SET_B) or reset (RESET_B) signals are asserted. If SET_B is low, the output Q will be set to high regardless of D, while a low signal on RESET_B will drive Q to low. The module also provides the complementary output Q_N, which is the inverse of Q.

    Q    ,
    Q_N  ,
    CLK  ,
    D    ,
    SET_B,
    RESET_B
);
