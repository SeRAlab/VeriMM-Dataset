
Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the fan speed based on the temperature input. The system should have three speed levels: low, medium, and high. The fan speed should change based on the following temperature thresholds:
- Temperature < 20°C: Low speed
- 20°C ≤ Temperature < 30°C: Medium speed
- Temperature ≥ 30°C: High speed

The system should include a clock signal and a reset signal. The fan speed should be updated on the rising edge of the clock.
