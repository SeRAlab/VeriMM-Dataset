Design a 3-input logic circuit that computes the output X based on the combinations of its inputs A, B, and C. It generates three intermediate signals representing the AND of each pair of inputs (A and B, A and C, B and C). The final output X is produced by ORing these intermediate signals together, resulting in an output that is true if at least two of the three inputs are true. This implementation effectively acts as a majority voter for three binary inputs.
module top_module(
    input A,
    input B,
    input C,
    output X
);
