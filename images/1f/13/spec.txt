Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on a temperature input. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The system should use a state machine to control the fan speed based on the temperature readings. If the temperature is below 20°C, the fan should be off. Between 20°C and 30°C, the fan should operate at low speed. Between 30°C and 40°C, the fan should operate at medium speed, and above 40°C, the fan should operate at high speed. The system should have an asynchronous reset input to turn off the fan immediately.
