Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs various operations based on the 3-bit ALU control signal (ALUCtr). The ALU can execute bitwise AND, OR, addition, subtraction, and arithmetic right shift operations depending on the value of ALUCtr. When ALUCtr is 000, it outputs the bitwise AND of inputs A and B; for 001, it outputs the bitwise OR; for 010, it performs addition; for 011, it executes subtraction; for 100, it performs an arithmetic right shift of B by the number of positions specified by the lower 5 bits of A. If none of the specified operations match, it outputs zero.
