
Design a simple SR latch using D flip-flops. The SR latch should have two inputs (S and R) and one output (Q). The SR latch should behave as follows: when S is high and R is low, Q should be set to high; when S is low and R is high, Q should be reset to low; when both S and R are low, Q should hold its previous state; when both S and R are high, the output should be treated as an invalid state.
