Design a combinational arithmetic and logic unit (ALU) that performs a variety of operations on two signed 32-bit inputs. The operations are determined by a 4-bit opcode, allowing for addition, subtraction, logical AND, logical OR, left shift, right shift, and arithmetic right shift, as well as comparison operations for greater than and less than. The module also accepts a 5-bit shift amount to specify how many positions to shift for the shift operations. The result of the selected operation is output as a signed 32-bit value.
module top_module(input signed [31:0] in1, input signed [31:0] in2, input [3:0] opCode, input [4:0] shiftAmt, output signed [31:0] result);
