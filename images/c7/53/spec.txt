

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that performs a bitwise OR operation between two 8-bit inputs, `A` and `B`, and outputs the result as an 8-bit output `X`. The module should be named `bitwise_or` and should have the following ports:

- `input [7:0] A`
- `input [7:0] B`
- `output [7:0] X`

The design should use only bitwise operators (`&`, `|`, `^`, `~`) and should not use any other operators or keywords (e.g. `if`, `case`, `assign`, etc.). You may use intermediate wires or variables to aid in the design.
