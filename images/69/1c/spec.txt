Design a 32-bit arithmetic and logic unit (ALU) capable of performing various operations based on a 4-bit control signal. The ALU supports logical operations (AND, OR), arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction), a comparison operation (set on less than), and a NOR operation. The module outputs the result of the selected operation and also indicates if the result of the operation is zero.
module top_module(
	 read1, 
	 read2,
	 control, 
	 result,
	 zero
    );
