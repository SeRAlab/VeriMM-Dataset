Design a register file with 16 registers, each 16 bits wide, capable of synchronous read and write operations. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is active, all registers are cleared to zero. If write_enable is asserted, the module writes the write_data to the register specified by the write_address. The module allows for simultaneous reading from two different registers, as indicated by read_address_1 and read_address_2, outputting their contents to read_data_1 and read_data_2, respectively.
module top_module(
    clk,
    rst,
    write_enable,
    write_address,
    write_data,
    read_address_1,
    read_address_2,
    read_data_1,
    read_data_2
);
