
Design a PID controller in Verilog. The controller should take a setpoint, a measured value, and outputs the control signal based on proportional, integral, and derivative calculations. The PID controller should have the following inputs: `setpoint`, `measured_value`, and `clk`. The outputs should be the control signal `control_signal`. The controller should have configurable gains for each of the PID components (`Kp`, `Ki`, and `Kd`).
