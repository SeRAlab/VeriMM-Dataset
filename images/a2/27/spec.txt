Design a three-input one-output multiplexer that selects one of three 4-bit wide inputs (A, B, or C) based on the combination of the input signals. The output Y is determined by the active input, where Y is high when exactly one of the inputs A, B, or C is high, corresponding to the unique condition that only one input is selected at a time, effectively performing a 3-to-1 selection function.
module top_module(
  output reg [3:0] Y,
  input [3:0] A, B, C
);
