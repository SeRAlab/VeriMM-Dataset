Design a 4-bit binary adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs (A and B) along with a carry-in (Cin). The output is a 4-bit sum (S) and a overflow indicator (V). The overflow flag is asserted when the resultant sum exceeds the maximum representable value for 4 bits (which is 15 in decimal). The module efficiently captures both the sum and potential overflow generated during the addition process.
