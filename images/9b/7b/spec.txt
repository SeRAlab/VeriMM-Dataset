Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that can perform left logical shifts based on a 2-bit control input (A). When A=00, the output (S) is the same as the input (D). When A=01, the input is shifted left by one bit with a zero filling in from the right. For A=10, the shift is by two bits, and for A=11, the input is shifted left by three bits, resulting in three zeros filled in from the right.
