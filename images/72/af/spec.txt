Please act as a professional verilog designer.

Design a Verilog module for a 10-bit Johnson counter with a synchronous reset. The counter operates on the rising edge of the clock and resets to zero when the reset signal is active.

Module name:  
    johnson_counter_10bit  

Input ports:
    clk: Chip clock signal.
    reset: Synchronous reset signal.

Output ports:
    count: Counter output signal with a data width of 10 bits.

Implementation:
Counter operation:
On every positive edge of the clock signal (clk), the Johnson counter shifts its bits to the right and the inverted last bit is fed back to the first bit if the reset signal (reset) is not active. If the reset signal is active, the counter resets to zero.
