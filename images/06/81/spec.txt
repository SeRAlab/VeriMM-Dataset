
Design a simple SRT (Sweeney, Robertson, and Tocher) divider in Verilog. The SRT divider should take a 4-bit dividend and a 4-bit divisor as inputs and produce a 4-bit quotient and a 4-bit remainder as outputs. The division operation should use a simple approximation method and handle basic edge cases such as division by zero. The output should be valid when the division operation completes.
