Design a Verilog module that performs Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (QAM) for 16-QAM. The module should take in a 4-bit input representing two bits each for in-phase (I) and quadrature (Q) signals, and output a corresponding 4-bit coded amplitude for transmission. The QAM mapping is as follows:
- 00 -> 00
- 01 -> 01
- 10 -> 10
- 11 -> 11
- 00 -> 00
- 01 -> 01
- 10 -> 10
- 11 -> 11
You should implement the logic so that I and Q can be modulated based on the input.
