Design a simple vending machine controller that responds to coin insertions and product selections via three buttons. The circuit outputs a signal for each selected product (product1, product2, product3) only when a valid selection is made without a coin present, indicating that a selection was attempted without payment. Additionally, it produces a change output when a coin is inserted but no valid product selection is registered, ensuring that users can receive their change when they do not choose a product after inserting a coin.
module top_module(
    input coin,
    input button1,
    input button2,
    input button3,
    output product1,
    output product2,
    output product3,
    output change
);
