This module functions as an audio mixer that combines tone and noise signals based on given amplitude settings for left and right channels. It takes inputs to enable tone and noise, as well as tone and noise signal values, which are used along with specified amplitude levels to compute the output levels for both left and right channels. When the tone or noise is enabled and received as active, it adds the corresponding amplitude values to the outputs. The results are updated on each clock cycle, producing a 5-bit output that reflects the combined audio levels for both channels.
module top_module(input wire clk,input wire en_tone,input wire en_noise,input wire tone,input wire noise,input wire [3:0] amplitude_l,input wire [3:0] amplitude_r,output reg [4:0] out_l,output reg [4:0] out_r);
