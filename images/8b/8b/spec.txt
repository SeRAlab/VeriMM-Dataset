Design a 4-bit wide 2-to-1 multiplexer. The module takes two 4-bit inputs (IN0 and IN1) and a single control signal (CTRL). Depending on the value of CTRL, it outputs either IN0 or IN1 on the 4-bit output (OUT1). When CTRL is 0, OUT1 reflects the value of IN0, and when CTRL is 1, OUT1 reflects the value of IN1, effectively selecting between the two 4-bit inputs based on the control signal.
