Design a 16-bit signed magnitude divider that computes both the quotient and remainder of two signed 16-bit integers (dividend and divisor). If the divisor is zero, the module indicates a divide-by-zero condition and outputs zero for both the quotient and remainder. The implementation handles the sign of the inputs appropriately, ensuring that the output quotient and remainder reflect the correct signed values based on the sign of the dividend and divisor. The results are provided through dedicated output ports for the quotient, remainder, and the divide-by-zero status.
module top_module(
    input signed [15:0] dividend,
    input signed [15:0] divisor,
    output signed [15:0] quotient,
    output signed [15:0] remainder,
    output divide_by_zero
);
