Design a circuit that performs a conditional output based on a single-bit input. If in1 is high (1), the output (out) will be the bitwise AND of in2 (2-bit) and in3 (4-bit), resulting in a 4-bit output that is expanded to 16 bits. If in1 is low (0), the output will be the bitwise OR of in2 (2-bit) and in4 (9-bit), which also results in a 9-bit output expanded to 16 bits.
module top_module(
  input wire in1,
  input wire [1:0] in2,
  input wire [3:0] in3,
  input wire [8:0] in4,
  output wire [15:0] out
);
