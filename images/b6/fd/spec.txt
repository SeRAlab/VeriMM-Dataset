Implement a 15-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) that generates pseudo-random sequences. The LFSR is clocked by the `clk` input and can be reset asynchronously with the `rst` input. When enabled via the `ena` signal, it shifts its state to the right, and the new bit introduced at the least significant position is generated by the XOR of the bits at positions 14 and 13. On reset, the state is initialized to 1.
