Implement a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a processor based on the workload. The controller has inputs for the workload, a clock signal, and an enable signal. The output is the selected voltage and frequency levels. The frequency can be set to 1x, 2x, or 4x based on the workload level, which ranges from 0 to 3 (where 0 is the lowest workload and 3 is the highest). The voltage levels are predefined as 0.8V for 1x, 1.0V for 2x, and 1.2V for 4x. When the enable signal is low, the controller holds the previous state.
