Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit operation code (opcode):  
- 0000: Addition  
- 0001: Subtraction  
- 0010: Bitwise AND  
- 0011: Bitwise OR  
- 0100: Bitwise XOR  
- 0101: Logical Shift Left  
- 0110: Logical Shift Right  
- 0111: Set on Less Than (SLT)  
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B), and output a 32-bit result based on the selected operation. Additionally, provide a zero flag output that indicates if the result is zero.
