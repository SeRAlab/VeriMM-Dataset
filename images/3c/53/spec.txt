
Create a Verilog module that computes the dot product of two 5-element vectors of 8-bit unsigned integers. The input vectors A and B will be provided as 5 separate 8-bit inputs. The output will be a 16-bit unsigned integer representing the dot product of the two vectors. The module should be combinational, and the dot product is calculated as follows:
Dot Product = A[0]*B[0] + A[1]*B[1] + A[2]*B[2] + A[3]*B[3] + A[4]*B[4]
