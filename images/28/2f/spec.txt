
Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simple graph coloring algorithm. Given a graph with 5 nodes (0-4) and their adjacency matrix, the circuit should output a valid coloring of the graph using three colors (0, 1, 2) such that no two adjacent nodes have the same color. The adjacency matrix will be provided as input, represented as a 5x5 matrix in a flattened form (25 bits).
