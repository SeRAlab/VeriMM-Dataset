
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple portfolio optimization logic. The module takes in two 5-bit binary inputs representing the weights of two different assets in a portfolio. The output should represent the optimized weight distribution of the two assets, based on a simple rule: if the sum of the weights exceeds a certain threshold (in this case, 16), scale down the weights proportionally so that their sum equals 16. If the sum is less than or equal to 16, the output should be the same as the input.
