Design a simple DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a system based on the current load. The controller has a 2-bit input representing the load (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high, 11 for very high) and produces a 2-bit output representing the frequency setting (00 for low frequency, 01 for medium frequency, 10 for high frequency, 11 for maximum frequency). The output frequency should increase with the load. The controller should have a reset input that sets the output frequency to low frequency.
