Design a JK latch using Verilog. The JK latch has two inputs J and K, and two outputs Q and Q' (the complement of Q). The latch should update its output on the rising edge of the clock signal. If J and K are both high, the latch should toggle its state. If J is high and K is low, Q should be set to high. If J is low and K is high, Q should be set to low. If both J and K are low, the state should remain unchanged.
