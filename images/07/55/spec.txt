Design a Voltage-Controlled Oscillator (VCO) that outputs a frequency signal based on a 3-bit control input. The output frequency should be a multiple of a base frequency (e.g., 1 MHz). The control input will determine how many times the base frequency is multiplied. For example, a control input of 000 corresponds to 1 MHz, 001 to 2 MHz, 010 to 4 MHz, 011 to 8 MHz, and 100 to 16 MHz.
