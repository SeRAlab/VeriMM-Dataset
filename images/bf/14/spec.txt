Design a serial input cell for a boundary scan architecture. This module captures input data from either an external pin or a previous boundary scan cell based on the CaptureDR signal and stores it in a latch on the rising edge of the TCK signal during either capture or shift operations. On the falling edge of TCK, the latched value is then output to the next boundary scan cell, facilitating the testing and verification of integrated circuits in a chain.
