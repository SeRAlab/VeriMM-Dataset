
Design a 16-bit serializer that takes a 16-bit parallel input and serializes it into a 1-bit serial output. The input data is loaded into the serializer on a rising clock edge, and the serialization occurs at each clock cycle, shifting out one bit at a time. The serializer should also include a control signal to indicate when the serialization is active. The serializer should output the serialized data on the `serial_out` output line and have a `done` signal that indicates when the serialization is complete.
