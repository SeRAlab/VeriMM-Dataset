Design a clock-gated D flip-flop with an enable signal. The module captures the value of the input 'd' at the rising edge of the clock 'clk' when the enable 'en' is asserted (high). If 'en' is de-asserted (low), the output 'q' retains its previous state at the falling edge of the clock, effectively preventing any state change during idle periods. This design is useful for reducing power consumption by disabling the flip-flop when it is not needed.
