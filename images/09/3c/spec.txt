
Design a Huffman coding combinational circuit that takes a set of 8-bit binary values representing characters and outputs their corresponding Huffman encoded binary values. Assume the following character frequencies for the encoding:
- 'A': 5
- 'B': 9
- 'C': 12
- 'D': 13
- 'E': 16
- 'F': 45

The circuit should output the encoded values based on the above frequencies.
