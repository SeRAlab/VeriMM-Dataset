The module produces an enable signal, a step direction output, and a 4-bit step pulse to control the motor's movement. It utilizes PWM to modulate power applied to the motor: when the duty cycle is non-zero, it generates pulses proportional to the duty cycle value. Additionally, the module implements an H-bridge driver to dictate motor direction and ensures synchronized stepping of the motor through a state machine for managing the step pulse sequence.
