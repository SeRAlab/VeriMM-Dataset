Design a power gating circuit with two inputs (A, B) and one output (Y). The output Y should be high (1) only when both A and B are high (1). When either A or B is low (0), Y should be low (0). Additionally, implement a power gating control signal P, where the output Y should only be active (reflect the values of A and B) when P is high (1). If P is low (0), the output Y should be low (0) regardless of A and B. This behavior represents a simple power gating mechanism.
