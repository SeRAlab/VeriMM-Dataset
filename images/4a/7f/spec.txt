The output of the multiplexer is stored in the 4-bit register mux_out. Additionally, when sel is at its maximum value (111), the module combines mux_out with the Johnson counter's current state to create a 12-bit output (final_out); otherwise, it outputs the Johnson counter prefixed by four zero bits. This design can be utilized for controlled data selection in digital systems, leveraging the properties of both the counter and multiplexer for versatile output generation.
