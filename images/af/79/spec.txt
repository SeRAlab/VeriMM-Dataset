If the `enable` signal is deasserted, the counter does not increment. Additionally, when the `rst` signal is low, the counter resets to zero, and the pulse output is set to low. The pulse output is asserted high for one clock cycle when the counter reaches 50,000 counts, corresponding to a duration of one millisecond assuming a 50 MHz clock frequency.
