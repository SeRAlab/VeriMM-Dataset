
Design a positive edge-triggered SR latch using D flip-flops. The SR latch should have two inputs, S (set) and R (reset), and one output Q. The behavior should be as follows: 
- When S=1 and R=0, Q should be set to 1.
- When S=0 and R=1, Q should be reset to 0.
- When S=0 and R=0, Q should hold its previous state.
- The output should only change on the positive edge of the clock.
