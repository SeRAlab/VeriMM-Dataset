Design a 32-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (opcode):
1. Addition (opcode 000)
2. Subtraction (opcode 001)
3. Bitwise AND (opcode 010)
4. Bitwise OR (opcode 011)
5. Bitwise XOR (opcode 100)

The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and produce a 32-bit output (Y) based on the operation specified by the opcode. The ALU should also provide a zero flag (Z) that indicates whether the output is zero.
