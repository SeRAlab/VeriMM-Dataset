Design a simple circuit that controls two LEDs based on the status of two input signals: RTS and DTR. LED0 will reflect the state of the DTR input, while LED7 will reflect the state of the RTS input. When DTR is high, LED0 lights up; when RTS is high, LED7 lights up.
module top_module(input wire RTS,
            input wire DTR,
            output wire LED0,
            output wire LED7);
