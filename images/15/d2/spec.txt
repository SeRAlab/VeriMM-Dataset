
Design a 4-bit Cyclic Redundancy Check (CRC) generator. The CRC should take a 4-bit input data and a 4-bit polynomial (generator) and produce a 4-bit CRC output. The CRC should be computed using a shift register approach, where the register is shifted left with each bit of input data, and the polynomial is XORed with the register when the most significant bit (MSB) of the register is 1. Your task is to implement this in Verilog and verify its correctness using a testbench.
