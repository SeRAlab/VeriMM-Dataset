Design a 4-bit up/down counter with specific control functionalities. The counter decrements its value on each clock cycle when enabled, and under the condition of Load being asserted or when the counter reaches zero, it loads a preset value of 10 (binary 1010). Additionally, it can be reset to zero through the Clear input. The output Q represents the current count value.
module top_module(
    input Clock,Enable,Clear,Load,
    output [3:0] Q
    );
