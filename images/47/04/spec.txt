Design a binary-coded decimal (BCD) counter that counts from 000 to 999, outputting the values across three four-bit registers (dig1, dig2, dig3). The counter increments the least significant digit (dig1) on each clock pulse while the enable signal (en) is active. When dig1 overflows (reaches 10), it resets to 0 and increments dig2. If dig2 overflows, it resets to 0 and increments dig3. The counter can be asynchronously reset to 0 by the active low reset signal (ar). The design effectively manages the carries between the three digits to ensure valid BCD counting.
