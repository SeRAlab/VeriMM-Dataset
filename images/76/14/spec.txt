Design a simple 4-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs four specific operations based on a 2-bit control signal `c`. When `c` is 00, the ALU outputs the sum of two 4-bit inputs, `in0` and `in1`. When `c` is 01, it outputs the difference of `in0` minus `in1`. For `c` equal to 10, it performs a bitwise AND operation, and for `c` equal to 11, it performs a bitwise XOR operation.
module top_module(
        input  wire [1:0] c,
        input  wire [3:0] in0,
        input  wire [3:0] in1,
        output reg  [3:0] alu_out
    );
