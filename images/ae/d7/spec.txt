Design a 4-bit adder/subtractor module that supports both addition and subtraction of two 4-bit input values, A and B. When the control signal 'sub' is high, the module computes the subtraction of B from A by first taking the two's complement of B; otherwise, it simply adds A and B. The result of the operation is provided as a 4-bit output S, while Cout indicates a carry out for addition or a borrow for subtraction, allowing for detection of overflow or underflow conditions.
