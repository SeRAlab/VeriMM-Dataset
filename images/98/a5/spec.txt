Design a 32-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) capable of performing addition or subtraction based on the ALUFun control signal. The module takes two 32-bit inputs (in1 and in2) and computes the sum or the difference by leveraging two separate operations: addition of in1 and in2 for the addition operation, and addition of in1 with the two’s complement of in2 for subtraction. It produces a 32-bit output S, as well as flags Z (zero flag), N (negative flag), and V (overflow flag) to indicate the operation's results. The flags provide feedback on whether the result is zero, negative, or indicates an overflow, respectively, and their values depend on whether the addition or subtraction operation is performed, guided by the ALUFun and Sign signals.
