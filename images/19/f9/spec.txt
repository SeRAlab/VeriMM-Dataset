Design a simple voltage and frequency controller for a system with dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS). The controller should adjust the output voltage (V_out) and frequency (F_out) based on the input request (Req, a 2-bit signal), which can be 00 (low), 01 (medium), 10 (high), or 11 (maximum). The controller should also have a clock signal (clk) and a reset signal (reset) to initialize the output to the lowest state. The frequency and voltage values can be represented as 4-bit binary values. Output the current voltage and frequency settings of the system.
