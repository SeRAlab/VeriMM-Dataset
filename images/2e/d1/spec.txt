Design a 24-bit DAC controller that takes a 24-bit input word, divides it into three 8-bit segments, and produces a 3-bit control output that indicates which segment has the highest value. The output should be encoded as follows: 000 for the first 8 bits, 001 for the second 8 bits, and 010 for the third 8 bits. If all segments are equal or if they are all zero, output 111.
