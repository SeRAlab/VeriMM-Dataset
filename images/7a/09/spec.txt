Design a clock gating circuit that generates an enabled clock output (ENCLK) based on input signals. When the enable signal (EN) is high, ENCLK follows the clock input (CLK) on the rising edge; otherwise, ENCLK is set to LOW. This design effectively reduces power consumption by turning off the clock signal when not needed, facilitating better control over clock distribution in digital systems.
