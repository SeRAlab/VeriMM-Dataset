The counting resets when a 0 is encountered, and the output will reflect a maximum count of 3, corresponding to the sequences of 1s detected. The circuit incorporates a state to track the previous input condition to determine if the counting should continue or reset.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] in,
    output [2:0] out
);
