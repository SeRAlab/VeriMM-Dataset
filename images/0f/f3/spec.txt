
Design a FIFO (First-In-First-Out) queue with the following specifications:
- The FIFO should have a data width of 8 bits.
- It should support two operations: enqueue (to add data) and dequeue (to remove data).
- The FIFO should have a capacity of 4 entries.
- It should have a full signal that indicates when the FIFO is full and an empty signal that indicates when the FIFO is empty.
- The signals should be synchronous to a clock signal.
