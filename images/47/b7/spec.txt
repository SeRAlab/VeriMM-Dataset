Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that takes a 4-bit input A and a 2-bit control signal B to perform left and right shift operations. The output Y will mirror the shifted value of A based on the value of B: when B=00, output A unchanged; when B=01, perform a left shift by one position; when B=10, perform a right shift by one position; and when B=11, perform a right shift by two positions, filling the vacated bits with zero.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [1:0] B,
    output reg [3:0] Y
);
