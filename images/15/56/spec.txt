Design a low dropout regulator (LDO) in Verilog that takes a reference voltage (Vref), an input voltage (Vin), and a load current (Iload) to produce an output voltage (Vout) while ensuring Vout is always less than Vin by a specified dropout voltage (Vdropout). The LDO should also include a simple enable signal (enable) to turn the regulator on or off.
