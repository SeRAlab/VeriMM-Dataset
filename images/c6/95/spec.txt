

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that performs a bit-wise AND operation between two 8-bit inputs and outputs the result. The module should have two input ports, `a` and `b`, and one output port, `out`. The operation should be performed on the rising edge of the clock signal, `clk`. If the inputs are not 8 bits wide, the module should truncate or zero-extend the inputs as necessary. Assume that the inputs are unsigned.

Implement the module `and8` that performs the above-mentioned operation.
