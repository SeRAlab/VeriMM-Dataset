Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (opcode):  
000 - Addition  
001 - Subtraction  
010 - AND  
011 - OR  
100 - XOR  
101 - NOR  
110 - NAND  
111 - Comparison (set output to 1 if A < B, else 0)  
The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs A and B, and produce an 8-bit output R along with a single-bit output Z indicating zero. The output R should be the result of the operation indicated by the opcode.
