Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simple Huffman encoding mechanism. The circuit should take a 4-bit input representing a symbol and produce a variable-length output encoding based on predefined Huffman codes for the symbols. The mapping for the symbols is as follows:
- Symbol 0: '00'
- Symbol 1: '01'
- Symbol 2: '10'
- Symbol 3: '110'
- Symbol 4: '1110'
- Symbol 5: '1111'
