Design a dual-function D flip-flop with reset and scan capabilities. The module captures the input data (din) on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when the active-low reset (rst_l) is not asserted, allowing it to either store new data or hold a low state when reset. It supports a scan functionality controlled by the scan enable input (se) to facilitate testing, allowing the shift-in data (si) to be loaded into the flip-flop. The output (q) reflects the stored value, which can be accessed as shift-out data (so), making it versatile for digital circuit design and testing.
