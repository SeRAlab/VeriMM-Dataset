Design a 16-bit shift register with load and shift functionality. On the rising edge of the clock, if the load signal is asserted, the register captures the input data; otherwise, if the shift enable signal is active, it performs a left shift operation, moving the current data left by one bit and inserting a zero at the least significant bit. The current content of the register is made available at the output.
