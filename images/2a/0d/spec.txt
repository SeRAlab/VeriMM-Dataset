
Design a 64-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
000: Add
001: Subtract
010: AND
011: OR
100: XOR
101: Shift Left
110: Shift Right
111: Set on Less Than (SLT)

The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs (A and B) and output a 64-bit result along with a zero flag that indicates if the result is zero.
