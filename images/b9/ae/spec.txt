Upon enabling with CRCEn, the circuit processes the input data on every clock cycle, updating the CRCResult based on the data bits and producing a ready signal to indicate when the CRC calculation is complete. The CRC is initialized to 0xFFFF on reset conditions and utilizes standard CRC polynomial logic for updating, ensuring reliable error detection capabilities in data communication systems.
