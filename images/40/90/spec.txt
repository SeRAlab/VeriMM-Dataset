Design a 24-bit DAC (Digital-to-Analog Converter) that takes a 24-bit binary input and outputs a corresponding 8-bit analog signal represented by the upper 8 bits of the 24-bit input. The output should be zero if the input is less than 8'b00000000 and should be saturated to 8'b11111111 if the input exceeds 24'b111111111111111111111111.
