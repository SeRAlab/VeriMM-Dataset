Design a basic Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs various arithmetic and logic operations based on a 4-bit control signal (ALUctl). It accepts two signed 32-bit inputs, A and B, and produces a 32-bit output, ALUOut, based on the selected operation. The operations include bitwise AND, OR, addition, multiplication, subtraction, a less-than comparison, and a bitwise NOR, with a default case that outputs zero. Additionally, the module includes a Zero output that indicates whether the ALUOut result is zero.
