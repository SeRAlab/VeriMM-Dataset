Design a synchronous four-bit adder circuit that computes the sum of two four-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, along with a carry-in bit, `cin`. The module has a reset functionality to initialize the outputs to zero on the `rst` signal. On each positive clock edge, if the reset is not activated, the adder updates the output `sum` with the result of the addition and calculates the carry-out signal, `cout`, based on the most significant bits of the inputs and the carry-in. The outputs `sum` and `cout` are reflected through corresponding registers to maintain state across clock cycles.
module top_module(clk, rst, a, b, cin, sum, cout);
