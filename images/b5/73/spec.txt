Design a 4-bit synchronous counter with a reset input. The counter increments on the rising edge of the clock signal (aclk), and is reset to zero when the areset signal is high. When the counter reaches its maximum value of 15 (4'b1111), it rolls over to zero and sets an overflow signal to indicate that a rollover has occurred. The overflow signal is cleared when the counter increments correctly.
module top_module(
  input aclk,
  input areset,
  output reg [3:0] count,
  output reg overflow
);
