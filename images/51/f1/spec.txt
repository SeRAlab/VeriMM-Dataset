Design a clock gating circuit that controls the propagation of a clock signal based on an enable signal (`en`) and a toggle enable signal (`te`). When `en` is active, the output `enclk` will follow the clock signal (`clk`), but only if `te` is also active; otherwise, `enclk` will be low. If `en` is inactive, `enclk` will always output low, effectively disabling the clock. This design helps in reducing dynamic power consumption by gating the clock to portions of a circuit when not in use.
