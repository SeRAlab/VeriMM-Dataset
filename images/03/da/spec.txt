Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog. The arbiter has 4 request inputs (req[3:0]) and one grant output (grant[3:0]). The request lines are prioritized as follows: req[0] has the highest priority, followed by req[1], req[2], and req[3]. If multiple requests are received simultaneously, the arbiter will grant access to the highest priority request. The arbiter should also have a reset signal (reset) that initializes the grant outputs to 0.
