
Design a 4-bit DRAM cell that can store a 4-bit value. The cell should have the following inputs: 
- `wr` (write enable), 
- `addr` (3-bit address), 
- `data_in` (4-bit data to write), 
- `clk` (clock signal).
The outputs should be:
- `data_out` (4-bit data read from the cell).
The cell should allow reading and writing operations based on the `wr` signal.
