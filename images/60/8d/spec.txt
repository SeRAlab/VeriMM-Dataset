Design a digital circuit that implements a four-input logic gate. The output X is high when either both A1 and A2 are high or both B1 and B2 are high, but it will be low when all four inputs A1, A2, B1, and B2 are high simultaneously. This design effectively prevents a logic high output when all inputs are activated, functioning similarly to a modified AND-OR configuration with additional gating.
module top_module(
    X,
    A1,
    A2,
    B1,
    B2
);
