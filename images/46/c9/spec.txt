Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (I0, I1, I2, I3) based on a 2-bit selection input (S0, S1). The output (O) will reflect the selected input, where S0 and S1 determine which input is routed to the output: if S0S1 is 00, O equals I0; 01 selects I1; 10 selects I2; and 11 selects I3. The selection and output are updated whenever the selection or any of the inputs changes.
