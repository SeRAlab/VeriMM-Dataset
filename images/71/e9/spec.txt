Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that accepts a 4-bit input (DIN) and a 2-bit shift control signal (SHIFT) to perform left shifts. The output (DOUT) will reflect the result of the shift operation based on the value of SHIFT, where a SHIFT of 00 produces no shift, a SHIFT of 01 performs a single left shift, a SHIFT of 10 performs a double left shift, and a SHIFT of 11 produces a left shift with the upper two bits replaced by the lower two bits of the original input. The design is clocked to ensure synchronous operation across the shifting stages.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] DIN,
    input [1:0] SHIFT,
    input clk,           // Added clock input
    output reg [3:0] DOUT
);
