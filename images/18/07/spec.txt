Design an 8-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) that generates pseudo-random binary sequences. The LFSR should use taps at positions 8, 6, 5, and 4 (1-indexed) for its feedback. The LFSR should be initialized with a non-zero seed value. The output should be a 1-bit output representing the least significant bit (LSB) of the current shift register value. The LFSR should shift on each clock cycle and update the feedback bit accordingly.
