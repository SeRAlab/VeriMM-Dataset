Design a 4-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer. The module selects between two 4-bit inputs, V and A, based on the control signal z. When z is 0, the output M takes the value of V; when z is 1, M takes the value of A. Each bit of the output is determined by the corresponding bits of the inputs and the state of z.
