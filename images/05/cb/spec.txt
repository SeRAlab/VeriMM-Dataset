
Design a Run-Length Encoding (RLE) circuit in Verilog. The circuit should take a stream of binary data (1-bit input) and encode it into a run-length format, outputting the count of consecutive bits followed by the bit itself. The output should reset when a different bit is encountered. The circuit should handle a maximum of 8 consecutive bits and output the encoded data as a 9-bit value (8 bits for count and 1 bit for the data).
