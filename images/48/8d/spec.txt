Design a 16-bit circular shift module that performs variable right circular shifts based on a 4-bit shift amount input. The circuit shifts the input data to the right while wrapping the shifted bits around back to the left side for the specified number of positions. Each bit in the shift_amount controls a corresponding shift operation: a 1 at position 0 shifts the input by 1 bit, a 1 at position 1 shifts by 2 bits, a 1 at position 2 shifts by 4 bits, and a 1 at position 3 shifts by 8 bits. The final output reflects the result of these combined shifts.
module top_module(
   out,
   in,
   shift_amount
);
