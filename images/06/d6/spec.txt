Design an 8-bit ALU that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit control input:  
000: Addition  
001: Subtraction  
010: AND  
011: OR  
100: XOR  
101: Comparison (set to 1 if A < B, otherwise 0)  
110: Shift Left A by 1  
111: Shift Right A by 1  
Inputs: two 8-bit operands A and B, and a 3-bit control signal. The output should be an 8-bit result.
