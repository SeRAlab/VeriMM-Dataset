Design a 4-bit down counter with synchronous load, enable, and clear functionalities. On the rising edge of the clock, if the Clear signal is high, the counter resets to 15 (binary 1111). If the Enable signal is high, the counter decrements its value by 1. Additionally, when the Load signal is asserted, the counter also resets to 15. This design allows for controlled counting operations while providing the ability to clear, enable counting, and load a specific value.
module top_module(
    input Clock, Clear, Enable, Load,
    output reg [3:0] Q
);
