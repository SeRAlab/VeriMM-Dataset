Design a 4-bit up counter that increments its count on each positive clock edge. The counter resets to zero when the reset signal is low and indicates when it has reached its maximum value of 15 (4'hF) by asserting a 'max' output. When the counter resets, both the count and the max output are cleared to zero.
module top_module(input clk, input reset,
   output reg [3:0] count, output reg max);
