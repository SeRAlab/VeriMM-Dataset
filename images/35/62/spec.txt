Design a Voltage-Controlled Oscillator (VCO) in Verilog that uses a control voltage input to generate a square wave output. The frequency of the output square wave should be proportional to the control voltage input. The VCO should have a frequency range of 1 MHz to 10 MHz based on a control voltage range of 0V to 5V. The circuit should include a clock input for timing and an output for the generated square wave.
