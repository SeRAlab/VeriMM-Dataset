Design a 3-bit comparator that evaluates three 3-bit inputs (A, B, and C) and produces a 2-bit output (Y) indicating the comparison results. The outputs represent four possible states: Y = 00 when A, B, and C are equal; Y = 01 when A is less than both B and C; Y = 10 when A is greater than both B and C; and Y = 11 when A is neither less than nor greater than both B and C. The comparator utilizes a combination of gates for comparison logic, ensuring that these conditions are checked effectively based on the values of A, B, and C.
module top_module(
    input [2:0] A,
    input [2:0] B,
    input [2:0] C,
    output reg [1:0] Y
);
