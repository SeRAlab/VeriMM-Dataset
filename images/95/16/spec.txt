Design a circuit that computes three distinct logical operations (AND, OR, XOR) on a 100-bit wide input. The module outputs the results as separate signals: out_and for the AND operation, out_or for the OR operation, and out_xor for the XOR operation. The selection mechanism, controlled by a multiplexer (though hardcoded in this instance), determines which operation is performed, although in its current form, only the AND operation output is effectively utilized due to the static mux_sel assignment.
module top_module( 
    input [99:0] in,
    output out_and,
    output out_or,
    output out_xor 
);
