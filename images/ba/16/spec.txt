The module implements a state machine that converts data from a PS/2 mouse into coordinates and button states suitable for a compatible K-mouse (keyboard mouse). It processes the input data in a structured sequence: first receiving button state information, followed by the delta movements in the X and Y directions. Upon receiving valid data, it transitions through four states—FIRST_FRAME, SECOND_FRAME, THIRD_FRAME, and CALCULATE_NEWXY—updating the mouse's X and Y positions based on the delta values and keeping track of button states. The design ensures that the output coordinates and button statuses are updated accurately based on the mouse's movement and button interactions, functioning effectively as a mouse data parser and state handler.
