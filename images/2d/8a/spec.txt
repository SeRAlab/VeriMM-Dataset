
Design a temperature sensor interface module in Verilog that converts a 10-bit binary temperature reading into a 5-bit binary output representing temperature categories. The output should classify the temperature into the following categories:
- 0: Below 0 °C
- 1: 0 °C to 10 °C
- 2: 11 °C to 20 °C
- 3: 21 °C to 30 °C
- 4: Above 30 °C

The input is a 10-bit binary number representing temperature in Celsius.
