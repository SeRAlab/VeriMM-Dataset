Design a control cell for a boundary scan architecture that manages data transfer between cells. It captures data on a rising edge of the TCK clock signal based on the CaptureDR or ShiftDR control inputs and latches it for sending to the next boundary scan cell on the falling edge of TCK. It also updates this latched data based on the UpdateDR signal and selects between providing output either from the latching process or directly from the OutputControl line depending on the extest signal. This control cell is designed to support operations typical in test access mechanisms, facilitating efficient data shifting during testing sequences.
