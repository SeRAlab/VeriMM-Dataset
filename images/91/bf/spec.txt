Design a 3-bit Johnson counter with asynchronous reset functionality. On each positive clock edge, if the reset signal is high, the counter initializes to zero. Otherwise, the counter shifts its bits to the left, with the most significant bit being fed back as the least significant bit. The current state of the counter is output as a 3-bit value.

  input clk,
