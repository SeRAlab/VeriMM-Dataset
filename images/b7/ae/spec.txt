This module implements a hazard detection and forwarding unit for a pipelined processor architecture. Its primary functionality is to analyze the registers involved in read and write operations to identify potential data hazards during execution. It generates forwarding signals to avoid stalls by directing data from the write-back stage to the execution stage as needed. Additionally, it determines when the pipeline should stall or flush to resolve read-after-write hazards. Specifically, the outputs include signals for stalling the fetch (stall_f) and decode (stall_d) stages, flushing the decode (flush_d) and execute (flush_e) stages, and forwarding data for the first (forward1_e) and second (forward2_e) operands during execution to ensure correct data flow and maintain pipeline efficiency.
