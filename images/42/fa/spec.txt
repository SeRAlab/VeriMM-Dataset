Design a module for calculating the remainder of a division operation, commonly known as the modulo operation. The module takes a 32-bit dividend and a 32-bit divisor as inputs, and it outputs the 32-bit remainder. The design includes error handling to ensure the divisor is not zero and the dividend is non-negative, displaying appropriate error messages in those cases. When valid inputs are provided, it computes the quotient by dividing the dividend by the divisor, calculates the product of the quotient and divisor, and finally determines the remainder by subtracting the product from the dividend.
module top_module(
  input [31:0] div, // the dividend
  input [31:0] divisor, // the divisor
  output reg [31:0] rem // the remainder
);
