Design a position counter for a paddle in a graphical interface, driven by two quadrature encoder signals (quadA and quadB). The module utilizes input signals to determine the direction and enable counting based on changes in the encoder state. It maintains a count that reflects the paddle's vertical position, restricted within defined limits based on the screen height and paddle size. The count is adjustable depending on an acceleration counter, which allows for variable movement speed, with additional constraints applied to prevent the paddle from moving off-screen. When reset, the count initializes to a mid-screen value.
