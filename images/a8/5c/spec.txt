Design a sequential circuit that performs two stages of addition with synchronous reset functionality. The circuit takes two 4-bit inputs and provides a final 4-bit output, which is the sum of the outputs from two internal 4-bit adders. The first adder computes the sum of the inputs, while the second adder adds the output of the first adder to the second input. The operation is clocked, and both adders reset to zero upon receiving a reset signal.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input reset,
    input [3:0] input_1,
    input [3:0] input_2,
    output [3:0] final_output
);
