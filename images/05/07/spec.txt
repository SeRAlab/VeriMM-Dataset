Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor. The predictor uses a 2-bit saturating counter for each branch. The state transitions are as follows:  
- 00 (strongly not taken)  
- 01 (weakly not taken)  
- 10 (weakly taken)  
- 11 (strongly taken)  
The state is updated based on the actual outcome of the branch prediction (1 for taken, 0 for not taken). The initial state is 00. The circuit has two inputs: `branch_outcome` (1-bit) and `clk` (1-bit). The output is the current state of the predictor (2 bits).
