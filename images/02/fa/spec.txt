Create an SR flip-flop that takes S (set) and R (reset) inputs, and outputs Q and Q'. The flip-flop should follow the characteristic behavior where Q is set to 1 when S is high (1) and R is low (0), reset to 0 when R is high (1) and S is low (0), and maintain its state when both S and R are low (0). If both S and R are high (1), the output is considered invalid.
