Design a 2-bit adder with carry-in functionality. The module takes two 2-bit inputs, I0 and I1, along with a carry-in input, CIN, and produces a 2-bit output, O, representing the sum of I0 and I1, along with the carry-in. The operation effectively adds the carry-in to I0 before performing the addition with I1, ensuring that the resulting sum takes into account any overflow from the lower significant bit.
module top_module(
    input [1:0] I0,
    input [1:0] I1,
    output [1:0] O,
    input CIN
);
