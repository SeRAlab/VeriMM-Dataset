
Design a simple decision tree inference module in Verilog. The module should take three input signals (features) and output a binary decision based on a predefined decision tree. The decision tree is as follows:
- If feature1 is less than 5, output 0.
- If feature1 is greater than or equal to 5 and feature2 is less than 10, output 1.
- If feature1 is greater than or equal to 5 and feature2 is greater than or equal to 10 and feature3 is less than 15, output 1.
- If feature1 is greater than or equal to 5 and feature2 is greater than or equal to 10 and feature3 is greater than or equal to 15, output 0.
