Design a 3-bit shift register that captures serial input data on each rising edge of the clock. The register serially shifts its contents, such that the output is the third bit of the shifted data. Additionally, the module incorporates a multiplexer feature, where the serial output can be selected from different bits within the shift register based on a selection signal, which is hardcoded to choose the second-to-last bit in this case. The overall purpose is to enable both shifting of data and selection of specific bits from the register.
