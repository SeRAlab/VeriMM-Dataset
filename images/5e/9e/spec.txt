Design a circuit for signed multiplication of two 16-bit numbers, A and B. When the multiply signal (mult) is asserted, the circuit computes the product of A and B, storing the result in a 32-bit temporary register. The module then outputs the high 16 bits of the result on the 'high' output and the low 16 bits on the 'low' output.
module top_module(input mult, input [15:0] A, input [15:0] B, output [15:0] high, output [15:0] low);
