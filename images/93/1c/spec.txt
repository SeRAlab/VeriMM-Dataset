Implement a BCD to 7-segment decoder where a 4-bit binary-coded decimal (BCD) input, `x`, is translated into a control signal for a 7-segment display. The module outputs a 4-bit active-low signal for the segment enable (an) and a 7-bit signal (seg) indicating which segments of the display should be illuminated based on the BCD value. The signal assignments include timing delays to simulate propagation delays in hardware.
