Design a Bluetooth communication controller that manages the state of a Bluetooth device. The device can be in one of three states: IDLE, CONNECTING, and CONNECTED. The controller has input signals to initiate the connection and to disconnect. The state transitions are as follows: 
1. From IDLE to CONNECTING when the `start_connection` signal is high. 
2. From CONNECTING to CONNECTED when the `connection_done` signal is high. 
3. From CONNECTED to IDLE when the `disconnect` signal is high. 
4. The state remains the same if none of the signals are triggered.
