Design a simple 2-bit predictor that predicts whether the next branch will be taken or not. The predictor uses a 2-bit saturating counter for each branch instruction. The counter increments when the branch is taken and decrements when the branch is not taken. The states are:  
00 - Strongly not taken  
01 - Weakly not taken  
10 - Weakly taken  
11 - Strongly taken  
The input to the predictor is a signal indicating whether the branch was taken (1) or not taken (0). The output is the predicted state (0 or 1) for the branch.
