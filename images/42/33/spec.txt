During the first stage, it stores the inputs; in the second stage, it computes the unsigned product of A and B. In the final stage, based on the value of the select signal `sel`, it outputs either the unsigned product or its signed equivalent as a 16-bit result.

    input [7:0] A,
    input [7:0] B,
    input sel,
