Design a 4-bit Cyclic Redundancy Check (CRC) generator using a linear feedback shift register (LFSR). The LFSR will use a polynomial of x^4 + x + 1 (binary representation: 10011). The CRC generator should take a 4-bit input data and produce a 4-bit CRC output. The data will be shifted into the LFSR, and the feedback will be taken from the specified taps. The output should be the final contents of the LFSR after processing the input data.
