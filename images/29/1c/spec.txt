
Design a Pulse Width Modulator (PWM) that takes a 2-bit input signal and generates a PWM signal with a frequency of 1 kHz. The duty cycle of the PWM signal should be determined by the 2-bit input, where:
- 00 corresponds to 0% duty cycle,
- 01 corresponds to 25% duty cycle,
- 10 corresponds to 50% duty cycle,
- 11 corresponds to 75% duty cycle.
The PWM signal should be output on a pin.
