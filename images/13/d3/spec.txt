
Design a combinational logic circuit to implement a simplified version of the Black-Scholes option pricing model. The model calculates the theoretical price of European call and put options based on the following parameters:
- S: Current stock price (10 bits)
- K: Strike price (10 bits)
- T: Time to expiration in years (5 bits, scaled by 100 for precision)
- r: Risk-free interest rate (5 bits, scaled by 100 for precision)
- σ: Volatility of the stock (5 bits, scaled by 100 for precision)

Your task is to create a combinational circuit that outputs the call option price (C) and put option price (P). The output prices should be 16 bits each. Assume all calculations are done using fixed-point arithmetic.
