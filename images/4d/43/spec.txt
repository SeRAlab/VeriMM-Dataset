Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that can perform both left and right shifts based on a 2-bit control input. The module takes a 4-bit input, A, and a 2-bit shift control signal, S. When S[1] is high, it indicates a right shift, while when S[1] is low, a left shift occurs. The direction of the shift is further determined by the S[0] bit, which, when high, shifts the output by one position. The output, B, is the modified 4-bit data after performing the specified shift operation.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [1:0] S,
    output [3:0] B
);
