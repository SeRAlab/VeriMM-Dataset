Design a stopwatch circuit that counts milliseconds, seconds, and minutes. The stopwatch increments the milliseconds every clock cycle when enabled and resets upon receiving a reset signal. When the milliseconds reach 999, it rolls over to zero and increments the seconds. Similarly, once seconds reach 59, they roll over to zero, and the minutes increment until they reach 63, at which point they also reset to zero. The stopwatch can be zeroed out upon receiving a specific signal while allowing continued counting when enabled.
