
Design a 32-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) using Verilog. The LFSR should have a feedback polynomial defined by the taps: 32, 22, 17, 16, 13, 11, 10, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. The circuit will shift on the rising edge of the clock and should also have an asynchronous reset input that resets the LFSR to a predefined seed value.
