Design a D flip-flop with asynchronous set and reset functionality. The module captures the input signal D on the rising edge of the CLK signal. If the SET signal is high, it forces the output Q to high and QN to low regardless of CLK or D. Conversely, if the RESET signal is high, it forces Q to low and QN to high. Otherwise, during normal operation, Q follows the value of D while QN remains its logical complement.
