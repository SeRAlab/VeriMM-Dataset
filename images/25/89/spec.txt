Design a UART (Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter) controller that handles the receiving of serial data. The UART should have a baud rate of 9600, 8 data bits, no parity, and 1 stop bit. The input to the UART should be a serial data line, and the output should be an 8-bit parallel data output once a full byte is received. The UART should also provide a signal indicating that data is ready to be read.
