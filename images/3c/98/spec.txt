Design a 16-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that can perform addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, and bitwise OR operations based on a 2-bit control signal. The input to the ALU should be two 16-bit data inputs, and the output should be a 16-bit result. The control signals are defined as follows: 00 for addition, 01 for subtraction, 10 for bitwise AND, and 11 for bitwise OR.
