Design a 4-bit wide binary full adder. This module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, along with a carry-in input, Cin. It outputs a 4-bit sum and a carry-out output (Cout), which reflects any overflow from the addition of A, B, and Cin. The full adder effectively combines the bits of the inputs, taking into account the carry-in to produce the correct sum and carry-out.
module top_module(
input[3:0] A,B ,
input Cin ,
output [3:0] Sum ,
output Cout
);
