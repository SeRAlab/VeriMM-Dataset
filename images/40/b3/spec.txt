
Create a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: ADD
- 001: SUB
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
- 101: NAND
- 110: NOR
- 111: XNOR
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs and provide a 32-bit output along with a zero flag that indicates if the output is zero.
