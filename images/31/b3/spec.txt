
Create a Verilog module that implements a simple Kalman filter. The filter will take in a noisy measurement and an estimate of the state and produce an updated estimate of the state. It should have the following inputs:
- `measured_value`: The noisy measurement input.
- `estimated_state`: The previous estimate of the state.
- `process_noise`: The expected process noise.
- `measurement_noise`: The expected measurement noise.

The outputs will be:
- `updated_state`: The new estimate of the state after applying the Kalman filter algorithm.
