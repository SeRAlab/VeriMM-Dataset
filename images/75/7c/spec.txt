Design a 3-bit synchronous counter with an up/down control signal. When the control signal is high, the counter counts up; when the control signal is low, the counter counts down.

    UpDownCounter

Output ports:
    count: 3-bit output representing the counter value.

Implementation:
The counter is a simple 3-bit register that either increments or decrements its value on each positive edge of the clock signal, based on the value of the up_down signal. The counter resets to zero on the assertion of the reset signal.

    input up_down,
