Design a 4-bit wide test pattern generator (TPG) that produces a sequence of output patterns based on various control inputs and clock signals. The module initializes the TPG output to zero upon a reset signal and increments the output value under certain conditions driven by a combination of control signal states and clock edges. The outputs include control signals for standby and power on conditions, derived from the internal state of the TPG. The design incorporates combinational logic to monitor various inputs, ensuring that the four-bit output updates according to the specified logic, allowing it to generate specific test sequences for circuit verification or debugging purposes.
