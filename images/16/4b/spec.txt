Design a 3-to-8 decoder with enable input. The decoder should have 3 input bits (A2, A1, A0) and an enable signal (EN). When EN is high, the decoder should output a low signal (0) on one of the 8 outputs (Y0 to Y7) corresponding to the binary value of the input (A2, A1, A0). When EN is low, all outputs should be high (1).
