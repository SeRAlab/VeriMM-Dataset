
Design a 1-D Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) module that takes a 4-bit input signal and produces a 4-bit approximation and a 4-bit detail coefficients. The DWT is defined such that:
- The approximation coefficients are calculated as (a[0] + a[1] + a[2] + a[3]) / 2
- The detail coefficients are calculated as (a[0] - a[1] + a[2] - a[3]) / 2
The output should be stored in two separate 4-bit registers: `approx` and `detail`.
