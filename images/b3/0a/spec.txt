Design a 4-bit up/down counter. The counter increments its value by 1 when the UP_DOWN input is high, and decrements by 1 when UP_DOWN is low. Upon receiving a RESET signal, the counter resets its value to zero. The operation of the counter is synchronized with the positive edge of the CLK input signal.
module top_module(
    input CLK, UP_DOWN, RESET,
    output reg [3:0] Q
);
