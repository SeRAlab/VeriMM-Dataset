Implement a 4-bit binary adder that sums two 4-bit inputs, A and B, along with a carry input (Cin). The addition operation occurs on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk), resulting in a 4-bit sum output (S) and a carry output (Cout). The carry out is generated based on the most significant bits of A, B, and Cin, indicating if an overflow has occurred during the addition.
module top_module(input [3:0] A, B, input Cin, clk, output [3:0] S, output Cout);
