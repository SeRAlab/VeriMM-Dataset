	clk: Clock signal used for synchronous operation.

Output ports:
    q: 4-bit output representing the shift register value.

Implementation:
Shift Register Logic:
The always block triggers on the positive edge of the clock signal (posedge clk) or the negative edge of the reset signal (negedge rst_n).
On reset, indicated by !rst_n, the shift register value is set to 0.
If the load signal (load) is active, the parallel data (parallel_data) is loaded into the shift register.
Otherwise, the data is shifted to the left by 1 bit.
