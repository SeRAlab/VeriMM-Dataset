Design a Verilog module that performs the QR decomposition of a 2x2 matrix. The inputs will be the elements of the matrix, and the outputs will be the Q and R matrices. The Q matrix is an orthogonal matrix, and the R matrix is an upper triangular matrix. The input will be four values representing the matrix, and the output will be a 4-bit wide signal for each of the elements of the Q and R matrices.
