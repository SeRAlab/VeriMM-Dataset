Design a month counter that increments the month value (0 to 12) with the ability to wrap around, and indicates whether the transition occurs at the end of the year. The circuit features a clock signal for counting and a reset signal to initialize the count. When enabled to add, the counter moves to the next month; if it reaches month 12 (which is represented as 1 month in this design), it resets to 0 and sets a carry output (`co_year`) to indicate that a year transition has occurred.
