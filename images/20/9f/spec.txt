Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simplified Huffman encoding mechanism. The circuit should take a 4-bit binary input representing a character (from 0 to 15) and produce a variable-length binary output encoded according to a predefined Huffman coding scheme. The output can be up to 8 bits long, and the mapping for the characters to their encoded representations is as follows:

- 0 -> 000
- 1 -> 001
- 2 -> 010
- 3 -> 011
- 4 -> 100
- 5 -> 101
- 6 -> 110
- 7 -> 111
- 8 -> 00
- 9 -> 01
- 10 -> 10
- 11 -> 11
- 12 -> 00000
- 13 -> 00001
- 14 -> 00010
- 15 -> 00011

The output should be a binary representation of the Huffman encoded value, and if the input is invalid (greater than 15), the output should be 00000000.
