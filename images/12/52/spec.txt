Design a simple phase detector that takes two binary inputs, A and B, and outputs a single bit indicating whether the phase of A is leading, lagging, or in phase with B. The output should be high (1) when A is leading B, low (0) when A is lagging B, and high for in-phase as well (1). Use the following encoding: 00 for lagging, 01 for in-phase, and 10 for leading.
