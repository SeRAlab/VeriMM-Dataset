Design a Verilog module that detects pitch frequency by taking a 5-bit binary input representing a frequency value and outputs a 3-bit binary signal indicating the pitch class. The pitch classes are defined as follows: 
- 000 for C (0)
- 001 for C# (1)
- 010 for D (2)
- 011 for D# (3)
- 100 for E (4)
- 101 for F (5)
- 110 for F# (6)
- 111 for G (7)
If the input frequency is outside the range of 0-7, the output should be 'xxx' (undefined).
