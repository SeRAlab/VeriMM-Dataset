

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that can perform a binary multiplication operation. The module should take two 8-bit inputs `a` and `b` and produce a 16-bit output `result`. You are not allowed to use any built-in verilog operators for multiplication.

Design the module `binary_multiplier` that takes the inputs `a` and `b` and produces the output `result`. The module should have the following ports:
- `input [7:0] a`
- `input [7:0] b`
- `output [15:0] result`

You may use any other built-in operators or modules. However, the design should be optimized for area and not for speed.
