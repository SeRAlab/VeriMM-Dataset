The comparison is achieved by calculating the difference between A and B and analyzing the most significant bits (MSBs) to determine the relationship between the two inputs, while also handling overflow cases based on the signs of the operands and the result. The design employs a state machine to manage the process of loading inputs and performing the subtraction operation.
