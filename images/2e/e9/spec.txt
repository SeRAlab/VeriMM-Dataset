
Design a FIFO (First-In-First-Out) queue with a configurable size. The FIFO should support the following operations: enqueue (to add an item), dequeue (to remove an item), and an empty signal that indicates whether the queue is empty. The FIFO should also have a full signal to indicate when it is full. The queue should accept 8-bit data elements, and the size of the FIFO should be configurable to 4, 8, or 16 entries.
