Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the speed of a fan based on a 3-bit input representing temperature levels. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The fan speed should change on the rising edge of a clock signal. The temperature levels are defined as follows: 0-2 (low), 3-5 (medium), and 6-7 (high). When the temperature input is outside the defined range (e.g., higher than 7), the fan should stop.
