Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor that uses a 2-bit saturating counter for each of the two possible outcomes (taken and not taken). The predictor updates its state based on the previous outcome of the branch instruction and predicts the next outcome. The states are as follows:  
00: Strongly Not Taken  
01: Weakly Not Taken  
10: Weakly Taken  
11: Strongly Taken  
The input to the branch predictor is the current outcome (0 for not taken, 1 for taken), and the output is the predicted outcome (0 for not taken, 1 for taken).
