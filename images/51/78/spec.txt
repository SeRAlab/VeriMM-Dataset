Design a 4-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that performs various operations based on a 3-bit opcode. The ALU accepts two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and produces a 4-bit output, Final, along with a status flag to indicate overflow conditions. The operations include bitwise NOT, NOR, NAND, XOR, addition, and subtraction. Specifically, the operations executed are: a bitwise negation of A, the bitwise negation of A AND B (NAND), the bitwise negation of A OR B (NOR), a bitwise XOR between A and B, the addition of A and B while monitoring for overflow, the subtraction of B from A with overflow detection, incrementing B by one with overflow detection, and decrementing B by one with overflow detection.
