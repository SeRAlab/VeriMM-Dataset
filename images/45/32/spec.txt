Design a 4-bit Johnson counter that utilizes a shift register to produce a sequence based on the states of its bits. The counter shifts left on each positive clock edge, reintroducing the inverted value of the most significant bit back into the least significant bit position. The output Q generates a value based on the XOR of the least significant bit and the most significant bit of the shift register, effectively creating a unique sequence of outputs that is characteristic of a Johnson counter.
