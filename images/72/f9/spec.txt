Design a 4-bit shift register with enable functionality for loading data. On each positive edge of the clock signal, if the enable signal (en) is asserted, the register shifts its current contents to the right by one bit, with the least significant bit (bit 0) being updated by the input data (din). The current value of the register is always available at the output (addr).
