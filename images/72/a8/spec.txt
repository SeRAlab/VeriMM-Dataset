The most significant bit of the counter is used to control four output LEDs (LED1 to LED4), which will all reflect the state of this bit. When the counter's most significant bit is high, all LEDs are turned ON; when it is low, they are turned OFF, effectively making the LEDs blink with a frequency determined by the input clock.
