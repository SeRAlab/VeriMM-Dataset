Design a 32-bit shift module that performs either a left or right shift on the input data. The module takes a 32-bit input `data_in`, a 5-bit `shift_amount` specifying how many positions to shift, and a `shift_direction` control signal that determines the direction of the shift. When `shift_direction` is 1, the module performs a right shift; when it is 0, it performs a left shift. The result is outputted as a 32-bit `data_out`.
module top_module(data_in, shift_amount, shift_direction, data_out);
