

You are tasked to design a verilog module that takes in 3 inputs, `A`, `B`, and `C`, and outputs `Y`. The output `Y` should be the result of the following expression: `(A & B) | (~B & C)`. 

Design a module named `logic_gate` with the following ports:
- `input A`
- `input B`
- `input C`
- `output Y`

Use the `and`, `not`, and `or` gates to implement the logic expression.
