
Design an 8-bit shift register with the following specifications:
1. The shift register should take a serial data input (data_in) and shift it into the register on the rising edge of a clock (clk).
2. The shift register should have a synchronous reset (reset) which, when asserted, clears the register to all zeros.
3. The output of the shift register should be an 8-bit parallel output (data_out).
