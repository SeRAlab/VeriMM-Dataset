
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Kalman filter for sensor fusion. The module should take in two sensor measurements (sensor1 and sensor2), and an estimated position as input. It should output a refined estimated position based on the Kalman filter equations. Assume fixed parameters for the Kalman filter such as process noise covariance and measurement noise covariance. Handle both the prediction and update steps of the Kalman filter in a clocked process.
