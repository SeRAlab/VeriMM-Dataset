Design a 3-bit wide adder that computes the sum of two 3-bit inputs, A and B. The module outputs a 3-bit sum (S) and a carry-out (Cout) signal. The carry-out reflects any overflow from the addition, while the sum is derived from the lower three bits of a temporary 4-bit result calculated in a combinational logic block.
module top_module(
   input [2:0] A,
   input [2:0] B,
   output [2:0] S,
   output Cout
);
