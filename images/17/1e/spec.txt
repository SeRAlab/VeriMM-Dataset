
Design an 8-bit ALU that takes two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and performs the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal (ALUCtrl):
- 000: A + B (Addition)
- 001: A - B (Subtraction)
- 010: A & B (Bitwise AND)
- 011: A | B (Bitwise OR)
- 100: A ^ B (Bitwise XOR)
- 101: A << 1 (Logical left shift)
- 110: A >> 1 (Logical right shift)
- 111: A ~ (Bitwise NOT of A)

The ALU should output an 8-bit result and a zero flag that indicates if the result is zero.
