Design a power supply controller that alternates the outputs VPB and VNB between high and low states in a cyclical fashion. On each rising edge of the clock, the module transitions through four distinct states, toggling VPB and VNB accordingly: when VPB is high, VNB is low, and vice versa. This creates a square wave-type driving signal that can be used, for example, to control a load or generate specific voltage levels in a power distribution application.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    output reg VPB,
    output reg VNB
);
