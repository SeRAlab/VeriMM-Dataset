Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (in0, in1, in2, in3) based on a two-bit selection input (sel0 and sel1). The output will reflect the selected input, where sel1 determines which pair of inputs is considered (in0 or in1 and in2 or in3) and sel0 further selects between those pairs to produce a single output.
module top_module(
    out,
    in0,
    in1,
    in2,
    in3,
    sel0,
    sel1
);
