Design a 64-bit ALU that takes two 64-bit inputs and a 3-bit operation code (op_code). The ALU should perform the following operations based on the op_code:
000: Addition
001: Subtraction
010: AND
011: OR
100: XOR
101: Increment (Add 1 to the first input)
110: Decrement (Subtract 1 from the first input)
111: Pass-through (Output the first input)
The output of the ALU should be a 64-bit result.
