Design a simple DVFS controller that dynamically changes the voltage and frequency based on the input load signal. The controller should have a 2-bit load input that represents low, medium, and high loads. The output should be a 2-bit frequency setting and a 2-bit voltage setting. The mapping is as follows:
- Load 00 (Low): Frequency = 00, Voltage = 00
- Load 01 (Medium): Frequency = 01, Voltage = 01
- Load 10 (High): Frequency = 10, Voltage = 10
- Load 11 (Invalid): Frequency = 11, Voltage = 11
