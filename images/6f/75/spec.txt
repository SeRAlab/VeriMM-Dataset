Design a 2-bit binary counter with synchronous reset functionality. The counter increments its value on each positive edge of the clock (CLK), cycling through the states 00, 01, 10, and 11. Upon receiving a reset signal, the counter resets to the initial state of 00. The outputs Q1 and Q0 represent the most significant bit and least significant bit of the counter, respectively, reflecting the current count value.
module top_module(
    input CLK,
    input RESET,
    output reg Q1,
    output reg Q0
);
