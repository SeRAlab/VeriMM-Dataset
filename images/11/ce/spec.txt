
Design a Verilog module that performs LU decomposition on a 2x2 matrix. The input to the module will be a 2x2 matrix represented as four separate inputs (a11, a12, a21, a22). The output will be the two matrices L and U, where L is a lower triangular matrix and U is an upper triangular matrix. The output will be provided as separate outputs (l11, l21, l12, u11, u12, u22).
