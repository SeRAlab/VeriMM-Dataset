
Design a DVFS controller in Verilog that adjusts the voltage and frequency based on a 2-bit input representing the current workload. The controller should have the following states:
00: Low Voltage & Frequency
01: Medium Voltage & Frequency
10: High Voltage & Frequency
11: Turbo Voltage & Frequency

The output should represent the corresponding voltage and frequency setting as 4-bit values. The output for each state is defined as follows:
- 00: 0001 (Low Voltage & Frequency)
- 01: 0011 (Medium Voltage & Frequency)
- 10: 0110 (High Voltage & Frequency)
- 11: 1111 (Turbo Voltage & Frequency)

The controller should have a clock input and a reset input, and it should update the output on the rising edge of the clock.
