The sum is calculated by directly adding the two 4-bit inputs and the carry-in, while the carry-out is derived from the carry generated during the addition of each bit position, ensuring correct propagation of carry values.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] a,
    input [3:0] b,
    input cin,
    output [3:0] sum,
    output cout
);
