Design a 6-bit shift register that shifts its contents to the left on each rising edge of the clock. When the reset signal is asserted low, the output register is cleared to zero. The least significant bit of the register is filled with the external input signal, effectively allowing the register to capture and shift in new data on each clock cycle.
