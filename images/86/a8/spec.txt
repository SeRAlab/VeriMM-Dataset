The circuit changes its output based on the values of J and K at the rising edge of the clock: if J and K are both low, the output remains unchanged; if J is low and K is high, the output resets to 0; if J is high and K is low, the output sets to 1; and if both J and K are high, the output toggles its state. The circuit also provides the complementary output, qb.
