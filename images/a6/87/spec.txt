Design a finite state machine (FSM) that processes incoming serial data. This module utilizes three states: STATE1 for idle and detecting the start of a transmission, STATE2 for capturing each bit of data into an 8-bit register, and STATE3 for signaling the completion of the reception. When a new data reception is initiated, the FSM transitions through these states to store the received bits in sequence, ultimately asserting an output signal to indicate that the data has been successfully received. On reset, the FSM clears its internal registers and returns to the idle state. The `data` output holds the received byte, while the `received` output signifies the completion of reception.
