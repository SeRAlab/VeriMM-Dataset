The multiplier operates on 16-bit segments of the inputs in a sequential manner, allowing it to generate partial products during clock cycles and accumulate the results over multiple stages. It loads the segments of A and B when the load signal is asserted, initializing the computation, and subsequently calculates the products of the segments, combining them to form the final 64-bit product. The design enhances throughput by utilizing pipelining, enabling it to process additional multiplications while the previous ones are still being computed.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input [31:0] A,
    input [31:0] B,
    input load,
    output reg [63:0] C
);
