Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage of a processor based on the workload. The controller should have an input signal indicating the workload (0 to 3), where 0 is low workload and 3 is high workload. Based on the workload, the controller outputs the corresponding voltage and frequency settings. The frequency can be set to 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the voltage can be set to 1.0V, 1.2V, 1.4V, or 1.6V.
