Design a pulse-width modulation (PWM) generator that toggles an output pin based on a specified period. The module counts clock cycles up to the given 8-bit period value. When the counter reaches the period, it resets to zero and alternates the output pin state, generating a PWM signal. The output pin remains high for the duration of the count and low otherwise, allowing for variable duty cycles depending on the input period.
