
You are tasked with designing a digital circuit that implements a 2-input XOR gate using Verilog HDL. The circuit should be implemented using only one module and should take two inputs (A and B) and output their XOR result (Z). The module should also have an active-high enable input (EN) that when asserted, allows the circuit to function and when deasserted, sets the output to 0. The circuit should be synthesizable and operate at a clock frequency of 50 MHz. Assume that the inputs A, B, and EN are synchronous to the clock, and the output Z is registered on the rising edge of the clock.
