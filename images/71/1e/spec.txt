Design a Verilog module based on the following description. Assume signals are positive clock/clk edge triggered unless otherwise stated.
Create a circuit that takes an input signal h and generates an output signal r. The input h is connected to three different gates: a NOR, an XOR, and a NAND gate. Each gate's output is connected to a D flip-flop. The outputs of the flip-flops are then fed into a three-input NAND gate to produce the output r. The second input of the XOR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's output, and the second input of the NOR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output.
