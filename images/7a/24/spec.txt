Design a dual-function flip-flop module. It has a single bit output (Q0) and a 2-bit output (Q1) that store their respective input values (D0 and D1) on the rising edge of the clock (CLK). Upon receiving a reset signal (RST), both outputs are asynchronously set to zero. This module effectively acts as a register capable of holding and updating two different data inputs, while also featuring a reset functionality.
module top_module(output reg Q0,output reg [1:0] Q1,input wire D0,input wire [1:0] D1,input wire CLK,input wire RST);
