It supports multiple codec types, including G.711, G.722, G.726, and G.729, with G.711 applying no compression, G.722 halving the audio input, G.726 quartering it, and G.729 reducing it to an eighth. The choice of compression type is controlled by a parameter, enabling the module to adjust its behavior accordingly.
