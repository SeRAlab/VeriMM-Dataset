

You are tasked with designing a verilog module for a simple calculator that can perform basic arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. The calculator should have two input ports for the operands and a third input port to select the operation to be performed. The result of the operation should be output through a single output port.

The module should have the following input and output ports:
- `input [7:0] operand1`: the first operand
- `input [7:0] operand2`: the second operand
- `input [1:0] operator`: the operation to be performed (00 for addition, 01 for subtraction, 10 for multiplication, and 11 for division)
- `output [15:0] result`: the result of the operation

You may assume that the operands are always positive integers and that division by zero will not occur. You may also assume that the result of any operation will always fit within 16 bits.
