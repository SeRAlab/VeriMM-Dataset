Design a simple WiFi connection controller that maintains a connection state. The controller has three inputs: `connect`, `disconnect`, and `reset`. The state transitions are as follows:  
- When `reset` is high, the state resets to 'IDLE'.  
- When in 'IDLE', if `connect` is high, transition to 'CONNECTED'.  
- When in 'CONNECTED', if `disconnect` is high, transition to 'IDLE'.  
- The state should be stored in a register and output as a 2-bit value: '00' for IDLE and '01' for CONNECTED.
