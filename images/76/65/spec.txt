Design a 4-to-16-line decoder with an enable input. The module takes two 2-bit inputs, A and B, which together determine the selection of one of the 16 output lines (Y). When the enable (EN) input is asserted, the corresponding output line is driven LOW, while all other outputs are driven HIGH. If the enable signal is deasserted, all outputs remain HIGH regardless of the inputs.
module top_module(
    input [1:0] A, B,
    input EN,
    output reg [15:0] Y
);
