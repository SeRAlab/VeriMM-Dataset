Design a combinational logic circuit that evaluates a polynomial of the form P(x) = a0 + a1*x + a2*x^2 + a3*x^3, where a0, a1, a2, and a3 are coefficients and x is the input value. The circuit should take x as a 4-bit input and the coefficients as 4-bit inputs, producing a 16-bit output for the evaluated polynomial.
