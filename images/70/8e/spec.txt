Design a D flip-flop with additional control functionalities for loading and transferring data. This module captures an input signal D when the LOAD signal is high, storing it in an internal register. Upon the assertion of the TRANSFER signal, the stored value is output to Q. The module also includes a reset functionality, which sets both Q and the internal register to a logic high (1) upon receiving a reset signal (RST).
