Design a synchronous FIFO (First-In First-Out) queue with configurable capacity. The module supports enqueue (ENQ) and dequeue (DEQ) operations while maintaining status flags for empty (EMPTY_N) and full (FULL_N) conditions. It resets its internal pointers and counters upon receiving a reset (RST) signal and can be cleared (CLR) to initialize all states. The FIFO is capable of providing warnings regarding enqueueing to a full queue or dequeueing from an empty queue, ensuring safe operational integrity. The design includes a guarded parameter feature to control enqueue operations during concurrent dequeuing.
