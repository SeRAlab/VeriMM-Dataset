Design a one-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer that selects between two input signals, din_0 and din_1, based on the value of the select line, sel. If sel is low (0), the output mux_out will be equal to din_0; if sel is high (1), the output will be equal to din_1. The module utilizes a combinational always block to implement the selection logic.
module top_module(
   din_0      , 
   din_1      , 
   sel        , 
   mux_out      
   );
