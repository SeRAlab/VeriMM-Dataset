
Design a 2nd-order IIR filter that takes a 16-bit signed input and produces a 16-bit signed output. The filter should have two coefficients for feedback and two for feedforward. The filter should use the following difference equation:
y[n] = b0*x[n] + b1*x[n-1] + b2*x[n-2] - a1*y[n-1] - a2*y[n-2]
where y is the output, x is the input, b0, b1, b2 are feedforward coefficients, and a1, a2 are feedback coefficients.
