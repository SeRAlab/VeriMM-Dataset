
Design a 2nd-order Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) filter in Verilog. The filter should have two input signals (x[n] and x[n-1]) and two output signals (y[n] and y[n-1]). The filter coefficients (b0, b1, b2, a1, a2) should be defined as parameters. The filter equation is given by:
y[n] = b0*x[n] + b1*x[n-1] - a1*y[n-1] - a2*y[n-2]
The design must include a clock input and a reset signal. The output should be updated on the rising edge of the clock.
