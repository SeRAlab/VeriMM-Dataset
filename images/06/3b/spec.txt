Design a Verilog module that converts RGB color values to YUV color space. The input will be 8-bit values for Red, Green, and Blue channels, and the output will be 8-bit values for Y, U, and V channels. The conversion formulas are as follows:
Y = 0.299*R + 0.587*G + 0.114*B
U = -0.14713*R - 0.28886*G + 0.436*B + 128
V = 0.615*R - 0.51499*G - 0.10001*B + 128
Ensure that the output values are properly clamped to the range of [0, 255].
