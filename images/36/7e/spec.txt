Design a JK latch using Verilog. The JK latch should have inputs J, K, Clock, and an output Q. The behavior of the JK latch should follow these rules: when the clock transitions from low to high, the output Q should toggle if J and K are both high, maintain its state if J and K are both low, set Q to high if J is high and K is low, and reset Q to low if J is low and K is high.
