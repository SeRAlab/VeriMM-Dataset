Please act as a professional Verilog designer.

Design a 4-bit shift register with synchronous reset and serial input. The shift register shifts its contents to the right by one bit on each positive clock edge. The serial input value is shifted into the most significant bit. When the reset signal is active, all bits of the shift register are cleared.

Module name:
    shift_register

Inputs:
    clk: Clock signal.
    rst: Reset signal (active high).
    serial_in: Serial input value.

Outputs:
    q[3:0]: A 4-bit output representing the current contents of the shift register.
