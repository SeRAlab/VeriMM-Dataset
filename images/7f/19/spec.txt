Design a control unit for an Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that generates a 3-bit ALU control signal based on a 2-bit ALU operation input and a 6-bit function code. The module outputs specific ALU control signals for various operations such as addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, OR, XOR, shifts, and set on less than (slt). When the ALUOp input indicates addition or bitwise AND, the corresponding ALU control signals are generated directly. For other operations, specified by the funct input, the appropriate control signals are determined through a case structure, enabling versatile arithmetic and logic operations tailored for the ALU.
