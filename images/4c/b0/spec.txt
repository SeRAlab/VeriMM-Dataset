This Verilog module implements a double-precision floating-point left-shift operation with handling for special cases such as zero or overflow. It takes a 64-bit input `dataa` representing the floating-point number (with sign, exponent, and mantissa) and a 32-bit `datab` input indicating how many bits to shift left. The module processes the inputs to adjust the exponent based on the value of `datab`, while managing the mantissa and sign, ensuring the correct representation even in edge cases. It includes a valid signal mechanism to control data flow and is equipped to handle stalling conditions. The final output is a 64-bit result that combines the sign, modified exponent, and mantissa, along with associated valid and stall signals for synchronization in a larger pipeline.
