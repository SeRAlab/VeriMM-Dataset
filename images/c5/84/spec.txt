
You are to design a verilog module that takes in two 8-bit inputs and returns the sum of the two inputs. The circuit should also have an additional input, `Valid_add`, which is a single bit that is high when the inputs are valid and low when they are not. The output, `Sum`, should only be valid when `Valid_add` is high.

Assume that the inputs and the output are unsigned integers and that overflow is not a concern.

Define a module named `adder_8bit_valid` that takes the following inputs and outputs:
- `input [7:0] A`
- `input [7:0] B`
- `input Valid_add`
- `output reg [8:0] Sum`
