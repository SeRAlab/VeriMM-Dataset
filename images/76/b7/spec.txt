Design a control unit for an Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that generates corresponding control signals based on the ALU operation codes (ALUOp) and function codes (Funct7 and Funct3). The module takes a 2-bit ALU operation input and evaluates it; for ALUOp values of 00 and 01, it outputs predefined control signals. For ALUOp value of 10, it further discriminates between different operations by examining the combination of Funct7 and Funct3, assigning specific control signals for addition, subtraction, and logic operations. In the absence of valid inputs, it outputs an undefined state.
