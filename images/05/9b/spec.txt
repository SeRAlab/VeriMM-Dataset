Design a 16-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit operation select input (op):
- 000: AND
- 001: OR
- 010: XOR
- 011: ADD
- 100: SUBTRACT
- 101: NAND
- 110: NOR
- 111: XNOR

The ALU takes two 16-bit inputs (A and B) and produces a 16-bit output (Result). The carry-out from the addition and subtraction operations should also be provided as a separate output (CarryOut).
