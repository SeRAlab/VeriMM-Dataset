
Design a simple Verilog module that simulates a neural network layer with a single neuron. The neuron receives 5 inputs (x1, x2, x3, x4, x5) and has 5 corresponding weights (w1, w2, w3, w4, w5). The output of the neuron is calculated as the weighted sum of the inputs plus a bias (b). The neuron also includes a simple activation function: if the weighted sum is greater than a threshold, the output is 1; otherwise, it is 0. Implement the neuron with a clock input and enable signal, where it computes the output only when enabled.
