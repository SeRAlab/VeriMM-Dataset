Design a fan speed control system. The fan has three speed levels: low, medium, and high. The system takes an input from a control signal (`control`) and changes the speed of the fan accordingly. The input `control` is a 2-bit signal where `00` means OFF, `01` means LOW, `10` means MEDIUM, and `11` means HIGH. The current speed of the fan should be stored in a register and updated on the rising edge of a clock signal.
