Design a voltage sensor that takes a 5-bit binary input representing a voltage level (0 to 31 volts) and outputs a 3-bit binary signal. The output should indicate whether the input voltage is "low" (0-10 volts), "medium" (11-20 volts), or "high" (21-31 volts). The output encoding should be as follows: 000 for low, 001 for medium, and 010 for high.
