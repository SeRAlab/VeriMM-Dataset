Design an 8-bit barrel shifter that can perform logical shifts left (LSL), logical shifts right (LSR), and rotate right (ROR). The shifter should take the following inputs: an 8-bit data input, a 3-bit shift amount, and a 2-bit operation code to select the type of shift (00 for LSL, 01 for LSR, and 10 for ROR). The output should be the resulting 8-bit data after the shift operation.
