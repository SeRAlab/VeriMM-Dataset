Design a one-bit wide FIFO (First-In-First-Out) buffer synchronizer with a register-based mechanism. The module takes a clock input (`rd_clk`) and an input signal (`in0`), capturing the value of `in0` on the rising edge of the clock. The synchronizer outputs a flag (`AS`) controlling the reset of its internal state. Additionally, it uses a D flip-flop to link `out` to an internal register (`Q_reg`), ensuring that `out` is synchronized with the clock. The purpose of this design is to facilitate the reliable transfer of data across potential clock domain boundaries, preventing metastability issues.
