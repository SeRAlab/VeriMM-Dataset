
Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that can perform left and right shifts based on a 2-bit control signal. The control signal determines the number of positions to shift the input data. The barrel shifter should take a 4-bit input and a 2-bit control signal and produce a 4-bit output. The possible values for the control signal (00, 01, 10, 11) represent the following operations:
00 - No shift
01 - Shift left by 1
10 - Shift left by 2
11 - Shift right by 1
