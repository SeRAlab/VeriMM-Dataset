
Design a Verilog module that calculates the IP checksum of a 16-bit wide data. The module should take a 16-bit data input and a signal to indicate when the computation should be performed. The IP checksum is the one's complement sum of all 16-bit words in the data. If the data consists of 32 bits, it will require two 16-bit additions. The output should be the 16-bit checksum.
