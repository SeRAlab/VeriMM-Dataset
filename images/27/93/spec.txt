Design a module that implements a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller receives a request signal (`req`), a target voltage (`target_voltage`), and a target frequency (`target_frequency`). When the request signal is asserted, the controller will gradually change the voltage and frequency to the target values. The voltage and frequency can change by one unit per clock cycle. The current voltage and frequency should be outputted as `current_voltage` and `current_frequency` respectively.
