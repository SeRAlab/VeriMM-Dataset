Design a combinational circuit in Verilog that evaluates a polynomial of the form P(x) = a0 + a1*x + a2*x^2 + ... + an*x^n, where the coefficients (a0, a1, a2, ..., an) are provided as inputs, and x is the variable input. The circuit should have inputs for the coefficients, the value of x, and output the computed polynomial value.
