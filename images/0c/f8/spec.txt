Design a 2-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift operations. The shift register should have a clock input, a load input, a shift input, and a 2-bit output. When the load input is high, the 2-bit input should be loaded into the register. When the shift input is high, the register should shift its contents to the right, discarding the least significant bit and introducing a '0' at the most significant bit position. The output should reflect the current state of the register.
