Design a SR latch using D flip-flops. The latch should have two inputs, S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs, Q and Q_n (not Q). Implement the behavior of the latch such that:
- When S is high and R is low, Q is set to high.
- When S is low and R is high, Q is reset to low.
- When both S and R are low, Q maintains its previous state.
- Both S and R should not be high at the same time, as this is an invalid condition.
