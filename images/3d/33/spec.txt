Design a simple Direct Memory Access (DMA) controller in Verilog. The DMA controller should be capable of transferring data from a source address to a destination address in memory. It should have the following inputs: `start`, `clk`, `rst`, `src_addr`, `dest_addr`, and `data_in`. The DMA controller should also have outputs: `data_out` and `done`. The `done` signal should indicate when the DMA transfer is complete.
