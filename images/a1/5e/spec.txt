Design a 2-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs different operations based on a 2-bit operation selector (op). Specifically, it implements a bitwise AND operation on the least significant bits (Z[0]) when op is "00", a bitwise OR operation on the most significant bits (Z[1]) when op is "01", and it performs addition or subtraction on the entire inputs A and B when op is "10" (for ADD) or "11" (for SUBTRACT), respectively. The module outputs the result in a 2-bit output Z.
module top_module(output reg [1:0] Z, input [1:0] op, input [1:0] A, B);
