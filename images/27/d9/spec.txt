Design a SPI master controller that can send and receive data on the SPI bus. The controller should have the following specifications: it should have a 4-bit data input, a clock input, a chip select (CS) input, and a serial output (MOSI). Upon the assertion of the CS signal, the SPI master should transmit the 4-bit data on the rising edge of the clock signal. The SPI master should also be able to read 4 bits of data from the serial input (MISO) during the transmission. The data should be latched on the falling edge of the clock signal.
