Design a 2-to-1 audio multiplexer in Verilog. The multiplexer should take two audio signals as inputs and output one of them based on a select signal. The audio signals will be represented by 16-bit binary values, and the select signal will be a 1-bit binary value. If the select signal is 0, the output should be the first audio signal; if it's 1, the output should be the second audio signal.
