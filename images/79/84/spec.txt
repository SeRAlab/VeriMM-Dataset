Design a 4-bit ring counter that cycles through its states on each positive edge of the clock signal. Upon reset, the counter initializes to the state 1000, activating the first bit and deactivating the others. In the absence of a reset, the counter shifts the active bit sequentially through its outputs (t0, t1, t2, t3) with each clock cycle, allowing only one bit to be high at any given time, thus producing a repeating sequence of states.
module top_module(
		input clk, rst,
		output reg t0, t1, t2, t3
	);
