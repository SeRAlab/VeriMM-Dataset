Design a simple RSA key pair generator that generates a public and private key pair based on two prime numbers. The module should take two prime numbers as inputs and output the public key (n, e) and private key (d). The public key components should be computed as follows:
- n = p * q
- e = 65537 (a common choice for e)
- d = e^-1 mod phi(n), where phi(n) = (p-1)(q-1).
