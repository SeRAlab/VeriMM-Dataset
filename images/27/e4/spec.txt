
Design a Low Dropout Regulator (LDO) controller in Verilog. The controller should take a 3-bit input representing the desired output voltage level (000 for 0V, 001 for 1V, ..., 111 for 7V). Based on the input, it will generate a control signal that turns the LDO on or off. The LDO should be considered "on" when the desired voltage is greater than 0V and "off" otherwise. The controller also has a clock input for synchronization.
