Design a synchronous serial data receiver that captures incoming bits on each tick of the clock signal (tick) and outputs a byte of data. The module shifts incoming bits (Rx) into an 8-bit register (Reg_Out) whenever the date signal is high and the tick signal is asserted, maintaining a counter to track the number of bits received. After receiving 8 bits, the module signals completion (Check) and outputs the captured byte (Dout) for further use. If the date signal is low or the tick is not asserted, the output signal defaults to low, effectively indicating no valid data has been received.
