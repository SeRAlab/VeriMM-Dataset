The multiplication is carried out using a combinational logic approach that shifts the MULTIPLICAND left based on the active bits of the MULTIPLIER and accumulates the result, effectively calculating the product of the two inputs.
module top_module(

    input [3:0] MULTIPLIER,
    output reg [7:0] PRODUCT
