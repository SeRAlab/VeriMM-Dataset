Design a JK flip-flop in Verilog that can be controlled by an active-high clock and an asynchronous reset. The flip-flop should toggle its state based on the J and K inputs. When J and K are both high, the flip-flop should toggle its output. If J is high and K is low, the output should be set to high, and if J is low and K is high, the output should be set to low. The reset input should reset the output to low regardless of the J and K inputs.
