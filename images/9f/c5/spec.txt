Design a forwarding control unit for a pipelined processor. The module determines which values to forward to the execution stage based on the current instruction registers and control signals, effectively resolving data hazards by indicating whether the source registers `rs` and `rt` should be sourced from the `EXMEM` or `MEMWB` stages. When reset, the module clears the forwarding signals, while under normal operation, it prioritizes forwarding from the `MEMWB` stage when appropriate, with checks to prevent conflicts with the `EXMEM` stage. The outputs `forwardA` and `forwardB` signal the respective forwarding paths for arithmetic operations in the pipeline.
