Design a 64-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal: 
1. 000: Addition
2. 001: Subtraction
3. 010: AND
4. 011: OR
5. 100: XOR
6. 101: NOT (only for the second input)
7. 110: Shift Left (logical)
8. 111: Shift Right (logical)

The ALU takes two 64-bit inputs and produces a 64-bit output based on the specified operation.
