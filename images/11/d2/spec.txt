
Design a fan speed controller that takes a temperature input and controls the fan speed accordingly. The fan should operate at different speed levels based on thresholds defined for temperature ranges. The temperature input is a 4-bit binary number, and the fan speed output is a 2-bit binary number, where:
- 00: Off
- 01: Low speed
- 10: Medium speed
- 11: High speed
The temperature thresholds are as follows:
- 0-3: Off
- 4-7: Low speed
- 8-12: Medium speed
- 13-15: High speed
The system should have a clock and reset signal. On the rising edge of the clock, the fan speed should be updated based on the current temperature input. The reset signal should initialize the fan speed to Off.
