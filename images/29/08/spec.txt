Design a phase detector that can compare the phase of two input signals, `signal_a` and `signal_b`. The phase detector should output a signal indicating whether `signal_a` is leading, trailing, or in phase with `signal_b`. The output should be a 2-bit signal: `00` for in phase, `01` for `signal_a` leading, and `10` for `signal_b` leading. The output should also reset after each clock cycle.
