Design a VGA controller that generates horizontal and vertical synchronization signals for a display, while also providing the pixel coordinate counters for both the X and Y axes. It uses a clock signal to increment the pixel counters, resetting them when they reach specified values to create a scanning effect. The module outputs active low synchronization signals (vga_h_sync and vga_v_sync) based on the X and Y counter values, and indicates whether the current coordinates are within the display area (inDisplayArea) defined by a resolution of 640x480 pixels.
