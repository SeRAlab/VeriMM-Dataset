Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog that grants access to one of multiple requesters based on their priority. The arbiter should have 4 request inputs (req[3:0]) and a single grant output (grant). The priority order is fixed as req[0] > req[1] > req[2] > req[3]. The arbiter should also include a clock input (clk) and a reset input (rst) to initialize the state.
