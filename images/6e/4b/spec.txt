Design a one-bit binary adder module that calculates the sum and generate intermediate signals. The module takes three one-bit inputs (a, b, c) and outputs the sum (s) as well as the carry generation (g) and propagation (p) signals. The sum is computed using the XOR operation, while the carry generation is determined by the AND operation of inputs a and b, and propagation is indicated by the OR operation of inputs a and b.
module top_module(
	input a,b,c,
	output g,p,s
	);
