Design an SR-latch using Verilog. The latch should have two inputs S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs Q and Qn (the complement of Q). The behavior should be as follows: when S is high and R is low, Q should be high. When S is low and R is high, Q should be low. If both S and R are low, Q should retain its previous value. If both S and R are high, it should be treated as an invalid state. Ensure to handle the invalid state properly without changing the output.
