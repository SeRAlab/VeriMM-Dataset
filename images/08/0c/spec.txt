Design a convolutional encoder with a constraint length of 3 and a rate of 1/2. The encoder should take a binary input stream and produce two output streams based on the specified generator polynomials: G1 = [1, 1, 0] and G2 = [1, 0, 1]. The input should be a sequence of bits, and the output should be the encoded bits. The design should handle a finite number of input bits and maintain state across clock cycles.
