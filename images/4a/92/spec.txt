Design a 4-bit ripple-carry adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs, `a` and `b`. The module outputs a 5-bit result, `c`, which includes the sum along with a carry-out signal, `co`, that indicates whether there was an overflow from the most significant bit. The carry-out is generated by the addition of the two inputs.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] a,
    input [3:0] b,
    output reg [4:0] c,
    output reg co
);
