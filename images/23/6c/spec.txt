Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a processing unit based on the input performance requirement. The module should have a clock input, an enable signal, and a performance level input. The output should be the selected voltage and frequency levels. The performance level input will be a 2-bit value where '00' corresponds to the lowest performance (lowest voltage and frequency), '01' to medium performance, '10' to high performance, and '11' to maximum performance. The voltage and frequency levels can be represented by 2-bit and 3-bit values, respectively.
