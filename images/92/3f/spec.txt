Design a circuit that outputs a HIGH signal under most conditions, except when specific bits of a 3-bit input are set to 1. The output goes LOW if either the two most significant bits (bits 2 and 1) or the two least significant bits (bits 1 and 0) of the input `data` are both HIGH (i.e., equal to `11`). In all other cases, the output remains HIGH.
module top_module(
  input [2:0] data,
  output reg out
);
