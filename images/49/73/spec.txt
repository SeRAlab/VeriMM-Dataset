Design a clock gating module that controls the output clock signal, ENCLK. When the enable signal (EN) is high and the reset signal (RESET) is low, the module allows the input clock (CLK) to pass through to the output; otherwise, it outputs a low signal. Additionally, the output can be inverted based on the test enable signal (TE); when TE is high, the output becomes the inverse of the gated clock signal. This module is useful for power management in digital circuits by selectively enabling or disabling the clock signal.
