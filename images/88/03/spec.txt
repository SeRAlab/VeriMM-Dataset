This module implements a phase alignment circuit that synchronizes an enable signal, ENA_COMMA_ALIGN, to the rising edge of a received clock signal, RX_REC_CLK. It utilizes two flip-flops to delay the incoming enable signal, effectively creating a 2-clock cycle latency before producing the output, ENA_CALIGN_REC. This design ensures that ENA_CALIGN_REC is stable and aligned with the clock, enabling reliable timing for subsequent operations in a digital communication system.
