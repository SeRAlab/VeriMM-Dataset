Design a result counter that manages and outputs the status of a result based on a specific ID. The module accepts a 2-bit input, `resultID`, and a signal `newresult` to indicate when a new result is available. Upon receiving a new result, it decrements a 4-bit counter (`count`) from an initial value of 8, while storing the active `resultID` in a 2-bit register (`curr`). The output `done` represents the current status; it remains the value of `curr` until the `count` reaches zero, at which point `done` outputs a zero value. The module also has mechanisms to reset the counter and store, with both `reset` and `globalreset` inputs to ensure correct initialization and management of states upon clock edges.
