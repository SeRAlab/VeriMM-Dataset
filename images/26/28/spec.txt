Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The module should take a clock signal, a reset signal, and a request signal that indicates when the system needs to change its voltage and frequency. Based on the request signal, the module will provide output signals for a new voltage level and a new frequency level. The voltage and frequency can be represented as 2-bit values. The output should be updated on the rising edge of the clock when the request signal is high.
