Design a 4-bit binary adder with a carry-in (Cin) and enable (En) input. When En is high, the module computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs, A and B, along with Cin, producing a 4-bit output S and a carry-out (Cout). If En is low, the output S is forced to zero, effectively disabling the adder functionality. The circuit also includes logic to generate carry bits for the addition process, ensuring proper computation of the carry-out signal.
module top_module(
    A   ,
    B   ,
    Cin ,
    En  ,
    S   ,
    Cout
);
