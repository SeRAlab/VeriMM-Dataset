Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that performs left and right shifts based on a 2-bit control signal. The control signal specifies the type of shift operation: a left shift of one bit with zeros filled on the right (ctrl=00), a left shift of two bits with zeros filled on the right (ctrl=01), a right shift of one bit with a zero filled on the left (ctrl=10), and a right shift of two bits with zeros filled on the left (ctrl=11). In case of an invalid control signal, the output defaults to zero.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] in,
    input [1:0] ctrl,
    output reg [3:0] out
);
