The module implements a delay calculation circuit designed for processing signals in a digital system, notably for controlling the timing of Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC) functionalities. It takes in a clock signal and several input parameters, including offsets and modifiers for delays, and generates specific output delays for ADC clock and data signals, as well as a "ready" signal. The design accommodates resetting to initialize the state and employs a conditional mechanism triggered by a strobe signal to compute the delays based on the inputs. Additionally, it ensures that the calculated delays do not exceed specified limits while flagging saturation conditions if the computed delays fall outside acceptable ranges, providing critical feedback for signal integrity management.
