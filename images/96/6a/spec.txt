The output, `bit_parity_out`, is generated by performing an XOR operation between `bit_a` and `bit_b`, resulting in a one-bit output that indicates whether the number of '1' bits is odd (1) or even (0). The `done` output serves as a signal that reflects the status of the `start` input, indicating when the parity calculation process is complete.
module top_module(bit_a, bit_b, bit_parity_out, start, done);
