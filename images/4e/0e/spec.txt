Implement a digital input processing circuit that manipulates analog input signals. When the enable signal is active, the module combines the most significant 15 bits of the analog inputs (`i_in_ana` and `q_in_ana`) with the least significant bit of the respective digital inputs (`i_in_dig` and `q_in_dig`), effectively shifting the analog data and adding a new least significant bit from the digital inputs. If the enable signal is inactive, the outputs directly reflect the analog inputs. This creates a controlled path for integrating digital and analog signals based on the enable condition.
