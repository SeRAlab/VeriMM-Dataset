

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that takes in a 4-bit input `A` and outputs a 2-bit value `Z`. The output `Z` should be the sum of the two least significant bits of `A`. For example, if `A` is `4'b1010`, `Z` should be `2'b10` since the two least significant bits are `10` and their sum is `2`.

You must name your module `sum2lsb` and it must have the following ports:
- `input [3:0] A`: a 4-bit input
- `output [1:0] Z`: a 2-bit output

You may assume that the input `A` will always have a value and that the output `Z` will always be valid.
