Design a 16-bit stack pointer (SP) adder that adjusts the stack pointer based on the IorD control input. When IorD is low, the adder increments SP by 1, and when IorD is high, it increments SP by 16383 (0x3FFF). The upper 4 bits of the stack pointer are preserved, while the lower 10 bits are used in the addition, and the resulting new stack pointer is output with the least significant two bits set to zero.
