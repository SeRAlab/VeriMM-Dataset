
Design a Reed-Solomon encoder that takes in a message of 8 bits and generates a codeword of 16 bits. The encoder should implement a simple (15, 7) Reed-Solomon code over GF(2^4) with a generator polynomial. The encoder should be able to take 8-bit inputs and produce a 16-bit output based on the encoding process. The design should include a clock and reset signal to control the state of the encoder.
