

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that implements a simple 8-bit multiplier. The module should have two 8-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, and an 8-bit output, `c`. The output `c` should be the result of multiplying `a` and `b`.

Write a verilog module called `multiplier` that implements this functionality. You may assume that the inputs `a` and `b` are already available and that the output `c` will be assigned to a wire.
