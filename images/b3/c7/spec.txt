Design a circuit that calculates the generate (g) and propagate (p) signals for two input bits (a and b). The generate signal (g) is produced by the logical AND of the two inputs, indicating if a carry is generated, while the propagate signal (p) is produced by the logical OR, indicating if a carry will be propagated to the next stage. This module is commonly used in the design of carry lookahead adders.
module top_module(input wire a, b,
           output wire g, p);
