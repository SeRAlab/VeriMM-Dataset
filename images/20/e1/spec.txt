
Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that can perform left and right shifts. The circuit should accept a 4-bit input and a 2-bit control signal that determines the shift direction and the number of positions to shift. The control signal should be defined as follows:
- `00`: No shift
- `01`: Shift left by 1
- `10`: Shift left by 2
- `11`: Shift right by 1
- `00`: Shift right by 2 (but only for clarity, it's actually a repeat of 'No shift' in control bits)

The output should be the shifted 4-bit value.
