

You are tasked with designing a Verilog module that takes in a 4-bit input and outputs a 2-bit output. The output should be the sum of the two most significant bits of the input and the two least significant bits of the input, with the most significant bit being the output's MSB. If the input is less than or equal to 2, the output should be 2'b00. 

Design a Verilog module that satisfies the above requirements.
