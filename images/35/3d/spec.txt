
Design a 32-point FFT module in Verilog that computes the Fast Fourier Transform of a complex input signal. The input should be a stream of complex numbers (represented as two separate 16-bit values for real and imaginary parts). The output should also be a stream of complex numbers. The FFT should be capable of processing the input in a pipelined manner, where each stage of the FFT can accept inputs as soon as they are available. Ensure that the module has proper synchronization and valid signals for input/output.
