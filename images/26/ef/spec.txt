Design a Verilog module that implements the point addition operation in elliptic curve cryptography over a finite field. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) as inputs and produce the resulting point (x3, y3) as the output. The inputs and outputs should be 8-bit wide, and the operation should be performed based on the coordinates defined by the elliptic curve equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b. Include necessary control signals for the module.
