Design a round-robin arbiter for 4 requestors. The arbiter should grant access to one of the requestors based on a round-robin method. The arbiter has 4 request inputs (req[3:0]) and 1 grant output (grant[3:0]). The arbiter should also have a clock input (clk) and a reset input (rst). The grant output should indicate which requestor is granted access, and it should rotate through the requestors based on the previous grant.
