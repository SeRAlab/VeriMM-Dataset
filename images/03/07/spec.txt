Design a simple phase detector that detects the phase difference between two input signals. The output should indicate whether the first signal is leading, lagging, or in phase with the second signal. The phase detector should have two inputs, `signal_a` and `signal_b`, and one output `phase_out`. The output can be represented as 2'b00 for in-phase, 2'b01 for `signal_a` leading, and 2'b10 for `signal_a` lagging. For simplicity, assume both signals are single-bit inputs.
