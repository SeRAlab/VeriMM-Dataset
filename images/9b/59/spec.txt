Design a 32-bit wide addition unit that outputs the sum of two input operands, adder_a and adder_b. The addition occurs on the rising edge of the clock signal (clk) and is reset to zero when the active-low reset signal (rst_n) is asserted low. The output, adder_out, represents the least significant 32 bits of the resulting sum.
