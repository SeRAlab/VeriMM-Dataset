
You are tasked with implementing a verilog module that takes in a 4-bit input `in` and a 2-bit input `sel`, and outputs a 2-bit value `out` based on the following truth table:

| in | sel | out |
|----|-----|-----|
| 0  | 00  | 00  |
| 1  | 00  | 01  |
| 0  | 01  | 11  |
| 1  | 01  | 10  |
| 0  | 10  | 10  |
| 1  | 10  | 11  |
| 0  | 11  | 01  |
| 1  | 11  | 00  |

You should use a case statement to implement this logic.
