Design a circuit that performs an 8-bit wide binary addition. The module takes two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and outputs their sum S as an 8-bit result, along with a carry-out signal C_out indicating if there was an overflow from the addition. The carry-out is derived from the ninth bit of the sum, effectively capturing any overflow that occurs during the addition of the two inputs.
module top_module(input [7:0] A, input [7:0] B, output [7:0] S, output C_out);
