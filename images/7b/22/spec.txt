Design a signed adder circuit that accepts two 16-bit signed inputs and produces a 17-bit signed output. The output is updated on the rising edge of the clock signal, where the sum of the two inputs is calculated and assigned to the output. If the most significant bit of the sum indicates a negative result, the output is extended to maintain sign representation; otherwise, it is truncated to 16 bits.
module top_module(
    input signed [15:0] in1,
    input signed [15:0] in2,
    input clk,
    output signed [16:0] out
);
