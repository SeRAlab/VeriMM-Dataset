Design a one-bit wide multiplexer for ALU source selection. The module takes a control signal ALUSrc and two 32-bit data inputs, datain and num. When ALUSrc is 0, the output (dataout) will be equal to datain; when ALUSrc is 1, the output will be set to num, thereby allowing for the selection of one of the two data sources for further processing in the ALU.
