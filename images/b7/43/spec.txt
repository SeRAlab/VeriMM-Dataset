Design a 32-bit binary adder with carry-in and carry-out capabilities. The module takes two 32-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, and a carry-in input, `cin`, to compute the sum of the two numbers. It employs a chain of full adder instances to handle the addition of each corresponding bit, propagating the carry through the additional bits. The 32-bit sum is output as `sum`, while the carry-out, indicating an overflow condition, is output as `cout`.
module top_module(
    input a,
    input b,
    input cin,
    output sum,
    output cout
);
