Implement a combinational logic circuit that converts RGB color values to YUV color space. The RGB inputs are 8-bit values, and the YUV outputs should be represented as 8-bit values as well. The conversion formulas are as follows:  
Y  = 0.299*R + 0.587*G + 0.114*B  
U  = -0.14713*R - 0.28886*G + 0.436*B + 128  
V  = 0.615*R - 0.51499*G - 0.10001*B + 128  
Assume that R, G, and B are 8-bit unsigned integers ranging from 0 to 255. The output Y, U, and V should also be 8-bit unsigned integers.
