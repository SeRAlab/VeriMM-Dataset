Design a 24-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) output decoder in Verilog. The decoder takes a 24-bit input representing the ADC output and generates a 4-bit output representing the range of the ADC value. The output should indicate which of the 16 ranges (0 to 15) the 24-bit input falls into. The ranges are defined as follows: 
- 0: 0 to 1, 
- 1: 2 to 3,
- 2: 4 to 5,
- ...
- 14: 28 to 29,
- 15: 30 to 31 (values larger than 31 should be clamped to 15).
