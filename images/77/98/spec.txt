Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that performs left logical shifts based on a 2-bit shift amount. When the shift amount is 00, output the input data unchanged; for a shift amount of 01, shift the data left by one bit, filling the least significant bit with 0; for a shift amount of 10, shift the data left by two bits, adding two 0s to the least significant positions; and for a shift amount of 11, shift the data left by three bits, resulting in three 0s at the least significant end of the output.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] data_in,
  input [1:0] shift_amt,
  output [3:0] data_out
);
