Design a D-type flip-flop that captures the value of the input D on the rising edge of the clock signal C. The module outputs Q, which holds the value of D at the time of the clock edge, and nQ, which is the inverse of D, indicating the complementary state. This functionality is essential for synchronous data storage in digital circuits.
