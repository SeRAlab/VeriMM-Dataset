
Design a fan speed controller that takes an input temperature value and controls the fan speed based on predefined speed levels. The fan has three speed levels: Low, Medium, and High. The temperature input will determine the fan speed as follows:
- If the temperature is below 20 degrees, the fan runs at Low speed.
- If the temperature is between 20 and 30 degrees, the fan runs at Medium speed.
- If the temperature is above 30 degrees, the fan runs at High speed.
The fan speed is represented as a 2-bit output: 00 for Low, 01 for Medium, and 10 for High.
