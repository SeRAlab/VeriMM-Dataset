It utilizes an adaptive filter approach, where it maintains a buffer of past input values to estimate the echo based on adjustable coefficients. The output is the original signal minus the estimated echo, effectively removing the echo component from the audio signal. The circuit implements a simple least mean squares (LMS) algorithm to continuously update the filter coefficients based on the error between the received echo and the estimated echo.
