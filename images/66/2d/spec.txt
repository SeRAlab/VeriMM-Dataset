The module includes a control signal to toggle between BCD (utilized when control is 2'b00 or 2'b01) and hexadecimal (utilized when control is 2'b10), as well as an additional display selection signal to alter its behavior under specific conditions. The output (seg_out) provides the correct pattern for lighting up the segments of the display based on the value of num_in, allowing representation of decimal digits (0-9) and hexadecimal digits (A-F).
module top_module(
		num_in,
		control,
		seg_out,
		display_sel
);
