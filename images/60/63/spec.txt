Design a 32-bit wide, 4-to-1 multiplexer. The module accepts four 32-bit inputs (a, b, c, d) and a 2-bit selector (decider). Based on the value of decider, it outputs one of the four inputs, where decider=00 selects a, decider=01 selects b, decider=10 selects c, and decider=11 selects d.
module top_module(input [31:0] a, b, c, d, input[1:0] decider, output [31:0] choosen);
