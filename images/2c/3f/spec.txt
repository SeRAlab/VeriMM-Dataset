Design a Low Dropout Regulator (LDO) control logic that can adjust the output voltage level based on a 3-bit control signal. The LDO should provide an output voltage of 1.2V, 1.5V, and 1.8V corresponding to the control signals '000', '001', and '010', respectively. For control signal '011', the output should be disabled (0V). For control signals '100' and above, the output should stay at 0V.
