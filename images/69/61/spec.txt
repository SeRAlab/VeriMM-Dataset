Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer with an enable signal. When the enable (EN) signal is asserted, the module selects between four possible outputs based on the 2-bit selector (SEL): if SEL is 00, output A; if SEL is 01, output B; if SEL is 10 or 11, output a LOW signal (0s). When the enable signal is not asserted, the output is consistently a LOW signal regardless of the selector input.
