Design a 1-bit SR flip-flop in Verilog. The SR flip-flop should have inputs S (set), R (reset), and a clock signal CLK. The output Q should be set to 1 when S is high and R is low, reset to 0 when R is high and S is low, and maintain its state when both S and R are low. The flip-flop should be sensitive to the rising edge of CLK.
