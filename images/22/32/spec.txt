Design a 2-to-4 decoder that takes 2 input signals (A1, A0) and outputs 4 signals (Y0, Y1, Y2, Y3) where each output corresponds to one of the input combinations. The output Y0 is active when A1A0 is 00, Y1 is active when A1A0 is 01, Y2 is active when A1A0 is 10, and Y3 is active when A1A0 is 11. All outputs should be active HIGH.
