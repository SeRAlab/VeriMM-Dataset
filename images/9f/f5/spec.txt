The transmitter takes an 8-bit input data signal, modulates it into a 16-bit output by applying operations such as inversion and zeroing, based on a finite state machine (FSM) that cycles through three states. The receiver captures a 16-bit signal, demodulates it to reconstruct the original data signal, and performs similar state-based operations to produce an 8-bit output. The module is capable of resetting its states and the internal registers based on the reset signal, synchronizing its operations with a clock signal.

  input [data_width-1:0] data,
  input clk,
  input rst,

  input [rx_width-1:0] rx,
  output [data_width-1:0] data_out
);
