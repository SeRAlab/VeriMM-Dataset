
Design an 8-bit shift register with a synchronous load and shift operation. The shift register should have the following specifications:
- It has an 8-bit wide input (data_in) and an 8-bit wide output (data_out).
- It should shift the data to the left on every positive edge of a clock signal (clk).
- If the load signal (load) is high, it should load the data from data_in into the register instead of shifting.
- The shift should be circular, meaning the bits shifted out on the left will come back in on the right.
