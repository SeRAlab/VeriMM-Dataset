Design a synchronous 8-bit adder with an active-low reset. The module takes two 8-bit input signals (in1 and in2) and sums them, producing a 9-bit output (out) that accommodates the possible carry from the addition. Upon receiving a clock pulse, if the reset signal (rst_n) is low, the output resets to zero; otherwise, it updates the output with the sum of the two inputs.
