Design a digital piggy bank circuit that accumulates and manages credit based on the insertion of coins and the purchase of items. The module takes a clock and reset signal along with inputs representing coins (penny, nickel, dime, quarter) that increase the credit by their respective values, constrained to a maximum of 255. Additionally, it allows for the deduction of credit by consuming inputs corresponding to items (apple, banana, carrot, date) with specified costs. The total credit is stored as an 8-bit output, which is reset to zero when the reset signal is activated.
