

In digital circuit design, a latch is a circuit that has two stable states and can be used to store one bit of information. A latch can be implemented using a simple feedback loop. In this problem, you are required to implement a latch using Verilog.

You need to create a module named "Latch" that takes two inputs and two outputs. The inputs are named "D" and "CLK", representing the data and clock signals respectively. The outputs are named "Q" and "Q_bar", representing the latch output and its complement respectively.

The latch should operate as follows: when the clock signal is low, the latch should hold its previous state. When the clock signal transitions from low to high, the latch should capture the value of the data signal and hold it until the next clock transition. When the clock signal transitions from high to low, the latch should continue to hold its previous state.

You are required to implement the latch using only combinational logic and a single flip-flop. You may use any standard Verilog gates, such as AND, OR, NOT, XOR, etc.

Your implementation should be contained within a single module and should not exceed 200 lines of Verilog code.
