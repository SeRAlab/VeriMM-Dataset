Design a circuit to compute the carry-out for a 24-bit adder using the carry lookahead method. The module takes into account the generate (g) and propagate (p) signals from three stages of the adder, along with the initial carry-in (cZero), to determine the carry-out (cTwentyFour). The carry-out is asserted when either the second stage generates a carry, or when the propagation from previous stages leads to a carry being generated.
module top_module(gZero, pZero, cZero, gOne, pOne, gTwo, pTwo, cTwentyFour);
