Design a simple Direct Memory Access (DMA) controller that can transfer data from a source address to a destination address in memory. The DMA controller will have a clock input, a reset signal, a start signal to initiate the transfer, and outputs to indicate the transfer status. The controller should manage the transfer in a simple manner by incrementing the addresses and transferring a fixed amount of data (e.g., 8 bits) from source to destination. There should also be an output signal indicating when the transfer is complete.
