Design a 2-bit phase detector that detects the phase difference between two 2-bit input signals. The output of the detector should indicate whether the phase difference is 0, 1, or 2 clock cycles. The outputs should be 2 bits, where '00' indicates 0 cycles, '01' indicates 1 cycle, and '10' indicates 2 cycles. If the inputs are invalid or do not match these conditions, output '11'.
