
Design a 2-channel DMA controller that can transfer data to and from two devices. The controller should have the following inputs: `start`, `channel_select`, and `data_in`. The `start` signal initiates the transfer, and `channel_select` determines which channel (0 or 1) will be used for the transfer. The output should be `data_out`, which is the data being transferred from the selected device. After the transfer is complete, a signal `done` should be asserted.
