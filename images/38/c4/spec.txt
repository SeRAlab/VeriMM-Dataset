
Design a 4th degree polynomial evaluator in Verilog. The polynomial to evaluate is given by the formula:
P(x) = a * x^4 + b * x^3 + c * x^2 + d * x + e
where 'a', 'b', 'c', 'd', and 'e' are the coefficients of the polynomial, and 'x' is the input variable. The module should take 5 coefficients and a value of 'x' as inputs and produce the polynomial result as output.
