Develop a JK flip-flop with asynchronous reset functionality. The flip-flop has two inputs, J and K, which determine its state based on the clock signal and a reset signal. When the reset is asserted, the flip-flop outputs are set to LOW. In normal operation, the outputs (Qn and its complement QnBar) are updated on the rising edge of the clock according to the current values of J and K, allowing for hold, reset, set, and toggle operations based on the input conditions.
