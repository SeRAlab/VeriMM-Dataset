Design a simple state machine that simulates a basic mapping process for a robot navigating a grid. The robot can move in four directions (up, down, left, right) and should maintain its current position in a 4-bit register. The input will be 2 bits representing the direction (00: up, 01: down, 10: left, 11: right), and the output will be the current position in terms of x and y coordinates, also represented in 4 bits each.
