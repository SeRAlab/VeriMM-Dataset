Design a clock selection circuit that outputs a clock signal (clk_out) based on the input select signal (sel) and two clock inputs (clk_0 and clk_1). When sel is LOW, clk_out follows clk_0; when sel is HIGH, clk_out follows clk_1. Additionally, if both clk_0 and clk_1 are HIGH, clk_out will also be HIGH, providing a combined output when both clocks are active.
module top_module(
input   wire  sel,
input   wire  clk_0, 
input   wire  clk_1,
output  wire  clk_out
);
