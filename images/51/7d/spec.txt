Design a 4-bit Johnson counter with synchronous reset. The counter should have one input, clk (clock), and one output, out (a 4-bit wide bus). The counter generates a sequence of values by shifting a '1' bit through the register with wrapping feedback, and it should reset to 4'b0000 when the reset is high.
