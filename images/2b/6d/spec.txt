Design a SPI master controller in Verilog which sends and receives data to/from a SPI slave device. The master should have the following functionalities: 
1. Send data on the MOSI line when the SS (Slave Select) pin is low.
2. Receive data on the MISO line simultaneously.
3. Control the clock (SCK) line.
4. Acknowledge successful transmission after 8 clock cycles.
5. Provide a status signal indicating if the transmission is complete.
