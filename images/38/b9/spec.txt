
Design a LIFO stack module in Verilog. The stack should have the following specifications:
- Inputs: `push` (1-bit), `pop` (1-bit), `data_in` (8-bit), `clk` (1-bit), `reset` (1-bit).
- Outputs: `data_out` (8-bit) which is the data popped from the stack, and `empty` (1-bit) which indicates if the stack is empty.
- The stack should have a depth of 8.
- When `push` is high on the clock edge, the `data_in` is pushed onto the stack.
- When `pop` is high on the clock edge, the top of the stack is popped, and `data_out` is updated.
- If `reset` is high, the stack is cleared.
