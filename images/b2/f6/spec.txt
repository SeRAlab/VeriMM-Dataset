Design a test control unit that facilitates scan testing in digital circuits. The module outputs a scan enable signal (se) based on the negation of a test scan disable input combined with a global shift enable signal. Additionally, it selects between two output paths for the scan output (so_0): when in short chain mode, it forwards the short chain output, while in other conditions, it forwards the long chain output. This design allows for flexible configuration of scan patterns during hardware testing.
module top_module(se, so_0,global_shift_enable, ctu_tst_scan_disable,ctu_tst_short_chain, long_chain_so_0, short_chain_so_0);
