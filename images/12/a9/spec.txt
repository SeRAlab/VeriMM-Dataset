
Design a simple Sliding Window protocol for a sender and receiver in Verilog. The sender can send data packets (up to 4 packets due to a window size of 4) and the receiver will acknowledge the packets using a simple acknowledgment signal. If an acknowledgment is not received within a certain time, the sender will retransmit the packets. Implement a finite state machine that manages the sending and receiving of packets based on the sliding window concept.
