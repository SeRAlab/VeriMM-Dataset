Design a 2-to-4 line decoder with an enable input. The module outputs one of the four outputs (Y0 to Y3) high based on the two-bit input combination of A and B when the enable (EN) is high. If EN is low, all outputs remain low. Specifically, if A and B are both low (00), Y0 goes high, if A is low and B is high (01), Y1 goes high, if A is high and B is low (10), Y2 goes high, and if both A and B are high (11), Y3 goes high.
module top_module(
    input A, B, EN,
    output reg Y0, Y1, Y2, Y3
);
