Design a 4-bit synchronous counter with load and enable capabilities. On a rising edge of the clock, if the reset (RST) signal is high, the counter resets to zero. If the load (LOAD) signal is high, the counter loads the value from DATA_IN. When the enable (EN) signal is high, the counter increments its value until it reaches the maximum of 15 (4'b1111), at which point it wraps around back to zero.
module top_module(CLK, RST, LOAD, EN, DATA_IN, DATA_OUT);
