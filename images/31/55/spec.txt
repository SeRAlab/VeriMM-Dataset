Design a 1-bit SRAM cell that can store a single bit of data. The SRAM cell has two inputs: `write_enable` and `data_in`, and one output: `data_out`. When `write_enable` is high, the input `data_in` should be written to the SRAM cell. When `write_enable` is low, the output `data_out` should reflect the stored data in the SRAM cell.
