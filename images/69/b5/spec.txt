Design a 2-to-1 multiplexer for 16-bit wide data inputs. The circuit selects between two 16-bit inputs (din0 and din1) based on two separate selection signals (sel0 and sel1). If sel0 is asserted, the output (dout) will reflect the value of din0; if sel1 is asserted, dout will reflect din1. This module effectively allows for controlled data routing based on the select signals provided.
