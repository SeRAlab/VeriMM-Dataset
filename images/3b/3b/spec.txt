Create a simple GPIO controller that allows reading and writing to a GPIO pin. The controller has two inputs: a `write_enable` signal, a `data_in` signal to write to the GPIO pin, and a `read_enable` signal. When `write_enable` is high, the `data_in` is written to the GPIO output. When `read_enable` is high, the current GPIO output should be made available on the `data_out` signal.
