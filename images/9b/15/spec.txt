Design a system that implements the Bluetooth physical layer functionality, including encoding, transmission, reception, and decoding of data packets. The module accepts an n-bit wide input signal and processes it into a p-bit packet for wireless transmission using Bluetooth technology. It includes a transmitter that triggers on clock edges to send the encoded packet, and a receiver that captures incoming packets, storing the received data. Finally, it decodes the received packet back into the n-bit format and outputs this decoded data. This design effectively handles the essential operations required for Bluetooth communication.
