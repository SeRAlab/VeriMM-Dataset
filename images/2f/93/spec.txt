
Design a up/down counter that counts from 0 to 15 and then rolls over to 0 when counting up, and counts down to 0 and then rolls over to 15 when counting down. The counter should have an enable signal and a mode signal where mode = 0 indicates counting up and mode = 1 indicates counting down. The counter should also have an asynchronous reset signal that resets the counter to 0.
