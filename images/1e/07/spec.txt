Design a 2-channel audio mixer in Verilog. The mixer should take two 8-bit audio inputs (audio1 and audio2) and a 1-bit control signal (mute). When mute is asserted, the output should be set to zero. When mute is de-asserted, the output should be the sum of the two audio inputs truncated to 8 bits. Ensure to handle overflow by wrapping around the output.
