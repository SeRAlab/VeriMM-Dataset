A FIFO (First-In-First-Out) queue is to be implemented in Verilog. The FIFO should be able to enqueue (add) and dequeue (remove) elements. It will have a fixed size of 4 elements. The enqueue operation should insert an element at the end of the queue, while the dequeue operation should remove the element from the front of the queue. Additionally, the FIFO should provide a status signal to indicate whether it is full or empty.
