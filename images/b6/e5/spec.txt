When the reset (RN) is low, the flip-flop outputs a low state for Q and a high state for QN. On the rising edge of the clock, if J is high and K is low, the output Q is set high; if J is low and K is high, Q is set low. If both J and K are high, Q toggles its state. The output QN always reflects the inverse of Q.
