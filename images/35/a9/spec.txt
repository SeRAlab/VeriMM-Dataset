
Design a DVFS controller in Verilog that adjusts the voltage and frequency levels based on the input workload levels. The controller has an input `workload` (5 bits, representing workload levels from 0 to 31) and outputs `voltage` (3 bits) and `frequency` (3 bits). The mapping is as follows:
- Workload 0-10: Voltage = 3'b000, Frequency = 3'b000
- Workload 11-20: Voltage = 3'b001, Frequency = 3'b001
- Workload 21-30: Voltage = 3'b010, Frequency = 3'b010
- Workload 31: Voltage = 3'b011, Frequency = 3'b011
