Design an Ethernet MAC controller state machine that manages the transmission and reception of frames. The controller should have states for IDLE, TRANSMIT, and RECEIVE. It should transition between these states based on input signals: 'start_tx' to begin transmission and 'start_rx' to begin reception. The controller should output the current state as a 2-bit value: 00 for IDLE, 01 for TRANSMIT, and 10 for RECEIVE.
