A pulse width modulation (PWM) signal is generated to control the brightness output, with the duty cycle modulated according to the lesser of the ambient light reading and the desired brightness level. The system includes a reset capability to initialize values. This design effectively manages lighting based on environmental conditions, allowing for energy-efficient brightness adjustments.
