
Design a Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) in Verilog that adjusts the output frequency to match a reference frequency. The PLL should have an input clock frequency (ref_freq), an output clock frequency (out_freq), and a control signal (control) to adjust the frequency. The PLL should include a phase detector, a low-pass filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO). The PLL should lock onto the reference frequency and adjust the output accordingly.
