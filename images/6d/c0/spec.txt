Conversely, the LO output goes high when both VPWR and VNB are low, and it defaults to low under conditions where VGND is activated or either VPWR or VNB is high. This module can be interpreted as part of a power management system that controls output signals based on specified voltage conditions.

    HI  ,
    LO  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND,
    VPB ,
    VNB
