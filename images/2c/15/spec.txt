Design a WiFi connection state machine that manages the states of a WiFi module. The states are: IDLE, CONNECTING, CONNECTED, and DISCONNECTED. The state transitions occur based on the following conditions: 
- From IDLE to CONNECTING on 'start' signal
- From CONNECTING to CONNECTED on 'connected' signal
- From CONNECTED to DISCONNECTED on 'disconnect' signal
- From DISCONNECTED to IDLE on 'reset' signal
The state machine should also have a clock input and a reset input.
