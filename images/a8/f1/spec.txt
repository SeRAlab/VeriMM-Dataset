Design a 3-to-8 line decoder that activates one of its eight output lines based on a 3-bit input value. The module takes a 3-bit input `abc` and, on the rising edge of the clock signal `clk`, it shifts a binary one to the left by the value of `abc`, producing a 1 at the corresponding output line while all other outputs remain low.
module top_module(

    input wire [2:0] abc,
