Design a JK flip-flop with asynchronous reset functionality. The flip-flop has two inputs, J and K, which determine its state based on the clock signal (clk). When the reset signal (R) is active, the flip-flop output is reset. The output Q reflects the flip-flop's current state, which can toggle based on the values of J and K at the rising edge of the clock. The design utilizes NAND gates to implement the logical behavior of the JK flip-flop while maintaining the necessary timing characteristics.
