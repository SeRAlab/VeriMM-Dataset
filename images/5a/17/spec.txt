Design a signed binary divider module that computes both the quotient and remainder from two signed 32-bit inputs: the dividend and the divisor. The module handles positive and negative inputs by calculating the absolute values for the division operations, while appropriately setting the sign of the output quotient based on the signs of the inputs. The computed quotient and remainder are returned as signed 32-bit outputs.
module top_module(
  input signed [31:0] dividend,
  input signed [31:0] divisor,
  output reg signed [31:0] quotient,
  output reg signed [31:0] remainder
);
