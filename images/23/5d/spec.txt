Design a voltage sensor circuit that takes a 5-bit binary input representing voltage levels (0 to 31). The output should indicate whether the voltage is below a certain threshold (let's say 10), at or above the threshold, or invalid if the input is out of range (greater than 31). The output should be a 2-bit signal: '00' for below threshold, '01' for at or above threshold, and '11' for invalid input.
