Design an arithmetic logic unit (ALU) capable of performing several operations on 16-bit inputs. The ALU accepts two 16-bit inputs (alu_in1 and alu_in2) and a 2-bit operation selector (alu_sel) which determines the desired operation: addition, subtraction, or incrementing alu_in1 by one. The output (alu_out) is a 16-bit result of the selected operation, accompanied by a zero flag (z) that indicates if the result is zero and an overflow flag (ovf) that signals if an arithmetic overflow occurred.
