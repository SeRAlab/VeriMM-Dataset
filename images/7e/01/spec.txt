Implement a BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) to seven-segment display converter for two segments, oSegA and oSegB. The module takes four binary inputs (iA, iB, iC, iD) and produces two outputs based on a calculated internal operation signal (rOperacion). The output oSegA is determined by specific logical combinations of input signals and the operational signal, while oSegB directly reflects the state of the operational signal. This design allows for the representation of binary inputs in a format suitable for driving segments of a seven-segment display, making it useful for visualizing decimal values in digital circuits.
