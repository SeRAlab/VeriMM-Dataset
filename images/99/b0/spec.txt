Design a D flip-flop with asynchronous SET and RESET inputs and a controlled delay for state update. The flip-flop captures the input D on the rising edge of the CLK (clock) signal, sets its output Q to 1 on the SET input, and resets Q to 0 on the RESET input. Additionally, on the falling edge of CLK, if the DELAY signal is activated, the next state captured during the rising edge is assigned to Q, ensuring controlled timing for output changes. QN is the inverted output of Q.
