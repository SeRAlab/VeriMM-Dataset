Design a Verilog module that implements a simple switching regulator control logic. The module has two inputs: `vin` (input voltage) and `vref` (reference voltage), and one output `vout`. The output `vout` should be set to `vin` when `vin` is greater than `vref`, otherwise `vout` should be `vref`. Additionally, the output should be clamped between `0` and `5` volts.
