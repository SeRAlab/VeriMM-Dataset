Design a 4-bit universal shift register with the ability to perform different operations based on the MODE input. It can hold its current data, perform a right shift with the least significant bit filled by DATAIN, perform a left shift with the most significant bit filled by DATAIN, or load new data from DATAIN directly. The operation is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and it can be reset to zero when the reset signal is activated.
