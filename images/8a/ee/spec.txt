Each bit of the output Y is determined by applying a series of NOR gates to the inputs, effectively generating results that reflect the negation of the logical OR of the corresponding bits of A and B, as well as cascading the results to form the final output.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output [3:0] Y
);
