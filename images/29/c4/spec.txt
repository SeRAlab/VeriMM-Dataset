
Design an 8-bit serializer that takes in an 8-bit parallel input and outputs the bits serially over a specified clock cycle. The serializer should have a `start` signal that initiates the serialization process, an `enable` signal to control the output, and a `done` signal to indicate that serialization is complete. The output should be a single serial bit (`serial_out`), and the serialization should occur on the rising edge of the clock.
