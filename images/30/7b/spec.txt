Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the fan speed based on the temperature input. The system should have three states: Low, Medium, and High speed. If the temperature is below 20°C, the fan should be in Low speed. If the temperature is between 20°C and 30°C, the fan should be in Medium speed. If the temperature is above 30°C, the fan should be in High speed. The fan speed should change only on the rising edge of a clock signal.
