
Design a WiFi module controller that manages connection states. The controller has three states: IDLE, CONNECTING, and CONNECTED. The state changes based on the following inputs:
- `start`: When high, the controller transitions from IDLE to CONNECTING.
- `connected`: When high, the controller transitions from CONNECTING to CONNECTED. 
- `reset`: When high, the controller resets to IDLE regardless of the current state.
The output should indicate the current state with a 2-bit code: 
- IDLE: 00
- CONNECTING: 01
- CONNECTED: 10
