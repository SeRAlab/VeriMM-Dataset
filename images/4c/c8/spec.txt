Implement an 8-bit ring counter that cycles through a sequence of states. The counter is initialized to the value 1 when the reset signal (rst_n) is low, and, when enabled (en), it shifts its bits leftward on each rising clock edge, effectively circulating the highest bit back to the lowest position. This design enables tracking of a single '1' among a series of '0's in a rotating fashion, which can be useful for applications like state machines or sequence generation.
