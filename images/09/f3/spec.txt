Design a simple Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifier in Verilog. The SVM will take 2-dimensional input data points (x1, x2) and classify them into two categories based on a linear decision boundary represented by coefficients (w1, w2) and a threshold (b). The output should be 1 for category A and 0 for category B. The classification rule is given by the sign of the expression w1*x1 + w2*x2 + b.
