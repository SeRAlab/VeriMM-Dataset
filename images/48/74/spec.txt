It features input signals for resetting and readiness status of the simulation and handles command, address, data, and data count transfers through various output signals. Specifically, it conveys the simulation's operational state while passing along incoming commands and associated data to the master module. The design employs standard input-output bridging and synchronizes control signals to ensure coherent data exchange, ultimately enhancing simulation capabilities by modeling a responsive environment for the master controller.
