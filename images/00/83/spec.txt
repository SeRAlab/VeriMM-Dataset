Design a 2-bit phase detector that compares the phases of two input clock signals (clk_a and clk_b). The output should indicate whether clk_a leads, lags, or is in phase with clk_b. The design should have three outputs: "lead", "lag", and "in_phase", which should be high (1) when the corresponding condition is met. Design it using a state machine approach where the states represent the detected phases.
