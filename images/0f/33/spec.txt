Design a clock-gated flip-flop circuit. The circuit should include a clock enable signal that controls whether the flip-flop captures the input data or retains its previous state. The flip-flop should only update its output when the clock enable signal is high, and the clock signal is also high. Design the flip-flop to be resettable. Include the following inputs: `clk`, `rst`, `clk_en`, and `d`, and an output `q`.
