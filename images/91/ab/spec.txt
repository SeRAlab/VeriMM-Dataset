The output bg indicates carry generation at different levels based on logical combinations of g and p, while output bp indicates whether all bits at each corresponding level propagate the carry. This structure enhances the efficiency of carry calculation in parallel arithmetic operations.
module top_module(
    input [15:0] p,
    input [15:0] g,
    output [3:0] bp,
    output [3:0] bg
    );
