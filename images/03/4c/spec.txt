Design a Bluetooth state controller that manages the state transitions of a Bluetooth device. The device can be in one of three states: IDLE, CONNECTING, and CONNECTED. The state transitions are triggered by two inputs: 'start' and 'disconnect'. The state should remain the same until one of the inputs is activated. The state transitions should be as follows: 
- From IDLE to CONNECTING on 'start' input.
- From CONNECTING to CONNECTED on 'start' input.
- From CONNECTED to IDLE on 'disconnect' input.
- From CONNECTING to IDLE on 'disconnect' input.
