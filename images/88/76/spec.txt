Design a simple Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs three primary operations on two 8-bit inputs, rx and ry. The ALU outputs the bitwise AND of the two inputs, the sum of the inputs, and a flag indicating if the first input, rx, is zero. Specifically, the and_out output provides the result of the AND operation, the adder_out output gives the sum of rx and ry, and is_zero_out indicates whether rx is equal to zero, with a non-zero output if true.
module top_module(
    input [7:0] rx,
    input [7:0] ry,
    output [7:0] and_out,
    output [7:0] adder_out,
    output [7:0] is_zero_out
);
