Design a 4-bit signed adder that takes two 4-bit signed integers, `a` and `b`, along with a carry-in bit `cin`. The module outputs a 4-bit signed sum and a carry-out signal. The sum is computed as the addition of `a`, `b`, and `cin`, where the overflow generated from the addition is indicated by the carry-out signal.
module top_module(
    input signed [3:0] a,
    input signed [3:0] b,
    input cin,
    output signed [3:0] sum,
    output cout
);
