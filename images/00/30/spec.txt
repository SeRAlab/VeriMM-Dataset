Design a JK-latch in Verilog. The JK-latch should have two inputs, J and K, and one output, Q. The latch should toggle its output Q based on the inputs J and K whenever the clock signal (clk) transitions from low to high. If J and K are both high, the output Q should toggle. If J is high and K is low, the output Q should set to high. If J is low and K is high, the output Q should reset to low. If both J and K are low, the output Q should maintain its previous state.
