Design a non-restoring divider in Verilog that takes an 8-bit dividend and an 8-bit divisor as inputs and produces a 16-bit quotient and a 8-bit remainder. The division should be performed in a non-restoring manner, with the input values being provided to the divider and the results being ready after a few clock cycles. You should handle division by zero gracefully, setting the quotient to 0 and the remainder to the dividend in that case.
