
Design a simple I2C master controller that can send a start condition, transmit data, and send a stop condition. The controller should have the following inputs: `clk` (clock), `start` (trigger to begin transmission), `data_in` (8-bit data to transmit), and the outputs should be `SDA` (data line), `SCL` (clock line), and `ack` (acknowledge signal). The controller should operate in such a way that upon receiving the `start` signal, it sends the `data_in` followed by the `stop` condition, while generating the correct SCL clock signals.
