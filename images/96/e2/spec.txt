Design a control module for a pipelined CPU that manages various stall and flush signals to handle data hazards and control flow interruptions. The module generates conditions to freeze the program counter and various pipeline stages (instruction fetch, instruction decode, execute, and write-back) based on input signals related to stalls and flush requests. Specifically, it asserts the `pc_freeze` and `flushpipe` signals when there are flush exceptions or branch flushes, while also coordinating stalls between the stages, thus ensuring proper synchronization and avoidance of data corruption during the execution of multiple instructions in a pipeline.
