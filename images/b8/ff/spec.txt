Design a signal converter that outputs a single bit, X, based on the states of two active-low inputs (A1_N and A2_N) and two regular inputs (B1 and B2). The output is determined by the combination of A1_N and A2_N: when both inputs are LOW, output B1; when A1_N is LOW and A2_N is HIGH, output B2; when A1_N is HIGH and A2_N is LOW, output the negation of B1; and when both A1_N and A2_N are HIGH, output the negation of B2. This design serves as a conditional logic selector based on the states of the inputs.
module top_module(
    input A1_N,
    input A2_N,
    input B1,
    input B2,
    output reg X
);
