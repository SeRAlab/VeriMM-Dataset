Design a 4-bit wide FIFO (First In First Out) queue that can hold up to 8 entries. The FIFO should have the following operations: enqueue, dequeue, and check if the FIFO is full or empty. Use a clock signal for synchronization and an active-high enable signal for the enqueue and dequeue operations. Provide appropriate signals for writing data to the FIFO, reading data from the FIFO, and indicating the status of the FIFO (full or empty).
