Design a clock gating cell that controls the flow of a clock signal based on an enable signal. When the enable signal is high, the gated clock outputs the current clock signal; when the enable signal is low, the gated clock is forced to LOW. This functionality reduces power consumption by preventing unnecessary clock activity in inactive portions of a digital circuit.
