Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations: addition, subtraction, AND, OR, and XOR based on a 3-bit control signal. The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs and produce a 32-bit output. The control signal will define the operation:  
- 000: ADD  
- 001: SUB  
- 010: AND  
- 011: OR  
- 100: XOR  
The ALU should also include an overflow detection mechanism for the ADD and SUB operations.
