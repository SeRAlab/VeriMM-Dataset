Design a fan speed controller that operates based on temperature readings. The controller should have three speed settings: low, medium, and high, determined by the input temperature. If the temperature is below 20°C, the fan runs at low speed (00). If the temperature is between 20°C and 30°C, the fan runs at medium speed (01). If the temperature is above 30°C, the fan runs at high speed (10). The fan speed should change only on the rising edge of a clock signal.
