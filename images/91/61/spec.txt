Design a 32-bit wide arithmetic calculator module that performs operations on two input operands, A and B, based on a two-bit operation selector, op. The specified operations include addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, with the operation determined by the value of op: 00 for addition, 01 for subtraction, 10 for multiplication, and 11 for division. The result of the selected operation is stored in the output, Z.
