Design a 2-bit up-counter that increments its count on the rising edge of a clock signal (clk) when the enable signal (en) is high. The counter resets to zero when the reset signal (rst) is low. It wraps around to zero when the maximum count of 3 (2'b11) is reached, providing a count sequence of 00, 01, 10, 11, and then returning to 00.
module top_module(input clk, rst, en, output reg [1:0] count);
