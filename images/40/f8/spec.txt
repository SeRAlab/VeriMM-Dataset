Design a JK flip-flop in Verilog. The JK flip-flop should have inputs J, K, a clock signal CLK, and an asynchronous reset signal RST. The output Q should reflect the behavior of the JK flip-flop, where it toggles based on the inputs J and K on the rising edge of the clock if RST is not asserted. If RST is asserted, Q should be reset to 0.
