The module supports writing and reading operations controlled by write enable (WEN) and read enable (REN) signals. It includes a reset functionality to initialize pointers and status flags, and it tracks the full and empty states of the buffer. The FIFO can handle a configurable depth and width for data storage, making it suitable for temporarily holding data in various applications such as buffering between different clock domains or processes.
