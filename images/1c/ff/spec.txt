
Design a simple WiFi module control unit in Verilog. The module should take a 3-bit input representing different states of a WiFi module (0: IDLE, 1: CONNECTING, 2: CONNECTED, 3: DISCONNECTING, 4: DISCONNECTED) and output a 1-bit signal indicating if the module is currently active (CONNECTED or CONNECTING). The state should change on the rising edge of a clock signal, and have a reset signal to reset to IDLE state.
