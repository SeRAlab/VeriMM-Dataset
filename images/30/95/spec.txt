
Design a 64-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode:
000 - ADD
001 - SUBTRACT
010 - AND
011 - OR
100 - XOR
101 - NAND
110 - NOR
111 - XNOR

The ALU should take two 64-bit input operands and output a 64-bit result. Additionally, an overflow flag should be generated for the ADD and SUBTRACT operations.
