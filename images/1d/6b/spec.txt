Design a simple interrupt controller that can handle multiple interrupt requests. The controller has three inputs: `int_req[2:0]` (3-bit interrupt request), `clk` (clock signal), and `reset` (active high reset). The output `int_ack` should indicate which interrupt request has been acknowledged based on priority. The highest priority interrupt is the first bit of `int_req`. The controller should acknowledge the interrupt on the rising edge of the clock.
