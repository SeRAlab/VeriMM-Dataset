Design a 32-bit barrel shifter capable of performing left and right shifts based on a 5-bit shift_amount input. When shift_amount is positive, the module performs a left logical shift on data_in, while a negative shift_amount will trigger a right logical shift. The result of the shift operation is output as data_out.
module top_module(
    input [31:0] data_in,
    input [4:0] shift_amount,
    output reg [31:0] data_out
);
