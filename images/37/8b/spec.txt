
Create a Verilog module for a temperature sensor that takes a 4-bit binary input representing temperature in Celsius (0 to 15 degrees) and outputs a 3-bit code indicating whether the temperature is low (0), moderate (1), or high (2). The thresholds are defined as follows: 
- Low: 0 to 5 degrees
- Moderate: 6 to 10 degrees
- High: 11 to 15 degrees
