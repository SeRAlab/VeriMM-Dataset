
Design a DVFS controller in Verilog that adjusts the voltage and frequency based on the input clock frequency and a control signal. The controller should have a frequency range from 1 GHz to 3 GHz, and the corresponding voltages should be from 0.9V to 1.2V. The control signal should be a 2-bit value where '00' represents 1 GHz, '01' represents 2 GHz, '10' represents 3 GHz, and '11' is reserved (do nothing). The output should be the selected frequency and voltage.
