Design an Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs three basic operations: addition, subtraction, and multiplication, based on a 3-bit opcode input. The module takes two 16-bit input buses (a_bus and b_bus) and computes the result, which is stored in a 16-bit accumulator output. The opcode determines the operation to execute: when the opcode is '000', it adds the inputs; '001' denotes subtraction; and '010' signals multiplication. The module also includes a zero flag output that indicates whether the result stored in the accumulator is zero, providing essential feedback on the outcome of the operations.
module top_module(a_bus,b_bus ,accumulator,zero_flag,opcode);
