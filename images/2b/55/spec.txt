
Design a combinational logic circuit in Verilog that performs matrix multiplication for two 2x2 matrices and outputs the resulting 2x2 matrix. The matrices will be represented as 4-bit binary numbers to keep the design simple. The inputs will be two 4-bit binary numbers for each element of the matrices: A00, A01, A10, A11 for the first matrix and B00, B01, B10, B11 for the second matrix. The output will be the resulting matrix C represented by C00, C01, C10, C11.
