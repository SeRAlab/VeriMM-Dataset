Design an 8-bit binary divider that takes a numerator and a denominator as inputs and produces a quotient and a remainder as outputs. The division operation should be triggered by a clock signal, and the operation should be completed within a specified number of clock cycles. The numerator is an 8-bit input, the denominator is an 8-bit input, and the outputs are a 8-bit quotient and a 8-bit remainder. The design should handle division by zero by generating a specific output for the quotient and remainder in that case.
