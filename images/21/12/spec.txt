
Design a 4-tap Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filter in Verilog. The filter should accept a 4-bit input signal and produce a 4-bit output signal. The filter coefficients are fixed and given as follows: 
- Coefficient 0: 1
- Coefficient 1: 2
- Coefficient 2: 3
- Coefficient 3: 4

The output should be the sum of the product of the input signal samples with the respective coefficients. The input signal should be shifted in with each clock cycle. Ensure the design accommodates proper shifting and arithmetic operations.
