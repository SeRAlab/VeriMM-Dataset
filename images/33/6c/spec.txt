Design a hazard detection and resolution circuit for a simple 3-input combinational logic function. The circuit should detect potential hazards due to changes in input signals, and it should output the correct result while avoiding glitches. The inputs are A, B, and C, and the output is F, defined as F = A.B + B.C + A.C. Implement hazard detection by ensuring that the output remains stable when inputs change in a way that could cause a momentary glitch.
