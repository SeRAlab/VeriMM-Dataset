Design a parameterized 128-bit wide multiplexer that selects between two 128-bit inputs, D0 and D1, based on a single control signal, ctrl. When ctrl is low (0), the output S will reflect the value of D0; when ctrl is high (1), S will take the value from D1. This design employs a Look-Up Table (LUT) for each bit of the output to perform the selection operation across all 128 bits in parallel.
module top_module(ctrl,
    D0,
    D1,
    S);
