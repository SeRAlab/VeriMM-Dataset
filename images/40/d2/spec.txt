
Design a simple packet filtering module for a firewall in Verilog. The module should accept a packet represented by a 32-bit input and a control signal for filtering. The control signal determines whether to accept or drop the packet based on specific criteria: if the packet's first 8 bits are equal to `0xAB`, the packet is accepted; otherwise, it is dropped. The module should also provide an output indicating whether the packet was accepted or dropped.
