Design a simple branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should take the current branch address and the outcome of the previous branch (taken or not taken) and update the prediction state accordingly. The 2-bit counter should have four states: 00 (strongly not taken), 01 (weakly not taken), 10 (weakly taken), and 11 (strongly taken). The circuit should output the prediction for the next branch.
