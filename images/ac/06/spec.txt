Additionally, it generates a carry output Cout, which is the negation of the logical OR of the product terms A and B, as well as the carry implications of Cin with A and B.
module top_module(
	input wire A,
	input wire B,
	input wire Cin,
	output wire Cout,
	output wire S
	);
