Design a one-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer. The module selects between two input signals, A and B, based on the value of the select signal, SEL. When SEL is high (1), the output Y will be equal to A; when SEL is low (0), Y will be equal to B. The design also includes an inverter to obtain the negation of the SEL signal for the selection logic.
module top_module(
    A,
    B,
    SEL,
    Y
);
