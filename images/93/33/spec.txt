Design a 4-output, 12-bit wide demultiplexer. The module takes a 12-bit input and, based on a 2-bit selection signal, routes the input to one of four outputs (O0, O1, O2, O3), while the other outputs remain low. When sel equals 00, the input appears on O0; when sel equals 01, it goes to O1; sel equal to 10 routes it to O2; and sel equal to 11 directs the input to O3.
