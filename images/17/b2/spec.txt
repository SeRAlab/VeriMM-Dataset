Design an 8-bit ALU that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit operation select input:  
- 000: AND  
- 001: OR  
- 010: XOR  
- 011: ADD  
- 100: SUBTRACT  
- 101: NAND  
- 110: NOR  
- 111: XNOR  
The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs and produce an 8-bit output. It should also have a carry-out flag for addition and subtraction operations.
