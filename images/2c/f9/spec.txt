
Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog that selects one of several requests based on a fixed priority. The arbiter should have 4 request lines (req[3:0]) and one grant line (grant[3:0]). The priority order is fixed as follows: req[0] has the highest priority and req[3] has the lowest priority. When a request is granted, its corresponding grant line should go high, and the grants should be mutually exclusive (only one grant can be high at a time).
