
Design a simple temperature sensor that converts a 5-bit binary input (representing temperature in degrees Celsius) into a 3-bit binary output indicating whether the temperature is Low (000), Medium (001), or High (010). The temperature ranges are defined as follows: Low for 0-15°C, Medium for 16-25°C, and High for 26-31°C. If the input temperature is out of range (greater than 31°C), the output should be 111 (Invalid).
