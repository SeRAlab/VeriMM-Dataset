
Design a simple Ethernet MAC controller state machine that can handle three states: IDLE, SEND, and RECEIVE. The state machine should transition between these states based on the input signals:
- 'start_send' transitions from IDLE to SEND.
- 'end_send' transitions from SEND to IDLE.
- 'start_receive' transitions from IDLE to RECEIVE.
- 'end_receive' transitions from RECEIVE to IDLE.
