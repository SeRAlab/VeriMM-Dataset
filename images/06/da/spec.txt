Design a 5-bit WiFi status indicator that takes two 5-bit inputs: 'signal_strength' and 'connection_status'. The output 'wifi_status' should be a 5-bit value that indicates the status of the WiFi connection based on the following conditions: if the connection_status is 1 (connected) and signal_strength is greater than a threshold (let's say 10), then wifi_status should be '11111' (connected with strong signal). If connected but signal_strength is less than or equal to 10, then wifi_status should be '00001' (connected with weak signal). If not connected (connection_status is 0), then wifi_status should be '00000' (disconnected).
