Design a 4-bit adder with a special output behavior. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and produces a 4-bit output, Z. If the sum of A and B exceeds 9, Z will output the least significant 4 bits of that sum (modulo 10). Otherwise, Z will simply be the sum of A and B. This functionality effectively implements a form of digit adjustment, suitable for applications like BCD addition where results must remain within the range of a single decimal digit.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output reg [3:0] Z
);
