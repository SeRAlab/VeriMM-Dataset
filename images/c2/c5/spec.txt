Please act as a professional verilog designer. Develop a module that implements a 8-bit shift register with a parallel load capability. The module should have three inputs (data_in, shift, and load) and one output (data_out). When shift is high, the contents of the shift register should be shifted left by one bit. When load is high, the contents of the shift register should be loaded with the value of data_in. When both shift and load are low, the contents of the shift register should remain unchanged.
