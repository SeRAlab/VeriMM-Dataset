

You have been tasked with designing a module that takes in three inputs: `a`, `b`, and `sel`. If `sel` is 1, the output should be the sum of `a` and `b`. If `sel` is 0, the output should be the difference between `a` and `b`. 

Design and implement a Verilog module that accomplishes this task. 

You may assume that all inputs and outputs are 8-bit unsigned integers.
