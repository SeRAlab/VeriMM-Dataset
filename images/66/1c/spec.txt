Design a 4-bit binary countdown counter that increments on each rising edge of the clock (clk). The counter resets to zero when the reset (rst) signal is asserted. Additionally, when the counter reaches a maximum value of 9, it wraps around and resets to zero, effectively counting from 0 to 9 repeatedly.

  input clk,
