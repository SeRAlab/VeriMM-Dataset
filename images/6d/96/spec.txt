Design a 4-bit shift register with load, left shift, and right shift capabilities. On a reset signal, the register is cleared to zero. When the load signal is activated, it takes a serial input to fill the shift register. If the shift_left signal is asserted, the contents of the register are shifted left, with the bit that is shifted out being recycled to the least significant bit. Conversely, if the shift_right signal is activated, the bits are shifted right, with the most significant bit being recycled to the most significant position.
