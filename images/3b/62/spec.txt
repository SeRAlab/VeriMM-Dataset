Design a 5-stage pipelined multiplier that takes two 8-bit inputs and produces a 16-bit output. The stages of the pipeline should be defined as follows: 
1. Stage 1: Fetch inputs A and B.
2. Stage 2: Multiply the inputs.
3. Stage 3: Add partial results.
4. Stage 4: Store intermediate results.
5. Stage 5: Output the final result.
