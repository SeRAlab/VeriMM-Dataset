Please act as a professional Verilog designer.

Design a simple ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that supports addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, and bitwise OR operations. The ALU should take two inputs (A and B) of parameterizable width (parameter WIDTH = 8) and a 2-bit control signal to select the operation. 

Module name:  
    alu  

Input ports:
	A: First operand of the ALU.
	B: Second operand of the ALU.
	ctrl: Control signal to select the operation (00 - ADD, 01 - SUB, 10 - AND, 11 - OR).

Output ports:
	result: Result of the ALU operation.
	carry_out: Carry-out flag for addition and subtraction operations.

Parameter:
	WIDTH = 8
