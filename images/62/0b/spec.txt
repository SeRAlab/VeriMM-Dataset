Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (A, B, C, D) based on two selection signals (S0, S1). When both selection signals are low (S0=0, S1=0), the output Y is A; when S0=1 and S1=0, Y is C; when S0=0 and S1=1, Y is B; and when both S0 and S1 are high (S0=1, S1=1), Y is D.
module top_module(
    input A, B, C, D,
    input S0, S1,
    output Y
);
