Design a D-type flip-flop with additional control functionality for setting and clearing the output. The module produces a primary output Q and its complement Q_N. The output Q can be set to high or cleared to low through the SET_B and RESET_B asynchronous inputs, respectively, while being controlled by the CLK signal. The input D determines the output state when SCE (Set Clock Enable) or SCD (Set Clock Disable) signals are asserted. Depending on the state of SCE and SCD, the flip-flop can either load the value from D, toggle its state, or be set to high or low. This design serves as a versatile memory element suitable for various digital applications.
module top_module(
    Q      ,
    Q_N    ,
    D      ,
    SCD    ,
    SCE    ,
    CLK    ,
    SET_B  ,
    RESET_B
);
