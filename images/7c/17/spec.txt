The ALU can execute addition, subtraction, multiplication, bitwise negation of the AND operation, division, modulus, and comparison operations between two 16-bit operands. The output is updated on a positive clock edge and can be reset to zero with an active low reset signal. Additionally, the ALU maintains a status register that reflects the output after each operation, also resettable.
