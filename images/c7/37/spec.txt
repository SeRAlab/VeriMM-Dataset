

You are tasked with designing a Verilog module for a clock gate that is activated by a clock signal, an enable signal, and a test enable signal. The module should output an enable clock signal that is high when both the enable and test enable signals are high, and low otherwise.

Your module should be named "clock_gate" and should take the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- clk (1-bit): the clock signal
- en (1-bit): the enable signal
- te (1-bit): the test enable signal

Outputs:
- en_clk (1-bit): the enable clock signal

Assume that the inputs are synchronous to the rising edge of the clock signal.

Your module should be designed to be as small and efficient as possible, with a maximum of 200 lines of Verilog code. You may not use any pre-built Verilog modules.
