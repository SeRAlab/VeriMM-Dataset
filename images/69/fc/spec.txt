Design a 4-bit ripple carry adder that takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and produces a 4-bit sum output, S, along with a carry-out signal, C_OUT. The carry bits are calculated sequentially, where the carry from each bit addition is propagated to the next higher bit position. The overall design allows for the addition of two 4-bit binary numbers, providing both the resulting sum and an indication of overflow with the carry-out signal.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output reg [3:0] S,
    output reg C_OUT
);
