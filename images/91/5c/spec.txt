The output consists of the stored input signal (reg_out) directly, while two additional outputs (line0 and line1) are generated by duplicating the lower and upper halves of the input signal, respectively, into 45-bit wide registers (reg_line0 and reg_line1). The purpose of this module is to facilitate the transfer and processing of a wide data stream while also extracting and duplicating specific segments of that data for further use.
module top_module(
   input 			clk,
   input [89:0] 		in,
   output [89:0] 		out,
   output [44:0] 		line0,
   output [44:0] 		line1
);
