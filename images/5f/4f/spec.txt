The resulting 4-bit output (R) reflects the operation performed on two 4-bit inputs (A and B), and a carry-out (COUT) signal is generated for addition and subtraction to indicate overflow conditions. The design utilizes combinational logic with an always block to handle the different operations based on the selected OP.
