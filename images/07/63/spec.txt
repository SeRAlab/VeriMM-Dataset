Design a 32-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
0: Addition
1: Subtraction
2: Bitwise AND
3: Bitwise OR
4: Bitwise XOR
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs and produce a 32-bit output. The output should also include a carry-out signal for addition and subtraction operations.
