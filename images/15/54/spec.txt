
Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should take a 1-bit input representing the actual outcome of the branch (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and predict the outcome based on the current state of the saturating counter. The state transitions should be as follows:
- 00: Strongly Not Taken
- 01: Weakly Not Taken
- 10: Weakly Taken
- 11: Strongly Taken
The counter should increment/decrement based on the actual outcome and saturate at the limits of 00 and 11.
