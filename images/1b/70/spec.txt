Design a Verilog module that implements a simple elliptic curve point addition operation on a finite field. The module should take two points in affine coordinates (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) as inputs, and output the resulting point (x3, y3) after addition. For simplicity, assume the elliptic curve is defined over a prime field, and use the Weierstrass form y^2 = x^3 + ax + b. The parameters a and b will be constants. Implement the necessary logic for handling the point at infinity and the case when points are equal.
