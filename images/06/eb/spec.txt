Design a fan speed control system based on temperature input. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The fan speed should change according to the temperature levels defined as follows:
- If the temperature is below 20°C, the fan runs at low speed (00).
- If the temperature is between 20°C and 30°C, the fan runs at medium speed (01).
- If the temperature is above 30°C, the fan runs at high speed (10).
