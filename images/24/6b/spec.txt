Design a 1-bit DRAM cell in Verilog that can store a single bit of data. The DRAM cell should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Inputs: `d` (data input), `we` (write enable), `clk` (clock), `r` (read enable).
- Outputs: `q` (data output).
The DRAM cell should store the value of `d` when `we` is high on the rising edge of `clk`. When `r` is high, it should output the stored value on `q`.
