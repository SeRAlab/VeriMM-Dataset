It shifts the input data through a series of registers, achieving the desired delay defined by the `delay_amount` parameter. The final output reflects the data that has been delayed by the specified number of clock cycles, making the design suitable for timing adjustments and synchronization tasks in digital systems.

  input ref_clk,
