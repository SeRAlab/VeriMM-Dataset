Create a Verilog module that simulates a simple WiFi signal strength indicator. The module should take as input the signal strength measured on a scale of 0 to 255 and output a 3-bit binary representation of the signal strength category: 0 for weak (0-84), 1 for moderate (85-169), and 2 for strong (170-255). The output should be registered and updated on the rising edge of a clock signal.
