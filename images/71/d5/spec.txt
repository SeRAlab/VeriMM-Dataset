Design a synchronous 8-bit counter with enable and reset controls. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is activated, the counter resets to zero. If the `initOne` signal is high, the counter is initialized to one. If the `Cen` (count enable) signal is active, the counter increments its value by one on each clock cycle. The counter's current value is output through the `Out` signal.
