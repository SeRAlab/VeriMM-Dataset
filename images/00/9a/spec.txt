Design a combinational logic circuit that performs a Monte Carlo simulation to estimate the value of π. The circuit should take a number of random points (x, y) as inputs and determine if the point lies within the unit circle defined by the equation x² + y² ≤ 1. The output should indicate whether the point is inside the circle (1) or outside (0). The design should consider five sets of points and output a count of the points that lie inside the circle.
