Design a 1-bit SR flip-flop in Verilog. The flip-flop should have inputs S (Set) and R (Reset), and an output Q. The behavior should be as follows: When S is high, Q should be set to 1. When R is high, Q should be reset to 0. If both S and R are low, Q should maintain its previous state. If both S and R are high, it is an invalid state and Q should be held at its previous state.
