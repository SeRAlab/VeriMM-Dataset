
Design a digital phase shifter that takes a 4-bit input representing a phase in degrees (0° to 360°) and outputs a 4-bit phase shifted value based on a control signal. The control signal will determine the amount of phase shift: 
- 00: No shift
- 01: Shift by 90°
- 10: Shift by 180°
- 11: Shift by 270°

The output should wrap around if it exceeds 360°.
