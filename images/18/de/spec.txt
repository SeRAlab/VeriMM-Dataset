Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature readings. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high, which are represented by 2-bit values (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high). The system should use a clock signal and the temperature input to adjust the fan speed. If the temperature exceeds a threshold (e.g., 30°C), the fan should operate at high speed. If the temperature is between 20°C and 30°C, the fan should operate at medium speed. If the temperature is below 20°C, the fan should operate at low speed. The temperature input is a 7-bit value (0-127 for 0°C to 127°C).
