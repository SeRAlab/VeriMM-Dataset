
Design a simple WiFi signal strength indicator module. The module takes a 5-bit input representing the signal strength and outputs a 3-bit value indicating the strength level. The mapping is as follows:
- 00000 to 00011: Weak (000)
- 00100 to 00110: Moderate (001)
- 00111 to 01011: Good (010)
- 01100 to 01111: Strong (011)
- 10000 to 11111: Very Strong (100)
