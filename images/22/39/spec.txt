
Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: AND
- 001: OR
- 010: ADD
- 011: SUBTRACT
- 100: XOR
- 101: NOT
- 110: SHIFT LEFT
- 111: SHIFT RIGHT

The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and output an 8-bit result (R). The output should also include a carry-out (C_out) for the ADD and SUBTRACT operations.
