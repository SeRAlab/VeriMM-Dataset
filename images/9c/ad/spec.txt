Design a FIFO (First-In, First-Out) status monitoring module that tracks the read and write pointer states to determine the conditions of a FIFO buffer. It outputs signals indicating when the FIFO is full, empty, or reaches a specified threshold, as well as overflow and underflow conditions. The module counts the number of entries in the FIFO, updating on clock edges while considering reset conditions, and evaluates full/empty states based on the write and read operations performed.

    rptr,
    fifo_full,
    fifo_empty,
    fifo_threshold,
    fifo_overflow,
    fifo_underflow,
    clk,
    rst_n
);
