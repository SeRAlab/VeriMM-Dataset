
Design a hazard detection and resolution circuit that can detect and eliminate hazards in a combinational logic circuit. The circuit should have three input signals, A, B, and C, and produce an output signal Y based on the following conditions:
1. If A and B are both high (1), Y should be high (1).
2. If A is high (1) and C is high (1), Y should be low (0).
3. If B is high (1) and C is low (0), Y should be high (1).
4. In all other cases, Y should be low (0).

Ensure that the design can handle hazards gracefully.
