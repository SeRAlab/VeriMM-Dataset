Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency levels based on a 2-bit input control signal. The controller should have two outputs: voltage_level and frequency_level, where the voltage level can be 0V, 1V, or 2V, and frequency levels can be 1GHz, 2GHz, or 3GHz, depending on the control signal. The control signal should adaptively scale the outputs based on the following mapping:
- 00: voltage_level = 0V, frequency_level = 1GHz
- 01: voltage_level = 1V, frequency_level = 2GHz
- 10: voltage_level = 2V, frequency_level = 3GHz
- 11: voltage_level = 0V, frequency_level = 1GHz (reset state)
