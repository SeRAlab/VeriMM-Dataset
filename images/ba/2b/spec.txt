Implement a digital signal processing module that performs conditional output manipulation based on an enable signal. When `enable` is high, the module shifts the 16-bit inputs `i_in` and `q_in` right by one bit, appending a zero to form the 16-bit outputs `i_out_ana` and `q_out_ana`. Additionally, it assigns the least significant bit (LSB) of `i_in` and `q_in` to the outputs `i_out_dig` and `q_out_dig`, respectively, while outputting a zero if `enable` is low. This design can be utilized in applications requiring analog and digital signal conditioning.
