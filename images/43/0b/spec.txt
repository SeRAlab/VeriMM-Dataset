Design a frequency generator that produces two outputs: oOut131k oscillates at approximately 131 kHz, while oOut262k oscillates at approximately 262 kHz. The module utilizes a 19-bit counter that increments on each rising edge of the input clock (iClock) and resets to zero when the reset signal (iReset) is asserted. The two output signals are derived from the least significant bits of the counter, enabling the generation of these distinct frequencies.
module top_module(
		input wire iClock,
		input wire iReset,
		output wire oOut131k,
		output wire oOut262k
	);
