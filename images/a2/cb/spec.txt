Design a UART (Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter) receiver module that captures serial data from a single data line (rxd) at discrete baud clock intervals (baudclk). The module utilizes a shift register to collect incoming bits and transitions through a series of states to identify the start bit, data bits, and stop bit. Once a complete byte is received, it outputs the character represented by the data and checks for validity based on the presence of the stop bit. If the stop bit is absent, an error message is displayed.
