Design a 3-input XOR gate that produces an output based on the XOR operation applied to three binary inputs (a, b, and c). The output will be HIGH if an odd number of the inputs are HIGH, effectively implementing the functionality of a multi-input parity checker. The design includes both an assign statement and an always block to drive the output, although the assign statement alone is sufficient for combinatorial logic.
