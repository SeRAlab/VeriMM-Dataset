Design a 16-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, bitwise OR, and bitwise XOR operations based on a 3-bit control signal. The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs and produce a 16-bit output. The control signals are defined as follows: 
- 000: ADD
- 001: SUB
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
The ALU should also include an overflow flag for addition and subtraction operations.
