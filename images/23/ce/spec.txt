Implement a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) that generates a frequency output based on a control voltage input. The VCO should accept a 4-bit control voltage and produce a 3-bit frequency output. The relationship between control voltage and frequency can be defined as follows: for each increment in control voltage (0-15), the frequency increases (from 0 to 7) cyclically. Implement the VCO in Verilog.
