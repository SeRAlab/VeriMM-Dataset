Design a 2-channel DMA controller that transfers data from a source address to a destination address. The controller should be able to handle two channels independently and should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Input: `start` (to initiate transfer), `channel_select` (to select channel 0 or channel 1), `source_address` (address from where data is read), `destination_address` (address where data is written), `data_in` (input data).
- Output: `data_out` (output transferred data), `transfer_done` (indicates transfer is complete).
