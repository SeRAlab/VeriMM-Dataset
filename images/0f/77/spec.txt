
Design a combinational logic circuit that detects data hazards in a pipelined processor. The hazard detection logic should check for read-after-write (RAW) hazards between two instructions in the pipeline. The inputs to the circuit will be the destination register of the first instruction (src1), the source register of the second instruction (src2), and a signal indicating if the first instruction is a write operation (write_enable). The output should be a hazard signal (hazard) that indicates whether a hazard is present.
