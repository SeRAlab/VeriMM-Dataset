Design a Parallel-in Parallel-out (PIPO) shift register with the following specifications:  
- The shift register has a parameterizable width.  
- It can load data in parallel when a load signal is asserted.  
- It can shift out data serially when a shift signal is asserted.  
- It has a reset signal to clear the register to zero.  
- The output is available on an output bus when in parallel mode.  
Implement the PIPO shift register in Verilog.
