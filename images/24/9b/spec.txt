Design a combinational circuit that detects and resolves hazards in a 2-input multiplexer. The multiplexer should select one of two 4-bit inputs (A and B) based on a 1-bit select signal (sel). The circuit should include hazard detection logic to ensure that no glitches occur in the output when the select signal changes. The output should stabilize correctly after the select signal changes, and the circuit should handle cases where the two inputs may be the same or different.
