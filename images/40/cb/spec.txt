Design an I2C controller module in Verilog that handles the start, stop, read, and write signals for communication with an I2C slave device. The controller will have inputs for clock and reset, along with control signals for initiating a read or write operation. It will generate the necessary I2C signals, such as SDA (data line) and SCL (clock line), for up to 8 bits of data transfer and indicate when the transfer is complete.
