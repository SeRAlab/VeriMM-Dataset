Design a low dropout regulator (LDO) circuit in Verilog that takes a reference voltage (Vref), an enable signal (EN), and an input voltage (Vin). The LDO should output a regulated voltage (Vout) based on the conditions: if EN is high, Vout should equal Vref; if EN is low, Vout should equal Vin. The output should also ensure that Vout does not exceed Vin.
