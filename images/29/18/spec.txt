
Design a simple Model Predictive Control (MPC) system that predicts the next state of a system based on current inputs and previous states. The system will have two inputs: `current_state` (5 bits) and `input_signal` (5 bits). It will predict the next state by adding a scaled version of the `input_signal` to the `current_state`. The scaling factor will be a constant defined as `scale_factor` (3 bits). The `next_state` will be a 5-bit output.
