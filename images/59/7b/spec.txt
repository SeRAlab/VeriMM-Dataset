Implement a circuit that extracts two 4-bit slices from a 32-bit input data word based on a 3-bit index input. The upper slice (slice_up) is taken from the shifted position determined by the index, while the lower slice (slice_down) is taken from the following 4 bits. The index is left-shifted by 2 to accommodate the 4-bit wide slices, effectively allowing for selection from four possible 4-bit segments of the data word.
module top_module(input [2:0] idx, input [31:0] data, output [3:0] slice_up, slice_down);
