The outputs (VPB_OUT and VNB_OUT) are determined by the states of VPB and VNB, respectively; if either input is high, the corresponding output follows the VPWR level, otherwise, it transitions to VGND. The design ensures that the inherent voltage levels are maintained or adapted based on control signals, making it suitable for interfacing components operating at different voltage levels.
