Design a simple branch predictor that uses a 2-bit saturating counter for each branch instruction. The counter states are as follows: 00 (strongly not taken), 01 (weakly not taken), 10 (weakly taken), 11 (strongly taken). The branch predictor increments or decrements the counter based on the actual outcome of the branch. If the branch is taken, the counter is incremented (up to 11), and if not taken, it is decremented (down to 00). The predictor should take 2-bit inputs for the predicted outcome and the actual outcome of the branch.
