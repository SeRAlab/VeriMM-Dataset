

You are given a Verilog module called `RAT_slice_1_0_0` with two ports: `Din` and `Dout`. The `Din` port is a 18-bit input, while the `Dout` port is an 8-bit output. The module also has a wire `Din` of size 18 bits.

Your task is to implement the logic for the `RAT_slice_1_0_0` module such that the lower 8 bits of `Din` are assigned to the `Dout` output port. 

Write the Verilog code for this module.
