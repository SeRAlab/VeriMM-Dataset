Design a Verilog module that performs the QR decomposition of a 2x2 matrix. The input will be a 2x2 matrix represented as two 4-bit binary numbers (the elements of the matrix), and the output will be the orthogonal matrix Q and the upper triangular matrix R, each represented as two 4-bit binary numbers. The matrices can be represented as follows:
Input: A = {a11, a12, a21, a22}
Output: Q = {q11, q12}, R = {r11, r12, r21, r22}
The QR decomposition will be performed using the Gram-Schmidt process.
