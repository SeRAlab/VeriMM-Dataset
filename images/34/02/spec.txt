Design a simple combinational logic circuit that simulates a single neuron in a neural network. This neuron takes three binary inputs (x1, x2, x3) and produces a binary output (y) based on the weighted sum of the inputs. The weights are fixed as follows: w1 = 1, w2 = 2, w3 = 3. The output y is 1 if the weighted sum (w1*x1 + w2*x2 + w3*x3) is greater than or equal to a threshold of 4; otherwise, y is 0.
