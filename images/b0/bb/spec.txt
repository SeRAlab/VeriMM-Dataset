Design a slave finite state machine (FSM) for handling read and write operations in a master-slave communication protocol. The FSM operates based on a clock signal and is reset asynchronously. It features three states: IDLE, READ_WAIT, and WRITE_WAIT. In the IDLE state, the FSM transitions to READ_WAIT or WRITE_WAIT based on the incoming read or write requests, respectively. While in the READ_WAIT or WRITE_WAIT states, it awaits a signal from the master indicating that the read or write operation has completed. During these wait states, the FSM generates appropriate read and write request signals, as well as a waitrequest signal to indicate that it is busy. The design enables controlled read and write operations, facilitating coordination between the master and slave components.
