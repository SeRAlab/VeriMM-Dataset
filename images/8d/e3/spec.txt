Implement a D-type flip-flop with enable, asynchronous clear, and asynchronous preset features. The flip-flop captures the value of input D on the rising edge of the clock (CLK) when the enable (ENA) signal is high. It also resets the output (Q) to LOW asynchronously when the clear (CLRN) signal is asserted and sets it to HIGH asynchronously when the preset (PRN) signal is asserted. The output Q reflects the current state of the internal register Q_reg.
