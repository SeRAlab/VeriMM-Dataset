Design a flush multiplexer that manages a range of control signals for a processor pipeline stage. The module receives a flush signal (`flushIDEX`) that, when asserted (active), overrides all input control signals and sets the outputs to predefined default values, effectively clearing or resetting the respective pipeline registers. If the flush signal is not active, the outputs will directly reflect the incoming signals like `RegDstin`, `RegWrin`, `ALUSrc1in`, `ALUSrc2in`, `ALUFunin`, `Signin`, `MemWrin`, `MemRdin`, and `MemtoRegin`. This functionality is critical for maintaining the correct behavior of the pipeline during control operations, particularly when resetting or flushing stale data during operations such as branches or exceptions.
