Design a 1-bit full adder circuit that computes the sum of three input bits: Ain, Bin, and Cin. The module outputs the Sum as their binary sum and produces a carry-out (Cout) signal to indicate if there was an overflow from the addition. The Sum is calculated using the XOR operation, while the Cout is determined using a combination of AND and OR operations.
module top_module(input Ain,input Bin,input Cin,output Sum,output Cout);
