
Design a 4-to-1 priority arbiter in Verilog. The arbiter should prioritize requests from four different sources (requestors) and grant one of them based on priority. The priority is defined as follows: Requestor 0 has the highest priority, followed by Requestor 1, Requestor 2, and finally Requestor 3. The arbiter should output a grant signal that indicates which requestor has been granted access.
