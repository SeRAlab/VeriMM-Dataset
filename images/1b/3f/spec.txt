Design a Voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) in Verilog that generates a signal based on a control voltage input. The output frequency should vary based on a 4-bit control input, which represents the control voltage. The VCO should have a clock input to sample the control voltage and generate a square wave output accordingly. The frequency should correspond to the following mapping: 0000 - 1 Hz, 0001 - 2 Hz, 0010 - 4 Hz, 0011 - 8 Hz, 0100 - 16 Hz, 0101 - 32 Hz, 0110 - 64 Hz, 0111 - 128 Hz, 1000 - 256 Hz, 1001 - 512 Hz, 1010 - 1 kHz, 1011 - 2 kHz, 1100 - 4 kHz, 1101 - 8 kHz, 1110 - 16 kHz, 1111 - 32 kHz.
