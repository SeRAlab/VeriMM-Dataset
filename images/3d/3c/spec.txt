Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The counter should have the following states: 00 (strongly not taken), 01 (weakly not taken), 10 (weakly taken), and 11 (strongly taken). The branch predictor should take a branch direction input (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and update the counter accordingly. The output of the predictor should be the prediction of whether the branch will be taken (1) or not taken (0) based on the current state of the counter.
