
Design a 24-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) output comparator. The comparator will compare the 24-bit output of an ADC with a reference value (also 24 bits) and produce a binary output indicating whether the ADC output is greater than, less than, or equal to the reference value. The output should be expressed as 2 bits: '00' for less than, '01' for equal to, and '10' for greater than.
