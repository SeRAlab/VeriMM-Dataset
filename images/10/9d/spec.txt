Design a 4th-order IIR filter that takes a 16-bit input signal and produces a 16-bit output signal. The filter should have 5 coefficients (b0, b1, b2, b3, b4 for the feedforward path and a1, a2, a3, a4 for the feedback path) that are predefined. The input signal will be clocked in with every rising edge of the clock, and the output should be available on the next rising edge.
