
Design a power gating circuit using combinational logic. The circuit has three inputs: `A`, `B`, and `C`, and one output `Y`. The output `Y` should be high (1) only when either `A` is high and `B` is low, or `C` is high. Implement a power gating mechanism where the output is also low (0) if both `A` and `C` are low, regardless of `B`.
