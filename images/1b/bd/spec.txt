Implement a Verilog module that performs point addition on an elliptic curve over a finite field. The module should take as inputs two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) and output the resulting point (x3, y3) according to the elliptic curve point addition rules. The input points should be represented in binary format, and the module should handle cases where the points are equal or when one of the points is the point at infinity.
