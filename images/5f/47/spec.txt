Design a 4-bit magnitude comparator that assesses two 4-bit inputs, A and B. The module outputs a signal indicating equality, with 'equal' signaling high (1) when A and B are the same, and low (0) otherwise. Additionally, the 'greater' output indicates if A is greater than B, outputting high (1) in that case and low (0) otherwise.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output equal,
    output greater
);
