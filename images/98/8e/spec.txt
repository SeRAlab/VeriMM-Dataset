Design a 16-bit ripple carry adder that computes the sum of two 16-bit binary numbers, A and B. The adder uses a series of XOR gates to compute the sum bits and a series of AND and OR gates to generate carry bits for each corresponding bit position, propagating carry values sequentially down to the least significant bit. This implementation allows the addition of two 16-bit values while handling intermediate carries, resulting in an accurate 16-bit output sum.

    input [15:0] A, B,
    output reg [15:0] SUM
