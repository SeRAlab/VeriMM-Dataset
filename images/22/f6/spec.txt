
Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should have two states: '00' (strongly not taken), '01' (weakly not taken), '10' (weakly taken), and '11' (strongly taken). Implement the behavior of the predictor for the following operations:
1. Update the state based on a 'taken' (1) or 'not taken' (0) signal.
2. Output the prediction state before the update.
