Design a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage based on the input workload. The controller takes a 3-bit input `workload`, which represents the current workload level. Based on predefined thresholds, it outputs a 2-bit `frequency` and a 2-bit `voltage` that correspond to the workload. The mapping is as follows:
- Workload 000 - Frequency 00, Voltage 00
- Workload 001-010 - Frequency 01, Voltage 01
- Workload 011-100 - Frequency 10, Voltage 10
- Workload 101-111 - Frequency 11, Voltage 11
