Design a combinational logic circuit to compute the Black-Scholes option pricing formula for a European call option. The inputs are: Stock Price (S), Strike Price (K), Time to Maturity (T), Risk-Free Rate (r), and Volatility (σ). The output will be the option price (C) computed using the Black-Scholes formula: 
C = S * N(d1) - K * e^(-rT) * N(d2), 
where d1 = (ln(S/K) + (r + (σ^2)/2) * T) / (σ * sqrt(T)) 
and d2 = d1 - σ * sqrt(T). 
Here, N(x) is the cumulative distribution function of the standard normal distribution.
