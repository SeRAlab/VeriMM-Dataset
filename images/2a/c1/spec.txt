Design a 7-stage pipelined processor that can perform addition and subtraction of two 8-bit numbers. The processor should have two inputs (A and B), a control signal (CTRL) to choose between addition (CTRL = 0) and subtraction (CTRL = 1), and an output (RESULT). The pipeline should be implemented with registers and should handle data hazards appropriately. The output should be available after the 7th clock cycle.
