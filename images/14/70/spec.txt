
Design a Restoring Divider in Verilog. The module should take two inputs: a dividend (numerator) and a divisor (denominator). Implement a restoring division algorithm that produces a quotient and a remainder as outputs. The division operation should be clock-controlled, such that each clock cycle updates the state of the division process. The quotient should be an integer value, and the remainder should be the part of the dividend that cannot be evenly divided by the divisor. Ensure that the design can handle positive integer inputs and the division by zero should be handled gracefully.
