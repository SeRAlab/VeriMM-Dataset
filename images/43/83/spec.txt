Design a 4-bit shift register that captures input data on the rising edge of a clock signal, controlled by an enable signal and a test enable signal. The register shifts its existing data left by one position, inserting the new data bit on each clock pulse when enabled. The clock gating mechanism, utilizing the ENCLK signal, optimizes power consumption by allowing the shift operations to occur only when both the enable and clock signals are active.
