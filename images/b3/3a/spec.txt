Design a circuit that processes a 3-bit input, SW, to control two output LEDs. The first LED (LED[0]) is turned on based on a combination of conditions involving SW bits, specifically requiring the first switch SW[0] to be high and considering the states of SW[1] and SW[2]. The second LED (LED[1]) is activated when SW[0] and SW[1] are high, while SW[2] is low, effectively functioning as a simple logic evaluator for user inputs on the switches.
module top_module(
   input  [2:0] SW,
   output [1:0] LED
   );
