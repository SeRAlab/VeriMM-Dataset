Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency based on the temperature input. The controller should take a 4-bit temperature input and output a 3-bit frequency setting. The mapping is as follows: 
- 0000: 000 (Low frequency)
- 0001: 001 (Medium frequency)
- 0010: 010 (High frequency)
- 0011 to 1111: 011 (Max frequency) 
The controller should also have an enable signal that disables the output when low, holding the last frequency setting when high.
