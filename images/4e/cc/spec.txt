Design a 4-bit to 2-bit priority encoder with an active-high zero output. The circuit outputs two bits, `q`, representing the highest priority non-zero input among the least significant two bits and the most significant two bits of the input `data`. Additionally, it produces a `zero` output that indicates when all input bits are zero. The priority is assigned such that inputs from the higher significant bits take precedence over those from the lower significant bits.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] data,
  output [1:0] q,
  output zero
);
