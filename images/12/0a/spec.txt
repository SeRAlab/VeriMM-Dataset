Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should take a 1-bit input representing the actual outcome of a branch (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and a 2-bit state register. The output should be a prediction for the next branch (0 or 1) based on the current state of the counter. The counter should increment or decrement based on the actual outcome, ensuring that it saturates at 00 and 11.
