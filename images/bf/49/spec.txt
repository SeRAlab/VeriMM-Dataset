Design a fault detection module for a Brushless DC (BLDC) motor encoder system. It monitors the encoder count and hall effect sensor count to determine if there's a fault condition. Specifically, it signals a fault when the encoder count is within a narrow range (-1 to 1) while the hall count is outside the range of -2 and 2, indicating potential inconsistencies in the motor's operation. The module operates synchronously with a clock and can be reset to clear the fault state.
