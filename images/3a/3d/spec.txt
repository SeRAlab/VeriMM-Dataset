Design a Verilog module that implements a basic portfolio optimization logic. The module should take five 8-bit inputs representing the weights of different assets in a portfolio. It should output the total weight of the portfolio and the average weight of the assets. The total weight is the sum of the individual weights, and the average weight is the total weight divided by the number of assets (5). Make sure to handle cases where the total weight exceeds 255 by wrapping around using modulo arithmetic.
