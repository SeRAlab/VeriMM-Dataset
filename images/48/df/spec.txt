Design a 32-bit multiplier circuit that computes the product of two 32-bit inputs, a and b, and provides a 64-bit output, p. The multiplication operation is triggered on the rising edge of the clock signal, where the inputs are first stored in registers before the product is computed and stored in another register. The result is continuously available at the output.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input [31:0] a,
    input [31:0] b,
    output [63:0] p
);
