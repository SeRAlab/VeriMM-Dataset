
Design a temperature-based shutdown system that triggers a shutdown signal when the temperature exceeds a predefined threshold. Use a 5-bit binary input to represent the temperature (0-31 degrees), and a single-bit output to indicate whether the system is in shutdown mode. The system should have a clock and reset signal. On reset, the shutdown signal should be low. If the temperature exceeds the threshold (let's say 25 degrees), the shutdown signal should go high on the next clock cycle and remain high until reset.
