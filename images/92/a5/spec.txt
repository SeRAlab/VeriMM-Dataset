Design a clock gating circuit that regulates the clock signal based on an enable input. When the enable signal (en) is active, the original clock (clk) is passed to a D flip-flop, producing a gated clock output (enclk). If the enable signal is inactive, the gated clock becomes LOW. Additionally, upon the positive edge of a test enable signal (te), the output enclk is reset to LOW, potentially serving as a means to disable the clock during specific conditions. This design conserves power by preventing unnecessary clock distribution when not needed.
