Design a 16-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit operation code (opcode):  
- 0000: ADD  
- 0001: SUBTRACT  
- 0010: AND  
- 0011: OR  
- 0100: XOR  
- 0101: NOT (only on the first input)  
- 0110: NAND  
- 0111: NOR  
- 1000: XNOR  
- 1001: SHIFT LEFT  
- 1010: SHIFT RIGHT  
The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs (A and B) and an opcode as input and produce a 16-bit output. It should account for overflow in addition and subtraction operations.
