Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 1-bit inputs based on two select signals (s0 and s1). The output y will reflect the value of the selected input: when s0=0 and s1=0, it outputs in[0]; when s0=0 and s1=1, it outputs in[1]; when s0=1 and s1=0, it outputs in[2]; and when s0=1 and s1=1, it outputs in[3]. Note that there is an apparent error in the code, where in[0] is mistakenly used twice, indicating a potential design oversight.
