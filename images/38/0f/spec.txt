
Design a data forwarding unit for a simplified CPU architecture that resolves data hazards in a pipelined architecture. The forwarding unit takes four inputs: two source registers (src1 and src2), one destination register (dest), and a valid signal indicating if the destination register is valid. The output should indicate which of the source registers should forward their data based on the destination register. The output includes two signals: forwardA and forwardB, which indicate whether to forward data from the destination register to src1 or src2, respectively.
