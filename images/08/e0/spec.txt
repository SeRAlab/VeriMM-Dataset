Design a voltage sensor circuit that can detect different voltage levels and output a corresponding binary value. The sensor should have three voltage level inputs: LOW (0V), MEDIUM (5V), and HIGH (10V). The output should be a 2-bit binary value representing these levels: 00 for LOW, 01 for MEDIUM, and 10 for HIGH. If the input voltage is outside these levels, the output should be 11, indicating an invalid level.
