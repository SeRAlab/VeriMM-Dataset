Design a 16-bit SRAM cell with read and write capabilities. The SRAM cell should have the following pins: `data_in` (16 bits), `data_out` (16 bits), `address` (4 bits), `we` (write enable), and `clk` (clock). The SRAM should store the data at the specified address on a rising edge of the clock when `we` is high and should output the stored data when `we` is low.
