
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller should take an input signal representing workload (in the form of a 4-bit number) and adjust the output voltage level (in 2 bits) and frequency level (in 2 bits) based on predefined thresholds. The voltage and frequency levels should change based on the workload signal, simulating a simple DVFS behavior.
