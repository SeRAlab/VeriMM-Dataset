Design a circuit that performs an 8-bit XNOR operation on two 8-bit inputs, x and y. The result is an 8-bit output, where the lower 4 bits are summed with the upper 4 bits of the XNOR result. The carry-out of this addition is provided as a 4-bit output, cout, while s0 indicates the least significant bit of the sum.
module top_module(x, y, s0, cout);
