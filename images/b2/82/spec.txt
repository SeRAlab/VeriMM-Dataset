Design a shift register with load and shift capabilities, operating on an 8-bit input. Upon receiving a reset signal (rstn), the register initializes to zero. When the load signal is activated, the register captures the value from the 8-bit parallel input. On each clock cycle, if the shift signal is asserted, the register performs a left shift, moving the bits to the higher positions while discarding the most significant bit and inserting a zero at the least significant bit position. The least significant bit (LSB) of the register is output continuously.
