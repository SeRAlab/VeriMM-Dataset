Design a sleep control circuit that manages a virtual power supply. When the SLEEP input is active (logic high), the circuit provides a low output for both VPWR and VPB, effectively shutting down the power. If SLEEP is inactive (logic low), it outputs high for both VPWR and VPB, allowing normal power operation. The VNB is always held low, and the output VIRTPWR mirrors the state of SLEEP, indicating the active power supply status in sleep mode.
module top_module(
    input  SLEEP  ,
    output VIRTPWR
);
