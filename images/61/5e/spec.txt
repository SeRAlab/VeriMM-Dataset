Develop a pseudo-random binary sequence (PRBS) generator that operates on a clock and reset signal. It utilizes two input signals, `a` and `b`, directing the selection of `b` or `a` based on the control signal `sel_b1`. The module maintains a 7-bit shift register that shifts and updates its contents to produce a PRBS output, with specific feedback logic based on tapped bits to ensure randomness. The output `prbs` is generated from the processed state of the shift register, incorporating conditional XOR operations to enhance the sequence's randomness characteristics. The design effectively combines multiplexing and feedback mechanisms to generate a complex binary sequence.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input reset,
    input a,
    input b,
    input sel_b1,
    output reg [6:0] prbs
);
