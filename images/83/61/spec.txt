Design a 4-bit adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit inputs, A and B. The module produces a 4-bit output S representing the least significant 4 bits of the sum. Additionally, it generates a carry-out signal Cout that indicates whether the addition results in a carry beyond 4 bits, effectively signaling an overflow condition when the sum exceeds the maximum value of 15 (4'b1111).
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    output [3:0] S,
    output Cout
);
