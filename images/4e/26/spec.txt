Design a 4-bit shift register with capabilities for parallel loading, left shifting, and right shifting. Upon receiving a clock pulse, the register resets to zero if the reset signal is activated. If the parallel_load signal is high, the register captures the 4-bit input provided. If shift_left is asserted, the contents of the register are shifted left with a zero filled in the least significant bit, and if shift_right is asserted, the register shifts right with a zero filled in the most significant bit. The current value of the register is output on 'q'.
module top_module(clk, reset, parallel_load, shift_left, shift_right, parallel_input, q);
