If the most significant bit of `offset16` is set (indicating a negative number in two's complement form), `offset32` is extended by replicating the sign bit via the upper 14 bits followed by the original `offset16` and two zero bits. If the sign bit is clear, it pads the upper 14 bits with zeros, also followed by `offset16` and two zero bits, thus maintaining the positive representation for the output.
