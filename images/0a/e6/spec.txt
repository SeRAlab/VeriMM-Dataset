
Design a 4-channel DMA controller that manages data transfers between memory and I/O devices. The controller should have the following inputs and outputs:
Inputs:
- clk: Clock signal
- reset: Active-high reset signal
- request[3:0]: Request signals from 4 devices (active high)
- ack: Acknowledge signal from the device currently being serviced
Outputs:
- grant[3:0]: Grant signals to the devices that are allowed to transfer data (active high)
- active_channel: The currently active channel (0 to 3)

The DMA controller should grant access to devices in a round-robin manner. Upon reset, the controller should initialize and grant access to the first device. The controller should cycle through the devices based on the request signals and the acknowledge signal from the currently active device.
