A 2-channel DMA (Direct Memory Access) controller that transfers data from a source address to a destination address. The DMA controller should include the following inputs: `start`, `clk`, `reset`, `src_addr`, `dest_addr`, and `data`. The controller will have outputs: `busy`, `done`, and `out_data`. The `busy` signal indicates that a transfer is in progress, and `done` indicates that the transfer is complete. The `out_data` signal outputs the data being transferred.
