Design a gated clock shift register with 8 bits of storage. When reset is asserted (reset_b=0), the register clears to zero. If the load signal is active, the register is loaded with an external 8-bit data input. When the Shift_control signal is high, the register shifts left, introducing a new bit (S_in) on the least significant bit side while discarding the most significant bit. The output (SO) provides the value of the least significant bit of the register.
