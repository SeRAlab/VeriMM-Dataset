Design a combinational circuit that evaluates a polynomial of the form P(x) = a0 + a1*x + a2*x^2 + ... + an*x^n, where the coefficients a0, a1, a2, ..., an are specified as inputs, and x is also an input. The output should be the result of the polynomial evaluation. Assume that the maximum degree of the polynomial is 4 for simplicity.
