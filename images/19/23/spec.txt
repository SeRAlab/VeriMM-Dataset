
Create an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: A + B (Addition)
- 001: A - B (Subtraction)
- 010: A & B (Bitwise AND)
- 011: A | B (Bitwise OR)
- 100: A ^ B (Bitwise XOR)
- 101: A << 1 (Left Shift A)
- 110: A >> 1 (Right Shift A)
- 111: NOT A (Bitwise NOT)
The ALU should also provide a carry-out signal for addition and subtraction operations.
