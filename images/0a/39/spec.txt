
Design a Verilog module that implements bilinear interpolation for a 2x2 pixel area. The inputs are the four pixel values (I00, I01, I10, I11) and the fractional coordinates (frac_x, frac_y), where frac_x and frac_y are fixed-point numbers representing the fractional parts of the interpolation in the x and y directions, respectively. The output should be the interpolated pixel value (I_interp).
