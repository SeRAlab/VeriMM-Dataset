Design a Verilog module that performs LU decomposition on a 2x2 matrix. The input will be a 2x2 matrix represented by four 16-bit integers (a11, a12, a21, a22), and the output will be two 2x2 matrices L and U, where L is a lower triangular matrix and U is an upper triangular matrix. The outputs L and U should be in the form of four 16-bit integers each (l11, l12, l21, l22 for L and u11, u12, u21, u22 for U).
