
Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should implement the following states:
00 - strongly not taken
01 - weakly not taken
10 - weakly taken
11 - strongly taken

The predictor should take an input `branch_outcome` (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and use this outcome to update the predictor's state on every clock cycle. The initial state should be 00.
