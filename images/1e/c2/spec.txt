
Design a DVFS (Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling) controller in Verilog that adjusts voltage and frequency based on a temperature input. If the temperature exceeds a threshold (e.g., 75°C), the circuit should lower the frequency and voltage; if the temperature is below 50°C, it should increase them. The controller should have three states: HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW, represented by a 2-bit state variable. The output should represent the current frequency and voltage settings.
