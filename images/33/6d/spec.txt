
Design a 128-bit AES encryption module in Verilog that takes a 128-bit plaintext and a 128-bit key as inputs and outputs a 128-bit ciphertext after performing 10 rounds of AES encryption. The module should include the necessary submodules for key expansion, AddRoundKey, SubBytes, ShiftRows, MixColumns, and the final round without MixColumns. Ensure that the module works properly with a given set of input test cases.
