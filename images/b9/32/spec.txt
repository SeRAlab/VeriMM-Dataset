Create a Verilog module where a D flip-flop's input is connected to the output of a 3-input AND gate. The AND gate takes as inputs 'x', 'y', and 'z'. The D flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of 'clk' and has an active-low asynchronous reset 'rst_n'. When reset is asserted, the flip-flop output should be '0'.
