
You are tasked with designing a module that takes in a 4-bit binary input and outputs the 2's complement of the input. The 2's complement of a binary number is obtained by inverting all the bits and adding 1 to the result. Your module should be named "twos_complement" and should have the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- `in` (4-bit binary input)

Outputs:
- `out` (4-bit binary output)

Your module should be self-contained and should not require any additional modules to function.
