Design a dual-edge triggered flip-flop that captures input data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock signal. The first stage stores the input data on the rising edge of the clock, which is then transferred to the second stage on the falling edge, and finally outputs the state of the second stage on the rising edge. This design allows for capturing and propagating data through both clock edges, effectively doubling the sampling frequency compared to a conventional single-edge trigger flip-flop.
