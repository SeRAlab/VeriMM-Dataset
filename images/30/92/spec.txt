Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations: addition, subtraction, AND, OR, and NOT. The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs and a 3-bit operation selector. The output should be an 8-bit result of the selected operation. The operation codes are defined as follows:
000 - ADD
001 - SUBTRACT
010 - AND
011 - OR
100 - NOT (only apply to the first input)
