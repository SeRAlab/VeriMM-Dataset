Design a digital circuit that performs various logical and bitwise operations on two 3-bit inputs, `a` and `b`. The module calculates the following outputs: a 3-bit bitwise AND result, a logical AND output indicating whether both inputs are non-zero, a 3-bit bitwise XOR result, and a 6-bit output consisting of the inverses of both inputs, with the upper 3 bits representing the inverse of `a` and the lower 3 bits representing the inverse of `b`.
module top_module( 
    input [2:0] a,
    input [2:0] b,
    output [2:0] out_and_bitwise,
    output out_and_logical,
    output [2:0] out_xor,
    output [5:0] out_not
);
