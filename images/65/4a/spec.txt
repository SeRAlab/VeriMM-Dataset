When EN is LOW, all outputs are forced to zero, while when EN is HIGH, the specific output corresponding to the binary value of ABC is set to HIGH, with all other outputs remaining LOW. This design is useful for applications requiring address decoding or for activating specific lines in a digital circuit based on input values.
