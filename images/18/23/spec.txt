
Design a 2-channel DMA controller that manages data transfers between memory and two peripheral devices. The controller should have the following inputs: `req1`, `req2` (request signals from the peripherals), `clk` (clock), and `rst` (reset). The outputs should be `ack1`, `ack2` (acknowledgment signals to the peripherals), and `busy` (indicates if the DMA is currently busy). The controller should give priority to `req1` over `req2`. When `req1` is asserted, `ack1` should be asserted on the next clock cycle, and the same applies for `req2` once `req1` is not active. The `busy` signal should be high when a transfer is in progress and low otherwise.
