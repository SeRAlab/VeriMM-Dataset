Design a double-data-rate (DDR) output driver that samples two input signals, D0 and D1, based on a transmit signal (TX) and a clock (CLK). The module conditionally updates its outputs, Q0 and Q1, depending on the state of TX and the polarity parameter (TXCLK_POL). When TX is high (or low, depending on TXCLK_POL), the module captures and holds the values of D0 and D1 at the rising edge and passes them to Q0 and Q1, respectively. Additionally, it provides an initialization option to reset the outputs and internal registers. This design is useful for applications requiring synchronized data transmission with dual outputs.
