Design a VGA synchronization signal generator that produces horizontal (hsync) and vertical (vsync) synchronization pulses based on a given clock input. The module counts pixel and line intervals to generate hsync pulses, toggling them at specified counts to signal the start and end of horizontal scanning. Similarly, it generates vsync pulses according to line counts, indicating the beginning and end of vertical refresh cycles. The module also calculates pixel coordinates (pixh, pixv) within a defined active display area, allowing for pixel positioning within the frame. This implementation targets the generation of video signals suitable for a VGA display.
module top_module(input clk,output reg hsync,output reg vsync,output reg[9:0] pixh,output reg[9:0] pixv);
