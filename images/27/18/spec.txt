Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the fan speed based on a 2-bit input representing temperature levels (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high, and 11 for off). The controller should output a 2-bit value representing the fan speed: 00 for off, 01 for low speed, 10 for medium speed, and 11 for high speed. The fan speed should only change when the temperature input changes.
