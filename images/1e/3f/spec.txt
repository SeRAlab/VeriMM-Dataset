
Design a 5x5 dot product calculator in Verilog. The circuit should take two 5-bit wide vectors (A and B) as inputs and produce a 10-bit wide output (result) that represents the dot product of these two vectors. The dot product is calculated as follows:
result = A[0]*B[0] + A[1]*B[1] + A[2]*B[2] + A[3]*B[3] + A[4]*B[4]
