
Design a simple audio mixer that takes two audio signals (each represented as 8-bit unsigned integers) and a 2-bit control signal to select the mixing operation. The control signal will determine how the two audio signals are mixed:
00 - Output the first audio signal
01 - Output the second audio signal
10 - Output the average of both signals
11 - Output the maximum of both signals
The output should also be an 8-bit unsigned integer.
