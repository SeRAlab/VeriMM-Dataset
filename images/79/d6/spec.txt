Design a 4-bit wide adder-subtractor that performs addition or subtraction of two 4-bit inputs, A and B, based on the control signal SUB. When SUB is low (0), the module outputs the sum of A and B; when SUB is high (1), it outputs the difference (A - B). The module also generates a carry-out signal (C_OUT) that indicates whether there was a carry from the most significant bit during addition or a borrow during subtraction.
module top_module(output reg [3:0] SUM, output reg C_OUT, input [3:0] A, input [3:0] B, input SUB);
