Design a simple arithmetic calculator that performs operations on two 8-bit inputs, 'a' and 'b', based on a 2-bit operation selector, 'op'. The operations include addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, corresponding to the op values 00, 01, 10, and 11, respectively. The output of the selected operation is provided on the 'out' signal, and the 'valid' signal indicates that the output is ready and valid after an operation is executed.
module top_module(a, b, op, out, valid);
