
Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the fan speed based on temperature readings. The fan can operate at three speeds: low, medium, and high. The system should take a 4-bit temperature input (0-15) and control the fan speed according to the following rules:
- If the temperature is below 5, the fan runs at low speed (00).
- If the temperature is between 5 and 10, the fan runs at medium speed (01).
- If the temperature is above 10, the fan runs at high speed (10). 
- The system should have a clock and reset signal.
