

You have been tasked to design a verilog module that implements a 4-bit adder. The module should have four inputs, `A`, `B`, `Cin`, and `Clk`, and two outputs, `Sum` and `Cout`. The `A` and `B` inputs are the two 4-bit numbers to be added, `Cin` is the carry-in bit, and `Clk` is the clock signal. The `Sum` output is the 4-bit sum of the two input numbers, and `Cout` is the carry-out bit.

You must ensure that the module is synchronous and that the output is updated only on the rising edge of the clock signal. You should also ensure that the module is designed using behavioral Verilog.

Write the Verilog code for the 4-bit adder module.
