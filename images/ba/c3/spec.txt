Design a positive edge-triggered D flip-flop with asynchronous reset and enable functionality. When the reset (R) signal is high, the output Q is set to low regardless of the clock (C) or data (D) inputs. When the enable (E) signal is high and a positive edge of the clock occurs, Q will capture and store the value of D. If enable is low during the clock edge, the output Q remains unchanged.
