Design a circuit that computes the remainder of a 32-bit division operation. The module takes two 32-bit inputs, `dividend` and `divisor`, and produces a 32-bit output `remainder`. If the `divisor` is zero, it outputs zero to prevent division by zero; otherwise, it calculates the remainder of the division of `dividend` by `divisor` using the modulo operation.
module top_module(
  input [31:0] dividend,
  input [31:0] divisor,
  output reg [31:0] remainder
);
