Design a 3-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of three single-bit inputs (D0, D1, D2) based on a single control signal (ctrl). When ctrl is HIGH, the multiplexer selects either D1 or D2 depending on the current state of ctrl, while when ctrl is LOW, it chooses between D0 and one of the other inputs. The output S provides the selected input signal.
module top_module(ctrl,
    D0,
    D1,
    D2,
    S);
