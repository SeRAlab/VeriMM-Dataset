Design a 64-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal: 
0 - Addition 
1 - Subtraction 
2 - Bitwise AND 
3 - Bitwise OR 
4 - Bitwise XOR 
5 - Bitwise NOT (only on the first input) 
The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs and produce a 64-bit output.
