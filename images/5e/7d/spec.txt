Design a hazard detection unit (HDU) for a pipelined processor. The module monitors the opcode and register signals to identify potential data hazards. When a specific halt instruction (HLT) is detected, or when a register in the execute stage (id_ex_rt) matches either of the registers in the instruction decode stage (if_id_rs or if_id_rt) while a memory read is in progress, it asserts a stall condition. This results in disabling program counter increments (pc_write) and preventing instruction fetch/decode (if_id_write), effectively stalling the pipeline to maintain correct operation.
