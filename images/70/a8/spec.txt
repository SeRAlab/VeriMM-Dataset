The controller cycles through a predefined sequence of traffic light states: green for NS, followed by yellow for NS, then green for EW, and finally yellow for EW. The green light for each direction lasts for a specified count of 10 clock cycles, while the yellow light lasts for 2 clock cycles. The controller utilizes a finite state machine to transition between states based on a clock signal and a counter for timing, ensuring safe and efficient traffic flow.
module top_module(
  input clk,
  output reg green_ns,
  output reg yellow_ns,
  output reg green_ew,
  output reg yellow_ew
);
