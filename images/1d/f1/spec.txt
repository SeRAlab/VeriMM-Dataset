
Design a DVFS controller that takes a temperature input and outputs the corresponding voltage and frequency settings. If the temperature is below 30 degrees Celsius, set voltage to 1.0V and frequency to 1.0GHz. If the temperature is between 30 and 70 degrees Celsius, set voltage to 0.9V and frequency to 900MHz. If the temperature is above 70 degrees Celsius, set voltage to 0.8V and frequency to 800MHz.
