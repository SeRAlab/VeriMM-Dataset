Design a debounce circuit for a push-button (PB) input. The circuit filters out noise from the button press by using a counter to ensure that only stable, long-press signals are recognized as valid button presses. It outputs a signal (PB_state) that reflects the debounced state of the button, and another signal (PB_down) that indicates when the button has been pressed down for a sufficient duration (400 clock cycles). The debounce logic is synchronized with a clock signal (CLK) to ensure reliable operation.
module top_module(input CLK,input PB,output reg PB_state,output reg PB_down);
