Design a 16-bit pseudo-random number generator (RNG) that generates a sequence of numbers based on a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) approach. Upon receiving a clock pulse, if the reset signal is active, the register initializes to a predefined value (16'hCAFE). Otherwise, it shifts the current value right by one bit and places the feedback value, calculated from a combination of specific bits, into the most significant bit. The generated random number is then output through the Return signal.
