Design a Run-Length Encoding (RLE) encoder in Verilog. The encoder takes an 8-bit input data stream and encodes sequences of repeated bits into a compact format. The output of the encoder should be a 9-bit representation where the first bit indicates the length of the run (0 for lengths 1-7, and 1 for 8), followed by 8 bits of the repeated data. The encoder should also include a mechanism to indicate when the input data has ended by outputting a specific end signal.
