
Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) module in Verilog that takes in a 16-bit analog input signal and produces a 4-bit digital output representing the most significant bits of the input. The ADC should also have a clock input for synchronization and an enable signal to control when the output is updated. When the enable signal is high, the output should be updated with the 4 most significant bits of the input signal on the rising edge of the clock.
