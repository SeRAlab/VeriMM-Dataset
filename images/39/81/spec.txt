
Design a 2-channel DMA controller that can transfer data from two source addresses to two destination addresses. The controller shall have a control signal to start the transfer. The state of the transfer should indicate which channel is currently active, and the transfer should proceed in a round-robin manner between the two channels. The controller should have the following inputs: clk, reset, start, src_addr1, dst_addr1, src_addr2, dst_addr2, and outputs should be channel_active, transfer_done, and data transferred.
