Design a 5-bit wide synchronous register with enable and reset functionality. The module samples its 5-bit input data (D) on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when the enable (en) signal is high. When en is low, the register maintains its current state, and when the reset (rst) signal is high, it clears the output (Q) to zero. Each of the five individual bits D[0] to D[4] can be stored in a flip-flop, which updates based on the defined conditions, thereby providing a simple but effective mechanism for controlled data storage in synchronous digital designs.
