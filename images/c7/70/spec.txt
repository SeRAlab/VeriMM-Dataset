

You are tasked with designing a Verilog module for a 2-input AND gate with additional power supply signals. Your module should take in two inputs, `A_N` and `B`, and output their logical AND result as `X`. The power supply signals `VPWR`, `VGND`, `VPB`, and `VNB` should also be included in the module.

Your task is to write a Verilog module that meets the following requirements:

1. The module should be named `and2b`.
2. The module should have three inputs and one output, as specified above.
3. The module should include four power supply signals, as specified above.
4. The module should implement a 2-input AND gate, as specified above.
5. The module should be synthesizable and meet timing requirements.

You should assume that all inputs and outputs are single-bit signals. You should also assume that the power supply signals are always present and do not need to be generated within the module.
