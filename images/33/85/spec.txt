Design an SR latch using D flip-flops. The latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn). The output Q should be set to 1 when S is high and R is low, and reset to 0 when R is high and S is low. If both S and R are high simultaneously, the output should be maintained (no change). The D flip-flops should be clocked on the rising edge of a clock signal.
