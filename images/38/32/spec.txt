
Design a Verilog module that implements a 16-QAM modulator. The modulator takes in a 4-bit input representing the symbol (00 to 15) and outputs a pair of 2-bit signals representing the I (In-phase) and Q (Quadrature) components. The mapping for the 16-QAM symbols is as follows:
- 00: (1, 1)
- 01: (1, 3)
- 10: (3, 1)
- 11: (3, 3)
- 12: (1, -1)
- 13: (1, -3)
- 14: (-1, 1)
- 15: (-1, 3)
- 16: (-3, 1)
- 17: (-3, 3)
- 18: (-1, -1)
- 19: (-1, -3)
- 20: (-3, -1)
- 21: (-3, -3)
- 22: (1, 0)
- 23: (0, 1)
