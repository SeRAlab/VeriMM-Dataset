Upon receiving a clock signal, if enabled (ena), the circuit shifts the current state left and introduces feedback based on an XOR operation between the two most significant bits. The counter can be reset asynchronously to zero when the reset signal is low. The output reflects the current state of the register, allowing for the generation of a variable-length sequence of binary values based on the defined feedback configuration.
