Design a 2-input logistic regression model that takes two binary inputs (x1, x2) and produces an output (y) of either 0 or 1 based on a simple logistic function: y = 1 / (1 + exp(-(w1*x1 + w2*x2 + b))). For simplicity, use fixed weights w1 = 1, w2 = 1 and bias b = -1. The output should be rounded to the nearest integer (0 or 1).
