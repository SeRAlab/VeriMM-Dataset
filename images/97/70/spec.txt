Design a 4-bit shift register with load and shift capabilities. When the load signal is asserted, the 4-bit input (din) is loaded into the register. On the rising edge of the shift signal, the register shifts its contents to the right by one position, with the least significant bit filled with a zero. The current contents of the register are available at the output (dout).
module top_module(
    input [3:0] din,
    input load,
    input shift,
    output [3:0] dout
);
