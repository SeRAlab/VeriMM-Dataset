Design a simple synchronous data synchronizer. On each rising edge of the clock, the module captures the value of the `data_in` input, while it resets the output `data_out` to LOW when the `rst` signal is asserted. This circuit is used to ensure that the output signal is properly synchronized with the clock, effectively mitigating issues related to metastability in asynchronous input signals.
