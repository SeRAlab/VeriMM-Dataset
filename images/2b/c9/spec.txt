A Turbo encoder is a type of error-correcting code that includes two or more convolutional encoders in parallel, followed by an interleaver. In this problem, you are required to implement a simple Turbo encoder in Verilog. The encoder will take a 4-bit input and generate a 6-bit output, combining the outputs of two convolutional encoders. The first encoder will have a rate of 1/2 and the second will also have a rate of 1/2. You need to manage the states and outputs accordingly.
