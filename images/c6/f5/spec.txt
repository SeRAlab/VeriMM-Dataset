- `input [3:0] in`
- `output [3:0] out`

Your module should make use of the following internal signal:
- `wire [3:0] neg_in`

Your module should make use of the following verilog constructs:
- `assign`
- `~`
- `+`
- `1'b1`

You may not use any other verilog constructs or operators that are not listed above.
