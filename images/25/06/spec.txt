
Design a fixed-priority arbiter that selects one of several requests based on a predefined priority. The arbiter should have inputs for multiple request signals and grant outputs for each request. The arbiter should be able to handle five request signals (req0 to req4) and grant the corresponding output (gnt0 to gnt4) based on priority, with req0 having the highest priority and req4 the lowest. The arbiter should also include an enable signal to activate the arbitration process.
