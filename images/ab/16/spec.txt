Create a Verilog module that represents an 8-bit shift register with an asynchronous positive edge triggered reset and a synchronous shift control signal. The shift register shifts left when the shift signal is high.
(1) reset: Resets the shift register to zero.
(2) shift: Controls the shift operation.
(3) in: Serial input data to be shifted into the register.
(4) q: The current content of the shift register.
