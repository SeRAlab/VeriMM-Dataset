Design a combinational circuit that processes two 8-bit inputs, A and B, to generate several outputs: an 8-bit NAND output, an 8-bit OR output, a 4-bit product output, and a 4-bit quotient output. The NAND output is derived from the logical NAND operation on A and B, while the OR output results from the logical OR operation. The product output captures the most significant 4 bits of the multiplication of A and B, and the quotient output provides the most significant 4 bits of the division of A by B, with division by zero handled by outputting zero.
module top_module(
    input wire [7:0] A,
    input wire [7:0] B,
    output wire [7:0] out_nand,
    output wire [7:0] out_or,
    output wire [3:0] out_prod,
    output wire [3:0] out_quot);
