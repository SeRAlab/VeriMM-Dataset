Design a binary counter capable of counting from 0 to 255. The counter increments its value on each positive edge of the clock signal. If the reset signal (rst) is asserted, the counter will reset its output to 0. Upon reaching the maximum value of 255 (8'hFF), the counter rolls over to 0 and continues counting.

  input clk,
