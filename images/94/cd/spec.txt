Design a timer circuit that generates a low signal output (out) for one clock cycle after a specified delay controlled by the parameter FREQ_DELAY. The timer counts clock cycles on each rising edge of the input clock signal (mclk), and it resets to zero when the reset signal (rst) is asserted or when the count reaches the FREQ_DELAY value. The output signal (out) indicates when the timer has completed its delay, becoming high only when the timer is at zero.
