Design a 2-bit ripple counter that increments its count on the rising edge of a clock signal. When the reset signal is asserted, the counter outputs are forced to zero. The least significant bit toggles with each clock cycle, while the most significant bit toggles on the rising edge of the least significant bit, allowing the counter to count from 0 to 3 in a binary sequence (00, 01, 10, 11).
