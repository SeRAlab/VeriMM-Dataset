Design a circuit that performs various bitwise operations on two 8-bit input vectors, in1 and in2. The module outputs four 8-bit results: out_and, which is the bitwise AND of in1 and in2; out_or, which is the bitwise OR of in1 and in2; out_xor, which is the bitwise XOR of in1 and in2; and out_not, which is the bitwise NOT of in1.
module top_module(
  input [7:0] in1,
  input [7:0] in2,
  output [7:0] out_and,
  output [7:0] out_or,
  output [7:0] out_xor,
  output [7:0] out_not
);
