
Design a 16-bit DRAM cell with the following functionality:
- The DRAM cell has a 16-bit wide data input (D), a 16-bit wide data output (Q), a write enable signal (WE), and a clock signal (CLK).
- When WE is high, the data from D should be written into the cell on the rising edge of CLK.
- When WE is low, the data from the cell should be available at the output Q on the falling edge of CLK.
