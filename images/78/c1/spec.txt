The sum is calculated as the direct addition of A, B, and CIN, while the carry-out is determined by the logic that captures overflow from the most significant bit additions.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    input CIN,
    output reg [3:0] S,
    output reg COUT
);
