Design a 4-bit SPI controller that can send and receive data. The controller should have the following inputs: `clk` (clock), `reset` (active high reset), `start` (to initiate transmission), `data_in` (4-bit input data), and a 1-bit `MISO` (Master In Slave Out). The outputs should be `data_out` (4-bit output data) and `MOSI` (Master Out Slave In). The SPI controller should shift data on the rising edge of the clock and operate in a simple mode where it transmits the data when `start` is asserted.
