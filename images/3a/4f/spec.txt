
Design a 2-stage pipelined processor that can perform two operations: addition and subtraction. The first stage will take two inputs and an operation code (0 for addition, 1 for subtraction) and perform the operation. The second stage will take the result from the first stage and apply a constant offset (e.g., +5) to it. The output of the second stage should be the final result. The design should include a clock signal and a reset signal.
