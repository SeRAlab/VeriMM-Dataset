
Design a 24-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) simulator with a Verilog module. Your ADC will take a 24-bit binary input representing an analog voltage level and convert it to a 12-bit digital output. The output should be scaled such that the maximum 24-bit input (0xFFFFFF) corresponds to the maximum 12-bit output (0x0FFF). The module should also handle the case where the input is less than 0, ensuring the output does not exceed the 12-bit limit.
