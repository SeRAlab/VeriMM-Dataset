Design a 16-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit control signal:
- 0000: ADD
- 0001: SUBTRACT
- 0010: AND
- 0011: OR
- 0100: XOR
- 0101: NAND
- 0110: NOR
- 0111: XNOR
- 1000: SLT (Set on Less Than)
- 1001: SLL (Shift Left Logical)
- 1010: SRL (Shift Right Logical)
- 1011: SRA (Shift Right Arithmetic)
- 1100: NOT
- 1101: PASS A
- 1110: PASS B
- 1111: ZERO

The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs, A and B, and output a 16-bit result based on the operation specified by the control signal. Additionally, the ALU should output a zero flag if the result is zero.
