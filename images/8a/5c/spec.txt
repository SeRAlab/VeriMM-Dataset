Design a circuit that performs multiplication of two 8-bit unsigned integers using a shift-and-add approach. The module accepts two 8-bit inputs, x and y, and produces a 16-bit output, prod, that represents their product. The operation is synchronized to a clock signal and toggled by a reset signal. When reset is activated, the inputs are loaded, and the product is initialized to zero. On each clock cycle, the first operand is right-shifted while the second operand is left-shifted, contributing to the product conditionally based on the least significant bit of the first operand. The ready signal indicates when the multiplication process is complete by monitoring if either operand has been completely processed.
