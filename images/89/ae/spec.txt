Design a synchronous 4-bit counter with reset functionality. The counter increments on each positive edge of the clock signal. When the reset signal is asserted (active low), the counter resets to zero. The circuit outputs a 'max_count' signal when the counter reaches its maximum value of 15 (4'hF), indicating a rollover event, after which the counter resets back to zero. The current count is provided as a 4-bit output.
