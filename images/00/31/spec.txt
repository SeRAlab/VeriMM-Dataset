Design a combinational logic circuit that converts RGB color values to YUV color space. The circuit should take three 8-bit inputs for the RGB components (R, G, B) and produce three 8-bit outputs for the Y, U, and V components. The conversion formulas are as follows:
Y = 0.299 * R + 0.587 * G + 0.114 * B
U = -0.14713 * R - 0.28886 * G + 0.436 * B + 128
V = 0.615 * R - 0.51499 * G - 0.10001 * B + 128
