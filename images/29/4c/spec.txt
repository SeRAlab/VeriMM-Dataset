
Design an 8-bit barrel shifter that can perform left and right shifts. The module should take a 4-bit control signal to determine the type and amount of shift:
- Control signal `0000`: No shift
- Control signal `0001`: Shift left by 1
- Control signal `0010`: Shift left by 2
- Control signal `0011`: Shift left by 3
- Control signal `0100`: Shift right by 1
- Control signal `0101`: Shift right by 2
- Control signal `0110`: Shift right by 3
- Control signal `0111`: Shift right by 4
The output should be an 8-bit shifted value based on the input value and control signal.
