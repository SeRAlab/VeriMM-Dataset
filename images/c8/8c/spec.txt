
You are tasked with designing a Verilog module for a simple combinational circuit that takes in two 4-bit inputs (A and B) and outputs a 4-bit result (Y). The output should be the sum of the inputs modulo 16 (i.e., if the sum is greater than 15, subtract 16 from the result).

Your module should have the following interface:
- Two 4-bit inputs, A and B
- One 4-bit output, Y

Your module should be named "mod16_adder" and should be contained within 200 lines of Verilog code. Your module should be synthesizable and should not contain any latches or flip-flops.
