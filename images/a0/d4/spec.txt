Design a clock gating circuit that controls the propagation of the clock signal (CLK) based on enable (EN) and test (TE) signals. When EN is high, the output clock enable (ENCLK) follows the CLK signal on its rising edge; when EN is low, ENCLK is set to LOW. Additionally, if the test enable (TE) signal is active, ENCLK is directly driven by the CLK signal, allowing for direct clocking during test conditions. This design is intended to save power by disabling the clock to portions of the circuit when not needed.
