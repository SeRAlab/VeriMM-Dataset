Design a module for fixed-point arithmetic operations, capable of handling addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of two signed 16-bit fixed-point numbers. The module takes two inputs, `x` and `y`, along with their respective scaling factors `s_x` and `s_y`, and produces a result `z` with a 32-bit output, which accommodates the potential overflow from arithmetic operations. The scaling factor for the output, `s_z`, is calculated based on the operation performed: it utilizes the maximum of the two input scaling factors for addition and subtraction, while it adjusts appropriately for multiplication and division.
module top_module(
  input signed [15:0] x,
  input signed [15:0] y,
  input [7:0] s_x,
  input [7:0] s_y,
  output reg signed [31:0] z,
  output reg [7:0] s_z
);
