The counting process is performed with a pipeline architecture that utilizes shift registers to sequentially hold previous values of A and the count B across multiple clock cycles. On a positive edge of the clock, if the least significant bit of the most recent stored value of A is '1', the output count B is incremented by one; otherwise, it retains its previous value. This structure allows for efficient counting while managing data dependencies through sequential storage.
