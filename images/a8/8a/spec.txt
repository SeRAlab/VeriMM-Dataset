Design a circuit that processes a 32-bit floating-point number. It extracts the significand (mantissa) from the input and shifts it based on the exponent value to generate an aligned mantissa. The output flag, `frac_flag`, is set high if any of the lower 24 bits of the mantissa are non-zero, indicating the presence of a fractional component. This module operates on a clock signal to synchronize the flag assignment and can be reset asynchronously.
module top_module(input clk,input rstn,input [31:0] afl,output reg frac_flag);
