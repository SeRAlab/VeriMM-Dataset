Design a D flip-flop with clock enable (ce) and asynchronous clear (clr) functionality. On the rising edge of the clock, if the clear signal is high, the output (q) is reset to LOW. If the clear signal is LOW and the clock enable is high, the output is updated to the value of the input data (d). If both the clear and clock enable signals are LOW, the output retains its previous value.
