Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that can perform circular shifts on the input data A based on the 2-bit select signal S. When S=00, output A unchanged; when S=01, perform a right circular shift by one position; when S=10, perform a left circular shift by one position; and when S=11, perform a right circular shift by two positions. The output Y is updated based on the selected shift operation.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    input [1:0] S,
    output reg [3:0] Y
);
