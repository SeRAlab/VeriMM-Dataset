Design a 2-bit wide decoder with additional functionality to generate a 4-bit output based on the values of two 2-bit inputs: `a1_0` and `op`. The output `be` is set to different predetermined 4-bit values depending on the value of `op`. Specifically, for `op` equal to `00`, the output is `1111`; for `01`, it checks the most significant bit of `a1_0` to select between `1100` and `0011`; for `10`, it recognizes specific values of `a1_0` to produce `0001`, `0010`, `0100`, or `1000`; and for any other value of `op`, it outputs `0000`.
