Design a synchronous D flip-flop with asynchronous clear and preset functionality. The flip-flop captures the value of input D on the rising edge of the clock signal (CLK). When the clear signal (CLR) is low, it sets the output Q to 0 and QN to 1, regardless of the clock. When the preset signal (PRE) is low, it sets Q to 1 and QN to 0, overriding the clock input. If both asynchronous controls are inactive, the flip-flop transfers the value of D to Q and provides the complementary output QN.
