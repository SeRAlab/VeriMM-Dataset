Design a combinational logic circuit that processes three 3-bit wide inputs (A, B, and C) to generate three 3-bit wide outputs (Y1, Y2, and Y3). Specifically, Y1 outputs the bitwise AND of inputs A and B, Y2 outputs the bitwise OR of inputs A and C, and Y3 outputs the bitwise XOR of inputs B and C.
module top_module(
  input [2:0] A,
  input [2:0] B,
  input [2:0] C,
  output reg [2:0] Y1,
  output reg [2:0] Y2,
  output reg [2:0] Y3
);
