Design a Verilog module that performs a simple QR decomposition on a 2x2 matrix. The inputs are two 2-bit binary numbers representing the rows of the matrix, and the output should be the orthogonal basis vectors obtained from the decomposition. Assume the input matrix is in the form of a 2x2 matrix with the first row as input_a and the second row as input_b. The output should consist of two 2-bit numbers representing the orthogonal vectors q1 and q2.
