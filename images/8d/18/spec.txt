Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that rotates the input DATA based on a 2-bit SHIFT control signal. The SHIFT value determines the amount and direction of the rotation: with SHIFT=00, the output is the same as the input; SHIFT=01 performs a one-bit left rotation; SHIFT=10 executes a two-bit left rotation; and SHIFT=11 conducts a three-bit left rotation. The design employs two stages of combinatorial logic to achieve the final shifted result, ensuring the bits are re-circulated as needed for the specified shift amount.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] DATA,
    input [1:0] SHIFT,
    output [3:0] RESULT
);
