Design a fan speed control system that varies the speed based on a temperature input. The system should have a 3-bit speed output (000 for off, 001 for low, 010 for medium, 011 for high, 100 for very high) depending on the temperature range. The temperature input can vary from 0 to 100 degrees Celsius. If the temperature is below 20 degrees, the fan is off. Between 20 and 40 degrees, the fan runs at low speed. Between 40 and 60 degrees, the fan runs at medium speed. Between 60 and 80 degrees, the fan runs at high speed. At temperatures above 80 degrees, the fan runs at very high speed. The fan speed should update every clock cycle.
