
Design a 5-stage pipeline processor in Verilog that performs a simple operation: adding two 8-bit numbers. The pipeline stages are:
1. Instruction Fetch (IF)
2. Instruction Decode (ID)
3. Execute (EX)
4. Memory Access (MEM)
5. Write Back (WB)

The processor should take two 8-bit input numbers and produce their sum as the output after going through the pipeline stages.
