
Design a Verilog module that performs matrix multiplication for two 2x2 matrices. The inputs are two 2x2 matrices, A and B, and the output is the resulting matrix C, also 2x2. Each element of matrix C is calculated as follows:
C[0][0] = A[0][0] * B[0][0] + A[0][1] * B[1][0]
C[0][1] = A[0][0] * B[0][1] + A[0][1] * B[1][1]
C[1][0] = A[1][0] * B[0][0] + A[1][1] * B[1][0]
C[1][1] = A[1][0] * B[0][1] + A[1][1] * B[1][1]
