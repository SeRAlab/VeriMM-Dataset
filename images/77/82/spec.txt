Design a 16-bit divider module that computes the quotient of two 16-bit inputs: the dividend and divisor. It utilizes a state machine with three states to sequentially manage the division operation: fetching the inputs, performing the division calculation by shifting and subtracting, and then writing the quotient to the output register. The module also calculates the remainder and accommodates negative divisors by taking their two's complement. The output is a 16-bit quotient that represents the result of the division operation.
module top_module(
    input [15:0] dividend,
    input [15:0] divisor,
    output reg [15:0] quotient
);
