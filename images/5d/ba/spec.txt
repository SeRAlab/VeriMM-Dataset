Design a simple calculator that performs addition and subtraction on two 8-bit input values, `a` and `b`. It uses a clock signal to synchronize operations and includes an active reset feature to clear the result. The operation type is selected via a 2-bit `op` input, with "00" representing addition and "01" representing subtraction. The output is an 8-bit result that updates based on the selected operation when the clock edge occurs and the chip enable (ce) signal is asserted.
module top_module(
    clk,
    reset,
    ce,
    a,
    b,
    op,
    result);
