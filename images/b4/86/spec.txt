Design a flexible arithmetic unit that performs either addition or subtraction on two 8-bit inputs, A and B. The operation is controlled by the ADDSUB signal: when ADDSUB is low (0), the module computes the sum of A and B; when ADDSUB is high (1), it computes the difference (A - B). The result is stored in an 8-bit output register RES.
module top_module(A, B, ADDSUB, RES);
