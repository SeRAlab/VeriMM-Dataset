Design a circuit that performs a four-bit XOR operation on two input vectors (vec1 and vec2) and then propagates the result through a four-stage pipeline. The final output (out_xor) corresponds to the result of the XOR operation after passing through the pipeline stages. Additionally, the module outputs the individual bits of the final XOR result (o4, o3, o2, o1) as separate wires and computes the bitwise AND of vec1 and vec2 to provide an additional output (o0).
module top_module( 
    input wire [3:0] vec1,
    input wire [3:0] vec2,
    output wire [3:0] out_xor,
    output wire o4,
    output wire o3,
    output wire o2,
    output wire o1,
    output wire o0  );
