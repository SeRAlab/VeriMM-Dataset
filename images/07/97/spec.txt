Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:  
000 - Addition  
001 - Subtraction  
010 - Bitwise AND  
011 - Bitwise OR  
100 - Bitwise XOR  
101 - Logical Shift Left  
110 - Logical Shift Right  
111 - Set on Less Than (SLT)  
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs and produce a 32-bit output based on the specified operation.
