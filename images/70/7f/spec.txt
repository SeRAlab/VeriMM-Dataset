Design a control signal generator for a VGA interface. The module, named `vgadumper`, outputs a signal (`vga_dump`) that is asserted high when specific conditions are met: a write operation occurs at a designated address (65599) and the reset signal is not active. The output is cleared on the next clock cycle after being set. Essentially, this module facilitates the triggering of an operation in response to specific memory address accesses for VGA-related functionality.
