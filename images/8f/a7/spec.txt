Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer for 2-bit wide inputs. It selects one of four 2-bit data inputs based on the 2-bit control signal `sel1_sel0`. When `sel1_sel0` is 00, the output is the first 2 bits; when 01, the output is the second 2 bits; when 10, the output is the third 2 bits; and when 11, the output is the fourth 2 bits from the 8-bit concatenated input `d0_d1_d2_d3`.
module top_module(
  input [1:0] sel1_sel0,
  input [3:0] d0_d1_d2_d3,
  output reg [1:0] out
);
