Design a circuit that computes the modulo operation for two 32-bit inputs, dividend and divisor, producing a 32-bit remainder as output. The module first calculates the quotient by dividing the dividend by the divisor, then determines the product of the quotient and the divisor. Finally, it subtracts this product from the dividend to obtain the remainder, effectively implementing the mathematical operation of modulus.
module top_module(
  input [31:0] dividend,
  input [31:0] divisor,
  output reg [31:0] remainder
);
