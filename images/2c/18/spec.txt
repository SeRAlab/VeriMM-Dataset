Design a Verilog module that simulates a simplified SLAM system for a robot. The module should take a sequence of sensor readings that provide distances from obstacles and a control signal to update the robot's estimated position. The robot will maintain its current position and update it based on new readings. The output should be the estimated position of the robot after processing the inputs. The inputs will be a 4-bit distance reading and a control signal. If the control signal is high, the robot updates its position with the distance reading; otherwise, it retains its current position.
