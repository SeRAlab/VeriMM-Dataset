Design an 8-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control input:
1. Addition (000)
2. Subtraction (001)
3. Bitwise AND (010)
4. Bitwise OR (011)
5. Bitwise XOR (100)
The inputs to the ALU are two 8-bit operands (A and B) and a 3-bit control signal (SEL) that selects the operation. The output should be an 8-bit result.
