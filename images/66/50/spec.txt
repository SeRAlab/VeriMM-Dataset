Design a synchronous edge detector module that identifies rising and falling edges of a single input signal (`sig`) based on a clock (`clk`) and an active-low reset (`rst_n`). The module outputs a signal indicating a rising edge (`rise_edge`), a falling edge (`fall_edge`), and a general edge condition (`sig_edge`). The rising edge is detected when `sig` transitions from low to high, while the falling edge is detected when `sig` transitions from high to low. The `sig_edge` output indicates a change in the signal state, highlighting any transitions regardless of direction.
module top_module(
    input clk,rst_n,sig,
    output rise_edge,fall_edge,sig_edge
);
