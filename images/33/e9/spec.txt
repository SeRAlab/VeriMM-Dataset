
Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog, where there are three request lines (req[2:0]) and one grant line (grant[2:0]). The arbiter should grant access based on a fixed priority: req[0] has the highest priority, followed by req[1], and req[2] has the lowest. The arbiter should also have an enable signal (en) that allows it to issue grants only when high. If multiple requests are made at the same time, the arbiter should grant access to the highest priority request.
