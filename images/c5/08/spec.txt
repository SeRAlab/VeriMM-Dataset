- Inputs: 
  - `a`: a 2-bit input 
  - `b`: a 2-bit input 
  - `cin`: a carry-in signal 
  - `clk`: a clock signal 
- Outputs:
  - `sum`: a 2-bit output representing the sum of `a` and `b`
  - `cout`: a carry-out signal 

Your module should be implemented using only combinational logic and should not use any built-in arithmetic operators or functions. You may use any other logical operators or functions available in Verilog. 

Your implementation should be contained within 200 lines of Verilog code.
