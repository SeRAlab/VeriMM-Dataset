
Design an 8-bit shift register with the following specifications:
- The shift register should accept an 8-bit input.
- On each rising edge of the clock, the shift register should shift its contents to the right by one bit.
- The leftmost bit should be filled with a new input bit called `shift_in`.
- The output of the shift register should be available on an 8-bit output wire.
- The shift register should be reset to 0 when the `reset` signal is high.
