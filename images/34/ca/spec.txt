Design a voltage sensor that takes a 5-bit binary input representing voltage levels and outputs a 3-bit binary code indicating whether the voltage is low (0), medium (1), or high (2). The thresholds are defined as follows: 
- Low: 0 to 15 (00000 to 01111)
- Medium: 16 to 31 (10000 to 11111)
- High: 32 to 63 (not represented in 5 bits, thus we can assume it will only handle 0 to 31 for this problem).
