Design a simple DVFS (Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling) controller that adjusts the operating frequency based on the input load. The controller takes a 3-bit load signal as input and produces a 2-bit frequency control output. The frequency output should correspond to the following mapping: 
- 000 - Low Frequency (00)
- 001 - Low Frequency (00)
- 010 - Medium Frequency (01)
- 011 - Medium Frequency (01)
- 100 - High Frequency (10)
- 101 - High Frequency (10)
- 110 - High Frequency (10)
- 111 - High Frequency (10)
