Design a logic circuit that outputs a signal Y based on the parity of four input bits (A1, A2, A3, A4) and a control signal B1. The circuit determines if the number of high inputs among A1 to A4 is even or odd. When B1 is low (0), output Y is asserted high if the number of high inputs is even; when B1 is high (1), output Y is asserted high if the number of high inputs is odd. Thus, the module effectively functions as a parity checker with a selection mechanism.
module top_module(
    Y,
    A1,
    A2,
    A3,
    A4,
    B1
);
