Design a JK latch in Verilog. The JK latch should have inputs J, K, and a clock signal CLK. The output should be Q. The latch should toggle the output Q on the positive edge of the clock based on the values of J and K as follows: if J=K=1, Q toggles; if J=1 and K=0, Q=1; if J=0 and K=1, Q=0; if J=K=0, Q remains unchanged.
