Design a synchronous presettable/resettable D flip-flop. The module operates on the rising edge of the CLK signal and has an asynchronous reset feature. When the ASYNC input is high, the output Q is immediately reset to 0. When SCD (Set Condition) is activated, Q is set to 1, and when SCE (Clear Enable) is activated, Q is reset to 0. In the absence of these control signals, the module captures the input D at each rising edge of CLK. The complementary output Q_N is also provided as the inverse of Q.
module top_module(
    Q    ,
    Q_N  ,
    CLK  ,
    D    ,
    SCD  ,
    SCE  ,
    ASYNC
);
