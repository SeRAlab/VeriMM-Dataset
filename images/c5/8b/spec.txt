

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that takes in 3 inputs and produces 1 output. The output is the logical AND of the inputs that are connected to the module. You must use the following signals in your design:

- `input A`
- `input B`
- `input C`
- `output Y`
- `supply1 VDD`
- `supply0 VSS`

Your module should be named `and_gate`.
