Design a simple multiply-accumulate (MAC) module that computes the product of two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and accumulates the result in a 16-bit accumulator register, acc. On each rising edge of the clock signal (clk), the output Z is updated to the sum of the current accumulator value and the product of A and B, allowing for the accumulation of multiple products over time.
module top_module(input wire clk,input wire [ 7:0] A,input wire [ 7:0] B,output wire [15:0] Z);
