Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog that grants access to one of several requestors based on their priority. The arbiter should have four request lines (req[3:0]), one grant line (grant[3:0]), and a clock signal. The priority order is fixed: req[0] has the highest priority, followed by req[1], req[2], and req[3]. If multiple requests are received, the arbiter should grant access to the highest priority request. The grant line should only indicate one active grant at a time.
