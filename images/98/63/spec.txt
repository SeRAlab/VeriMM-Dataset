Design a 13-bit wide comparator that determines the maximum and minimum values between two 13-bit inputs, A and B. The module operates by comparing the higher bits (bits 5-12) of both inputs, producing outputs Max and Min. If the higher bits of A are less than those of B, Max is assigned the value of B, and Min is assigned the value of A; otherwise, Max receives A and Min receives B. This comparator effectively identifies the larger and smaller of the two inputs based on their most significant bits.
module top_module(
	input [12:0] A, B, 
	output reg [12:0] Max, Min
);
