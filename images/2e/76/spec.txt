
Design a dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS) controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage based on the current system load. The controller takes a 3-bit input representing the load (000 for low load, 111 for high load) and outputs a 2-bit frequency setting (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high) and a 2-bit voltage setting (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high). The controller should have a clock input and a reset input, and the output should change on the rising edge of the clock.
