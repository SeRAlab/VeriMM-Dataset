Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor that uses a saturating counter. The predictor takes a 2-bit input representing the current state (00, 01, 10, 11) and a 1-bit input representing whether the last branch was taken (1) or not taken (0). The output is the next state of the predictor based on the last branch's outcome. The states should transition as follows:  
- 00 -> 00 (not taken)  
- 00 -> 01 (taken)  
- 01 -> 00 (not taken)  
- 01 -> 10 (taken)  
- 10 -> 01 (not taken)  
- 10 -> 11 (taken)  
- 11 -> 10 (not taken)  
- 11 -> 11 (taken)
