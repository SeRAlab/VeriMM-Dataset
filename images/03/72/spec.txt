Design a SPI (Serial Peripheral Interface) controller that operates in master mode. The SPI controller should handle data transmission and reception with the following signals: `clk`, `MOSI` (Master Out Slave In), `MISO` (Master In Slave Out), and `SS` (Slave Select). The controller will take a byte of data as input and send it out via MOSI while simultaneously receiving data on MISO. The controller should also provide an output signal indicating when the transmission is complete.
