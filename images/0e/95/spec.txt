
Design a Verilog module that evaluates a polynomial of the form P(x) = a0 + a1*x + a2*x^2 + a3*x^3, where a0, a1, a2, and a3 are the coefficients of the polynomial. The module should take a 4-bit input `x` and output a 10-bit result `P(x)`. Ensure the design is synthesizable and handles all possible 4-bit input values.
