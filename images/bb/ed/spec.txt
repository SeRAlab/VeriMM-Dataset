Design a control unit for a 3-bit binary multiplier that manages the multiplication process based on specific states. The module takes inputs for starting the process, a clock signal, a completion signal, and the least significant bit status. It operates through four states: "off," "on," "process," and "finish." The control unit initiates the process when the start signal is received and transitions to the "process" state when the least significant bit (LSB) is active. During the "process" state, it signals for shifting and counting up until completion is indicated, after which it transitions to the "finish" state and returns to "off." Outputs include signals to initiate processing, add, shift, and indicate completion.
module top_module(input start,input clk,input cnt_done,input lsb,output start_process,output reg add,output reg shift,output reg count_up,output reg done);
