Design a circuit that determines whether a pixel should be displayed as part of a moon shape in a graphical representation. The output, `moon`, is activated when the `blank` signal is low and the pixel's coordinates, defined by `hcount` and `vcount`, fall within a circular area centered at `(moonX, moonY)` with a specified radius. The radius is determined by the expression involving the square of the differences in coordinates, thereby creating a circular mask to render the moon.
module top_module(blank, hcount, vcount, moonX, moonY, moon);
