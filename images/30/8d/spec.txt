
Design a 16-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit control signal:
- 0000: Addition
- 0001: Subtraction
- 0010: Bitwise AND
- 0011: Bitwise OR
- 0100: Bitwise XOR
- 0101: Logical Shift Left
- 0110: Logical Shift Right
- 0111: Comparison (set to 1 if A < B, else 0)
The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs A and B, and output a 16-bit result.
