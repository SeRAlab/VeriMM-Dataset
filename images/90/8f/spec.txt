Design a four-bit ripple carry adder that computes the sum of two 4-bit binary inputs, A and B, along with a carry-in (Cin). On each rising edge of the clock signal (Clk), the module calculates the sum and stores it in the output register (Sum) while simultaneously determining the carry-out (Cout) based on the most significant bits and the carry-in. The results are made available at the outputs Sum and Cout for subsequent operations.
