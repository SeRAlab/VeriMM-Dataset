Implement a module to debounce a pushbutton input. In digital systems, mechanical switches or pushbuttons often generate spurious electrical noise (bouncing) when toggled. This noise can cause multiple unwanted transitions to be registered by the digital system, leading to erratic behavior. The `mPshBtnDbnce` module addresses this issue by ensuring that only a clean, stable transition is registered after a button press or release, effectively filtering out the noise generated during the mechanical action.

The module achieves this by implementing a simple debounce algorithm, which waits for the pushbutton signal to remain stable for a predefined period before considering the button press or release as valid. This module is crucial in systems where pushbuttons are used for input, ensuring reliable operation by mitigating the effects of signal bouncing.
