Design a clock gating circuit that produces an enable clock output (ENCLK) based on the state of the enable (EN) and test enable (TE) signals. When TE is asserted, ENCLK follows the input clock (CLK). If TE is not asserted but EN is active, ENCLK again follows CLK. When both TE and EN are inactive, ENCLK is driven low. This design helps to manage power consumption by gating the clock signal when not needed.
module top_module(input CLK, EN, TE, output reg ENCLK);
