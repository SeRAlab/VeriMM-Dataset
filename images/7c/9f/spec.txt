Design a 4-bit bi-directional rotate register with additional output signals for the most significant bit (MSB) and least significant bit (LSB). The module accepts a 4-bit input data, a 2-bit selector (sel), a clock signal, and an active-low reset signal. On each rising edge of the clock, if reset is low, the register outputs are cleared to zero. Otherwise, the functionality depends on the value of sel: it retains its current value when sel is 0, rotates its bits to the left when sel is 1, rotates its bits to the right when sel is 2, or loads new data from the input when sel is 3. The MSBout and LSBout outputs provide instantaneous access to the MSB and LSB of the register content, respectively.
