Design a 16-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit opcode: 
- 0000: Addition
- 0001: Subtraction
- 0010: Bitwise AND
- 0011: Bitwise OR
- 0100: Bitwise XOR
- 0101: Bitwise NOT (only on first operand)
- 0110: Logical Shift Left
- 0111: Logical Shift Right
The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs and output a 16-bit result, along with a 1-bit carry-out for addition and subtraction operations. The ALU should also include a zero flag that indicates whether the result is zero.
