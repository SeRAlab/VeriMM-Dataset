Design a 1ms timer using a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) that generates a pulse output. The module is activated by a clock signal and controlled by a reset and enable input. When enabled, the LFSR shifts its bits based on feedback from specific taps, and upon reaching a predefined count of 50,000 clock cycles, it outputs a high signal for 1ms before resetting the LFSR to its initial state. If the enable signal is low or the reset is activated, the LFSR is initialized and the output is set to low.
