Design a 4-bit shift register with parallel load capability. The shift register should have the following inputs: a 4-bit data input `data_in`, a clock signal `clk`, a load signal `load`, and a shift signal `shift`. When `load` is high, the `data_in` should be loaded into the register on the rising edge of the clock. When `shift` is high, the contents of the register should shift to the right on the rising edge of the clock. The output should be a 4-bit signal `data_out` that reflects the current state of the shift register.
