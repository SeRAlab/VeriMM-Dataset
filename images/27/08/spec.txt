
Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog for 4 request lines (req0, req1, req2, req3) and a single grant line (grant). The arbiter should grant the line with the highest priority that has requested access. The priorities are defined as follows: req0 has the highest priority, followed by req1, req2, and req3. The arbiter should also have a clock and a reset signal. On reset, no grants should be issued, and the arbiter should return to its idle state.
