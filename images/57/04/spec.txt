Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 32-bit wide input signals (q1, q2, q3, q4) based on a 2-bit selection input (reg_no). The output (regData) will be the selected input according to the value of reg_no, where reg_no = 00 corresponds to q1, 01 corresponds to q2, 10 corresponds to q3, and 11 corresponds to q4.
module top_module(output [31:0] regData,input [31:0] q1,input [31:0] q2, input [31:0] q3, input [31:0] q4,input [1:0] reg_no);
