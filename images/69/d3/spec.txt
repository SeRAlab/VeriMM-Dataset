Please act as a professional verilog designer.

Design a Verilog module for a 3-bit up/down counter with enable and reset. The counter increments or decrements based on a control signal and resets to zero on the reset signal.

Module name:  
    counter_3bit  

Input ports:
    clk: Chip clock signal.
    rst: Reset signal.
    en: Enable signal.
    up_down: Control signal to select increment (1) or decrement (0).

Output ports:
    count: 3-bit output count.

Implementation:
Counter operation:
The counter increments by 1 when up_down is 1 and decrements by 1 when up_down is 0.
The counter resets to zero when the reset (rst) signal is active.
