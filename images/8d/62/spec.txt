Design a Verilog module based on the following description. Assume signals are positive clock/clk edge triggered unless otherwise stated.
Create a circuit that takes an input signal m and generates an output signal n. The input m is connected to an XOR gate, a NAND gate, and a NOR gate. Each gate's output is connected to a D flip-flop. The outputs of the flip-flops are then fed into a three-input NOR gate to produce the output n. The second input of the NAND gate is its corresponding flip-flop's output, the second input of the XOR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output, and the second input of the NOR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output.
