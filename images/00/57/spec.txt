
Design a Verilog module for performing point addition on an elliptic curve over a finite field. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) as inputs and output the resultant point (x3, y3) according to the elliptic curve equation. Assume the curve is defined over a prime field and the prime number is predefined in the module. You need to handle the cases where points are the same or one of the points is the identity point (infinity).
