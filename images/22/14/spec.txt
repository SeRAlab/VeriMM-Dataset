Design a finite state machine (FSM) that operates as a simple RRT (Rapidly-exploring Random Tree) node generator. The FSM will have three states: IDLE, EXPLORE, and CONNECT. The node will be generated based on a random input signal when in EXPLORE state. The state transitions are as follows: from IDLE to EXPLORE on a start signal, from EXPLORE to CONNECT on a generated node signal, and back to IDLE on a done signal.
