Design a 5-bit linear voltage regulator FSM that outputs a regulated voltage based on a 5-bit input voltage level. The FSM should maintain the output voltage at a desired level while adjusting the output based on input voltage changes. The states will be as follows: 0 - Low, 1 - Below Nominal, 2 - Nominal, 3 - Above Nominal, 4 - High. The output will be a 5-bit regulated voltage level corresponding to the current state of the FSM.
