Design a module that generates power supply signals for digital circuits. On each positive edge of the input clock, it outputs a logic high (1) for VPWR and VPB, and a logic low (0) for VGND and VNB, effectively providing the necessary power and ground references for the operation of the circuit.
module top_module(
    input wire clk,
    output reg VPWR,
    output reg VGND,
    output reg VPB,
    output reg VNB
);
