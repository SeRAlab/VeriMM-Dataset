Design a 2x2 multiplier module that computes the product of two 2-bit inputs, A and B. The module captures inputs A and B on the rising edge of a clock signal and produces two outputs: P and Q. Output P represents the product of A and the least significant bit of B, while output Q is the product of A and the entire B value. The results are stored in registers and made available on the output. This module serves as a fundamental building block for more complex arithmetic functions in digital circuits.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input [1:0] A,
    input [1:0] B,
    output reg [3:0] P,
    output reg [3:0] Q
);
