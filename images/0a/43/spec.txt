
Design a simple SATA controller state machine that transitions between three states: IDLE, ACTIVE, and ERROR. The controller should transition from IDLE to ACTIVE on a signal 'start', from ACTIVE to IDLE on a signal 'stop', and from ACTIVE to ERROR on a signal 'error'. The state should be output as a 2-bit binary value representing the current state (00 for IDLE, 01 for ACTIVE, 10 for ERROR).
