
Design a DES (Data Encryption Standard) encryption module in Verilog. The module should accept a 64-bit plaintext input and a 56-bit key input, and produce a 64-bit ciphertext output after completing the DES algorithm. Implement a simplified version of DES that only performs the initial permutation and one round of Feistel function. The Feistel function should use a simple XOR operation with a portion of the key.
