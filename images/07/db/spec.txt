Design a 4-bit Serial-In Serial-Out (SISO) shift register with a clock and reset input. The shift register should accept serial input data on a 'data_in' line and shift the bits through the register on each clock cycle. The output should be a 4-bit parallel output 'data_out' that represents the contents of the shift register after each shift operation. Ensure that upon reset, the shift register is initialized to zero.
