Design a Verilog module that computes the dot product of two 5-bit vectors. The vectors will be provided as inputs, and the output will be an 11-bit result (since the maximum value of the dot product of two 5-bit vectors is 31 * 31 = 961, which requires 10 bits plus 1 for the sign). The two 5-bit vectors will be named `vec_a` and `vec_b`, and the output should be named `dot_product`. The module should also have a valid signal to indicate when the output is valid.
