Design a 2-channel DMA controller that can transfer data from a source address to a destination address. The controller should have the following inputs: clk, rst (reset), req1 (request for channel 1), req2 (request for channel 2), src_addr1 (source address for channel 1), src_addr2 (source address for channel 2), dest_addr1 (destination address for channel 1), and dest_addr2 (destination address for channel 2). The outputs should indicate when a transfer is active for each channel (active1, active2) and the data being transferred (data1, data2). The controller should initiate transfers based on the request signals and should complete transfers after a certain number of clock cycles, indicated by a transfer_done signal.
