Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: ADD
- 001: SUBTRACT
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
- 101: NOT (only on the first input)
- 110: NAND
- 111: NOR

The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs and output the 8-bit result. The ALU should also include a carry output for the ADD and SUBTRACT operations.
