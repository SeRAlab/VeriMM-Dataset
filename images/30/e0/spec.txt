
Design a 2-bit shift register in Verilog. The shift register should have two inputs: `shift` (to control the shifting operation) and `data_in` (the data to be shifted in). The shift register should shift its contents to the right on the rising edge of the clock when `shift` is high and should load `data_in` when `shift` is low. The output should be a 2-bit value representing the current contents of the shift register.
