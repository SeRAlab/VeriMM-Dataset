Design a simple Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) filter in Verilog that computes the output as a weighted sum of current and previous inputs and outputs. The filter should have a single-input and single-output interface with a 16-bit data width. Implement the following recursive formula for the IIR filter:  
\[ y[n] = a \cdot x[n] + b \cdot y[n-1] \]  
where \( a \) and \( b \) are the filter coefficients. Use a reset signal to initialize the output. The coefficients \( a \) and \( b \) will be parameters to the module.
