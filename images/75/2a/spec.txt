Design a 4-bit adder/subtractor circuit that allows for the addition or subtraction of two 4-bit inputs, A and B, based on a control signal, Sub. When Sub is low, the module performs addition, and when Sub is high, it performs subtraction by utilizing the 2's complement method. The module also includes a carry input, Cin, and generates a carry-out signal, Cout, indicating overflow in the case of addition. The output, S, represents the final 4-bit result of the operation.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A, B,
    input Cin, Sub,
    output [3:0] S,
    output Cout
);
