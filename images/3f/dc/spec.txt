
Design a temperature-based shutdown controller in Verilog. The controller should monitor the temperature and trigger a shutdown signal if the temperature exceeds a predetermined threshold. The system will have the following inputs:
- clk: Clock signal
- rst: Reset signal
- temp: 8-bit temperature input

The output will be a shutdown signal (shutdown) that goes high when the temperature exceeds 75 degrees Celsius. The shutdown signal should remain high until the system is reset.
