Design a 16-bit cyclical redundancy check (CRC) generator module. The module takes a single bit input (BITVAL) and operates on a 16-bit internal register (CRC), which is updated on the rising edge of the BITSTRB signal. When the CLEAR input is activated, the CRC register resets to zero. The ENABLE signal controls the updating process: when ENABLE is high, the register shifts its bits to the right, with certain bits being modified based on the current input (BITVAL) and the most significant bit of the CRC register, facilitating error detection in transmitted data.
