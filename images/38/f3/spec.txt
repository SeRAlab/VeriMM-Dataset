Design a 4-bit Restoring Divider in Verilog. The divider should take two 4-bit unsigned inputs: a dividend (D) and a divisor (S), and produce a 4-bit quotient (Q) and a 4-bit remainder (R). The division operation should be performed in a restoring manner, which means that for each bit of the quotient, the circuit will check if the current partial remainder is enough to subtract the divisor.
