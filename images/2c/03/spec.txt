
Create a Verilog module for a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller should take an input signal that indicates the workload level (0-3, where 0 is low and 3 is high) and adjust the output voltage level and frequency accordingly. The controller should have an output of 2 bits for voltage level (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high) and a 2 bits for frequency level (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high). The mapping should be as follows:

- Workload 0: Voltage = 00, Frequency = 00
- Workload 1: Voltage = 01, Frequency = 01
- Workload 2: Voltage = 10, Frequency = 10
- Workload 3: Voltage = 10, Frequency = 01
