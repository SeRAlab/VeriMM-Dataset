The module extracts the lower and upper 8 bits of the 16-bit input. It then employs a 3-bit shift register to obtain the most significant bit from the shifted value. The final output is an 8-bit result derived from an XOR operation between the lower byte, the upper byte, and the most significant bit of the shift register.
