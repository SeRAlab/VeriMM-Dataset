Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (in0, in1, in2, in3) based on a 2-bit selection signal (sel1, sel0). The module outputs the selected input signal on 'out', with sel1 and sel0 determining which input is passed through: when sel1=0 and sel0=0, in0 is selected; when sel1=0 and sel0=1, in1 is selected; when sel1=1 and sel0=0, in2 is selected; and when sel1=1 and sel0=1, in3 is selected.
module top_module(
    in0,
    in1,
    in2,
    in3,
    sel1,
    sel0,
    out
);
