Design a Verilog module that implements a simple RSA encryption process. The module should take a plaintext input, a public exponent, and a modulus as inputs and produce the ciphertext output. The encryption operation is defined as ciphertext = (plaintext^public_exponent) mod modulus. Ensure that the inputs are suitably sized for typical RSA parameters (e.g., 16 bits for plaintext, 8 bits for the public exponent, and 16 bits for the modulus).
