Design a forwarding logic module that resolves data hazards in a pipelined processor architecture. This module takes control signals indicating whether write operations occurred in memory (regwrite_mem) or write-back (regwrite_wb) stages and identifies the destination registers (rd_mem and rd_wb). Based on these inputs and the source registers (rs and rt), it determines the appropriate forwarding paths by outputting two 2-bit signals: `forwards` for the `rs` register and `forwardt` for the `rt` register. The output signals indicate whether to forward data from memory or the write-back stage to prevent delays caused by read-after-write hazards, enhancing the overall efficiency of instruction execution.
