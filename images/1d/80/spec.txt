
Design a Bluetooth state machine that manages the connection states of a Bluetooth module. The states are:
- IDLE (0): No connection
- CONNECTING (1): Attempting to connect
- CONNECTED (2): Successfully connected
- DISCONNECTED (3): Connection lost

The state transitions are as follows:
- From IDLE to CONNECTING on a 'start' signal.
- From CONNECTING to CONNECTED on a 'ack' signal.
- From CONNECTED to DISCONNECTED on a 'drop' signal.
- From DISCONNECTED to IDLE on a 'reset' signal.
- The state machine should remain in the current state if no valid signal is received.
