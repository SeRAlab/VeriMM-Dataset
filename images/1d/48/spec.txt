Design a simple audio mixer in Verilog that takes two audio signals (A and B) and mixes them to produce a single output signal (Y). The mixing process is defined as Y = (A + B) / 2. The audio signals A and B should be 8-bit wide and the output Y should also be 8 bits. The design should accommodate a clock signal for the mixing operation and have an enable signal to control when the mixing occurs. When the enable signal is low, the output should retain its previous value.
