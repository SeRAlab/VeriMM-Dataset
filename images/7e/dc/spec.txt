The `count` output is a two-bit wide register that initializes to zero and counts the number of positive edges detected on each of the individual clock inputs (`clkvec[0]` and `clkvec[1]`). Each instance of the counter operates independently, thus allowing for asynchronous counting based on the given clock signals.
