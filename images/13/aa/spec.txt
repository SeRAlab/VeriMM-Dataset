Design a Parallel-in Parallel-out (PIPO) shift register in Verilog. The shift register should have a 5-bit input data line, a load signal to load data into the register, and a clock signal to shift or load data. On the rising edge of the clock, if the load signal is high, the data should be loaded into the register. If the load signal is low, the register should maintain its current state until the next load or clock signal.
