
Design a Serial-in Serial-out (SISO) shift register in Verilog. The shift register should have the following characteristics:
- It has a width of 5 bits.
- It takes a serial input data bit and shifts it into the register on every positive edge of the clock.
- It outputs the contents of the register serially, one bit at a time, on every positive edge of the clock.
- There should be a reset input that sets the shift register to a known state (all bits 0) when activated.
