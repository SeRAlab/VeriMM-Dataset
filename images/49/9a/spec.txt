Design a 4-bit up/down counter that counts up or down based on the control signal. The counter increments its value when the up_down signal is low and decrements when the signal is high. Upon receiving a reset signal, the counter outputs are initialized to zero. The counter operates on the rising edge of a clock signal.
module top_module(
  input clk,
  input reset,
  input up_down,
  output reg [3:0] q
);
