Design a register module that manages two separate 32-bit wide registers, Hi and Lo, controlled by write signals and selected based on ALU operations. The module captures the current values of Hi and Lo when their respective write enables (HiWrite and LoWrite) are asserted. It outputs data based on three conditions: when ALUOut is active, it outputs ALUResult; when ALUControl signals a specific operation (010000), it outputs the value from the Hi register; and when another operation (011111) is signaled, it outputs from the Lo register. This design supports flexible data handling in a computational unit, such as an ALU or a processor's register file.
