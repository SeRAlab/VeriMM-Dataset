Design a BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) to 7-segment display decoder with additional control features. The module takes a 4-bit binary input representing a BCD digit and outputs the corresponding signals to drive a 7-segment display, indicating numbers 0 through 9 as well as an "F" for error indication. The design includes control signals to enable or disable the output based on the values of the control inputs, such as `nLT` for a "less than" condition, `nRBI` for reset, and `nBI` for blanking the display. When activated, it produces segments according to the input BCD value while ensuring the display remains off or shows a blank when certain conditions are met.
