
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Black-Scholes option pricing formula. The module should take in the following inputs:
- `S` (current stock price)
- `K` (strike price)
- `T` (time to expiration in years)
- `r` (risk-free interest rate)
- `σ` (volatility)

It should output the calculated option price for a European call option. For simplicity, assume all inputs are given in fixed-point format (integer for S, K, and T; fixed-point representation for r and σ). 

The output should be a fixed-point representation as well.
