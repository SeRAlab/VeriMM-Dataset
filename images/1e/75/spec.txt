Design a hazard detection circuit that detects data hazards in a pipeline architecture. The circuit should take two input signals representing instruction opcodes and an output signal indicating the presence of a hazard. If a hazard is detected, the output should be high (1), otherwise low (0). The opcodes are represented by 3-bit binary numbers. Assume the following conditions for hazards:
1. A hazard occurs when both opcodes are the same.
2. A hazard occurs when one opcode represents a load operation and the other represents a store operation.
Load operation opcode: 3'b010, Store operation opcode: 3'b011.
