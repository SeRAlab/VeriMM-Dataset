Design an 8-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control input: 
000 - AND 
001 - OR 
010 - XOR 
011 - ADD 
100 - SUBTRACT 
101 - SLT (Set Less Than) 
110 - NOR 
111 - NAND. 
The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs and output an 8-bit result. The output should be zero if the operation is not valid.
