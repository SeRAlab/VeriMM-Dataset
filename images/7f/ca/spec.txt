Additionally, the circuit incorporates a multiplexer that chooses between the computed sum and a zero output based on the COUT signal, ensuring SUM always remains within the 4-bit range. The module also includes power supply pins (VDD and VSS) for operational integrity.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] A,
  input [3:0] B,
  input CIN,
  output [3:0] SUM,
  output COUT,
  input VDD,
