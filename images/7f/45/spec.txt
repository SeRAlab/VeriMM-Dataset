Design a 4-bit synchronous register similar to the 74HC161. It captures the input data `cpu_d` on the rising edge of the `Ncpu_romsel` signal when `cpu_rw` is LOW (indicating a write operation). The stored data is then available at the outputs `hc161_out0` to `hc161_out3`, representing each of the 4 bits of the register. This module serves to temporarily hold data for a CPU-like architecture where control signals determine data flow into the register.

							input  wire [3:0] cpu_d
							, input  wire cpu_rw
							, input  wire Ncpu_romsel
							, output wire hc161_out0
							, output wire hc161_out1
							, output wire hc161_out2
							, output wire hc161_out3
							);
