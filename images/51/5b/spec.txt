Design a synchronous 4-bit binary counter with load capability. The counter increments on the rising edge of the clock (CLK), resets to zero when the reset (RST) signal is high, and loads a specified 4-bit value (D) when the load (LOAD) signal is asserted. The current count value is available at the output (Q).
