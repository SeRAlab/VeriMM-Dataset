Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that supports basic operations such as addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, bitwise OR, and bitwise XOR. The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B), a 3-bit control signal (ALU_op) to select the operation, and output the 32-bit result. The control signal should specify the following operations: 
000 - A + B 
001 - A - B 
010 - A & B 
011 - A | B 
100 - A ^ B
