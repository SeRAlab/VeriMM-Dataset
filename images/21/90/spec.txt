
Design a power gating circuit that has two inputs: 
1. `power_enable` (1 bit) - controls whether the power is enabled or not.
2. `data_input` (5 bits) - data input that should be passed through when power is enabled.

The output `data_output` (5 bits) should reflect `data_input` when `power_enable` is high, otherwise it should output all zeros.
