Design an 8-bit DRAM cell that can store a single bit of data. The cell should have two inputs: `data_in` (1-bit) and `write_enable` (1-bit), and one output: `data_out` (1-bit). When `write_enable` is high, the value of `data_in` should be stored in the cell. When `write_enable` is low, the cell should hold its previous state. The design should also include a read operation that outputs the stored value continuously while `write_enable` is low.
