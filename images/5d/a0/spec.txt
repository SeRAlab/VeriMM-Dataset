Implement a 3-input AND-OR-Invert (AOI) logic gate. The circuit produces an output Y that is the result of a logical OR operation between the AND outputs of two sets of inputs (A1 AND A2, B1 AND B2), which is then ANDed with a separate input C1. This design serves to create a compact function that integrates combination logic, commonly used in digital circuits for decision-making processes.
