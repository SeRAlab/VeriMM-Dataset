Design a simple OFDM modulator that takes a stream of bits and generates OFDM symbols. The OFDM modulator should consist of a serial-to-parallel converter, an Inverse Fast Fourier Transform (IFFT) block, and a cyclic prefix addition block. The input bit stream will be divided into groups of N bits, where N is the number of subcarriers. The output will be a series of OFDM symbols. Assume N=4 for this problem.
