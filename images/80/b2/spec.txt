If the reset input (R) is activated (HIGH), the flip-flop initializes the output to LOW (Q) and HIGH (Qn) and enters a reset state for a specified delay period before normal operation resumes. The reset behavior is managed by a simple counter that enforces the delay before allowing toggling based on the T input.
