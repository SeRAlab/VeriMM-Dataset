Design a one-bit full adder circuit. It takes three inputs: in1 and in2 represent the two bits to be added, and in3 serves as a carry-in from a previous stage. The circuit produces two outputs: sum, which is the result of adding all three inputs, and carry, which represents an overflow condition. The carry output is high if either both input bits are high or if one of the input bits is high along with the carry-in.
module top_module(in1, in2, in3, sum, carry);
