Design a 64-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations: addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, bitwise OR, and bitwise XOR. The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs and a 3-bit opcode to select the operation. The output should be a 64-bit result. The opcode is defined as follows:  
000 - ADD   
001 - SUB   
010 - AND   
011 - OR   
100 - XOR
