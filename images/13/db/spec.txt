Design a simple SATA controller state machine that manages the connection and data transfer states. The controller will have the following states: IDLE, CONNECTED, SENDING, and DISCONNECTED. The state transitions will be triggered by the following inputs: 'connect' (to transition from IDLE to CONNECTED), 'send' (to transition from CONNECTED to SENDING), and 'disconnect' (to transition back to IDLE from any state). The output will indicate the current state of the controller.
