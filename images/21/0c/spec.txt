Implement a finite state machine (FSM) for the point addition operation on an elliptic curve over a finite field. The FSM should take inputs for two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2), and output the resulting point (x3, y3) after performing the addition on the elliptic curve defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b. The coefficients a and b are fixed parameters.
