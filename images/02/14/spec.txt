Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature readings. The system should have three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The fan should operate in the following way: if the temperature is below 20 degrees, the fan should be off; if the temperature is between 20 and 30 degrees, the fan should operate at low speed; if the temperature is between 30 and 40 degrees, the fan should operate at medium speed; and if the temperature is above 40 degrees, the fan should operate at high speed. The state should change only on the rising edge of a clock signal.
