Design a circuit that serves as a power supply control logic. When the input voltage VPWR is high (1), the outputs VGND and VNB are set to low (0), while VPB is set high (1), indicating the active power state. Conversely, when VPWR is low (0), the outputs are inverted: VGND goes high (1), VPB goes low (0), and VNB goes high (1), indicating a standby or inactive state. This module effectively regulates ground and bias voltages based on the supply voltage status.
module top_module(
    input wire VPWR,
    output reg VGND,
    output reg VPB,
    output reg VNB
);
