

You have been tasked with designing a module that takes in two input signals, `i1` and `i0`, and combines them into a single 8-bit output signal, `o`. The `i1` signal is a 6-bit unsigned integer, and `i0` is a 2-bit unsigned integer. The `o` signal should be formed by concatenating `i1` and `i0` in that order, resulting in an 8-bit unsigned integer.

Design a Verilog module named `concat_module` that takes in the `i1` and `i0` signals and outputs the `o` signal. Your module should be self-contained and should not require any external modules or functions. Your module should be no more than 200 lines of code.
