Design a simple Low Dropout Regulator (LDO) circuit in Verilog that takes a control signal and two voltage inputs (Vin, Vout). The output Vout should be regulated based on the input conditions. The LDO should output a regulated voltage of Vout = Vin - Vdrop, where Vdrop = 0.5V if Vin > 0.5V, otherwise Vout = Vin.
