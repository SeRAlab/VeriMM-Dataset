Design a simple branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should take a 1-bit input representing the actual outcome of the branch (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and a clock signal. The predictor outputs a 1-bit prediction of whether the branch will be taken based on the current state of the counter. The counter has four states: 00 (strongly not taken), 01 (weakly not taken), 10 (weakly taken), and 11 (strongly taken). The counter increments or decrements based on the actual outcome.
