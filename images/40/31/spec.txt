
Design a Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) controller in Verilog. The PID controller should take a 10-bit input (error), a clock signal, and a reset signal. The PID controller will output a 16-bit control signal. The proportional, integral, and derivative gains are predefined constants. The output is calculated based on the formula:
output = (Kp * error) + (Ki * integral) + (Kd * derivative)
where:
- Kp, Ki, and Kd are the proportional, integral, and derivative gains respectively.
- The integral is the accumulation of error over time.
- The derivative is the difference between the current error and the previous error.
