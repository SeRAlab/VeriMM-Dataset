Design a simple 8-bit arithmetic calculator capable of performing addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division based on a 2-bit operation selection input. When `op` is 00, the module adds inputs A and B; when 01, it subtracts B from A; when 10, it multiplies A and B, taking the lower 8 bits of the result; and when 11, it divides A by B, outputting zero in the case of division by zero. The result is provided as an 8-bit output.
module top_module(
    A,
    B,
    op,
    result
);
