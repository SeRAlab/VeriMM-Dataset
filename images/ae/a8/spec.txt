Design a 10-bit counter module with reset and increment capabilities. The module increments the score by 10 with a pulse on the 'plus' input and resets the score to zero when the 'clr' input is activated. The current score and high score are stored as 10-bit values and are displayed as separate 4-bit outputs for each decimal digit, splitting both the score and high score into digit outputs (score0 to score3 and high_score0 to high_score3) corresponding to their respective decimal values. The module uses two-bit filters to debounce the input signals, ensuring reliable operation during state changes.
module top_module(input wire clk, clr,input wire plus,output wire [3:0] score0, score1, score2, score3, high_score0, high_score1, high_score2, high_score3);
