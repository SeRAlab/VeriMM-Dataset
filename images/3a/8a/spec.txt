Design a Verilog module that simulates a simple Bluetooth signal strength decoder. The module takes a 5-bit input representing the received signal strength and outputs a 3-bit value indicating the corresponding signal quality as follows:
- Signal strength 0-7: Quality 0 (Poor)
- Signal strength 8-15: Quality 1 (Fair)
- Signal strength 16-23: Quality 2 (Good)
- Signal strength 24-31: Quality 3 (Excellent)
