
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple ECC point addition operation on the elliptic curve defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b over a finite field. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) as inputs and produce the resulting point (x3, y3) as output. The coefficients a and b are also given as inputs. The operation should be performed in a clocked manner and the output should be valid after a clock cycle.
