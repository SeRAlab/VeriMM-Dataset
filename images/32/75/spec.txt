Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a processing unit based on the workload. The controller takes an input signal indicating the workload level (0-3), and based on this input, it sets the corresponding frequency and voltage levels. The mapping is as follows:
- Workload 0: Frequency = 1 GHz, Voltage = 1.0 V
- Workload 1: Frequency = 1.5 GHz, Voltage = 1.2 V
- Workload 2: Frequency = 2.0 GHz, Voltage = 1.4 V
- Workload 3: Frequency = 2.5 GHz, Voltage = 1.6 V

The output should be two 4-bit signals: freq_out and volt_out, representing the frequency and voltage levels, respectively. The state should change on the rising edge of a clock signal.
