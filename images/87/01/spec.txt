Design a 4-bit barrel shifter that performs left and right shifts based on a 2-bit control signal. The control signal directs the operation: a control value of 00 shifts the input left by 1 bit, 01 shifts it left by 2 bits, 10 shifts it right by 1 bit, and 11 shifts it right by 2 bits. The output reflects the transformed bits after the specified shift operation.

    input [1:0] control,
