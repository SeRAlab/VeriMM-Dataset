Design a simple FIFO (First-In-First-Out) queue with a depth of 4. The FIFO should have the following inputs and outputs: `clk`, `reset`, `data_in`, `write_enable`, `read_enable`, and `data_out`. When `write_enable` is high, the `data_in` should be pushed into the queue. When `read_enable` is high, the oldest data in the queue should be output on `data_out`. The FIFO should handle overflow and underflow conditions appropriately.
