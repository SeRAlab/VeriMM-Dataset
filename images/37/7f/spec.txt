Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) with a digital output that samples an input analog signal. The ADC will have a clock input to sample the signal and an enable signal to control when the sampling occurs. The output will be a 16-bit digital representation of the sampled analog signal. The input analog signal will be given in a 16-bit format, and on each clock cycle when the enable signal is high, the ADC will latch the input data into its output register.
