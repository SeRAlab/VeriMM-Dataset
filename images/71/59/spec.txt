Develop a D-type flip-flop with complementary outputs (Q and Qbar). This flip-flop captures the value of the data input (D) on the rising edge of the clock signal. The output Q reflects the state of the data input when the clock is high, while Qbar provides the inverse of Q. The design utilizes NAND gates and not gates to form the master-slave flip-flop configuration, ensuring proper synchronization and output stability.
