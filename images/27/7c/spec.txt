Design a simple I2C controller module that can initiate a start condition, send a byte of data, and then initiate a stop condition. The controller should have the following inputs: `clk` (clock), `rst` (reset), `start` (to begin transmission), `data_in` (8-bit data to be sent), and outputs: `sda` (data line), `scl` (clock line), and `done` (indicates completion of transmission).
