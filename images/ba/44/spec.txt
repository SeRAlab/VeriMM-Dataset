Design a control module for an elevator system that responds to floor requests. The module tracks the current floor and manages the elevator's state based on incoming requests. It has an input for the requested floor and generates outputs to control the door state, movement direction (up or down), and a stop indicator. When the elevator is reset, it initializes to the ground floor, opening the door and stopping any movement. Depending on the requested floor, it moves the elevator either up or down, updates the current floor, and signals when the door should open, ensuring that the elevator operates smoothly and intuitively in response to user requests.
