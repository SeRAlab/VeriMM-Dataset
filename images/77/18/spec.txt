Design a JK flip-flop that provides synchronous toggling functionality. The flip-flop changes its output state (Q) on the rising edge of the clock signal (CP) based on the inputs J and K: if J=0 and K=0, the output remains unchanged; if J=0 and K=1, the output resets to 0; if J=1 and K=0, the output sets to 1; and if both J and K are 1, the output toggles its current state. The complementary output (Qnot) is also generated as the inverse of Q.
