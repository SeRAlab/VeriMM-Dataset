
Design a 5-tap Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filter in Verilog. The filter should take a 5-bit input sample and produce a 5-bit output based on fixed coefficients. The coefficients for the FIR filter are to be defined as follows:
- Coefficient 0: 1
- Coefficient 1: 2
- Coefficient 2: 3
- Coefficient 3: 2
- Coefficient 4: 1
The output should be the sum of the products of the coefficients and the input samples.
