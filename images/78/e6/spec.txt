Design a combinational circuit that generates two outputs, HI and LO, based on the states of two input signals, pullup0 and pulldown0. The HI output is asserted (logic HIGH) when pullup0 is HIGH and pulldown0 is LOW. Conversely, the LO output is asserted (logic LOW) when pullup0 is LOW and pulldown0 is HIGH. This design effectively implements a simple control mechanism that uses the states of the input signals to determine the values of the outputs.
module top_module(
    input pullup0,
    input pulldown0,
    output HI,
    output LO
);
