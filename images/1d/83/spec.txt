Design a simple DVFS controller that adjusts the frequency based on the operational voltage level. The controller will have a `voltage` input (3 bits) that signifies different voltage levels, which in turn determine the `frequency` output (3 bits). The mapping is as follows: Voltage levels (000) to (111) correspond to Frequency levels (000) to (111) directly, but if the voltage is below a certain threshold (e.g., (011)), the frequency should be reduced (shifted down by one). The output must remain stable until the voltage changes.
