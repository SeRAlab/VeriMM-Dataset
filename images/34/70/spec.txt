
Design a 2nd order Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) filter using Verilog. The filter will take a 16-bit signed input sample and output a 16-bit signed sample. The filter coefficients will be hardcoded as follows: b0 = 1, b1 = 2, b2 = 1 for the feedforward part and a1 = 1, a2 = -1 for the feedback part. The filter will operate at a clock frequency and should maintain the previous states of the output.
