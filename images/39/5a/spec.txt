Design a Reset Controller that manages a module's reset state based on an input signal 'reset_n'. The reset controller should have the following behavior: When 'reset_n' is low (0), the output 'reset' should be high (1) and when 'reset_n' is high (1), the output 'reset' should follow the 'enable' signal. The output 'reset' should remain high for two clock cycles after 'reset_n' goes high.
