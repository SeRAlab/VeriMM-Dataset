
Design a 1-bit SRAM cell in Verilog. The SRAM cell should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Inputs: `data_in` (1-bit), `write_enable` (1-bit), `clk` (1-bit)
- Outputs: `data_out` (1-bit)

The SRAM cell should store the `data_in` value when `write_enable` is high and maintain the stored value when `write_enable` is low.
