
Create a Verilog module for a simple WiFi signal strength indicator. The module takes an input signal representing the WiFi signal strength (in dBm) and outputs a 3-bit binary value indicating the strength category: 
000 for very weak (less than -80 dBm), 
001 for weak (-80 to -70 dBm), 
010 for moderate (-70 to -60 dBm), 
011 for strong (-60 to -50 dBm), 
100 for very strong (-50 dBm or higher).
