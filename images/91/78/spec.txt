Design a simulation module that evaluates conditional macros based on predefined directives. The module initializes an integer variable and incrementally modifies its value through a series of nested conditional compilation checks, influenced by the presence of the defined macros `DEFINED` and `NEW_MACRO`. After executing the conditions, it verifies if the resulting value of the integer `i` equals 4, displaying a message indicating the test's success or failure. The overall design purpose is to demonstrate and test macro definitions, conditional compilation, and variable manipulation in a Verilog simulation context.
