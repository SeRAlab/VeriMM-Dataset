Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog that grants access to one of four requestors (REQ0, REQ1, REQ2, REQ3) based on fixed priority. The highest priority is assigned to REQ0 and the lowest to REQ3. The arbiter should output GRANT, indicating which requestor has been granted access. If multiple requests are active, the arbiter should grant access to the highest-priority requestor. The grant should remain active until the respective requestor deactivates their request.
