Design a simple SPI master controller that can send data to an SPI slave device. The master will have a clock signal (SCK), a chip select (CS), and a data line (MOSI). The master should be able to send a 8-bit data word on the rising edge of the clock signal while keeping CS low. The master should also have an input to receive the clock enable signal (SCK_EN) that allows the clock to be generated when high. The data is sent out on the MOSI line, and the CS line should be driven low when sending data and high after the transmission is complete.
