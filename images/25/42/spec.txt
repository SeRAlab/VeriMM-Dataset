Design a simple Direct Memory Access (DMA) controller that transfers data from a source address to a destination address in memory. The DMA controller should have the following inputs: `start` (signal to initiate the transfer), `clk` (clock signal), `reset` (active high reset), `src_addr` (source address), `dst_addr` (destination address), and `data` (data to be transferred). The output should be `done` (signal indicating that the transfer is complete) and `mem` (the value at the destination address after the transfer).
