Design a 4-bit shift register with parallel load capability. The shift register should have the following functionalities:
1. Shift left on the rising edge of the clock when the shift signal is high.
2. Shift right on the rising edge of the clock when the shift signal is low.
3. Load new data in parallel when the load signal is high, overriding the shift operations.
4. Maintain the current state when neither load nor shift signals are asserted.
