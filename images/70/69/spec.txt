Design a 4-bit up-counter with synchronous reset functionality. Upon receiving a clock pulse, the counter increments its value by one until it reaches the maximum count of 15 (4'b1111), at which point it resets to zero and indicates an overflow condition. If the reset signal is asserted, the counter will immediately reset to zero and clear the overflow flag.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input rst,
    output reg [3:0] count,
    output reg overflow
);
