Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer, which selects one of four input signals (A0, A1, A2, A3) based on the values of two selection inputs (S0 and S1). The output X reflects the chosen input—outputting A0 when both S0 and S1 are 0, A1 when S0 is 1 and S1 is 0, A2 when S0 is 0 and S1 is 1, and A3 when both S0 and S1 are 1. Additionally, the circuit specifies power and ground connections, VPWR and VGND, for its operational needs.
module top_module(
    X   ,
    A0  ,
    A1  ,
    A2  ,
    A3  ,
    S0  ,
    S1  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND
);
