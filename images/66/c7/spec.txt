Design a power management module that interfaces with an external clock and controls a set of indicators based on voltage levels. The module utilizes a 12-bit counter to sequentially output a 3-bit voltage selection on a GPIO interface, and it drives a seven-segment LED array indicating active voltage conditions. The field for voltage selection is determined by the upper three bits of the counter, and an active kill switch is implemented to control a specific GPIO pin. The LED indicators activate according to the current voltage state selected by the counter, allowing for visual representation of the power management state.
