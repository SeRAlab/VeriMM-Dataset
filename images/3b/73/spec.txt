
Design a simple GPIO controller that has the following specifications:
- It has a 4-bit data input (data_in), a 4-bit data output (data_out), an output enable (out_en) signal, and a clock (clk) signal.
- When out_en is high, the data from data_in should be latched to data_out on the rising edge of the clock. 
- When out_en is low, data_out should hold its previous value.
