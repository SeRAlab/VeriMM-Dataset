Implement a pipelined edge detector that identifies rising and falling edges of a binary input signal. The module outputs a high signal for `rise` when a transition from low to high is detected and a high signal for `down` when a transition from high to low occurs. It operates synchronously with a clock signal and includes an active-low reset to initialize its internal state and outputs. The design revolves around three states: IDLE, RISING_EDGE, and FALLING_EDGE, effectively allowing it to capture and signal the state changes of the input signal.
module top_module(
  input               clk,
  input               rst_n,
  input               a,
  output reg          rise,
  output reg          down
);
