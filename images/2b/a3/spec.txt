Design an 8-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control input:
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
- 101: Comparison (output 1 if A > B, 0 otherwise)
The ALU should take two 8-bit inputs A and B and produce an 8-bit output Z. Additionally, the comparison will output a single bit result in the MSB of Z, while the rest of the bits of Z will be set to 0 for the comparison operation.
