Design an 8-bit divider that takes two 8-bit inputs: a numerator (dividend) and a denominator (divisor), and produces an 8-bit quotient and a 8-bit remainder. The division operation should be performed using a clock signal, and the division should be completed in a fixed number of clock cycles. Ensure that the design handles the case where the divisor is zero by producing a specific output (e.g., quotient = 255, remainder = 255 for division by zero).
