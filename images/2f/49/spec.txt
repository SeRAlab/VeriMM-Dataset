
Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor using a saturating counter. The predictor should use a 2-bit saturating counter to predict whether a branch will be taken or not. The states of the counter are as follows: 
- 00: Strongly Not Taken
- 01: Weakly Not Taken
- 10: Weakly Taken
- 11: Strongly Taken

The input signal `branch` indicates whether a branch is taken (1) or not taken (0). The output should be the predicted outcome (1 for Taken, 0 for Not Taken) and the counter state should update based on the actual outcome of the branch. The counter should increment or decrement based on the actual outcome but should saturate at the maximum and minimum values.
