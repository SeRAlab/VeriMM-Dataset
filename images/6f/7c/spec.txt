Design a five-input logic selector that outputs a signal based on specific combinations of its inputs. The output, X, is high when all three inputs (A1, A2, A3) are high and both inputs (B1, B2) are low, or when any of the inputs (A1, A2, A3) are not all high while both (B1, B2) are high. This module effectively acts as a conditional logic gate that processes specific high and low states from the inputs to determine the output state.
module top_module(
    input  A1  ,
    input  A2  ,
    input  A3  ,
    input  B1  ,
    input  B2  ,
    output X   ,

    input  VPB ,
    input  VPWR,
    input  VGND,
    input  VNB
);
