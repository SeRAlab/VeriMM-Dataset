
Design an edge-triggered D-flip-flop (DFF) in Verilog that captures the value of the data input (D) on the rising edge of the clock (clk). The DFF should also have an asynchronous reset (reset) input that, when asserted, sets the output (Q) to 0 regardless of the clock or D input. The output (Q) should hold its value until the next rising edge of the clock or until the reset is activated.
