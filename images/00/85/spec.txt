Design an SR latch using Verilog. The SR latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn). The behavior of the latch should be such that when S is high and R is low, Q should be high. When R is high and S is low, Q should be low. If both S and R are low, Q should retain its previous state. If both S and R are high, the output should be considered invalid.
