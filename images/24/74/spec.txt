Design a data forwarding unit for a simple 5-stage pipeline processor. The forwarding unit should take three inputs: the destination register of the instruction in the EX stage, the destination register of the instruction in the MEM stage, and the destination register of the instruction in the WB stage. It should output two signals: ForwardA and ForwardB, which indicate whether the data should be forwarded from the EX or MEM stages for the first and second operands, respectively.
