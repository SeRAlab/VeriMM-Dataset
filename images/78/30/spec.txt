The shift register, controlled by a clock and an asynchronous reset signal, loads data when requested and shifts its bits on each clock cycle if enabled. The 16-bit input is divided into two 8-bit segments stored in separate shift registers, which retain the recently shifted bits. Finally, the circuit sums the values from the shift register and the two splitters, outputting a 12-bit result. This module supports asynchronous reset, allowing the shift registers to clear to zero at any time.
