Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifier. The SVM should take two 3-bit inputs representing feature vectors and output a decision value (1 or 0) based on a linear decision boundary. The decision boundary will be defined as follows: if the weighted sum of the inputs (using weights 2 and 3) exceeds a threshold value of 5, the output should be 1; otherwise, it should be 0.
