Design a Verilog module that performs matrix multiplication of two 2x2 matrices. The module should take two 2x2 matrices as input and produce a 2x2 matrix as output. The inputs and outputs should be declared as 4-bit wide buses. The multiplication should be performed as follows:

C[0][0] = A[0][0] * B[0][0] + A[0][1] * B[1][0]
C[0][1] = A[0][0] * B[0][1] + A[0][1] * B[1][1]
C[1][0] = A[1][0] * B[0][0] + A[1][1] * B[1][0]
C[1][1] = A[1][0] * B[0][1] + A[1][1] * B[1][1]
