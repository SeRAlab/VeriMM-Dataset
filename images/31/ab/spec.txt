Design a Verilog module that takes a 4-bit input representing the amplitude of a signal and a 2-bit input representing the phase (00, 01, 10, 11). The module should output a 6-bit QAM signal based on the amplitude and phase inputs. The mapping is as follows: 
- Amplitude (0000 to 1111) and Phase (00: 000, 01: 001, 10: 010, 11: 011) should combine to create the final output.
- The output should be the concatenation of the amplitude and the phase encoded as a 6-bit value.
