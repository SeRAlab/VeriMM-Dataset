Design a traffic light controller that manages the sequence of red, amber, and green lights based on a clock input. The controller operates in three states: turning the red light on for a predefined duration, activating both red and amber lights for a brief period, and then switching to the green light for its designated time. The timing for each state is controlled by specific parameters, defining how long each light remains on before transitioning to the next state in a cyclical manner, ensuring safe traffic flow at intersections.
module top_module(
    input clock,
    output reg red,
    output reg amber,
    output reg green
);
