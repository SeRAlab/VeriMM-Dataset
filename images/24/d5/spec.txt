
Design a Verilog module that implements a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller should adjust its output voltage and frequency based on a 2-bit input control signal that indicates the desired performance state. The controller will have three states:  
00 - Low power mode (lowest voltage and frequency)  
01 - Medium power mode (medium voltage and frequency)  
10 - High power mode (highest voltage and frequency)  
11 - Reserved state (do nothing)  
The output should be represented as a 2-bit voltage output and a 2-bit frequency output, where each state corresponds to specific voltage and frequency values.
