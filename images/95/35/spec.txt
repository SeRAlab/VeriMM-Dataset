Design a simple 8-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that performs various operations on two input operands, A and B, depending on a 3-bit operation code (op). The ALU supports addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, OR, XOR, and both left and right bit shifts. The output of the selected operation is registered on the rising edge of the clock, and the module also provides additional outputs to indicate carry and zero status. When performing addition, the carry out is indicated by a carry flag, while the zero flag is asserted if the output is equal to zero.
module top_module(
    A, B, op, clk,
    out, carry, zero
);
