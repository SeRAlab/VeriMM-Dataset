Design a convolutional encoder with a constraint length of 3 and a rate of 1/2. The encoder should take a 1-bit input and produce two output bits based on the given polynomial generators: G1 = 101 and G2 = 111. The system should be able to process a stream of input bits and output the corresponding encoded bits. The design must include a state register to keep track of the previous two bits (the states) used for encoding.
