Design a 2-input, 8-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs various operations based on a 3-bit select input. The ALU can output either one of the input data lines (DATA2), the sum of both inputs (DATA1 + DATA2), the bitwise AND, or the bitwise OR of the two inputs, depending on the value of the SELECT lines. Additionally, the module includes a ZERO output that indicates when the RESULT is zero.
