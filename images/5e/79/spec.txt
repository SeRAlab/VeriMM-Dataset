Design a remote control decoder circuit that processes a serial data input (din) on the rising edge of a clock (clk). The circuit utilizes a 10-bit shift register to store incoming bits, and when a specific pattern (0101) is detected in the upper nibble of the shift register alongside a parity check, it asserts a strobe signal. Upon the assertion of strobe, the decoded output (dout) provides a 4-bit value from the shift register, specifically bits 2 to 5. If the strobe is not active, dout outputs zero.
