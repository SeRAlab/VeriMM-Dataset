Implement a VGA timing controller that generates horizontal and vertical synchronization signals along with a blanking output for a VGA display. The module tracks pixel coordinates defined by `pixel_x` and `pixel_y` and resets them upon the active clock signal or when the FIFO is empty. It determines the horizontal sync (`hsync`) and vertical sync (`vsync`) signals based on specific pixel ranges, while the `blank` output indicates when the display is not active. The controller drives pixel coordinates in a 800x525 resolution format, enabling it to manage display output effectively.
