
Design a Verilog module that simulates a simple mapping system for a robot. The robot takes in its current coordinates (X and Y) and the direction it's facing (North, East, South, or West). The module should output the new coordinates after moving one unit in the direction the robot is facing. The coordinates and direction are represented as follows:
- X coordinate: 4 bits (0 to 15)
- Y coordinate: 4 bits (0 to 15)
- Direction: 2 bits (00 = North, 01 = East, 10 = South, 11 = West)
