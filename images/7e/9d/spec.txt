Design a combinational logic circuit that performs multiple logical operations on a 4-bit input. The module computes the AND, OR, and XOR results on pairs of input bits, and then produces three outputs: `out_and`, which is the AND of the first pair and the OR of the second pair; `out_or`, which is the AND of the second pair and the OR of the first pair; and `out_xor`, which combines the XOR of the first pair with the AND of the second pair.
module top_module( 
    input [3:0] in,
    output out_and,
    output out_or,
    output out_xor
);
