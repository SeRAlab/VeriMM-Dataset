Design a D flip-flop with an internal multiplexer and latch functionality. The module captures the input data signal `d` on the rising edge of the clock (`clk`) and drives it to a latch that stores the output state. The latch updates its output based on the value of the multiplexer output, which reflects the value of `d`. The final output `q` is updated on the next clock edge, resulting in a synchronized, storage element that holds the value of `d` until the next clock cycle, effectively acting as a D flip-flop with an additional stage that conditions the latch output.
