The module features a D latch, a D flip-flop, a T flip-flop, a RS latch, and a JK flip-flop. The T flip-flop toggles its output based on its input T, while the RS latch sets or resets based on inputs R and S. The JK flip-flop implements toggling behavior controlled by inputs J and K, with feedback from its output.
