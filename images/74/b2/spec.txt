Design a half-adder circuit that accepts two one-bit inputs, x and y, and produces a sum output z and a carry output carry_out. The sum output z represents the least significant bit of the addition of x and y, while the carry_out indicates whether there is a carry from the addition, which occurs if both inputs are 1.
module top_module(
    input  wire x, y, 
    output wire z,
    output wire carry_out
);
