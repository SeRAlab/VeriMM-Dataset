Implement a finite state machine (FSM) for a traffic light control system that operates two sets of traffic lights (A and B). The system has two input buttons for pedestrian requests: `button_a` for traffic light A and `button_b` for traffic light B. Upon pressing a button, the FSM transitions to grant green light for the corresponding traffic light for a duration of 5 clock cycles, while ensuring a brief yellow light (off-state) in between. The lights are reset to red when not active. The design utilizes state management to control the transitions and light signals, ensuring that only one set of traffic lights is green at a time, thereby promoting safe road usage.
