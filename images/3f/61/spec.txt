Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the speed based on temperature readings. The controller has a 3-bit temperature input (0-7), and it produces a 2-bit fan speed output. The mapping is as follows: 
- 0-2 degrees: Speed 00 (Off)
- 3-5 degrees: Speed 01 (Low)
- 6-7 degrees: Speed 10 (Medium)
The controller should also have an enable signal that, when low, sets the output speed to 00 regardless of the temperature input. The controller should operate on the rising edge of a clock signal.
