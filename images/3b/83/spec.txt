Design a Verilog module that converts RGB color values to YUV color values. The module should take in 8-bit RGB inputs (R, G, B) and output 8-bit Y, U, and V values. The conversion formulas are as follows:
Y = 0.299*R + 0.587*G + 0.114*B
U = 0.493*(B - Y)
V = 0.877*(R - Y)

Assume that the outputs are rounded to the nearest integer for the final YUV values.
