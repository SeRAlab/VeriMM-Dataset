Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) output decoder. The decoder should take the 16-bit input from the ADC and classify it into four ranges. The output should be a 2-bit code that indicates which range the input falls into: 
- 00 for 0-4095, 
- 01 for 4096-8191, 
- 10 for 8192-12287, 
- 11 for 12288-65535.
