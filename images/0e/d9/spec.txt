
Design a simple UART transmitter that takes an 8-bit data input and a "send" signal. When the "send" signal is high, the transmitter should output the bits serially, starting with a start bit (0), followed by the 8 data bits, and ending with a stop bit (1). The output should be available on a "tx" signal. The transmitter operates at a clock frequency of 1 MHz and should transmit the data at 9600 baud rate.
