Design a circuit that performs bitwise operations on a 32-bit input. The module takes a 32-bit input, splits it into two 16-bit segments, and outputs two results: one that is the bitwise XOR of the upper and lower halves, and another that is the bitwise AND of the same segments. The resulting outputs are 32 bits wide, representing the outcomes of the respective operations.
module top_module( 
    input wire [31:0] in,
    output wire [31:0] out_xor,
    output wire [31:0] out_and );
