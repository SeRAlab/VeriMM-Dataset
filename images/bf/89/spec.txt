Design a 4-bit magnitude comparator. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and generates three outputs: EQ (indicating equality), GT (indicating if A is greater than B), and LT (indicating if A is less than B). It accomplishes this by performing subtraction (A - B) and evaluating the resulting difference to determine the relational status of A and B.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A, B,
    output EQ, GT, LT
);
