
Design a 4-channel DMA (Direct Memory Access) controller with the following specifications:
- The DMA controller has 4 request lines (REQ0, REQ1, REQ2, REQ3) representing the requests from 4 different channels.
- It has 1 grant line (GNT) that grants permission to one of the requesting channels.
- The controller uses a round-robin arbitration scheme to grant access to the channels.
- The controller should have an enable signal (EN) that must be high for the controller to operate.
- The output should indicate which channel is currently granted access.
