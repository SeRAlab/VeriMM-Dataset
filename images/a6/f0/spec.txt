Design a wait control module that monitors various signaling conditions to manage a seven-bit wait status output. It captures the start of operations from two clock signals, `wait_start_gluclock` and `wait_start_comport`, setting corresponding bits in the `waits` register. When the `wait_end` signal is activated, it resets a segment of the wait status to zero. The module generates a negated interrupt signal (`spiint_n`) based on the status of the `waits` register and provides a tri-stated control output (`wait_n`) that is disabled (Z state) during simulation but actively driven low when `spiint_n` is high in synthesis. This module effectively regulates the initiation and cessation of wait states in a broader system.
module top_module(input wire rst_n,input wire wait_start_gluclock,input wire wait_start_comport,input wire wait_end,output reg [6:0] waits,output wire wait_n,output wire spiint_n);
