
Design a Bluetooth module controller in Verilog that manages the state transitions of a Bluetooth device. The controller should have the following states: IDLE, SCANNING, CONNECTING, and CONNECTED. The state transitions should occur based on the following inputs: start_scan (to transition from IDLE to SCANNING), connect (to transition from SCANNING to CONNECTING), and disconnect (to transition from CONNECTED to IDLE). The system should also have a clock and reset input.
