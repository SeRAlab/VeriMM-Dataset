Design an 8-bit Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC) simulation that takes a 3-bit binary input representing a voltage level (0 to 7) and outputs an 8-bit binary representation of that voltage level scaled to a maximum of 255. The output should be a multiplication of the input by 32 (since 255/7 is approximately 36.43, we can round it to 32 for simplicity).
