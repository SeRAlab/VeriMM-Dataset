Design a Verilog module for a power gating control system that takes an input signal to enable or disable power to a device. The module should have two inputs: 'enable' (a 1-bit signal) and 'control' (a 1-bit signal), and one output 'power' (a 1-bit signal). The output 'power' should be high (1) when 'enable' is high (1) and 'control' is also high (1), and should be low (0) otherwise.
