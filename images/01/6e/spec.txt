Design a simple switching regulator controller that takes a 2-bit input signal representing a desired output voltage (00, 01, 10, 11) and produces a 1-bit output enabling the regulator (1 for ON, 0 for OFF) based on predefined conditions. The conditions are: the regulator is ON for inputs 01 (1.2V), 10 (2.5V), and OFF for 00 (0V) and 11 (5V).
