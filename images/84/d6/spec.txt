Implementation:

The johnson_code register is updated in the always block, triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal (posedge clk) or the negative edge of the reset signal (negedge rst_n).
On reset, indicated by ~rst_n, the johnson_code register is cleared to 0.
The johnson_code is updated by shifting its bits to the right, and the leftmost bit is set to the complement of the rightmost bit before the shift.
