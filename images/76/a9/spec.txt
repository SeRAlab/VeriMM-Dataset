The module supports three operations: addition (op=SUM), subtraction (op=SUB), and a conditional output of the greater of the two inputs (op=MULT). Additionally, it outputs a carry or overflow indication (over) in case of addition. The resultant output is combined with the overflow signal to drive an LED display. The module is set to handle input lines operating at a specified IO standard voltage of 2.5V.
