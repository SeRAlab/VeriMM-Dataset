Design a simple internal read-only memory (ROM) controller that outputs a sequence of 8-bit instructions based on the current program counter (PC) value, which increments with each valid instruction retrieval. The module receives a clock and a clear signal, where the PC resets on the clear signal, counts up to a maximum of 9 in response to an 'inst_done' signal, and outputs a specific instruction for each PC state. Once all instructions have been outputted (after PC reaches 9), the module indicates completion by asserting a 'rom_done' output and outputs a default instruction.
module top_module(clk, clr, inst_done, rom_inst, rom_done);
