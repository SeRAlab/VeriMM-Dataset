Design a 4-bit wide adder/subtractor circuit. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a control signal M. When M is low (0), the output Y is the sum of A and B; when M is high (1), the output Y is the difference of A and B. The design uses 2's complement to perform subtraction, enabling it to seamlessly switch between addition and subtraction based on the value of M.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    input M,
    output [3:0] Y
);
