Design a simple fan speed control system that has a 2-bit input representing the temperature level (00 for low, 01 for medium, 10 for high, 11 for off) and a 1-bit output for the fan speed control. The fan should only operate in high speed when the temperature is high (10), in medium speed when the temperature is medium (01), and in low speed when the temperature is low (00). If the temperature is off (11), the fan should be off as well.
