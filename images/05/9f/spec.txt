
Design a fixed-priority arbiter that takes in 4 request signals (req0, req1, req2, req3) and grants access to one of the requests based on fixed priority. The priority is: req0 > req1 > req2 > req3. The arbiter should output the grant signal (grant) corresponding to the highest priority request that is active. If no requests are active, the grant signal should be low. The arbiter should also have a clock and reset signal.
