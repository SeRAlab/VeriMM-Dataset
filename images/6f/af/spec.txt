Design a one-bit full adder circuit that takes three inputs: two significant bits (a and b) and an input carry (in_carry). It produces two outputs: the sum of the inputs (sum) and the carry-out (carry). The sum is calculated using XOR gates to combine the inputs, and the carry-out is produced by AND gates that capture the carry conditions from both the input bits and the carry-in.
module top_module(input  a,
                  input  b,
                  input  in_carry,
                  output sum,
                  output carry);
