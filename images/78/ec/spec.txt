Design a circuit that performs saturated addition of two 12-bit signed integers based on a specified mode. The module first calculates the regular sum of the inputs a and b, while simultaneously checking for overflow conditions through sign and carry-out signals. Depending on the overflow status, it adjusts the saturated sum to either the maximum positive (0x7FF) or the minimum negative value (0x800) for signed additions. The circuit supports four different modes: it can output either the unsigned saturated sum, the signed saturated sum, or simply the raw sum, based on the selected mode input. The output y delivers the final processed result accordingly.
