Design a 4-bit adder-subtractor module. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a control input M. When M is low (0), the module performs addition, producing a 4-bit result Y and a carry-out O. When M is high (1), it performs subtraction, again resulting in a 4-bit output Y and providing a borrow indication through O. The output O signifies the overflow or borrow condition.
