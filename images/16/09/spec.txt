Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor that uses a 2-bit saturating counter to predict whether to take a branch or not. The counter should increment on a taken branch and decrement on a not-taken branch. The counter should wrap around at 0 and 3. The input signal is the branch decision (0 for not taken, 1 for taken), and the output is the prediction (0 for not taken, 1 for taken).
