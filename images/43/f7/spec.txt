Design a 12-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs bitwise AND operation and addition based on the input control signal (ALUop). When ALUop is 00, the module computes the bitwise AND of the two 12-bit inputs (in0 and in1), and when ALUop is 01, it adds the two inputs, setting a carry output (CY) for overflow detection. Additionally, the module provides a zero output that indicates if the ALU's result is zero.
