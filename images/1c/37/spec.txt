
Design a 24-bit ADC output processor that takes a 24-bit input representing the ADC value and outputs the following:
1. A 12-bit signed integer that represents the ADC value scaled to a range of -2048 to +2047.
2. A 1-bit flag indicating if the ADC value is in the upper half (above 127) or the lower half (127 or below) of the range.
