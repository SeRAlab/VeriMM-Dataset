Design a Set-Reset (SR) latch using feedback mechanism. The latch has two inputs, R (reset) and S (set), and two outputs, Q and Qbar. The output Q is set to HIGH when S is triggered and remains HIGH until R is activated, which resets Q to LOW. Conversely, Qbar is the inverse of Q, ensuring Q and Qbar hold complementary states. This design is fundamental for storing a single bit of memory in digital circuits.
