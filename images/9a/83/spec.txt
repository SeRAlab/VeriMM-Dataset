    register_with_control

Input ports:
    clk: Clock signal used for synchronous operation.
    clr: Asynchronous clear signal.

Output ports:
    data_out: 16-bit output representing the current state of the register.

Implementation:

When load_enable is high, data_in is loaded into the register on the rising edge of the clock.
