Design a 16-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) output comparator. The module should take two 16-bit inputs (signal_a and signal_b) and output a 1-bit signal "greater" that indicates if signal_a is greater than signal_b. If signal_a is equal to signal_b, the output should be 0, and if signal_a is less than signal_b, the output should also be 0.
