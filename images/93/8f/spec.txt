Implement a synchronous D Flip-Flop with additional control features. The circuit captures the value of the input D at the rising edge of CLK when the SCE (Set Control Enable) signal is high. The Q output is reset to LOW when RESET_B is asserted (LOW). If the SCD (Set Clear Disable) input is LOW, the output Q is also cleared to LOW regardless of CLK. This design allows for controlled data storage with reset and disable functionalities.
module top_module(
    Q      ,
    CLK    ,
    D      ,
    SCD    ,
    SCE    ,
    RESET_B,
    VPWR   ,
    VGND   ,
    VPB    ,
    VNB
);
