Design a sign extension circuit that outputs a 32-bit value based on a 16-bit input. The operation is determined by the 2-bit control signal EXTop: if EXTop is 'b00', it appends 16 zeros to the right of imm16; if 'b01', it places imm16 in the least significant 16 bits and pads the upper 16 bits with zeros; if 'b10', it sign-extends imm16 by replicating the most significant bit to fill the upper 16 bits; and for any other value of EXTop, it outputs a fixed 32-bit value of 32'b0001001000110100.
