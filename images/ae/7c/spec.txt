Design an arithmetic and logic unit (ALU) capable of performing various operations on two 32-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, based on a 5-bit operation code (`aluc`). The ALU supports addition, subtraction, bitwise operations (AND, OR, XOR, NOR), set less than comparisons (signed and unsigned), and bitwise shifts (logical left and right, arithmetic right). Additionally, it includes a load upper immediate (lui) operation. The output of the selected operation is provided as a 32-bit result. The design allows selection of the operation with a simple case statement driven by the `aluc` input.
