Design a circuit that controls a programmable read-only memory (PROM) module based on a given program counter (pc) input and specific control signals. The module enables the PROM only when certain conditions are met: the lower 1KB of the address space is selected (indicated by the upper bits of pc being LOW), the idebug signal is LOW, the promdisabled signal is LOW, and the iwrited signal is LOW. The output provides the appropriate address to the PROM by extracting the lower 9 bits of the 12 least significant bits from the pc input while also controlling the enable signal for the PROM.
module top_module(pc, idebug, promdisabled, iwrited, promenable, promaddr);
