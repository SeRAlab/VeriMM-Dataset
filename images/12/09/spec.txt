
Design a 32-bit serializer that takes a 32-bit input word and outputs the bits one at a time on the `serial_out` line, with a clock signal controlling the output. The serializer should also have a `start` signal to initiate the serialization process, and an `enable` signal that must be active for the `serial_out` to be valid. The output should be valid for one clock cycle, transitioning to the next bit in the subsequent clock cycles until all bits have been serialized.
