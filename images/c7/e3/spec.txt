

You are tasked with designing a module that takes in multiple inputs and provides a single output. The module should be named "multi_input_output" and should have the following inputs and outputs:

Inputs:
- A (1-bit)
- B (1-bit)
- C (1-bit)
- D (1-bit)
- E (1-bit)
- F (1-bit)
- G (1-bit)
- H (1-bit)

Outputs:
- Y (1-bit)

The output Y should be the result of the following logic expression:

Y = (A and B and C) or (D and E and F and G) or H

Design the module "multi_input_output" and test it using a testbench.
