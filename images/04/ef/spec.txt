Design a simple Bluetooth connection state machine that controls the connection states: IDLE, CONNECTING, CONNECTED, and DISCONNECTED. The state transitions are as follows: 
- From IDLE to CONNECTING on signal `start_connect`.
- From CONNECTING to CONNECTED on signal `connect_success`.
- From CONNECTING to DISCONNECTED on signal `connect_fail`.
- From CONNECTED to DISCONNECTED on signal `disconnect`.
The output should indicate the current state of the Bluetooth module.
