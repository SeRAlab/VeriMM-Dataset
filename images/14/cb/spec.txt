Design a Verilog module that implements a simple DVFS controller. The controller takes an input signal representing the system load (a 3-bit binary value) and generates a frequency control signal (a 2-bit output) based on predefined thresholds. The frequency control signal will determine the operating frequency of the system. The thresholds are as follows: 
- 000 (0) to 011 (3) -> Low Frequency (00)
- 100 (4) to 111 (7) -> High Frequency (01)
The module should also have an enable signal that allows or disables the output.
