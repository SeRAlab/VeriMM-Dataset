This module increments or decrements an 8-bit count based on the state of the `up_down` input; when `up_down` is high, the counter increments, and when low, it decrements. The `enable` signal controls whether the counting occurs, while the `reset` signal initializes the count to zero. Additionally, the module indicates when an overflow condition is met, signaling that the count has reached its maximum or minimum value, depending on the counting direction.
