
Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: AND
- 011: OR
- 100: XOR
- 101: NOR
- 110: NAND
- 111: Comparison (set on less than, output 1 if A < B, else output 0)

The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and the 3-bit control signal as inputs and provide a 32-bit output result.
