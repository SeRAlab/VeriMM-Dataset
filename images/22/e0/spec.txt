
Design an 8-bit shift register with the following specifications:
- The shift register should have a parallel load feature.
- When the load signal is high, the 8-bit input data should be loaded into the shift register.
- When the load signal is low, the shift register should perform a right shift operation on the stored data.
- The least significant bit (LSB) should be filled with a zero during the shift operation.
- The output of the shift register should be available at all times.
