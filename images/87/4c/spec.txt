Design a 3-to-8 internal register bank that operates on a rising clock edge. The module allows for reading and writing 16-bit data to and from one of eight registers, based on the provided address inputs. When the enable signal is high and the write enable signal (wr_en) is activated, data is written to the register specified by rd_adrs. If wr_en is not active, the values of the registers specified by ra_adrs and rb_adrs are output as ra_out and rb_out, respectively. This functionality enables efficient data storage and retrieval in synchronous digital circuits.
module top_module( clock, enable, rd_adrs, ra_adrs, rb_adrs, wr_en, data_in, ra_out, rb_out);
