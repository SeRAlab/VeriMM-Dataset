Design a simple branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should have an input for the current branch address and the actual outcome of the branch (taken or not taken). The output should be a prediction of whether the branch will be taken or not taken. The 2-bit counter works as follows: 
00 - strongly not taken
01 - weakly not taken
10 - weakly taken
11 - strongly taken
The counter increments or decrements based on the actual outcome of the branch.
