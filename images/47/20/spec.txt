Design a clock gating circuit that gates the input clock signal based on an active control signal. When the clock is low, the current state of the active signal is stored; later, during clock high cycles, the output `clk_gated` will be the result of a logical AND operation between the input clock and the stored active state. This allows for reducing power consumption by disabling the clock when not actively needed, thereby controlling the flow of clock pulses to downstream circuits.
