It operates on a clock signal (bclk) to synchronize address and data handling, using a reset signal to initialize states. The module captures a combined address formed from high and low bits and tracks control signals such as read/write operations and chip select status. Therefore, it effectively serves as a bridge for communication with the memory subsystem while managing control signals and data flow.
