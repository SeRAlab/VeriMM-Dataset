
Design a sliding window protocol that simulates the sender and receiver in a communication system. The sender can send a maximum of 4 packets at a time (window size of 4). The sender will maintain a window of sent packets and will receive acknowledgments (ACKs) for packets that have been successfully received by the receiver. The sender should only send new packets when it has received an acknowledgment for the oldest unacknowledged packet in the window. The receiver should acknowledge packets in the order they are received. Implement the state machine for both the sender and receiver along with their respective controls.
