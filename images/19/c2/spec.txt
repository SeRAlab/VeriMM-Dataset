Design a Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG) using a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) in Verilog. The LFSR should have a length of 5 bits and utilize taps corresponding to the polynomial x^5 + x^3 + 1. The output of the generator should be a 1-bit pseudo-random number generated on the rising edge of the clock.
