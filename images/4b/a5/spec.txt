Design a one-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer with power supply connections. The output X is selected based on the value of the select input (sel): when sel = 0, output A is chosen, and when sel = 1, output B is selected. The design also includes provisions for voltage power and ground inputs (VPWR and VGND) to enhance circuit stability and integration.
module top_module(
    X    ,
    A    ,
    B    ,
    sel  ,
    VPWR ,
    VGND
);
