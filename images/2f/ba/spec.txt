Design an interrupt controller that manages two types of interrupts: a regular interrupt and a high-priority interrupt. The controller should have the ability to enable/disable these interrupts and provide a way to acknowledge them when they have been serviced. The inputs to the controller are: `reg_int` (regular interrupt signal), `high_int` (high-priority interrupt signal), `enable` (to enable interrupts), and `ack` (to acknowledge the interrupt). The output is `interrupt_out`, which should signal when an interrupt is active.
