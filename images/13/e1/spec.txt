Design a Verilog module that computes the dot product of two 5-dimensional vectors, A and B. Each vector element is a 4-bit unsigned integer. The output should be a 10-bit unsigned integer representing the dot product of the two vectors. The inputs A and B should be provided in the form of 5 separate 4-bit inputs (A0, A1, A2, A3, A4 and B0, B1, B2, B3, B4) and the output should be a single 10-bit output (result).
