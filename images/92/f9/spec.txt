Design a clock-gating flip-flop module that employs a transparent latch mechanism. The module outputs an enabled clock signal (ENCLK) based on the control inputs EN (enable) and TE (test enable). When EN is high and TE is low, the flip-flop captures the data input D on the rising edge of ENCLK, effectively controlling when data is latched based on the clock and enable signals. This design efficiently reduces power consumption by gating the clock signal, ensuring that the flip-flop operates only when needed.
