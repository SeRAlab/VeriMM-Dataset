Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (in0, in1, in2, in3) based on two select lines (sel0 and sel1). The output (out) corresponds to the selected input based on the combination of the select lines: when sel0 and sel1 are both low, the output is in0; when sel0 is low and sel1 is high, the output is in1; when sel0 is high and sel1 is low, the output is in2; and when both sel0 and sel1 are high, the output is in3.
module top_module(
    out,
    in0,
    in1,
    in2,
    in3,
    sel0,
    sel1
);
