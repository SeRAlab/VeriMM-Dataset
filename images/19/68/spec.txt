
Design a 2-channel DMA controller that transfers data from a source to a destination. The controller should have the following features:
- Two channels (Channel 0 and Channel 1)
- Control signals: `start`, `stop`, `channel_select`, and `transfer_done`
- Each channel should have a data register (`data0` for Channel 0 and `data1` for Channel 1)
- The `start` signal initiates the transfer for the selected channel
- The `stop` signal halts the transfer
- The `transfer_done` signal indicates when a transfer is complete
- The data for each channel should be transferred on the rising edge of a clock signal
