Design a temperature sensor controller that takes a 5-bit binary input representing temperature in degrees Celsius (0-31 degrees) and outputs a 2-bit binary status based on the following conditions: 00 for 'Low' (0-10 degrees), 01 for 'Normal' (11-20 degrees), and 10 for 'High' (21-31 degrees). The output status should only change on the rising edge of a clock signal and hold until the next rising edge.
