
Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature input. The fan has three speed settings: Low, Medium, and High. The temperature input (in degrees Celsius) determines the fan speed as follows:
- If the temperature is less than or equal to 20°C, the fan speed is Low (00).
- If the temperature is between 21°C and 30°C, the fan speed is Medium (01).
- If the temperature is greater than 30°C, the fan speed is High (10).
Assume the temperature is provided as a 6-bit binary input.
