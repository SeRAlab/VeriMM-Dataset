Design a Verilog module that computes the dot product of two 5-bit vectors. The module should accept two 5-bit inputs (A and B) and output a 10-bit result (R). The dot product is defined as R = A[0]*B[0] + A[1]*B[1] + A[2]*B[2] + A[3]*B[3] + A[4]*B[4]. The output should be valid when the enable signal is high.
