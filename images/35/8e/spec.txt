
Design a 1-bit DRAM cell. The DRAM cell should have the following inputs and outputs:
- Inputs: `data_in` (1-bit input), `we` (write enable, 1-bit input), `clk` (clock signal, 1-bit input).
- Outputs: `data_out` (1-bit output).
The DRAM cell should store the input data when `we` is high on the rising edge of the `clk`. When `we` is low, the output `data_out` should reflect the stored value.
