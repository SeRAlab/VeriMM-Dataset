It sets the AM/PM output signal to 0 for AM times (00:00 to 11:59) and to 1 for PM times (12:00 to 23:59). Special handling is included for both the input hour 0, converting it to 12 AM, and hour 12, maintaining it as 12 PM.

                    output reg am_pm,
                    output reg [7:0]twelve_hour_out
    );
