
Design a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog. The arbiter should take 4 request signals (req[3:0]) and grant a signal (grant[3:0]) based on a fixed priority (where req[0] has the highest priority and req[3] has the lowest priority). When a request is granted, it should remain granted until the corresponding request signal is deactivated. The arbiter should handle one grant at a time according to the priority of the request signals.
