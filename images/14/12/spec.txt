Design a simple NAT (Network Address Translation) module in Verilog that supports a basic port forwarding functionality. The module should take an internal IP address and port, and map it to an external IP address and port. The module should have a 2-bit operation mode: 00 for mapping internal to external, 01 for undoing the mapping, and 10 for passing traffic through. If the operation is valid, the output should reflect the mapped external address and port; otherwise, it should signal an error.
