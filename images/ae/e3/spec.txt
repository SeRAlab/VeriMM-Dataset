Design a modulo-N counter capable of counting in binary-coded decimal (BCD) format, where N is defined by the parameter TOP_VALUE (set to 9). The counter increments on each clock pulse unless the set_ena signal is high, which disables the counting. It has control inputs for counting direction—up and down. When the up signal is low, the counter increments; when the down signal is low, it decrements, wrapping around at the boundaries (from 9 to 0 and 0 to 9). The output TC indicates when the counter reaches its maximum value (TOP_VALUE). The counter's output is stored in a register and updated on the rising edge of the clock.
module top_module(clk, set_ena, up, down, BCD_out_next, TC);
