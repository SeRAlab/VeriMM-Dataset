A fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature sensor readings. The fan has three speed levels: off, low, and high. The system uses a clock signal to sample the temperature input and adjust the fan speed accordingly. If the temperature is below 20 degrees Celsius, the fan is off; if the temperature is between 20 and 30 degrees Celsius, the fan runs at low speed; if the temperature is above 30 degrees Celsius, the fan runs at high speed.
