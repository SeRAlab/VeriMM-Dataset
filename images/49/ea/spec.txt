Design a Knight Rider LED sequence generator. The module takes a clock signal as an input and drives an 8-bit output corresponding to a shifting pattern of lights. The LEDs initially light up in a specific configuration, and on each clock pulse, the lit LEDs shift left or right based on a directional control derived from the current position. The shifting reverses direction after reaching the boundaries of the array, creating a visual effect similar to the iconic automobile lighting pattern. The output presents a subset of the shifted LED states.
module top_module(input clk,output [7:0] led_out);
