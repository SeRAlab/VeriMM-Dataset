
You are tasked with designing a simple 4-bit adder/subtractor circuit in Verilog. The circuit should have two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a control input, C. If C is 0, the circuit should perform addition and output the result as a 4-bit sum, S. If C is 1, the circuit should perform subtraction and output the result as a 4-bit difference, D. You may use any Verilog syntax you find appropriate, but the circuit must be synthesizable.
