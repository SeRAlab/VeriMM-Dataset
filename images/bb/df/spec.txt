The module uses a gated clock approach, where the output Q is updated with input D on the rising edge of a clock signal that is controlled by both the enable (EN) and test (TE) signals. When TE is asserted, the standard clock input CLK is utilized; otherwise, the output follows the gated clock signal generated by the combination of EN and CLK.
