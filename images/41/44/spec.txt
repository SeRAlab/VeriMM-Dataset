Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of the four input signals (a, b, c, or d) based on a 2-bit select signal (sel). The output (out) reflects the chosen input, where sel=00 outputs a, sel=01 outputs b, sel=10 outputs c, and sel=11 outputs d. In the case of invalid sel signals, the output defaults to the state of input a.
