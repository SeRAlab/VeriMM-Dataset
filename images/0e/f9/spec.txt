Implement a simple elliptic curve point addition operation using Verilog. Given two points P1 (x1, y1) and P2 (x2, y2) on the elliptic curve, compute the resulting point P3 (x3, y3) after performing the addition P1 + P2. The points are represented in a simplified form suitable for simulation. The elliptic curve equation used is y^2 = x^3 + ax + b.
