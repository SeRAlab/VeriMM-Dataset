
Design a 2-stage pipelined system in Verilog that takes a 5-bit input, processes it in two stages, and produces a 5-bit output. The first stage should perform a simple bitwise NOT operation, and the second stage should perform a bitwise AND operation with a fixed 5-bit value (e.g., 5'b10101). The system should also include a clock input and a reset signal to initialize the pipeline.
