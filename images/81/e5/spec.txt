Design a 4-bit barrel shifter capable of performing left and right shifts based on a 2-bit control input. The module accepts a 4-bit input (`data_in`) and shifts the bits either left or right as specified by the `shift` input. A shift value of `00` retains the original data, `01` shifts the data left by 1 bit, `10` shifts it right by 1 bit, and `11` shifts left by 2 bits, filling vacant positions with zeros.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] data_in,
    input [1:0] shift,
    output reg [3:0] out
);
