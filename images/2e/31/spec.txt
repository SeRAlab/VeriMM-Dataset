
Design a 16-bit Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) controller that takes a 16-bit binary input and converts it to a corresponding analog voltage output. The output voltage should be in the range of 0V to 5V, where the binary input of 0 corresponds to 0V and the binary input of 65535 corresponds to 5V. The DAC has a simple equation to convert the digital value `D` to voltage `V` as follows: V = (D / 65535) * 5V.
