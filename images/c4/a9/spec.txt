

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that takes in three inputs A1, A2, A3, and two inputs B1, C1. The module should output a signal Y, which is determined by the following rules:
- If A1 is greater than or equal to A2, and A1 is greater than or equal to A3, then Y should be equal to B1.
- If A2 is greater than or equal to A1, and A2 is greater than or equal to A3, then Y should be equal to C1.
- If A3 is greater than or equal to A1, and A3 is greater than or equal to A2, then Y should be equal to B1 XOR C1.

Additionally, the module should have four power inputs: VPB, VPWR, VGND, and VNB.

Design this module in verilog and name it `signal_selector`.
