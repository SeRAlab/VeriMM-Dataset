Design a 4-bit modulo 16 adder with reset functionality. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, and computes their sum modulo 16 on the positive edge of the clock, outputting the result as `out`. If the reset signal is asserted, the output is set to zero. This design ensures the output wraps around, remaining within the range of 0 to 15, effectively implementing a circular addition operation.
