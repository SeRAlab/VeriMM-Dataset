Design a 2-bit branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The predictor should have two states: "taken" and "not taken." The output should predict whether the next branch will be taken or not based on the current state and the actual branch outcome. The state transitions are as follows: from "00" (strongly not taken) to "01" (weakly not taken) on a "not taken" outcome, and to "10" (weakly taken) on a "taken" outcome. From "01" to "00" on a "not taken" outcome, and to "10" on a "taken" outcome. From "10" to "01" on a "not taken" outcome, and to "11" (strongly taken) on a "taken" outcome. From "11" back to "10" on a "not taken" outcome, and stays at "11" on a "taken" outcome. The prediction output should indicate if the branch is predicted to be taken (1) or not taken (0).
