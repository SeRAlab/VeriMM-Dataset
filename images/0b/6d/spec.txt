Design a 2-bit shift register that takes a 1-bit input `serial_in` and shifts its contents to the right on each clock cycle. The two bits are to be stored in a 2-bit register `shift_reg`. The most significant bit (MSB) of the register will be filled with the `serial_in` value on each clock cycle. The shift register should have a reset input `reset` which, when high, resets the register to `00`.
