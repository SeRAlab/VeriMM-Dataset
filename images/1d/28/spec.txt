
Design an 8-bit SRAM cell that can read and write data. The SRAM cell should have the following inputs and outputs: 
- Input: `data_in` (8 bits), `write_enable` (1 bit), `clk` (clock signal), and `addr` (address, 3 bits).
- Output: `data_out` (8 bits).
The cell should store the data when `write_enable` is high on the rising edge of `clk` and provide the stored data when `write_enable` is low.
