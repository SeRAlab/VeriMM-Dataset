
Design a 2nd order Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) filter with the difference equation:
y[n] = a1 * y[n-1] + a2 * y[n-2] + b0 * x[n] + b1 * x[n-1]
where x[n] is the current input, y[n] is the current output, and a1, a2, b0, and b1 are filter coefficients. The coefficients will be defined in the module. The inputs and outputs should be 16-bit signed integers.
