Design a 1-bit SRAM cell that can store a single bit of data. The SRAM cell should have two inputs: `data_in` (the bit to be written) and `write_enable` (which controls whether to write the data or not). It should also have one output: `data_out` (the stored bit). The SRAM cell should retain its value unless a write operation is performed when `write_enable` is high.
