Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage of a processor depending on the workload. The controller takes a workload signal (a 2-bit input) and adjusts the voltage (2 bits) and frequency (2 bits) outputs accordingly. The mapping is as follows:  
- Workload 00: Voltage = 00, Frequency = 00  
- Workload 01: Voltage = 01, Frequency = 01  
- Workload 10: Voltage = 10, Frequency = 10  
- Workload 11: Voltage = 11, Frequency = 11  
The controller should respond to a clock signal and a reset signal. On reset, both voltage and frequency should drop to 00.
