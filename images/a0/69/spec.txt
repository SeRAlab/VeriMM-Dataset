The module implements a 5-bit up-counter with load functionality. On the rising edge of the clock or when a reset signal is activated, the counter is either reset to zero or updated based on the input `upc_next` if the `load_incr` signal is high. If `load_incr` is low, the counter increments its current value by one. This design serves as a counter that can be reset, loaded with a specific value, or incremented, providing flexibility for various counting applications.
module top_module(input clk,input reset,input load_incr,input [4:0] upc_next,output reg [4:0] upc);
