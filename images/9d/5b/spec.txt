Design a 4-to-16 decoded ROM (Read-Only Memory) that outputs a single bit, determined by the 4-bit input address formed from A0, A1, A2, and A3. The content of the ROM is initialized with a parameterized 16-bit value, INIT, from which the output O is derived based on the decoded address input. When the inputs A3, A2, A1, and A0 are combined, they select one of the 16 bits in the INIT value to output.
module top_module(output O,input A0, A1, A2, A3);
