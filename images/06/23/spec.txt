Design a 16-bit SRAM cell with read and write functionality. The SRAM cell should have the following inputs: `address` (4 bits), `data_in` (16 bits), `we` (write enable), and `clk` (clock signal). The SRAM should have an output `data_out` (16 bits) that reflects the data stored at the specified address when `we` is low and captures `data_in` when `we` is high on the rising edge of `clk`.
