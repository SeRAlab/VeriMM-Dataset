Design a thermal sensor calibration circuit that takes a raw sensor reading and outputs a calibrated temperature value. The calibration factor is applied to the raw sensor reading to adjust the temperature output. The calibration factor is a 4-bit binary value, and the raw sensor reading is a 12-bit binary value, producing a 12-bit calibrated temperature output. The calibration factor represents a fixed-point value, where the lower 4 bits represent the fractional part. The calibrated temperature is computed as: Calibrated_Temperature = Raw_Reading * Calibration_Factor / 16.
