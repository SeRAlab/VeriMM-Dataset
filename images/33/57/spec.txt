Design a digital phase shifter that takes a 3-bit binary input and outputs a 3-bit binary output that represents the input rotated to the left by a specified number of positions defined by a 2-bit control input. The control input values will dictate the degree of left rotation: 00 for no rotation, 01 for rotating by 1 position, 10 for rotating by 2 positions, and 11 for rotating by 3 positions.
