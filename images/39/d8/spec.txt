Design a 2-channel DMA controller that allows two channels to transfer data simultaneously. Each channel has a read-enable (rd_en), write-enable (wr_en), and a 4-bit address. The DMA controller should have a clock input and a reset input. Upon reset, the controller should initialize its state. The controller should transfer data from a source to a destination based on the control signals and addresses provided. The output should indicate the status of both channels (active/inactive).
