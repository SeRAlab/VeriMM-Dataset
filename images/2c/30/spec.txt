
Design a 2-bit barrel shifter that can shift a 2-bit input to the left or right based on a 1-bit control signal. The shifter should have the following ports:
- Input: 2-bit data (input_data)
- Control: 1-bit control signal (shift_dir), where 0 means left shift and 1 means right shift
- Output: 2-bit shifted data (output_data)
