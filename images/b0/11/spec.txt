Design a 4-bit wide zero-padding shift module. The module takes a 4-bit input and shifts it left by one position, appending a zero on the least significant bit. The most significant bit (MSB) of the input is used as an output signal to indicate whether data was lost during the shift process, where 'lost' is asserted if the MSB of the input is high.

    input [3:0] in,
    output [3:0] out,
