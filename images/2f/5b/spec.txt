
Design a Non-Restoring Divider in Verilog that takes two binary numbers as input: a numerator and a denominator. The output should be the quotient and the remainder of the division operation. The divider should have a clock input and should be able to handle 8-bit input values. The operation should be completed in a finite number of clock cycles, and the final quotient and remainder should be available once the division operation is complete.
