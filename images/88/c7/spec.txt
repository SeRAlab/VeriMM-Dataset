Design a simple 8-bit calculator capable of performing basic arithmetic operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on two 8-bit inputs, a and b. The operation to be executed is selected via a 2-bit input, op. The result is updated on the rising edge of the clock signal, and a clear input resets the result to zero.
module top_module(
    clk,
    a,
    b,
    op,
    clear,
    result
);
