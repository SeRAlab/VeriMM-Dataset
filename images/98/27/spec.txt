Design a digital calculator module that performs arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division) on two 8-bit inputs. The module operates synchronously with a clock and includes reset functionality. It produces an 8-bit result along with flags indicating overflow, zero result, and valid operation. The specific operation to execute is determined by a 2-bit operation code. In case of division by zero, the module sets the result to zero and indicates a valid operation without generating an overflow. This design facilitates basic arithmetic computation in a controlled digital environment.
