Design a JK flip-flop using Verilog. The flip-flop should have inputs J, K, and a clock signal (clk), and an output Q. The flip-flop should toggle its output Q based on the states of J and K on the rising edge of the clock signal. The behavior should be defined as follows: 
- If J=0 and K=0, Q remains the same (no change).
- If J=0 and K=1, Q resets to 0.
- If J=1 and K=0, Q sets to 1.
- If J=1 and K=1, Q toggles (changes from 0 to 1 or from 1 to 0).
