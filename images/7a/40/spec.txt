On each positive clock edge, the 3-bit shift register updates to include a new bit from the 2-bit input, effectively shifting its contents left. Simultaneously, on each negative clock edge, the PISO shift register captures 8 input bits and shifts them out to produce a serial output. The final output is a concatenation of the contents of both registers, producing an 11-bit wide output signal.
