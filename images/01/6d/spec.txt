Design a simple ECC point addition module that adds two points on an elliptic curve over a finite field defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) as inputs and output the resulting point (x3, y3) after performing the point addition operation. The module should also include a reset to initialize the output to (0, 0) when activated.
