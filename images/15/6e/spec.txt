Design a 4-bit Serial-In Serial-Out (SISO) shift register. The SISO shift register should have a serial input called `serial_in`, a clock input called `clk`, and a 4-bit parallel output called `parallel_out`. On each rising edge of the clock, the `serial_in` bit should be shifted into the register. When the register is full (after 4 bits have been received), the output `parallel_out` should reflect the state of the register.
