

You are tasked with designing a Verilog module that can calculate the sum of two 8-bit unsigned numbers. Your module should have two 8-bit input ports (`A` and `B`) and one 8-bit output port (`sum`). The module should be able to perform the addition operation in a single clock cycle.

Your module should be named `add8bit` and should be designed in a single Verilog file. You may not use any pre-built Verilog modules or functions.
