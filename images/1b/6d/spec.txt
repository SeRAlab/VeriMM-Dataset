Design a 4-input priority arbiter that grants access to one of the four requestors based on their priority. The requestors are named R0, R1, R2, and R3, where R0 has the highest priority and R3 has the lowest. The arbiter should output a grant signal indicating which requestor has been granted access. If multiple requestors assert their request signal simultaneously, the arbiter should grant access to the highest priority requestor. The output grant should be a 4-bit signal where only the bit corresponding to the granted requestor is high.
