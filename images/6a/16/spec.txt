Design a control signal generator for an SPI slave device. The module outputs a slave select (ss) signal that is active LOW during data transmission when the transmit signal is high and returns to HIGH when the done signal is asserted or when a reset condition occurs. The ss signal is driven high by default upon reset and transitions according to the transmission states coordinated by the transmit and done inputs.
