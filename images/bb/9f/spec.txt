Design a clock gating circuit with enable functionality to control the propagation of the clock signal. This module incorporates a D flip-flop that captures the clock input when the enable (EN) signal is active. The output enable clock (ENCLK) will reflect the clock signal (CLK) when a test enable (TE) condition is met; otherwise, it will output low. When the enable signal is inactive, the output is reset to low, effectively minimizing power consumption by disabling the clock when not needed. This design is intended for efficient clock management in digital circuits.
