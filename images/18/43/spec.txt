Design a SR flip-flop in Verilog. The SR flip-flop has two inputs, S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs, Q and Qn (not Q). When S is high, Q should be set to high. When R is high, Q should be reset to low. If both S and R are low, the output Q should hold its previous state. If both S and R are high, it’s considered a forbidden state; in this case, Q should remain unchanged.
