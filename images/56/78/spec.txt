It takes as inputs the propagate signals \( P \) and generate signals \( G \) for each bit, along with a carry-in signal \( Cin \). The module outputs the four carry signals \( C \), the final carry-out \( Cout \), and the overall propagate \( PP \) and generate \( GG \) signals. This allows for more efficient addition in digital circuits by minimizing the delay associated with carry propagation.
