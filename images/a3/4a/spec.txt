Implement an 8-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer. The module selects one of the two 8-bit data inputs, D0 or D1, based on the control signal, ctrl. When ctrl is low (0), the output S reflects the value of D0, and when ctrl is high (1), S reflects the value of D1. This design effectively allows for the selection of one of two data sources for further processing or output.
module top_module(ctrl,
    D0,
    D1,
    S);
