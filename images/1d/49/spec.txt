Design a packet filtering (firewall) module in Verilog that checks whether a packet should be allowed or blocked based on its source IP address and destination port. The module should output a signal indicating whether the packet is allowed (1) or blocked (0). The allowed source IPs are predefined as 192.168.1.1 and 10.0.0.1, and the allowed destination ports are 80 (HTTP) and 443 (HTTPS).
