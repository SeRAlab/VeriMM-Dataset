It includes a mechanism to indicate whether the received CRC matches a predefined value (16'h1d0f), signaling a successful check. The operation is synchronized to a clock and resets on an active low signal, ensuring that the CRC calculations are updated only when a package is complete or synchronous operation is enabled. The design allows for verification of either the primary data or a response, facilitating error detection in data communication protocols.
