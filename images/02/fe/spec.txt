
Design an I2C master controller in Verilog that initiates a communication sequence with an I2C slave device. The controller should handle start and stop conditions, acknowledge signals, and data transmission. The I2C master should have the following inputs: `clk` (clock signal), `start` (start signal), `stop` (stop signal), `data_in` (data to be sent), and outputs: `sda` (data line), `scl` (clock line), and `ack` (acknowledge signal).
