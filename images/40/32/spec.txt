Design an SR latch using Verilog. The latch should have two inputs (S and R) and two outputs (Q and Qn). The behavior should be such that when S=1 and R=0, Q should become 1. When S=0 and R=1, Q should become 0. If both S and R are 0, Q should maintain its previous state. If both S and R are 1, it should be considered an invalid state, and the latch should maintain its previous state.
