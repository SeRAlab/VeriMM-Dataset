Design a JK latch using Verilog. The JK latch should have two inputs (J and K), a clock signal (clk), and two outputs (Q and Qn). The latch should toggle its output based on the values of J and K on the rising edge of the clock. If both J and K are low, the latch should hold its previous state. If J is high and K is low, Q should be set to high. If J is low and K is high, Q should be set to low. If both J and K are high, Q should toggle its current state.
