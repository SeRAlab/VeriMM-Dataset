
Create a simple DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency based on the input workload. The controller takes a 2-bit input `workload` (00: low, 01: medium, 10: high, 11: very high) and outputs a 2-bit `frequency` value (00: low frequency, 01: medium frequency, 10: high frequency, 11: very high frequency). The frequency should increase with the workload.
