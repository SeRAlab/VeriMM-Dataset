Implement the Verilog module based on the following description.
Create a Verilog module for a 2-bit comparator. It should take two 2-bit inputs (a, b) and produce three 1-bit outputs (a_gt_b, a_lt_b, a_eq_b). The outputs should indicate whether a is greater than, less than, or equal to b, respectively. The outputs should be updated on the positive edge of the clock signal.
