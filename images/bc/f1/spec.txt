Design a collision detection module that outputs a signal indicating whether an object represented by coordinates (bx, by) is colliding with a defined rectangular area. The rectangle is specified by its top (ptop) and bottom (pbot) edges, with a fixed width (pw) starting from a reference position (px). The module activates its checking logic on the rising edge of a clock signal (cclk) or when reset, and it asserts the 'collide' output high when the object falls within the boundaries of the rectangle, taking into account its positioning along the y-axis with a slight offset. If the clock is low, the module resets the 'collide' output to low.
module top_module(cclk, bx, by, ptop, pbot, reset, collide);
