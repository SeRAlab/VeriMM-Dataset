Design a 7-bit counter with asynchronous reset and enable control signals. The counter increments its value on the rising edge of the clock signal (c) when enabled (en). When the reset signal (r) is asserted, the counter asynchronously resets to zero. The module outputs the least significant three bits of the counter as q1, the next two bits as q2, and the most significant two bits as q3, effectively providing three separate outputs representing different segments of the counter's state.
module top_module(c, r, en, q1, q2, q3);
