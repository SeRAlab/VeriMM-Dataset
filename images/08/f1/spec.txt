Design a Verilog module for a simple Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (QAM) transmitter. The module should take in two 4-bit inputs representing the I (In-phase) and Q (Quadrature) components. The output should be a 8-bit signal representing the modulated QAM signal. The QAM signal is formed by combining the I and Q components. Ensure that the module has a clock input to synchronize the operation.
