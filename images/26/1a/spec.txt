Design a 3-input K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classifier that classifies the input based on the following criteria:  
- If the sum of the three inputs is less than or equal to 1, the output should be classified as '0'.  
- If the sum is greater than 1 but less than or equal to 3, the output should be classified as '1'.  
- If the sum is greater than 3, the output should be classified as '2'.  
The inputs are binary (0 or 1).
