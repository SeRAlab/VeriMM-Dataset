The output (Y) is determined by the value of sel: when sel is 00, output A; when sel is 01, output B; when sel is 10, output C; and when sel is 11, output D. The design utilizes a combination of logic gates, including inverters, NAND gates, AND gates, and an OR gate, to implement the selection logic and ensure that the correct input is routed to the output based on the selection signal.
