Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that accepts two 4-bit wide inputs (in0 and in1) and selects one of them as the output (out) based on a 2-bit selection signal (sel). When sel is 00, 01, 10, or 11, it will route the respective selected input through intermediate signals out0 to out3, ultimately determining the output. Note that each selector state redundantly evaluates the two inputs for which selected output exists, though only one output will be effectively routed at a time.
