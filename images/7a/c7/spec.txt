The counter increments on each clock cycle, and its output is defined by a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) configuration that generates new bits based on the XOR of the current highest and lowest bits. When the reset signal is inactive (high), the counter maintains its output structure and behavior, otherwise, it initializes the output and the temporary register to zero.
