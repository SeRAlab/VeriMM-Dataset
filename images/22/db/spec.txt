
Design a digital phase shifter that takes a 3-bit input representing the phase of a signal and outputs a 3-bit phase-shifted signal based on a 2-bit control input. The control input determines the amount of phase shift: 
00 for no shift, 
01 for a 90-degree shift, 
10 for a 180-degree shift, 
11 for a 270-degree shift. The phase shifts should wrap around the 3-bit output.
