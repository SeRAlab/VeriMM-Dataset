The flip-flop outputs two signals: Q, which represents the stored value, and Q_bar, the inverted output. On the positive edge of the clock, the input data is sampled, while on the negative edge, it transfers the previously sampled data to the output. The logic ensures that the outputs reflect the combined conditions of the sampled data and their complement state.

  input clk,
