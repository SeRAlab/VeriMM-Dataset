Design a control module that manages the routing of clock signals and reset signals to four different clock outputs (clk0, clk1, clk2, clk3) based on a 2-bit selection input (sell). This module takes an input clock (clk_in) and a reset signal (rst), and depending on the value of sell, it activates one of the four clock outputs while controlling the associated reset states and additional outputs (p0 to p2, p00 to p02, ps, pd, ph) accordingly. The output signals are set to specific values based on the selected case, facilitating a versatile clock management strategy in digital circuits. The selected output (sel_o) is also passed through for feedback.
module top_module(
	clk_in,clk0,clk1,clk2,clk3,
	rst,rst0,rst1,rst2,rst3,
  sel_o,sell,
  pa,pb,pc,p0,p1,p2,p00,p01,p02,ps,pd,ph,
  ddd,ddd0,df_0,df,
);
