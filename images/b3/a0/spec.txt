In the first stage, a barrel shifter shifts the input to the right by 8 bits, resulting in a 16-bit output. In the second stage, upon the rising edge of the clock, it extracts the upper 8 bits from the shifted output to form the `out_hi` and retains the lower 8 bits of the original input to form `out_lo`. Thus, the module outputs the high and low 8-bit segments as separate outputs.
