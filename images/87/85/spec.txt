Design a Verilog module based on the following description. Assume signals are positive clock/clk edge triggered unless otherwise stated.
Create a circuit that takes an input signal p and generates an output signal q. The input p is connected to an AND gate, an OR gate, and an XNOR gate. Each gate's output is connected to a D flip-flop. The outputs of the flip-flops are then fed into a three-input XOR gate to produce the output q. The second input of the AND gate is its corresponding flip-flop's output, the second input of the OR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's output, and the second input of the XNOR gate is its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output.
