Design a parallel loadable shift register that can operate under the control of a clock signal and an active-low clear signal. The module supports two modes of operation: it can load an 8-bit input when the load signal is high, or shift its contents right by one bit when the shift signal is high, with the least significant bit being outputted. When the clear signal is activated, the register resets its contents to zero. This design effectively combines the functionalities of a shift register with a load capability, facilitating versatile data handling in digital systems.
