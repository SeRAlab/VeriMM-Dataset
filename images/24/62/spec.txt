
Design a 4-bit Up/Down counter with the following specifications:
- The counter should increment when the up signal is high and decrement when the down signal is high.
- The counter should reset to 0 when the reset signal is high.
- The counter should wrap around when it exceeds its maximum value (15) or goes below its minimum value (0).
- The counter should have a clock signal for synchronization.
- The output should be a 4-bit value representing the current count.
