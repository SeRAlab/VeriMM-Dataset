Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four 4-bit wide input signals (A0, A1, A2, A3) based on a 2-bit selection input (M_select). When M_select is '00', output A0; '01' selects A1; '10' chooses A2; and '11' outputs A3. The module produces a 4-bit output (M_out) corresponding to the selected input.
module top_module(input [3:0]A0, A1, A2, A3 , input [1:0]M_select, output [3:0]M_out);
