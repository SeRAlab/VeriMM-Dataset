Design a 4-bit comparator that outputs three status signals based on the comparison of two 4-bit inputs, A and B. The outputs include `out_greater` indicating A is greater than B, `out_equal` indicating A is equal to B, and `out_less` indicating A is less than B. This module provides a way to determine the relational status of two 4-bit binary numbers.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] A,
  input [3:0] B,
  output reg out_greater,
  output reg out_equal,
  output reg out_less
);
