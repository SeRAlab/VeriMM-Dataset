The module takes inputs for reset, clock, clock enable, and a zero signal, as well as a 3-bit frequency selection signal defining the oscillation speed. It maintains a counter that counts up to a predetermined limit based on the selected frequency; when the counter reaches this limit, it triggers an increment of the output modulation value. The LFO stops operation when reset or disabled, enabling control over the modulation signal's frequency for applications such as audio synthesis or signal processing.
