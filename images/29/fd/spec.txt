
Create a Verilog module that simulates a simple SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) algorithm. The module should take in a 2-bit direction input (00 for North, 01 for East, 10 for South, 11 for West) and a 2-bit distance input, and update the current position (x, y) accordingly. The initial position is (0, 0). The output should be the current position (x, y) after applying the movement from the given direction and distance.
