The output X is high when the input A is asserted (active) and the SLEEP_B signal is not asserted (inactive), while also depending on the auxiliary power signals VPWR, KAGND, VPB, and VNB being all active. This module effectively acts as a power gate, ensuring that the power is only turned on under specific conditions.
module top_module(
    X,
    A,
    SLEEP_B,
    VPWR,
    KAGND,
    VPB,
    VNB
);
