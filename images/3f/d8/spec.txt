
Design a Verilog module that performs RSA encryption. The module should take a plaintext input, an exponent (e), and a modulus (n), and output the ciphertext. The encryption formula is \( C = P^e \mod n \), where P is the plaintext, e is the public exponent, n is the modulus, and C is the ciphertext. The exponentiation should be implemented using a simple square-and-multiply algorithm for efficiency.
