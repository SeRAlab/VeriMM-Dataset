Design a clock gating circuit that controls the propagation of the clock signal based on enable and test signals. When the enable (EN) signal is active and the test enable (TE) signal is inactive, the circuit passes the input clock (CLK) to the output (ENCLK). If either EN is inactive or TE is active, the output clock is set to LOW, effectively disabling the clock signal to downstream components. This design is useful for power management in digital systems, allowing for selective activation of clock signals to reduce power consumption.
