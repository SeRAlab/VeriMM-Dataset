
Design a simple clock gating circuit using Verilog. The circuit should include an active-low enable signal that controls the clock signal to a D flip-flop. When the enable signal is low, the clock should be gated and the flip-flop should hold its previous state. When the enable signal is high, the flip-flop should respond to the clock signal. The flip-flop should accept a 1-bit input and output the stored value.
