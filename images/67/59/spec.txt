Design a 4-bit binary adder that takes three 4-bit inputs (a, b, and c) and produces a 5-bit output (sum). The module computes the sum of the three inputs and provides the result, ensuring that the carry out from the addition is represented in the highest bit of the output. The output is structured to accommodate the possible overflow resulting from the addition of three 4-bit numbers.
module top_module(
    input [3:0] a,
    input [3:0] b,
    input [3:0] c,
    output [4:0] sum
);
