
Design a sample rate converter that takes an input clock signal and an input data stream, and produces an output data stream at a different sample rate. The converter should support a sample rate conversion factor of 2 (downsampling) or 0.5 (upsampling). The input data will be 8 bits wide, and the output will also be 8 bits wide. The design should handle the clock domain crossing properly and include a simple state machine to manage the conversion process.
