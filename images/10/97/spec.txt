
Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency based on the current power mode. The controller has a register that holds the current mode (0 for low power, 1 for normal power). When the mode changes, the voltage and frequency outputs should change accordingly:
- Mode 0: Voltage = 1V, Frequency = 1GHz
- Mode 1: Voltage = 2V, Frequency = 2GHz
The controller should also respond to a reset signal that sets the mode to 0.
