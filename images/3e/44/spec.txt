Design an 8-to-3 priority encoder in Verilog. The encoder should take 8 input lines (I0 to I7) and produce a 3-bit output (Y2, Y1, Y0). The output should be the binary representation of the highest priority input that is active (i.e., high). If no inputs are active, the output should be '000'. The priority is assigned from I7 (highest) to I0 (lowest).
