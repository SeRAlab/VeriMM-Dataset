Specifically, the JRControl output is asserted HIGH (1) when the combined 6-bit signal formed by concatenating the 2-bit alu_op and 4-bit funct equals the specific binary value representing the junior instruction, which is 6'b001000. Otherwise, the output is LOW (0).
module top_module( input[1:0] alu_op, 
       input [3:0] funct,
       output JRControl
    );
