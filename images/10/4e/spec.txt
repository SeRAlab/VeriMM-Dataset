Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on the 3-bit control input: 
0: ADD
1: SUBTRACT
2: AND
3: OR
4: XOR
5: NOT
6: NAND
7: NOR
8: XNOR
9: SHIFT LEFT (logical)
10: SHIFT RIGHT (logical)
11: SHIFT LEFT (arithmetic)
12: SHIFT RIGHT (arithmetic)
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and produce a 32-bit output (result).
