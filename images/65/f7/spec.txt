Design a tone generator module that produces a square wave output signal based on the selected octave and frequency input. The module uses a counter to generate timing pulses determined by the octave, with each octave scaling the counter's maximum value. When the count reaches the configured pulse frequency, it toggles the output signal. Additionally, the module generates a separate pulse output that indicates when a timing pulse occurs. This design emulates the behavior of a tone generator found in sound synthesis applications.
module top_module(input wire clk,input wire [2:0] octave,input wire [8:0] freq,output reg out,output reg pulseout);
