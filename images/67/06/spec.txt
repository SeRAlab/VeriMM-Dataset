Design a 4-bit shift register circuit with bidirectional shifting capabilities. The module shifts its contents to the right on each rising edge of the clock when the shift signal is high, inserting the input value at the leftmost position. Conversely, when the shift signal is low, it shifts left, pushing the current input value into the rightmost position. The output is the least significant bit of the register.
