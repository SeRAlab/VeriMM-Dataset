The module takes two 8-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, and adds them along with a carry from the previous operation, outputting a 9-bit sum. The addition occurs on the rising edge of the clock signal, and if the reset signal is active, both the sum and carry are initialized to zero. This implementation allows for sequential arithmetic operations while managing carry propagation between additions.
