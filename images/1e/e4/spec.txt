Design a simple DVFS controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage levels based on a 2-bit input representing performance levels. The controller has two outputs: one for frequency and one for voltage. The mapping is as follows:  
- 00: Frequency = 1 GHz, Voltage = 1.0 V  
- 01: Frequency = 2 GHz, Voltage = 1.2 V  
- 10: Frequency = 3 GHz, Voltage = 1.4 V  
- 11: Frequency = 4 GHz, Voltage = 1.6 V
