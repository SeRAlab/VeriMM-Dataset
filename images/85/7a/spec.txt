Design a 4-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that operates based on a 2-bit mode selection input. The ALU can perform four operations: addition, bitwise AND, comparison for greater-than, and right bitwise shifting of the first operand, A, based on the second operand, B. The selected operation is determined by the MODE input, and the corresponding output is an 8-bit result. The module uses various enable signals to selectively activate the results of each operation based on the mode, ensuring that only the output of the desired operation is presented on the OUT signal.
