
Design a Verilog module that implements a basic 16-QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation) demodulator. The module should take a 4-bit input representing the received signal and output a corresponding 2-bit symbol for the in-phase (I) and quadrature (Q) components. The demodulator will map the 4-bit input to one of the 16 symbols based on predefined mapping.
