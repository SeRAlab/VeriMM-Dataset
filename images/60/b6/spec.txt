Design a 64-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer. The module takes two 64-bit inputs, in0 and in1, and a select signal (sel). Depending on the value of sel, the output (out) will be assigned to either in0 or in1, with a propagation delay of 2 time units for switching between the inputs. When sel is 0, out reflects in0; when sel is 1, out reflects in1.
