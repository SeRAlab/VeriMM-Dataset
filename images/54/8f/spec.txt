Design a frequency divider circuit that generates a 6-bit LED output and a 2-bit user I/O signal from a 60 MHz clock input. The circuit increments a register every rising edge of the clock, effectively dividing the input frequency. The most significant 5 bits of the register are inverted and assigned to the LED output, while the 2 bits representing the next lower significant bits are routed to the user I/O, providing a visual and interactive representation of the clock divider's state.
module top_module(input clk_60mhz, output [5:0] led, output [1:0] user_io);
