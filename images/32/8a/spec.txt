Design a simple Stop-and-Wait protocol in Verilog. The protocol should have a sender and a receiver. The sender sends a single bit over a channel and waits for an acknowledgment (ACK) from the receiver. If the ACK is received, the sender can send the next bit. If not, it will resend the bit. The sender should have a toggle signal to indicate a new bit to send. The receiver should acknowledge the received bit with an ACK signal.
