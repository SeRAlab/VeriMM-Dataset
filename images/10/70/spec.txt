
Design a Verilog module that performs bilinear interpolation on a 2x2 pixel grid. The module should take four inputs representing the pixel values (I00, I01, I10, I11) and two fractional inputs (frac_x, frac_y) representing the interpolation factors in the range of 0 to 1. The output of the module should be the interpolated pixel value (I_out). 
The bilinear interpolation formula is:
I_out = (1 - frac_x) * (1 - frac_y) * I00 + (1 - frac_x) * frac_y * I01 + frac_x * (1 - frac_y) * I10 + frac_x * frac_y * I11.
