

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that will take in two input signals and output their sum. The inputs are both 8-bit unsigned binary numbers represented as `in1` and `in2`. The output should also be an 8-bit unsigned binary number represented as `out`. You can assume that the inputs will always be valid and that overflow will not occur.

Create a module named `adder` that takes in two 8-bit inputs and outputs their sum.
