Design a circuit that implements an asynchronous reset with a delay release feature. When the reset input is activated, the output remains LOW and a 4-bit counter begins counting clock cycles up to a defined delay period. After the specified delay, the output transitions to HIGH, thereby releasing the circuit from the reset state. This design is useful for controlling system initialization sequences, ensuring that the system does not activate until a certain condition has been met after a reset.
