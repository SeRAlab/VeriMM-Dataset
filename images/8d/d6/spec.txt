Develop a four-bit multiplier circuit that computes the product of two 4-bit binary numbers, x and y. The module processes the inputs to generate an 8-bit output called product. It achieves this by performing bitwise AND operations for each bit of the inputs, effectively calculating partial products and combining them to form the final result. The design uses temporary wire assignments to hold intermediate calculation results before outputting the complete 8-bit product.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] x,
  input [3:0] y,
  output [7:0] product
);
