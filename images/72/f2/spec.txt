Design a JK flip-flop with asynchronous reset and enable functionality. The flip-flop toggles its output Q on the rising edge of the clock signal (C) based on the inputs J, K, and the enable signal (E). When E is low or the reset signal (R) is low, the flip-flop resets Q to 0 and Qn to 1. If both J and K are high, the output Q toggles from its previous state. If J is high and K is low, the output sets to 1, and if J is low and K is high, the output resets to 0. Qn is always the complement of Q.
