
Design a simple 2-bit branch predictor with two bits of state for each entry. The states are defined as follows:
- 00: Strongly Not Taken
- 01: Weakly Not Taken
- 10: Weakly Taken
- 11: Strongly Taken

The predictor takes a 2-bit address and a signal representing whether the branch was taken or not, and updates its state based on the actual outcome after a prediction. The output should be the prediction for the next branch outcome based on the current state.
