
Design a combinational logic circuit that performs a Monte Carlo simulation for estimating the value of Pi. The circuit should take two inputs: `x` and `y`, both of 8 bits, representing random points in the unit square [0, 1). The output should be a 1-bit signal `inside_circle` that indicates whether the point (x, y) lies inside the unit circle. The condition to check is: x^2 + y^2 < 1. The circuit should also output a 10-bit counter `count` that counts how many points lie inside the circle after 5 clock cycles.
