
Design a simple branch predictor using a 2-bit saturating counter. The branch predictor will predict whether a branch will be taken or not based on the previous outcomes. The states of the counter are:
00 - Strongly Not Taken
01 - Weakly Not Taken
10 - Weakly Taken
11 - Strongly Taken

The predictor should take a branch input (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) and update the counter based on the actual branch outcome. The output should indicate the prediction (0 for not taken, 1 for taken) based on the counter state.
