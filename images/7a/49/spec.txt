Design a circuit that performs a 4-bit multiplication operation on the least significant 4 bits of two 8-bit inputs, IOA and IOB. The module has a clock and reset input; upon reset, it initializes the output IOE to zero. On each positive clock edge, it computes the product of the lower 4 bits of IOA and IOB, storing the result in the least significant 4 bits of the output IOE, while the upper 4 bits of IOE are set to zero.
