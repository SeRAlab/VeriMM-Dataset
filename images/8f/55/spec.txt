The output Y is determined such that when S0 and S1 are 00, A0 is selected; when S0 is 01, A1 is selected; when S0 is 10, A2 is selected; and when S0 and S1 are 11, A3 is selected.

    Y,
    A0,
    A1,
    A2,
    A3,
    S0,
    S1
);
