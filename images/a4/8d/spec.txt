Design a 4-bit binary counter that increments on the rising edge of a clock signal and resets to zero when a reset signal is asserted. The counter outputs a value that wraps around after reaching a maximum of 15 (binary 1111). Additionally, it incorporates a 2-to-1 multiplexer that selects between a constant zero value and the current count output, based on the least significant bit of the count, serving as a simple selector mechanism within the module.

    input clk,
    input rst,
