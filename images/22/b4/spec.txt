Design a Verilog module that implements the Point Addition operation of Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) over a finite field defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) as inputs and produce a third point (x3, y3) as output. The module should handle the special case of point doubling when the two input points are the same. Assume the inputs are valid points on the curve.
