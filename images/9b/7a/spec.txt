The module takes in two signed 16-bit inputs: the microphone signal (mic_in) and the speaker output signal (spkr_in). It employs a configurable number of filter taps (N) to create an adaptive filter that updates its coefficients based on the error calculated from the difference between the filtered microphone input and the filtered speaker output. The output is a processed signal intended to reduce echo by adapting filter coefficients over time, effectively enhancing the clarity of the microphone input in the presence of speaker audio.
