Design a simple 2-bit digital phase shifter that takes a 2-bit input phase and produces a 2-bit output phase that is shifted based on the input. The phase shifter should have the following behavior:  
- 00 -> 00  
- 01 -> 10  
- 10 -> 11  
- 11 -> 00  
The phase shifter wraps around the values according to the specified transformations.
