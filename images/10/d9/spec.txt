Design a 16-bit shift register with synchronous load and shift operations. The shift register should have the following inputs: a 16-bit data input (`data_in`), a clock signal (`clk`), a load signal (`load`), and a shift signal (`shift`). When the `load` signal is high, the register should load `data_in` on the rising edge of `clk`. When the `shift` signal is high, the register should shift its contents to the right on the rising edge of `clk`. The output should be the current value of the shift register (`data_out`).
