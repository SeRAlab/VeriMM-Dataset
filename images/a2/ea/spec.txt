Design a circuit that implements a priority encoder followed by a multiplexer and decoder system. The priority encoder receives an 8-bit input and determines the position of the highest set bit, outputting the 3-bit position value. The multiplexer selects one of the 256 possible 8-bit inputs based on a 3-bit select signal. The decoder interprets a 2-bit enable signal to activate one of four outputs, which can receive the multiplexed data based on the enabled condition. The final outputs include four 8-bit buses that are controlled by the enable decoder's selection, allowing for dynamic routing of the selected multiplexer output.
