Design a simple state machine that simulates the process of a robot moving in a grid and mapping its environment. The robot can be in one of four states: 'IDLE', 'MOVE', 'SCAN', or 'RETURN'. The state machine should respond to the following inputs: 'start' (to begin the process), 'scan' (to perform a scan of the surroundings), and 'return' (to return to the starting position). The output should indicate the robot's current state.
