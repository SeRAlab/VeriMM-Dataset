
Design a simple Turbo Encoder in Verilog. The Turbo Encoder takes a 5-bit input data and produces a 10-bit encoded output using two convolutional encoders in parallel and an interleaver. The first convolutional encoder has a coding rate of 1/2. The output of the first encoder is interleaved and passed to the second convolutional encoder, which operates on the interleaved data.
