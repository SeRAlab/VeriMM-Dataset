The module has states for IDLE, TRANSMIT, RECEIVE, and DONE, controlling the MOSI (Master Out Slave In), MISO (Master In Slave Out), chip select (CS), and clock (SCLK) signals based on inputs and internal state transitions. Upon completion of the data exchange, the module correctly toggles the CS signal and returns to the IDLE state.
