Design a 64-bit ALU that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode: 
0: ADD 
1: SUB 
2: AND 
3: OR 
4: XOR 
5: NOT (only applies to the first operand) 
6: NAND 
7: NOR. 
The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs and output a 64-bit result. Also, include a zero flag that indicates if the result is zero.
