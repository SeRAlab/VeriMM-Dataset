Design a logic circuit that implements two operations. The second output, y2, is the negation of the logical XOR operation of inputs a and b, yielding y2 as HIGH when both inputs are the same (both are either HIGH or LOW).
module top_module(
  input a,
  input b,
  input c,
  output reg y1,
  output reg y2
);
