 Design a fixed-point adder that takes two 8-bit fixed-point numbers and produces an 8-bit fixed-point sum. The two inputs are both represented in 2's complement format, with the 4 most significant bits representing the integer part and the 4 least significant bits representing the fractional part. The output should also be in 2's complement format. The design should handle overflow correctly by saturating the output to the maximum or minimum fixed-point value if overflow occurs.
