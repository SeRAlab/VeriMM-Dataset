

You are tasked with designing a Verilog module that implements a 4-bit adder-subtractor. The module should have two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a 1-bit input, SUB. When SUB is 0, the module should perform addition, and when SUB is 1, the module should perform subtraction. The module should output a 4-bit sum/difference, S, and a 1-bit overflow, OVF.

Your module should be named "adder_subtractor" and should be contained within 200 lines of Verilog code. Your design should adhere to the following requirements:

- Your design should use only basic Verilog constructs (i.e., no system tasks or functions).
- You may use any combination of gates and flip-flops, but you may not use any pre-designed adder or subtractor modules.
- Your design should be synthesizable and should not contain any latches or gated clocks.
- Your design should be fully tested to ensure correct functionality.
