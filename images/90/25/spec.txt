Please act as a professional verilog designer.

Design a Verilog module for a 4-bit binary counter with a synchronous reset. The counter increments its value on the rising edge of the clock and resets to 0 when the reset signal is active.

Module name:  
    counter_4bit  

Input ports:
    clk: Chip clock signal.
    rst: Synchronous reset signal.

Output ports:
    count: Counter output signal with a data width of 4 bits.

Implementation:
Counter operation:
On every positive edge of the clock signal (clk), the counter (count) is reset if the reset signal (rst) is active. Otherwise, it increments its value.
