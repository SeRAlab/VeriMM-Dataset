Design a finite state machine that controls a data communication process based on a 10-bit instruction input. The module interprets the two most significant bits of the instruction to determine the operation mode: if the bits indicate a "write data" command, it sets chip select (cs) low and data/command (dc) high; for a "write command," cs remains low and dc low; for a "begin delay" command, it sets cs high and enables a delay with the delay enable signal. The lower eight bits of the instruction are directly routed to an output parallel data bus.
