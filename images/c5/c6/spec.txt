

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that implements a 4-bit magnitude comparator. The module should have two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and two outputs, EQ and GT. EQ should be high if A is equal to B, and GT should be high if A is greater than B. Otherwise, both outputs should be low. You may assume that the inputs are unsigned and that the module should be synthesizable.

Create a module named "magnitude_comparator" that implements the above functionality.
