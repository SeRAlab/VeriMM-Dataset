Design a 3x3 Gaussian blur filter in Verilog. The filter should take a 3x3 pixel block as input and output a single pixel value, which is the result of applying the Gaussian blur. The Gaussian weights for the filter are as follows:  
| 1 | 2 | 1 |  
| 2 | 4 | 2 |  
| 1 | 2 | 1 |  
The output value is calculated by multiplying each pixel by its corresponding weight, summing the results, and then dividing by 16 (the sum of the weights).
