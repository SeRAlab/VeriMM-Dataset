
Create a DVFS controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency levels based on the performance requirement input. The controller should take a performance level input (in 2 bits) and output the corresponding voltage (in 3 bits) and frequency (in 3 bits). The mapping is as follows:
00 -> V: 3'b000, F: 3'b000 (Lowest)
01 -> V: 3'b010, F: 3'b010 
10 -> V: 3'b100, F: 3'b100 
11 -> V: 3'b111, F: 3'b111 (Highest)
The controller should retain the last values for V and F until a new performance level is received.
