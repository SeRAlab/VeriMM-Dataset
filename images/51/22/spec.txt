Design a clock gating module with a D flip-flop and a transparent latch. The module captures input data on the rising edge of the CLK signal if the enable (EN) signal is asserted, and it stores the value in the flip-flop. Additionally, a transparent latch is employed to pass the flip-flop's output (Q) to the output (ENCLK) whenever the test enable (TE) signal is active, effectively controlling the clock signal. If TE is deasserted and EN is asserted, ENCLK follows the CLK signal; otherwise, ENCLK outputs LOW. This design enhances power efficiency by gating the clock based on the enable conditions.
