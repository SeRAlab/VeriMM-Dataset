

You are tasked with creating a module that takes in a 4-bit input signal `V` and outputs a 4-bit signal `A`. The module should be named `circuitA`. The output `A` is determined by the following rules:

- The least significant bit of `A` is equal to the least significant bit of `V`.
- The second least significant bit of `A` is the logical NOT of the second least significant bit of `V`.
- The third least significant bit of `A` is determined by the following logic expression: `((NOT V[3]) AND (NOT V[1])) OR ((V[2]) AND (V[1]))`.
- The most significant bit of `A` is determined by the following logic expression: `(NOT V[3]) AND (V[1])`.

Write a Verilog module that implements this logic and test your module with the provided testbench.
