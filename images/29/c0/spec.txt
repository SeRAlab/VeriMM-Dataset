Design an 8-bit ADC (Analog to Digital Converter) that takes a 3-bit binary input representing voltage levels and produces an 8-bit digital output. The ADC should map the 3-bit input values (0 to 7) to 8 different output values, for example, mapping 0 to 0x00, 1 to 0x33, 2 to 0x66, 3 to 0x99, 4 to 0xCC, 5 to 0xFF, 6 to 0xFF, and 7 to 0xFF. Implement the mapping logic in Verilog.
