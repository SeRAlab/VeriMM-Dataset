Design a digital circuit that operates as a controlled address generator influenced by a clock input. On each rising edge of clk3, if the enable signal is active, the circuit increments a location counter and evaluates a condition comparing the input cd3 against the input maxval. When the counter is at zero and cd3 exceeds maxval, it sets addr_temp with the current location value concatenated with three zero bits, and increments the counter. The counter, which can count up to ten, resets after reaching that limit, facilitating multiple address calculations over time based on the state of the enable signal and the comparison results.
module top_module(input clk3,input enable,input [15:0]maxval,input [15:0]cd3,output reg [31:0] addr_temp);
