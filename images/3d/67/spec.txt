Design a 32-bit ALU that performs the following operations based on a 3-bit opcode input:  
000 - ADD  
001 - SUBTRACT  
010 - AND  
011 - OR  
100 - XOR  
101 - NAND  
110 - NOR  
111 - XNOR  
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and produce a 32-bit output (result) based on the operation defined by the opcode. Additionally, an output flag (zero) should be set to 1 if the result is zero, otherwise it should be 0.
