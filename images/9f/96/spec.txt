The module calculates the current and desired positions, computes the error, and generates PWM signals based on the desired position, constrained within specified minimum and maximum duty cycles. The output PWM signal allows for precise control of the servo motor's position, ensuring smooth operation while adhering to defined bounds on duty cycle.
