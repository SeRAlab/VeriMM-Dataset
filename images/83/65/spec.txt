Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four data inputs (D0, D1, D2, D3) based on the two control signals (S0 and S1). The output (Y) reflects the selected input corresponding to the binary value represented by S0 and S1, where S0 is the least significant bit. When both select signals are low, D0 is output; when S0 is high and S1 is low, D1 is selected; when S0 is low and S1 is high, D2 is chosen; and when both select signals are high, D3 is directed to the output.
module top_module(
    Y,
    D0,
    D1,
    D2,
    D3,
    S0,
    S1
);
