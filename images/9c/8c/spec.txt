Design a circuit that functions as an oscillator using a counter to generate three specific output frequencies derived from the clock input. The circuit increments a 17-bit counter on each rising edge of the clock, and it can be reset asynchronously. The outputs are square waves at 64, 128, and 256 Hz, extracted from specific bits of the counter's value, specifically the 15th, 14th, and 13th bits. When the reset signal is active, the counter is cleared to zero, ensuring that the outputs can be restarted.
