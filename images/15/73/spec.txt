Design a simple 2D Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifier in Verilog. The SVM will classify points in a 2D plane based on their coordinates. The input will be two 8-bit values representing the x and y coordinates of a point. The classifier will output a single bit representing the class of the point (0 or 1). The decision boundary will be defined by the equation `y = mx + b`, where `m` is the slope and `b` is the y-intercept. For this problem, use fixed values for `m` and `b` (for example, `m = 1` and `b = 0`).
