Design a simple 4-bit calculator that performs addition and subtraction based on the operation selected by a 2-bit input. When the `op` input is 00, it computes the sum of inputs `a` and `b`. When `op` is 01, it performs the subtraction of `b` from `a`. For any other value of `op`, the output defaults to zero.
