Design a simple packet filtering circuit that decides whether to accept or reject packets based on a set of predefined rules. Each packet has a 16-bit address and a 4-bit protocol type. The circuit should maintain a state that keeps track of whether the last packet was accepted or rejected. If a packet matches a predefined rule (e.g., specific address and protocol type), it should be accepted, otherwise rejected. The output should indicate the acceptance or rejection of the packet.
