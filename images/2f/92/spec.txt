Design a SR flip-flop with asynchronous reset and set inputs. The flip-flop should have two outputs, Q and Qn, where Q is the output and Qn is the inverted output. The flip-flop should respond to the Set and Reset inputs when they are high, and it should follow the S and R inputs when both are low. If both S and R are high, the output is considered invalid (or should remain unchanged). The design should include a clock input to synchronize the flip-flop operation.
