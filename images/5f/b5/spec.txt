Design a 10-bit binary counter that outputs a 2-bit selector signal based on its count value. The counter increments on the rising edge of the clock unless paused, in which case it retains its current state. The output selector 'sel' is determined by the most significant bits of the count. The count can operate in two modes: standard mode, where it uses bits [9:8] for the selector, and quick mode, activated by the 'quick' input, which uses bits [8:7]. When the counter reaches a maximum of 1023, it resets to zero, allowing continuous cycling of the counter and corresponding selector output.
