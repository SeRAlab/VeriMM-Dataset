Design a voltage and frequency scaling controller that adjusts the operating frequency of a system based on a 2-bit voltage level input. The controller should have a 1-bit enable signal that determines whether the system is in DVFS mode. If enabled, the output frequency should change based on the voltage input. The 2-bit voltage input can represent four levels: 00 (lowest frequency), 01 (medium frequency), 10 (high frequency), and 11 (maximum frequency).
