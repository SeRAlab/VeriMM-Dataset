Design a circuit that performs unsigned division of two 4-bit binary numbers, specifically yielding a 4-bit quotient and a 4-bit remainder. The module takes in a 4-bit dividend and a 4-bit divisor as inputs. Upon receiving a clock edge, if the divisor is non-zero, it iteratively shifts the dividend and calculates the quotient and remainder based on the division operation, simultaneously providing the results as outputs.
module top_module(
    input clk,
    input [3:0] dividend,
    input [3:0] divisor,
    output reg [3:0] quotient,
    output reg [3:0] remainder
);
