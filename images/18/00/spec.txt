Design a 2-channel DMA controller that can switch between two channels based on a control signal. The controller should have the following inputs: clk (clock), reset (active high), req1 (request from channel 1), req2 (request from channel 2), and it should output: grant1 (grant to channel 1) and grant2 (grant to channel 2). The channel with the request should be granted access to the bus based on a round-robin scheduling approach.
