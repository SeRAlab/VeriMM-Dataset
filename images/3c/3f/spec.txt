Design a Verilog module that implements a simple SLAM algorithm to maintain the current position and map of a moving object. The module should take in a series of sensor inputs indicating movement (X and Y displacements) and produce the current position as output. The inputs will be in the form of two 8-bit signed integers representing the displacements, and the output will be a 16-bit signed integer representing the current position in a compact format (using 8 bits for X and 8 bits for Y).
