
Design a combinational circuit for Huffman encoding. The circuit should take an 8-bit binary input and produce a 3-bit Huffman encoded output based on the frequency of occurrence of certain characters. The mapping of characters to their encoded outputs is predetermined. For this problem, you can assume the following mappings:
- 000: A
- 001: B
- 010: C
- 011: D
- 100: E
- 101: F
- 110: G
- 111: H

You must build a combinational logic circuit to encode a character based on its 8-bit binary representation.
