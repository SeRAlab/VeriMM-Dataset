Design a simple Verilog module that implements a low dropout regulator (LDO) by simulating a basic voltage regulation logic. The module should take an input voltage (Vin) and a control signal (enable) to produce an output voltage (Vout) while ensuring that Vout is regulated based on Vin and the enable signal. When enable is high, Vout should be equal to Vin; when enable is low, Vout should be zero.
