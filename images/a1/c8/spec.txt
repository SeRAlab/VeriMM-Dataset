Design a T flip-flop with asynchronous clear functionality. The flip-flop toggles its output, `q`, on the rising edge of the clock (`Clk`) when the T input is high. Upon activation of the `Clear` signal (when low), the flip-flop resets `q` to 0 and `qbar` to 1. The output `qbar` is always the inverse of `q`.
module top_module(
    input t,Clk,Clear,
    output reg q,qbar
    );
