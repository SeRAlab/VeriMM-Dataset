
Design a 2-bit digital phase shifter that can shift the input phase by 0, 90, 180, or 270 degrees based on a control signal. The phase shift should be represented in 2 bits (00 for 0°, 01 for 90°, 10 for 180°, and 11 for 270°). The phase shifter should take a clock signal and a reset signal, and output the shifted value based on the input signal and control signals.
