Upon receiving a write request, the FIFO stores the incoming data at the head pointer's position, updates the head pointer, and tracks the number of utilized entries. Conversely, on a read request, it outputs the data at the tail pointer's position, updates the tail pointer, and decrements the count of used entries. The design effectively manages data flow and ensures that operations remain within the FIFO's defined boundaries.
