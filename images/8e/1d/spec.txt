Design a universal shift register that can perform various operations based on a 2-bit select input. When select is `00`, the output remains unchanged; with `01`, it loads a new 8-bit input signal; with `10`, it performs a left shift by inserting a new bit on the least significant end and outputs the shifted-out bit; and with `11`, it performs a right shift by inserting a new bit on the most significant end. The functionality is synchronized with a clock signal, and the module generates both an 8-bit output and a serial output.
