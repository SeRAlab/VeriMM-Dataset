
Design a FIFO (First-In-First-Out) queue with a depth of 4. The FIFO should have the following functionalities:
1. `enqueue` operation to add data to the queue.
2. `dequeue` operation to remove data from the queue.
3. Control signals for `write_enable` and `read_enable` to control the enqueue and dequeue operations respectively.
4. A signal `full` indicating when the queue is full and `empty` indicating when the queue is empty.
The data width should be 8 bits.
