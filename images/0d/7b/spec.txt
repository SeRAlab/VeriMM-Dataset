Design a 2-bit divider that takes a 2-bit dividend and a 2-bit divisor as inputs and outputs the quotient and remainder. The output should be defined as follows: `quotient` is the integer result of dividing the dividend by the divisor and `remainder` is the leftover value after the division. Make sure to handle the case where the divisor is zero by setting the quotient to 2'b00 and the remainder to the dividend value.
