Design a 3-bit binary counter that increments on each clock cycle and resets when the reset signal is activated. The counter counts from 0 to 6 (representing states 0 to 6) and wraps back to 0 after reaching 6. The output is determined by a combinational logic that maps specific counter values to predefined outputs: it outputs the binary values of 0 to 3 directly, outputs 6 when the counter is at 4, outputs 5 when the counter is at 5, and outputs 7 when the counter is at 6.
