Design a clock gating cell that controls the propagation of a clock signal based on an enable input. When the clock signal goes high (on the positive edge), if the enable signal is asserted, the output clock will follow the input clock; otherwise, the output will be driven LOW, effectively halting the clock signal. This design is typically used to reduce power consumption in digital circuits by disabling the clock to certain components when they are not in use.

  input clk,
  input enable,
