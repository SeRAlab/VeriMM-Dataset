Design a synchronous up/down counter with a set input. When the load signal (L) is high, the counter output (Q) is loaded with the value from R. When the enable signal (E) is active, the counter increments or decrements based on the state of the up_down signal; if up_down is high, the counter increments by 1, otherwise it decrements by 1. The counter operates on the rising edge of the Clock signal and is parameterized to handle n-bit wide values, defaulting to 8 bits.
