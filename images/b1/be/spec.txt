Develop a filter circuit that processes an 8-bit input (DIN) and produces a 3-bit output (OPOS) and a validity signal (VALID). The filtering mechanism adjusts the contents of a 7-bit register based on the 3-bit address input (IPOS), effectively shifting the bits of DIN left and appending zeros, depending on the chosen position. The circuit checks the resulting modified data to determine the status of OPOS bits, ultimately indicating whether the filtered data is valid (VALID) when it is not equal to zero. Additionally, if the INIT signal is activated, it directly outputs the DIN data.
