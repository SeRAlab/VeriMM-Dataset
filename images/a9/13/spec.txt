Design a simple stopwatch circuit that counts milliseconds based on clock input and a user-interactive key. The circuit maintains a count of clock cycles in a 32-bit register, with a parameter defining the clock ticks per millisecond. The stopwatch enters different states based on the key press, allowing it to start counting, pause, or reset back to the initial count. When in the first or second state, the current clock cycles are incremented; in the third state, no action is taken, and in the fourth state, it resets to the clock count at the first key press. The output reflects the elapsed time in milliseconds derived from the accumulated clock cycles.
