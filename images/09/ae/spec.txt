
Design a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a system based on the workload. The controller takes a workload signal that varies from 0 to 100 (representing percentage of workload) and adjusts the output frequency and voltage accordingly. The output frequency should be one of the three levels (1GHz, 2GHz, 3GHz) and the voltage should be one of the three levels (1V, 1.5V, 2V). The scaling logic should be defined as follows:
- Workload 0-33%: Frequency = 1GHz, Voltage = 1V
- Workload 34-66%: Frequency = 2GHz, Voltage = 1.5V
- Workload 67-100%: Frequency = 3GHz, Voltage = 2V
