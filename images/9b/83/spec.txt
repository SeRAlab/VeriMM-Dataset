Design a transparent D latch with asynchronous reset functionality. The circuit captures the value of input D when the enable signal G is high. If the reset signal RN is asserted low, the output Q is forced to low, and QN is forced to high, regardless of the states of D or G. Otherwise, when G is low, the latch maintains its current state, outputting a low for Q and high for QN until G goes high again. This module ensures that the output can be controlled both by the data input and an override reset signal.
