Design a voice activity detection (VAD) module that monitors incoming audio signals to determine the presence of speech. This module takes a signed 16-bit audio input and processes it using a sliding window approach to calculate the energy of the audio samples. By maintaining a circular buffer of energy values derived from overlapping windows of audio input, the module sums these energies and checks against a specified threshold. If the summed energy exceeds the threshold, it signals that speech is detected. This functionality is essential for applications requiring speech recognition or audio processing where distinguishing between speech and silence is necessary.
module top_module(
  input signed [15:0] audio_in,
  output reg speech_detected
);
