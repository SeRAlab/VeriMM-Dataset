Design a round-robin arbiter that grants access to one of four requesters (R0, R1, R2, R3) in a round-robin manner. Each requester sends a request signal, and the arbiter grants the access signal (G0, G1, G2, G3) to one of the requesters based on a round-robin algorithm. The arbiter should remember the last requester that was granted access and start from the next one in the next clock cycle.
