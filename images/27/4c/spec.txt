Design a Verilog module for a Run-Length Encoding (RLE) encoder. The RLE encoder should take a stream of binary data as input and output a compressed version of that data, where consecutive identical bits are represented by their value and the count of their occurrences. The input will be a binary stream of 8 bits at a time, and the output should be in the form of a 16-bit signal, where the first 8 bits are the value and the last 8 bits are the count. The state must store the last bit and the count of consecutive bits.
