The output X is high (1) if either A1 is true while A2 is false, or A2 is true, A1 is false, A3 is true, and B1 is false, or all of A1, A2, and A3 are false while B1 is true. Otherwise, the output X will be low (0).
module top_module(
    X,
    A1,
    A2,
    A3,
    B1
);
