Design a fan speed controller that adjusts the speed of a fan based on temperature readings. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The controller should take a 4-bit input representing the temperature (from 0 to 15) and output a 2-bit signal representing the fan speed. The mapping should be as follows: temperatures from 0 to 5 result in low speed (00), from 6 to 10 result in medium speed (01), and from 11 to 15 result in high speed (10). The output should change based on the temperature input, and the controller should hold its state until the next temperature input is given.
