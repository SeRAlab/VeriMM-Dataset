
Design a 64-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit operation code (opcode):
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
- 101: Increment
- 110: Decrement
- 111: Logical Shift Left (LSL)

The ALU should take two 64-bit inputs (A and B), a 3-bit opcode, and produce a 64-bit output (result) along with a carry-out signal for addition and subtraction operations.
