Design a D flip-flop with asynchronous reset functionality. On the rising edge of the clock (CLK), the flip-flop captures the value of input D and outputs it on Q, while output Q_N is the complement of Q. If the asynchronous reset signal (SET_B) is low, the flip-flop resets Q to 0 and Q_N to 1, regardless of the clock. The design includes power supply inputs (VPWR and VGND) for proper operation.
