Design a baseband QPSK (Quadrature Phase Shift Keying) demodulator. This module takes two signed 16-bit inputs representing the in-phase (re) and quadrature (im) components of the signal and outputs a 2-bit decision based on the quadrant where the received signal falls. The functionality includes determining if each component is less than, equal to, or greater than zero to create an address for a lookup table (LUT). The LUT translates the quadrant information into a hard decision output, which indicates the transmitted symbol.
