Design a pulse-width modulation (PWM) controller that generates a square wave signal on the speaker output based on a configurable slow rate input. The module increments a counter on each clock cycle and toggles the speaker output when the counter matches the specified slow rate value. If the slow rate changes, the counter and speaker state are reset. When the slow rate is zero, the output remains low, effectively disabling the PWM signal.
