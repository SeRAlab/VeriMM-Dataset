Design a 128-bit Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG) using a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) with a polynomial of x^128 + x^7 + x^6 + x^5 + 1. The generator should be able to initialize with a seed and produce a new pseudo-random number on each clock cycle. The output should be a 128-bit value.
