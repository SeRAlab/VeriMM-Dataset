Design a module for sales data processing that scales four separate 4-bit input counts by specific fixed factors. Each input count is multiplied by a constant value: `count1` by 12 (binary `1100`), `count2` by 15 (binary `1111`), `count3` by 2 (binary `0010`), and `count4` by 3 (binary `0011`). The results are then output as four corresponding 4-bit values: `sale1`, `sale2`, `sale3`, and `sale4`, effectively representing the computed sales figures based on the input counts.
module top_module(
    input[3:0] count1, 
    input[3:0] count2,
    input[3:0] count3,
    input[3:0] count4,
    output[3:0] sale1,
    output[3:0] sale2,
    output[3:0] sale3,
    output[3:0] sale4
    );
