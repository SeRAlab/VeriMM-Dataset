Design a 16-bit to 32-bit sign extension unit. When ExtSel is high and the most significant bit (MSB) of the 16-bit input (ime) is set, it extends the input by filling the upper 16 bits with ones; otherwise, it fills the upper 16 bits with zeros. This allows for proper sign extension of negative values when converting from a smaller bit-width to a larger one.
