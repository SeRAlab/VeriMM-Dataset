Design a 24-bit Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) that takes a 24-bit binary input and converts it into a corresponding analog output value. The output should be represented as a 32-bit fixed-point number where the top 8 bits represent the integer part and the lower 24 bits represent the fractional part. The DAC should have an enable signal that, when asserted, allows the conversion to take place. If the enable signal is low, the output should be held at zero.
