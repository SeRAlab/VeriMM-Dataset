Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal: 
- 000: Addition
- 001: Subtraction
- 010: Bitwise AND
- 011: Bitwise OR
- 100: Bitwise XOR
- 101: Greater Than (sets a single output bit indicating if A > B)
The ALU should accept two 32-bit inputs A and B, produce a 32-bit output for operations that yield 32 bits, and a 1-bit output for the greater than operation.
