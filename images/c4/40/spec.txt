

You are tasked with designing a Verilog module that implements a 4-bit ripple carry adder. The module should have three inputs and two outputs. The inputs are two 4-bit binary numbers, A and B, and a carry-in bit, Cin. The outputs are a 4-bit binary number, S, which represents the sum of A and B, and a carry-out bit, Cout, which represents the carry generated by the addition.

The module should be named "ripple_adder" and should be completely self-contained. You may use any Verilog constructs and modules that you deem necessary, but the solution should be contained within a single module that is no more than 200 lines of code.

Your module should be synthesizable and should not contain any logic errors. You may assume that the inputs A and B will always be valid 4-bit binary numbers, and that the carry-in bit Cin will be either 0 or 1.
