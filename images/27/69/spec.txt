
Design a Verilog module that performs bilinear interpolation. The module should take four pixel values (P0, P1, P2, P3) and fractional coordinates (X_frac, Y_frac) as inputs, where P0 is the top-left pixel, P1 is the top-right pixel, P2 is the bottom-left pixel, and P3 is the bottom-right pixel. The output should be a single interpolated pixel value (result). The fractional coordinates should be 8 bits, where the upper 4 bits represent the integer part and the lower 4 bits represent the fractional part.
