Design a power gating circuit that controls the power to a device based on a power enable signal. The circuit should have the following inputs: 
- `power_enable` (1-bit): signal to enable or disable power to the device.
- `device_input` (4-bit): data input for the device.
The output should be `device_output` (4-bit), which will be equal to `device_input` when `power_enable` is high, and should be all zeros when `power_enable` is low.
