Design a circuit that performs a modified XNOR operation followed by a binary addition of its outputs. The module takes two 8-bit input vectors, `x` and `y`, and computes the XNOR of each corresponding bit pair, yielding an 8-bit result. It then groups the resulting XNOR outputs into two 4-bit segments, adding these together along with a carry-in bit (`cin`). The least significant bit of the sum is output as `s0`, while `s1` replicates `s0`. The module also provides a 2-bit carry-out (`cout`) based on specific bits from the sum of the segments, specifically the second least significant and the zeroth bits.
module top_module(x, y, s1, s0, cin, cout);
