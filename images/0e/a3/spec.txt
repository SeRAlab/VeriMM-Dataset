Design a fan speed control system that takes an input signal representing the desired temperature and adjusts the fan speed accordingly. The fan has three speed levels: low, medium, and high. The system should have a clock signal to synchronize the speed adjustments. The fan speed should change based on the temperature range: if the temperature is below 20°C, the fan should run at low speed; between 20°C and 30°C at medium speed; and above 30°C at high speed.
