
Design a combinational logic circuit for a linear voltage regulator. The circuit should take an input voltage (Vin) and an enable signal (EN) and produce an output voltage (Vout) based on the following conditions:
1. If EN is low (0), Vout should be 0V.
2. If EN is high (1) and Vin is less than a defined threshold (e.g., 5V), Vout should equal Vin.
3. If EN is high (1) and Vin is greater than or equal to the threshold, Vout should be a fixed value (e.g., 5V).
Assume Vin is a 5-bit binary number representing the voltage level.
