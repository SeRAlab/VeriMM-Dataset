Design a temperature sensor controller that takes a 5-bit binary input representing temperature in Celsius, and outputs a 2-bit binary value representing a warning level. The output warning levels are defined as follows: 00 - Normal (temperature < 20°C), 01 - Caution (20°C ≤ temperature < 30°C), 10 - Warning (30°C ≤ temperature < 40°C), and 11 - Critical (temperature ≥ 40°C).
