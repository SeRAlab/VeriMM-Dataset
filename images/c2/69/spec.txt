

You are tasked with designing a verilog module for a 2-to-1 multiplexer (MUX) with an enable input. The module should have three inputs: A, B, and EN, and one output Y. When EN is high, the output Y should be equal to A. When EN is low, the output Y should be equal to B. 

In addition, the module should include voltage supply signals for VPWR (voltage power), VGND (voltage ground), VPB (voltage power for B input), and VNA (voltage ground for A input).

Design the verilog module for the 2-to-1 MUX with enable input according to the specifications above.
