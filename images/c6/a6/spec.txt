Please act as a professional Verilog designer. Implement a 3-bit comparator module that takes two 3-bit inputs A and B, and outputs three signals: EQ, GT, and LT. EQ is high if A is equal to B, GT is high if A is greater than B, and LT is high if A is less than B. You can use any method to implement the comparator, but you are not allowed to use any arithmetic or comparison operators. You can only use bitwise operators and logical operators.
