Please act as a professional Verilog designer.

Design a Verilog module for an 8-bit Johnson counter with a synchronous reset. The counter cycles through a sequence of 8 states, each representing a bit pattern in which a single '1' bit moves through a series of '0' bits. The counter resets to the initial state when the reset signal is active.

Module name:
    johnson_counter_8bit

Input ports:
    clk: Chip clock signal.
    reset: Synchronous reset signal.

Output ports:
    count: Counter output signal with a data width of 8 bits.

Implementation:
Counter operation:
On every positive edge of the clock signal (clk), the Johnson counter (count) shifts its bit pattern left and wraps the inverted leftmost bit to the rightmost position if the reset signal (reset) is not active. If the reset signal is active, the counter resets to its initial state.
