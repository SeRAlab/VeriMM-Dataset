
Design a Verilog module that implements a 4-stage CORDIC (COordinate Rotation DIgital Computer) for computing the sine and cosine of an angle in radians. The module should take a 16-bit angle as input and produce 16-bit sine and cosine outputs. The CORDIC algorithm will be implemented with a fixed number of iterations (4 iterations) for simplification. The module should have a clock and reset signal.
