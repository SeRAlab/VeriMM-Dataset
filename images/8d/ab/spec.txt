It manages readiness signals for both input and output to control the flow of data. The `reset_n` signal initializes internal registers, while the clock signal triggers data capture and output generation, effectively functioning as a data buffer and error handler in a synchronous environment.

    ast_sink_data,
    ast_sink_valid,
    ast_source_ready,
