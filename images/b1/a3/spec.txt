Design a circuit that generates two output validity signals, o_valid0 and o_valid1, based on an input signal, i_inverse, and another input signal, i_valid. When i_inverse is high (1), o_valid0 is set to the value of i_valid while o_valid1 is set to LOW (0). Conversely, when i_inverse is LOW (0), o_valid0 is set to LOW (0), and o_valid1 takes the value of i_valid. This effectively creates a conditional routing mechanism for the validity signal based on the status of i_inverse.
module top_module(
          i_inverse   ,
	  i_valid     ,
         
	  o_valid0    ,
	  o_valid1     
);
