Design a linear voltage regulator in Verilog that takes a 3-bit binary input representing a voltage reference and outputs a regulated voltage level. The output should be a 2-bit binary value that represents the closest lower regulated voltage level from predefined levels: 00 for 0V, 01 for 1V, 10 for 2V, and 11 for 3V. The circuit should ensure that the output does not exceed the maximum regulated voltage level based on the input voltage reference.
