Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simple Support Vector Machine (SVM) decision function. The circuit will take three inputs: two features (x1 and x2) and a bias (b), and will output a single bit indicating the predicted class (0 or 1). The decision function will be based on the equation: f(x1, x2) = sign(w1*x1 + w2*x2 + b), where w1 and w2 are weights that are fixed as constants in the design.
