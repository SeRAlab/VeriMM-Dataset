Design a 4-channel priority arbiter in Verilog. The arbiter should prioritize requests from four different channels (req0, req1, req2, req3) based on their priority (req0 has the highest priority and req3 has the lowest). The output should be a signal (grant) that indicates which channel is granted access. If multiple requests are made at the same time, the arbiter should grant access to the channel with the highest priority.
