Design a Low Dropout Regulator (LDO) in Verilog. The LDO should take a reference voltage (Vref) and a supply voltage (Vin) as inputs, and provide a regulated output voltage (Vout) that should follow the reference voltage provided that the supply voltage is greater than the dropout voltage (Vdrop). The design should include a simple control logic to enable the LDO and ensure that Vout does not exceed Vin. The output should be valid when the enable signal is high.
