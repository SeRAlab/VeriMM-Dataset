Design a combinational logic circuit that implements a simplified version of an A* search algorithm. The circuit takes three inputs: 'current_cost', 'heuristic_cost', and 'total_cost', which are 4-bit binary numbers. The output 'next_step' is a 4-bit binary number that represents the next step in the search process, calculated as the sum of 'current_cost' and 'heuristic_cost'. If 'total_cost' is less than or equal to the sum of 'current_cost' and 'heuristic_cost', then 'next_step' should output the sum; otherwise, it should output 'total_cost'.
