
Design a simple sliding window protocol that manages the transmission of packets between a sender and a receiver. The sender can send a maximum of 'N' packets before needing to wait for an acknowledgment (ACK) from the receiver. The receiver can only acknowledge packets that are received in order. Implement a counter to keep track of the number of packets sent and received, and generate control signals for the sender to stop sending when the window limit is reached and for the receiver to acknowledge packets.
