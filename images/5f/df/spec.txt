Design a 4-bit synchronous counter with enable and asynchronous reset functionality. On each rising edge of the clock, if the enable signal (en) is active, the counter increments its value. When the asynchronous reset signal (rst_n) is low, the counter resets to zero regardless of the clock or enable state.
