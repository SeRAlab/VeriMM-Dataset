
Design a fan speed control system that regulates the speed of a fan based on the temperature input. The fan has three speed settings: OFF, LOW, and HIGH. The system accepts a 2-bit temperature input: 
00 (low temperature), 01 (medium temperature), 10 (high temperature), and 11 (very high temperature). 
The fan should operate as follows:
- Temperature 00: Fan OFF
- Temperature 01: Fan LOW
- Temperature 10: Fan HIGH
- Temperature 11: Fan HIGH
