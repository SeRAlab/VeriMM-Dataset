

You are tasked to design a verilog module that takes two 4-bit inputs (A and B) and outputs a 4-bit result (Y). The output Y should be the sum of the inputs A and B. If the sum is greater than 15, the output should be truncated to the least significant 4 bits. Your module should be named "add4bit" and should have the following ports:
- A: 4-bit input
- B: 4-bit input
- Y: 4-bit output
