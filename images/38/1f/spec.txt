Design a simple CSPRNG using a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) with a 5-bit register. The output should be a pseudo-random bit generated from the state of the LFSR. The LFSR will be initialized with a non-zero seed, and it will shift and produce a new bit on each clock cycle. The feedback polynomial for this LFSR is x^5 + x^2 + 1.
