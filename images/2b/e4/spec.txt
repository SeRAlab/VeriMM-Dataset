
The problem is to implement a simple Elliptic Curve Point Addition operation in Verilog. Given two points \( P(x_1, y_1) \) and \( Q(x_2, y_2) \) on an elliptic curve, compute the resulting point \( R(x_3, y_3) \) after the addition operation. The Verilog module should handle the elliptic curve point addition in projective coordinates, which avoids the need for division operations and provides more efficient computation.
