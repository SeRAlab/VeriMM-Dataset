
Create a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog, where there are 4 request lines (req[3:0]) and 4 grant lines (grant[3:0]). The arbiter should grant access based on fixed priority: req[0] > req[1] > req[2] > req[3]. Ensure the arbiter can handle multiple requests and give priority to the highest one. When a grant is given, the corresponding request should be cleared.
