
Design a Verilog module that implements a 16-QAM (Quadrature Amplitude Modulation) encoder. The module should take a 4-bit input representing the symbol to be encoded and produce two outputs (I and Q), each being a 2-bit value corresponding to the in-phase (I) and quadrature (Q) components of the modulation. The mapping for 16-QAM should be as follows:
- Symbol (Input) | I (Output) | Q (Output)
- 0000            | 00         | 00
- 0001            | 00         | 01
- 0010            | 00         | 10
- 0011            | 00         | 11
- 0100            | 01         | 00
- 0101            | 01         | 01
- 0110            | 01         | 10
- 0111            | 01         | 11
- 1000            | 10         | 00
- 1001            | 10         | 01
- 1010            | 10         | 10
- 1011            | 10         | 11
- 1100            | 11         | 00
- 1101            | 11         | 01
- 1110            | 11         | 10
- 1111            | 11         | 11
