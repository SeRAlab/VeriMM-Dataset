Design a data forwarding unit that determines the correct source of an operand for a given instruction in a pipelined processor. The forwarding unit will take two inputs: `src1` and `src2`, representing the source registers for an operation. It will also take two additional inputs `forwardA` and `forwardB`, which indicate whether to forward the value from a register. The output will be the correct value for each source register after considering the forwarding signals.
