Design a module that performs arithmetic operations on two 16-bit input values. The module outputs the results of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of the inputs, providing four separate 16-bit outputs: y_add for the sum, y_sub for the difference, y_mul for the product, and y_div for the quotient.
module top_module(
    input [15:0]a,b,
    output [15:0]y_add,y_sub,y_mul,y_div
    );
