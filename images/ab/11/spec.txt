Design a combinational logic circuit that outputs X based on the inputs A1, A2, A3, and B1. The output X is determined by the following conditions: it outputs high when either all inputs A1, A2, and A3 are high (A1 AND A2 AND A3), or when B1 is high while none of the inputs A1, A2, and A3 are high; otherwise, it outputs low when at least one of A1, A2, or A3 is high while B1 is also high. This functionality encapsulates a combination of OR, AND, and NOT operations to achieve the desired output logic.
module top_module(
    input A1,
    input A2,
    input A3,
    input B1,
    output X
);
