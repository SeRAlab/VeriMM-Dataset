
Design a Verilog module for performing point addition on an elliptic curve defined over a finite field. The module should take two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) on the curve and return their sum (x3, y3). The addition should follow the standard formulas used in ECC. 
The input coordinates should be 8 bits each, and the output should also be in 8 bits.
Assume the curve is defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b, where a and b are constants defined as part of the module parameters.
