Implement a 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) that operates on the rising edge of the clock signal. The LFSR shifts its bits while generating new output bits based on a feedback mechanism derived from the XOR of the third and fourth bits. The four output signals represent the individual bits of the internal register state, providing a pseudo-random sequence typically used in applications like digital communications and cryptography.
