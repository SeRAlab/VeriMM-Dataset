Design a digital echo cancellation module using a Least Mean Squares (LMS) adaptive filter approach. This module accepts two signed 16-bit input signals: `osignal` (original signal) and `esignal` (echoed signal). It employs a finite impulse response (FIR) filter of configurable length (32 by default) to adaptively estimate and subtract the echoed component from the original signal. The coefficients of the FIR filter are updated based on the computed error signal, which is derived from the difference between the original signal and the filtered output. This design effectively removes unwanted echo from the input signal, producing a cleaned output signal (`outsignal`).
