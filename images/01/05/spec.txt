
Design a Verilog module that performs bilinear interpolation on a 2D grid of pixel values. The module should take four pixel values (P00, P01, P10, P11) and the fractional coordinates (x, y) as inputs and output the interpolated pixel value. The pixel values are 8-bit unsigned integers, and the coordinates are represented as fixed-point numbers (4 bits for the integer part and 4 bits for the fractional part).
