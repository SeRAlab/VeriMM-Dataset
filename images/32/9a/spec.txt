
Design a combinational logic circuit that simulates a simple Monte Carlo method to estimate the value of π. The circuit will take as input a number of random points generated within a square of size 1x1. It will determine how many of those points fall within a quarter-circle inscribed within the square. The output will be the estimated value of π based on the ratio of points inside the circle to the total points sampled. Use a 4-bit input to represent the number of points.
