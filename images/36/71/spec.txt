Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The module takes three inputs: `current_load`, `desired_voltage`, and `desired_frequency`. Based on the `current_load`, the module outputs the `adjusted_voltage` and `adjusted_frequency`. The output voltage and frequency can be adjusted based on the load as follows: if `current_load` is high (greater than a threshold), decrease voltage and frequency; if low, increase them.
