Design a Verilog module for a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller that adjusts the operating frequency and voltage of a processor based on the workload. The module should take current workload and a control signal as inputs and output the selected frequency and voltage levels. The frequency can be set to low, medium, or high, and the voltage levels should correspond to these frequencies. The workload is a 3-bit input representing low (000), medium (001), and high (010) workload. The control signal is a 1-bit input that enables or disables the DVFS feature.
