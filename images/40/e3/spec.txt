
Design a 24-bit ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) with a 2's complement output. The Verilog module should take a 24-bit unsigned input representing the analog voltage and convert it to a 24-bit signed output. The conversion should be such that the maximum unsigned input (2^24 - 1) corresponds to the maximum signed output (2^23 - 1), and the minimum unsigned input (0) corresponds to the minimum signed output (-2^23). Your task is to implement this logic in Verilog.
