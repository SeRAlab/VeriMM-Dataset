Design a 4-bit up counter with asynchronous reset functionality that counts from 0 to 15 (00 to 1111 in binary). Upon receiving a reset signal (r), the counter resets to 0, and the carry-out (cout) signal is set to 0. On each rising edge of the clock signal (c), the counter increments its value. When the counter reaches its maximum value of 15, it rolls over back to 0 and sets cout to 1, indicating a rollover event; otherwise, cout remains 0. This design is useful for counting applications requiring a 4-bit binary representation with rollover detection.
