Design a forwarding logic unit to resolve data hazards in a pipeline architecture. The module takes inputs indicating register addresses and write enables from the Execute-Modify (EM) and Memory-Write (MW) stages of the pipeline, along with the source and target register addresses from the Instruction Execute (IE) stage. Based on this information, it generates two, 2-bit outputs (ForwardA_o and ForwardB_o) that indicate whether to forward values from the EM or MW stages to the IE stage for both source and destination registers, ensuring that correct data is used without stalling the pipeline.
