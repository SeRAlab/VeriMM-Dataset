Please act as a professional verilog designer.

Design a Verilog module for a 16-bit up/down counter with a synchronous reset and a control signal to switch between up and down counting. The counter increments or decrements its value on the rising edge of the clock based on the control signal and resets to zero when the reset signal is active.

Module name:  
    up_down_counter_16bit  

Input ports:
    clk: Chip clock signal.
    reset: Synchronous reset signal.
    up_down: Control signal to switch between up and down counting.

Output ports:
    count: Counter output signal with a data width of 16 bits.

Implementation:
Counter operation:
On every positive edge of the clock signal (clk), the counter (count) increments its value by 1 if the up_down signal is high and decrements its value by 1 if the up_down signal is low, provided the reset signal (reset) is not active. If the reset signal is active, the counter resets to zero.
