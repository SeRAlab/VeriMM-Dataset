Design a control logic circuit to manage input/output requests for a bus system. It takes signals for input/output request (iorq_n), read (rd_n), and write (wr_n), along with additional control signals (m1_n, iorqge1, iorqge2, and porthit). The module produces two inverted output signals (iorq1_n and iorq2_n) that determine the status of the input/output request, and a drive_ff output, which indicates whether the bus should be driven based on the combination of input signals. The design facilitates proper operation in scenarios where input/output operations and bus accesses are coordinated based on control and status signals.
module top_module(input iorq_n,input rd_n,input wr_n,input m1_n,output iorq1_n,output iorq2_n,input iorqge1,input iorqge2,input porthit,output drive_ff);
