Design a countdown timer that counts down from 3 minutes (represented as 3 in the 'ten' digit and 0 in the 'one' digit) to 0. The timer's state is updated on the rising edge of the clock signal, with a reset functionality that initializes the time to 3 minutes when the reset signal is asserted low. When the 'one' digit reaches 0, it resets to 9 and decrements the 'ten' digit by 1, effectively reducing the total countdown time by 1 minute for each full decrement of the 'one' digit. The module outputs a signal indicating whether the countdown has finished, which is true when both the 'ten' and 'one' digits are zero.
module top_module(output reg [3:0]ten,output reg [3:0]one,output isFinished,input clk,input rst_n);
