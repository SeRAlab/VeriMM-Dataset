Design an 8-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) that generates a pseudo-random sequence of bits. The LFSR should be initialized with a non-zero seed value and use the polynomial x^8 + x^6 + x^5 + x^4 + 1 for feedback. The output of the LFSR should be a single bit, and the LFSR should shift and update its state on each clock cycle.
