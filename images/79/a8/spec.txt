The module implements a dual-phase latch mechanism that captures and outputs an 8-bit data signal based on two clock phases (pla_ph1 and pla_ph2) and a reset signal (dsp_reset). When the reset signal is activated, both the internal register (latched_r) and the output register (out2_r) are cleared to zero. On the rising edge of pla_ph1, if the reset is not active, the module captures the 8-bit input (padd) into the internal register. Subsequently, on the rising edge of pla_ph2, the value stored in latched_r is passed to the output register (out2_r), effectively creating a synchronous data storage device with clear/reset functionality suitable for digital signal processing applications.
