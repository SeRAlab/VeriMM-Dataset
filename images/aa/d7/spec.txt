Develop a 4-bit adder/subtractor circuit. The module takes two 4-bit inputs, A and B, and a control signal, sub, to determine whether to perform addition or subtraction. If the sub signal is asserted, the module computes A minus B using two's complement by inverting B and adding one; otherwise, it computes A plus B. The final output is a 4-bit result Y, which is registered on the rising edge of the clock signal CK, along with Cout indicating a carry or borrow in the operation.
