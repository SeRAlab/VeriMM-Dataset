

In digital circuit design, decoupling capacitors are used to reduce noise and voltage fluctuations in the power supply. In this problem, you are tasked with designing a Verilog module that models a decoupling capacitor.

You need to implement a Verilog module named `decap` that has two input ports and no output ports. The input ports are `VPWR` and `VGND`, which represent the power supply voltage and ground, respectively. The module should simulate the behavior of a capacitor by charging and discharging based on the voltage levels of its input ports.

Your module should have the following behavior:
- When `VPWR` is high and `VGND` is low, the capacitor should charge up to the voltage level of `VPWR`.
- When `VPWR` is low and `VGND` is high, the capacitor should discharge down to the voltage level of `VGND`.
- When both `VPWR` and `VGND` are low or high, the capacitor should maintain its current voltage level.

You may assume that the voltage levels of `VPWR` and `VGND` are always either high or low, and that the module will be used in a larger circuit that will handle any necessary timing or clocking.
