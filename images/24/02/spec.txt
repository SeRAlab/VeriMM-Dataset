Design a 2-input combinational logic circuit that detects and resolves data hazards in a simplified manner. The circuit takes two 1-bit inputs, `a` and `b`, representing two signals, and outputs `hazard_detected`, which is high (1) if both inputs are high (1) at the same time, indicating a potential hazard. The circuit should also output `resolved`, which is high (1) if at least one of the inputs is low (0), indicating that the hazard is resolved.
