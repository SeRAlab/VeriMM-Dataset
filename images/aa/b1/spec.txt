Design a BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) adder that sums two 4-bit BCD numbers, A and B, along with an input carry-in. The module outputs a 4-bit sum Y and a carry-out signal. The adder accounts for BCD overflow by checking if the sum exceeds 9; if it does, it adjusts the output Y by adding a correction factor to ensure the result remains valid in the BCD format. The carry-out indicates whether there was a carry from the addition of A and B as well as any correction applied.
