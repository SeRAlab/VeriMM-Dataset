
Create a fixed-priority arbiter in Verilog that grants access to one of four requestors (REQ[3:0]) based on their priority. The highest priority is given to REQ[3], followed by REQ[2], REQ[1], and REQ[0]. The arbiter should have one output GRANT indicating which requestor has been granted access. The arbiter should maintain its state and not grant access again until the current requestor releases it.
