Implement a delay buffer circuit that stores and outputs a delayed version of the input signal A. The delay duration is specified by the DELAY input, allowing the circuit to hold the incoming data in a shift register until the counter reaches the set delay value. Upon reaching the delay threshold, the circuit outputs the most recently shifted data, effectively creating a controlled delay in signal propagation.
