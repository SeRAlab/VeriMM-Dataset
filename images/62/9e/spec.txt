Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer. The module selects one of four input signals (A, B, C, D) to output on Y based on the two selection lines (S0 and S1). When S0 and S1 are both low, input A is chosen; with S0 low and S1 high, input B is chosen; if S0 is high and S1 low, input C is selected; and when both S0 and S1 are high, input D is outputted. The design employs NOT and AND gates to decode the selection lines and an OR gate to consolidate the selected input.
