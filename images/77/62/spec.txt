Design a synchronous counter with load, increment, and decrement functionalities. The module increments or decrements its output value based on the UP and DN control signals while adhering to a maximum and minimum value defined by its width. It can also reset asynchronously or load a specified value into the counter. The counter operates in a cyclic manner, wrapping from MAX_VALUE to 0 when incrementing, and vice versa when decrementing. Additionally, it provides output signals indicating when the counter is at its maximum or minimum values.
