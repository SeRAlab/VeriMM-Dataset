Design a shift register that stores an 8-bit input. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is asserted, the register is cleared to zero. If the shift signal is active, the register shifts its contents left by one bit, and the new input bit is inserted at the least significant bit position. The current state of the shift register is output as an 8-bit wide signal.
module top_module(
    in,
    shift,
    reset,
    clk,
    out
);
