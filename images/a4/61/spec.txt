Design a forwarding unit for a processor to handle data hazards in a pipelined architecture. The module takes inputs from the instruction decode (ID) stage, including the register source identifiers and control signals indicating if the previous stages have written to their destination registers. Based on this information, it determines the appropriate forwarding paths for two source registers (Rs and Rt). The outputs, ForwardA and ForwardB, provide the forwarding control signals that indicate whether the values from the execute (EX) or memory (MEM) stages should be forwarded to avoid stalling the pipeline. This unit ensures that the most up-to-date values are used in the ALU operations, thereby enhancing performance by preventing data hazards.
