Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the fan speed based on the temperature levels. The fan has three speed settings: low, medium, and high. The system takes a 2-bit input representing temperature levels, where 00 = low, 01 = medium, 10 = high, and 11 = off. The output is a 2-bit representation of the fan speed settings: 00 = off, 01 = low, 10 = medium, and 11 = high. The fan speed should only change when the temperature input changes.
