Design a four-input logic gate that realizes a functionality similar to an exclusive OR (XOR) operation between two derived signals A and B. Signal A is generated as the negation of the logical OR of inputs A1_N and A2_N, while signal B is formed by negating the logical OR of inputs B1 and B2. The output X will be high when either A or B is high, but not both simultaneously, effectively functioning as a two-input XOR gate. The module is powered by VPWR and grounded via VGND, with additional power and ground support provided by VPB and VNB.
module top_module(
    X   ,
    A1_N,
    A2_N,
    B1  ,
    B2  ,
    VPWR,
    VGND,
    VPB ,
    VNB
);
