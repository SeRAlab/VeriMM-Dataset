Design a circuit that functions as a gated D flip-flop. The flip-flop captures the input data (D) on the rising edge of the clock (CLK) when the clock enable (SCE) is asserted. When SCE is low, the output Q is forced to a LOW state, and Q_N provides the complement of Q. The module also includes asynchronous set (SET_B) and other control signals (SCD) along with power and ground connections, ensuring proper functionality within a digital system.
module top_module(
    CLK  ,
    D    ,
    Q    ,
    Q_N  ,
    SCD  ,
    SCE  ,
    SET_B,
    VPWR ,
    VGND
);
