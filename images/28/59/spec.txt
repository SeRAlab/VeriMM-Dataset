Design a simple Kalman filter in Verilog. The filter should take a sequence of noisy measurements and estimate the true value through a state update. The state update equations are defined as follows:
1. Predict the state: `x_k = x_(k-1) + u`
2. Update the state with the measurement: `x_k = x_k + K*(z_k - x_k)`
Where `K` is the Kalman gain, `u` is the control input, `z_k` is the measurement at time step `k`, and `x_k` is the estimated state.
