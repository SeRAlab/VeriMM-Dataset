Design a 4th-order IIR filter in Verilog that processes a stream of input samples and produces a stream of output samples. The filter should have the following coefficients: a1, a2, b0, b1, b2, where the filter is defined as:
output[n] = b0 * input[n] + b1 * input[n-1] + b2 * input[n-2] - a1 * output[n-1] - a2 * output[n-2]
The filter should be capable of handling an input signal continuously.
