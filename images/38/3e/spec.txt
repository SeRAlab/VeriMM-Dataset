
Design a 2-bit adder that takes two 2-bit binary numbers as inputs and produces a 3-bit binary sum as output. The inputs are A1A0 and B1B0, where A1 and B1 are the most significant bits, and A0 and B0 are the least significant bits. The output should be S2S1S0, where S2 is the carry-out of the addition.
