Design a 1-bit SRAM cell in Verilog. The cell should have the following inputs: `write_enable`, `data_in`, and `clk`. The output should be `data_out`. When `write_enable` is high, the `data_in` value should be written to the SRAM cell on the rising edge of `clk`. When `write_enable` is low, the stored value should be output to `data_out` regardless of the `clk` signal.
