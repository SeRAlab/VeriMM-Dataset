Design a 2-bit binary adder with hazard detection. The adder should add two 2-bit binary numbers (A1 A0 and B1 B0) and output a 2-bit sum (S1 S0) and a carry-out (C_out). Implement hazard detection to ensure that the outputs do not change unexpectedly during changes of the inputs. You need to detect potential hazards and produce stable outputs.
