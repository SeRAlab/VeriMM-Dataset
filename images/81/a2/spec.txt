Implement the Verilog module based on the following description.
Design a 8-bit linear feedback shift register (LFSR) with taps at bit positions 8, 6, 5, and 4. The LFSR should have one input, clk (clock), and one output, out (an 8-bit wide bus). The LFSR should shift its contents to the right on each positive clock edge, and the new bit should be the XOR of the tap positions.
