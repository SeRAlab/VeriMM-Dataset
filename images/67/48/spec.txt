Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer circuit that selects one of four input signals (D0, D1, D2, D3) based on a 2-bit control signal composed of ctrl1 and ctrl0. The output, S, reflects the value of the selected input; when ctrl1 and ctrl0 are 00, it outputs D0, 01 outputs D1, 10 outputs D2, and 11 outputs D3. This multiplexer enables efficient signal selection in digital systems, allowing for the routing of one signal from multiple sources to a single output.
module top_module(input ctrl0, ctrl1, 
     input D0, D1, D2, D3, 
     output reg S);
