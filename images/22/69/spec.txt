
Design a simple Verilog module that performs point addition for an elliptic curve over a finite field GF(p). The module should accept two input points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) and produce a resulting point (x3, y3) as the output. The addition will be defined for points on the curve defined by the equation y^2 = x^3 + ax + b (which can be simplified for the purpose of this problem). Assume that the finite field is defined by a prime number p, and the points are represented in a format suitable for binary representation.
