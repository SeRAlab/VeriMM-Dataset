
Design a reset controller that has two inputs: an active-high reset signal (`rst_n`) and a clock signal (`clk`). When the reset signal is asserted (active), the output signal `reset_out` should be held in a low state (0). Once the reset signal is deasserted (inactive), `reset_out` should be released and remain high (1) until the next reset. The module should also include a debouncing mechanism to ensure that short glitches in the input reset signal do not affect the output.
