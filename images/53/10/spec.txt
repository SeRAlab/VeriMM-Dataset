Design a D-type latch with an active-low reset. The module captures the value of the input D on the rising edge of the GATE signal. When RESET_B is low, the output Q is reset to LOW. Otherwise, the output Q takes the value of D. The design effectively stores a bit of information under the control of two signals: GATE for data capture and RESET_B for resetting the stored value.
