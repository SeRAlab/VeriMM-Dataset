Design an 8-bit serializer that takes an 8-bit parallel input and serializes it into a single output bitstream. The serialization occurs on the rising edge of a clock signal, and the output should be valid for a specific number of clock cycles (in this case, 8 cycles). After the 8 bits have been serialized, the output should remain low until the next serialization process is initiated. The circuit should have a reset signal to initialize the state.
