Design a 2-bit binary adder that computes the sum of two 2-bit inputs, A and B, along with a carry-in bit, C. The module produces a 3-bit output, S, representing the sum, where S[2] is the carry-out resulting from the addition. The implementation involves two full adder stages to handle the bits of A and B, propagating the carry bit through the stages to calculate the correct total sum.
module top_module(
    input [1:0] A,
    input [1:0] B,
    input C,
    output reg [2:0] S
);
