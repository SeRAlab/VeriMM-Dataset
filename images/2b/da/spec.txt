
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple elliptic curve point addition operation on a defined finite field. The module should take as input two points on the elliptic curve (X1, Y1) and (X2, Y2) and output the resulting point (X3, Y3) after performing the addition. Assume the finite field is defined over GF(p) where p is a prime number. The inputs and outputs should be 8 bits wide.
