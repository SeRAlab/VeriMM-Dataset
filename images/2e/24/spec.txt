Design a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) in Verilog that generates a frequency output based on a control voltage input. The VCO should have the following parameters: a control voltage input (ctrl_voltage) which ranges from 0 to 255, and a clock input (clk) that triggers the oscillation. The output frequency should be proportional to the control voltage, where a higher control voltage results in a higher frequency. Ensure that the output frequency can toggle between a minimum of 1 Hz to a maximum of 10 KHz based on the control voltage.
