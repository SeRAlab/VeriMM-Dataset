Design a Parallel-in Parallel-out (PIPO) shift register with the following specifications: 
1. The shift register has a width of 8 bits.
2. It has a parallel load input `data_in` and an output `data_out`.
3. A clock signal `clk` and a reset signal `reset` are provided.
4. On the rising edge of the `clk`, if `reset` is high, the register should clear to 0. 
5. If `reset` is low, the `data_in` should be loaded into the register on a high `load` signal. 
6. On a low `load` signal, the `data_out` should reflect the current state of the register.
