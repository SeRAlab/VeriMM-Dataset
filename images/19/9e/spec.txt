Design a 1-bit SRAM cell with the following functionality: The SRAM cell should have two inputs, `D` (data input) and `W` (write enable), and one output `Q` (data output). When `W` is high, the value at `D` should be stored in the SRAM cell. When `W` is low, the output `Q` should be the stored value. There should also be a reset signal `RST` that resets the cell to 0 when activated.
