The module takes five inputs (IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4, IN5) and generates an output (QN) that is the logical NAND of the OR operation applied to the product of IN1 and IN2 combined with IN3, ANDed with IN4 and IN5. The output QN is thus the negation of the final result, effectively providing the inversion of the desired logical expression. Additionally, it utilizes power supply inputs (VDD, VSS) to operate correctly within the intended electronic environment.
module top_module(input IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4, IN5, output QN, input VDD, VSS);
