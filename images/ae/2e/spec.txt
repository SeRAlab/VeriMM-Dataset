When the SLEEP signal is LOW, indicating that the buffer is active, the output will reflect the value of A. If SLEEP is HIGH, the buffer is effectively disabled, ensuring that the output X remains in a low-power state. The design incorporates power-good circuitry to manage the power supply connections (VPWR and VGND) and ensure that the buffer functions correctly under specified conditions.
