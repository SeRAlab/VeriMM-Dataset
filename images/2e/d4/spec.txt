
Design a fixed-priority arbiter that takes five request signals (req[4:0]) and outputs a grant signal (grant). The arbiter should prioritize requests in order, with req[0] having the highest priority and req[4] having the lowest. The arbiter should grant the highest-priority request that is active, and it should grant only one request at a time. If multiple requests are active, it should grant the one with the highest priority. The grant signal should remain high until the requesting unit de-asserts its request.
