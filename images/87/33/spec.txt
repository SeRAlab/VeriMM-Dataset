Design a 4-bit binary counter with asynchronous reset and enable functionality. Upon the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is active, the counter outputs reset to zero. When the enable signal is asserted, the counter increments its value. Once the counter reaches its maximum value of 15 (or binary 1111), it wraps around back to zero, effectively creating a modulo-16 counter.
