Design a 4-bit binary multiplier that computes the product of two 4-bit signed inputs, a and b, producing an 8-bit output p. The module determines the sign of the result based on the signs of the inputs, with appropriate handling for negative values using 2's complement representation. The resulting product's sign is indicated by the 'sign' output, which reflects whether the product is positive or negative. The design efficiently calculates the partial products and combines them to produce the final multiplication result.
module top_module(
  input [3:0] a,
  input [3:0] b,
  output [7:0] p,
  output sign
);
