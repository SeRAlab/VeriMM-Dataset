Implement a 32-bit status register that captures two specific status flags: 'unaligned' and 'ovf' (overflow). On a rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal 'rst' is high, the register is cleared to zero. Otherwise, the register updates the least significant bit (LSB) to reflect the value of the 'ovf' signal and the second least significant bit to reflect the 'unaligned' signal. The output reflects the current state of the status register.

    rst, 
    clk, 
    unaligned, 
    ovf, 
    status
);
