Implement a 17-to-32-bit sign extender. The circuit takes a 17-bit signed input and extends it to a 32-bit output while preserving the sign. If the sign bit (the most significant bit of the input) is 1, it zero-fills the upper 15 bits with ones to indicate a negative value. If the sign bit is 0, it fills the upper 15 bits with zeros, signaling a positive value.
