Design a multi-functional logic gate module that performs one of four logical operations based on a 2-bit operation selector (OP). The module accepts two single-bit inputs (A and B) and outputs the result (Y) of the selected operation: a logical AND when OP is 00, a logical OR when OP is 01, a logical XOR when OP is 10, and a logical NOT of A when OP is 11. This design effectively serves as a configurable logic unit for various Boolean logic applications.
module top_module(
    A,
    B,
    OP,
    Y
);
