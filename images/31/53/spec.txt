Design a 4-bit shift register with the following specifications:
- The shift register has an input `data_in` of 4 bits and a `shift` control signal.
- On the rising edge of the `clk`, if `shift` is high, the shift register should shift left, and the least significant bit (LSB) should be filled with `0`.
- If `shift` is low, the data in the register should hold its value.
- The output should be a 4-bit signal `data_out` representing the current state of the shift register.
