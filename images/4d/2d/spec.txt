Design a 32-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that performs addition or subtraction based on the control signal Add_Sub. The module takes two 32-bit input operands, DataA and DataB, along with a carry-in (Cin) signal. When Add_Sub is high (1), the ALU computes the sum of DataA, DataB, and Cin, outputting the 32-bit result and a carry-out (Cout). Conversely, when Add_Sub is low (0), it calculates the difference of DataA and DataB, adjusting for the carry-in. The final outputs are the 32-bit Result and the Cout, indicating any overflow from the operation.
