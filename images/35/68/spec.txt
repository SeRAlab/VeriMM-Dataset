Design an SR flip-flop with asynchronous reset. The flip-flop should have two inputs, S (set) and R (reset), and one output Q. The output Q should be set to 1 when S is high and R is low, reset to 0 when R is high and S is low, and maintain its previous state when both inputs are low. Additionally, there should be an asynchronous reset input (RESET) that sets Q to 0 regardless of the states of S and R.
