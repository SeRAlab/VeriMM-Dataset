Design a 3-bit linear feedback shift register (LFSR) that generates a pseudo-random sequence of bits. On each positive edge of the clock (CLK), the most significant bit is shifted out, and new bits are generated based on the XOR of the second and third bits of the register. The outputs Q0, Q1, and Q2 represent the individual bits of the LFSR state.
module top_module(
    input CLK,
    output reg Q0,
    output reg Q1,
    output reg Q2
);
