Design a 1-bit SRAM cell in Verilog. The SRAM cell should have inputs for data (D), a write enable (WE), and a clock (CLK). When WE is high and on the positive edge of the clock, the value of D should be written to the SRAM cell. When WE is low, the output (Q) should hold the stored value. The initial state of the SRAM cell should be 0.
