Design a SR latch using Verilog. The latch should have two inputs S (Set) and R (Reset), and two outputs Q and Q_not (the complement of Q). The latch should maintain its state until a new input is received. When S = 1 and R = 0, Q should be set to 1. When S = 0 and R = 1, Q should be reset to 0. When both S and R are 0, the state should remain unchanged. The case where both S and R are 1 is considered invalid and should not change the state.
