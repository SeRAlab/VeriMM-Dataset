Design a traffic light controller using a finite state machine. The module cycles through three states for red, yellow, and green lights at specific durations: the red light remains on for 10 seconds, followed by a yellow light for 2 seconds, then a green light for 10 seconds, and finally another yellow light for 2 seconds before returning to red. The timing is managed using a clock signal and a counter to ensure accurate light transitions.
module top_module(input clk,
                     output reg red,
                     output reg yellow,
                     output reg green);
