Module name:
    counter_4bit_async_clear

Input ports:
    clk: Clock signal used for synchronous operation.
    clear: Asynchronous clear signal. Defined as 1 for clear and 0 for normal operation.

Output ports:
    q: 4-bit output representing the current counter value.

Implementation:
The module includes a register, q, which is updated in the always block, triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal (posedge clk) or the positive edge of the clear signal (posedge clear).
On clear, indicated by clear being high, q is cleared to 0.
On every rising edge of the clock, if clear is not high, q increments by 1.
