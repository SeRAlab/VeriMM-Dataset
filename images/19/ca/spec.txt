Design a combinational logic circuit that performs a basic portfolio optimization calculation for two assets. The inputs are the expected returns and risks for each asset, and the output is a decision variable indicating whether to invest in asset A or asset B based on the Sharpe ratio. The Sharpe ratio is calculated using the formula: (Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Risk. The circuit should output a binary decision (1 for asset A, 0 for asset B) based on which asset has a higher Sharpe ratio. Assume a fixed risk-free rate for simplicity.
