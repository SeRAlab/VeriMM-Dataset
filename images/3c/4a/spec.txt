Design a Parallel-in Parallel-out (PIPO) shift register in Verilog. The shift register should have the following specifications:
- It has a width of 8 bits.
- It has a synchronous load enable signal (load_en) that loads data into the register on a rising edge of the clock when load_en is high.
- It has a shift enable signal (shift_en) that shifts the data to the right on a rising edge of the clock when shift_en is high.
- The output should be available as an 8-bit data bus (data_out).
