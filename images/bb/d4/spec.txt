Design a circuit that performs a 32-bit wide bitwise OR operation between two input buses. The operation is synchronized to a clock signal, and the output is reset to zero when the reset signal is asserted. When the reset is not active, the module continuously produces the result of the bitwise OR of the two input buses on each rising edge of the clock.
