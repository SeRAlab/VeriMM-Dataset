Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs various arithmetic and logical operations based on the input control signals. It accepts two 32-bit operands (i_a and i_b), a 4-bit control signal (i_alucontrol) to specify the operation, and a 5-bit shift amount (i_sa) for shift operations. The ALU can perform bitwise AND, OR, addition (with possible inversion of the second operand), selecting the sign of the result, and logical shifts (left or right). Additionally, it provides an output signal (o_zero) that indicates if the result of the operation is zero, helping to facilitate conditional logic or branching in a larger system.
