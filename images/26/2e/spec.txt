
Design a fan speed control system that adjusts the speed of the fan based on the temperature input. The system has a temperature input (5 bits) and outputs a fan speed (3 bits). The mapping should be as follows:
- 0-15 (temperature): 0 (fan speed)
- 16-31 (temperature): 1 (fan speed)
- 32-47 (temperature): 2 (fan speed)
- 48-63 (temperature): 3 (fan speed)
- 64-127 (temperature): 4 (fan speed)
The system should have an enable signal that, when low, sets the fan speed to 0 regardless of the temperature input.
