Design a combinational logic circuit that performs LU decomposition for a 2x2 matrix. The inputs are two 2-bit numbers representing the rows of the matrix, and the outputs are the elements of the L and U matrices after decomposition. The output should be in the following format: L = [l11, l21, 0], U = [u11, u12, u21, u22], where l11 and u11 are the leading coefficients of L and U respectively.
