Design a 3-tap Finite Impulse Response (FIR) filter in Verilog. The filter takes 5-bit signed inputs and produces a 5-bit signed output. The filter coefficients are: 1, 2, and 1. The output is calculated as: Output = (Input[n] * 1) + (Input[n-1] * 2) + (Input[n-2] * 1), where n is the current input sample index.
