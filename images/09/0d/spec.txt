Design a DVFS controller that can switch between three different frequency and voltage levels based on a control signal. The controller should respond to a 2-bit input control signal (00, 01, 10, 11) which selects the operating level. The output will be a voltage and frequency combination represented as a 4-bit value, where the first two bits represent the voltage and the last two bits represent the frequency. The levels are defined as follows:  
00: Voltage = 00 (0V), Frequency = 00 (0Hz)  
01: Voltage = 01 (1V), Frequency = 01 (1Hz)  
10: Voltage = 10 (2V), Frequency = 10 (2Hz)  
11: Voltage = 11 (3V), Frequency = 11 (3Hz)
