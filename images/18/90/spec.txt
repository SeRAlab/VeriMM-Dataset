Design a 16-bit ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) that can perform the following operations based on a 4-bit control input:
00 - Addition
01 - Subtraction
10 - Bitwise AND
11 - Bitwise OR

The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs (A and B) and output a 16-bit result. Additionally, the ALU should output a zero flag (Z) that indicates whether the result is zero.
