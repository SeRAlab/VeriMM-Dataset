The flip-flop incorporates asynchronous SET and RESET inputs, with SET_B prioritizing to force Q to HIGH and Q_N to LOW when asserted, while RESET_B forces Q to LOW and Q_N to HIGH when asserted. The outputs are sourced from internal registers, ensuring proper operation in synchronous and asynchronous modes.

    output Q,
    output Q_N,

);
