Design a Verilog module for a Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The DVFS controller will take an input signal representing the current workload (in the range of 0 to 255) and output the appropriate voltage level (0 to 5V) and frequency (in Hz). The voltage and frequency should be scaled based on predefined thresholds. For example, if the workload is less than 85, the output voltage should be 1V and frequency should be 1MHz. If the workload is between 85 and 170, the output voltage should be 3V and frequency should be 5MHz. If the workload is more than 170, the output voltage should be 5V and frequency should be 10MHz.
