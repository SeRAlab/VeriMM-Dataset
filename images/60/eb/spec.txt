Design a circuit that generates a tone signal that ramps up and down based on clock cycles. The tone output is a 16-bit value that increases until it reaches 0xFFFE, then reverses direction and decreases until it reaches 0x0001, at which point it starts increasing again. The circuit can be reset asynchronously to initialize the tone to 0 and set the direction of ramping to down.
module top_module( input rst, input clk, output reg [15:0] tone );
