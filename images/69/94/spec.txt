Design a digital circuit that functions as a XOR gate, where the output is determined by the exclusive OR operation between the inputs VPB and VNB. The module also includes power and ground inputs (VPWR and VGND) to support standard cell implementation, although they are not directly involved in the logic operation. The XOR_output will be high when either, but not both, of the inputs VPB and VNB are high.
module top_module(
    input VPWR,
    input VGND,
    input VPB,
    input VNB,
    output reg XOR_output
);
