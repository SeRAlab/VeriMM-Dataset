Design a simple four-function digital calculator that performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on two four-bit inputs, `a` and `b`. The operation to be performed is selected by a two-bit `op` input, where `00` corresponds to addition, `01` to subtraction, `10` to multiplication, and `11` to division. The calculation occurs on the rising edge of the clock signal, and the result is stored in a four-bit output register. Upon receiving a reset signal, the calculator outputs zero.
