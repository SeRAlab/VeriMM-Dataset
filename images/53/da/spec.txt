The output, ENCLK, is generated by combining the clock (CLK) with the logical AND of EN and the inverse of TE, effectively allowing the clock to pass through only when the enable signal is active and the test enable signal is inactive. Additionally, the module incorporates a flip-flop mechanism that toggles its internal state (Q) based on the gated clock, providing a means for conditional clock signal management.
module top_module(
    input CLK,
    input EN,
    input TE,
    output ENCLK
