Additionally, it employs selection signals (sel_shamt and sel_imm) to choose between the shift amount and forwarded data for busA, and the extended immediate or forwarded data for busB, effectively preparing the proper inputs for the ALU operations.

            input [31:0] rsd,

            input fwRs,

            output [31:0] busA,
