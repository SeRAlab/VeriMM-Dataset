

You are tasked with designing a verilog module that implements a simple circuit with 8 inputs and 4 outputs. The circuit should take in 8 binary inputs and output the following:
- The first output should be the logical OR of the first four inputs.
- The second output should be the logical AND of the first four inputs.
- The third output should be the logical OR of the last four inputs.
- The fourth output should be the logical AND of the last four inputs.

Design a module named "logic_circuit" that takes in 8 binary inputs named "in[7:0]" and outputs the four binary outputs named "out[3:0]".
