Design a 16-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that performs the following operations based on a 4-bit operation code (opcode):  
- 0000: Addition  
- 0001: Subtraction  
- 0010: Bitwise AND  
- 0011: Bitwise OR  
- 0100: Bitwise XOR  
- 0101: Logical NOT (first operand only)  
- 0110: Shift Left  
- 0111: Shift Right  
The ALU should take two 16-bit inputs and produce a 16-bit output. The output should be determined by the opcode provided.
