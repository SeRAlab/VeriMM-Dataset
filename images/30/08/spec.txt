Design a 32-bit ALU that can perform the following operations based on a 3-bit control signal:  
- 000: Addition  
- 001: Subtraction  
- 010: AND  
- 011: OR  
- 100: XOR  
The ALU should take two 32-bit inputs (A and B) and output the result of the operation as a 32-bit output. Additionally, the ALU should output a carry-out signal for the addition and subtraction operations.
