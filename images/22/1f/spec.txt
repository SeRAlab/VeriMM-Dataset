Design a frequency divider that takes a clock input and outputs a divided clock signal. The frequency divider should divide the input clock frequency by a factor of 2, 4, and 8 based on a 2-bit control signal. The control signal will select the division factor as follows: 00 for divide by 2, 01 for divide by 4, and 10 for divide by 8. The output should remain stable until the next clock edge.
