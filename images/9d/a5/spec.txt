Design a 16-bit wide binary adder that computes the sum of two 16-bit inputs, A and B, along with a carry-in (CIN). The module outputs a 16-bit sum (SUM) and a carry-out (COUT) signal. The addition operation is performed combinatorially, with the resulting sum and carry being assigned to their respective outputs.
module top_module(
    input [15:0] A,
    input [15:0] B,
    input CIN,
    output [15:0] SUM,
    output COUT
);
