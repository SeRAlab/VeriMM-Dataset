Design a 32-bit arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that supports a variety of operations based on a 4-bit operation code (ALUop). The module can perform functions such as addition, subtraction, logical operations (AND, OR, NOR, XOR), shifts (both logical and arithmetic), and comparisons. Additionally, it supports multiplication with the result stored in two 32-bit registers, HI and LO, for holding higher and lower results respectively. The ALU can also transfer data between these registers, providing capabilities to move data into HI and LO. The results of operations are output through a 32-bit result register, and the module reacts to a clock signal for certain operations such as multiplication and register transfers.
module top_module(ALUop, a, b, result, clk, hi_data, lo_data);
