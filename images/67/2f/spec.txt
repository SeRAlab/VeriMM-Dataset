Design a pseudo-random number generator (PRNG) that uses multiple linear feedback shift registers (LFSRs) to produce a 32-bit random output. The module accepts three 32-bit seeds as inputs and operates on the rising edge of the clock or when reset is activated. Upon reset, the outputs are initialized to zero. During normal operation, the LFSRs are driven by the input seeds to generate intermediate values, which are combined to produce the final output. The output is accompanied by a valid signal indicating that a new valid random number is available.
