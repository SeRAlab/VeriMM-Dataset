Design a 32-bit comparator that checks for equality between the lower and upper halves of two 32-bit inputs, vra and vrb. The output, vrt, is a 32-bit wide signal where the least significant 16 bits are set to 0xFFFF if the lower 16 bits of vra and vrb are equal, otherwise they are set to 0x0000. Similarly, the most significant 16 bits of vrt are set to 0xFFFF if the upper 16 bits of vra and vrb are equal, otherwise they are also set to 0x0000. This module effectively provides a means to compare divided segments of two 32-bit words.
