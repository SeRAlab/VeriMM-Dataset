
Implement a Verilog module for an elliptic curve point addition circuit that takes two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) on the elliptic curve and produces a third point (x3, y3) as the result. The addition must handle the special case of points at infinity. Use the Weierstrass form of the elliptic curve equation: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b.
