
Design a Verilog module that implements a simple Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) controller. The controller adjusts the voltage and frequency based on an input load signal. The load signal is a 2-bit input where '00' indicates low load, '01' indicates medium load, and '10' indicates high load. The output frequency should be set to 1 MHz for low load, 2 MHz for medium load, and 4 MHz for high load. The voltage output should correspondingly be set to 1V, 1.5V, and 2V for low, medium, and high loads respectively. Provide a mechanism to hold the last state when the load signal is '11' (invalid load).
