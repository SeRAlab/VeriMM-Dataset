Design a shift register that can shift bits to the right on the rising edge of the SHIFT signal or load a new 8-bit value from the SER input on the rising edge of the LOAD signal. When the SHIFT signal is activated, the register shifts its contents to the right and fills the most significant bit with a zero. When the LOAD signal is activated, the register captures the value from SER and outputs it in the Q register.
module top_module(
    input SER,
    input SHIFT,
    input LOAD,
    output reg [7:0] Q
);
