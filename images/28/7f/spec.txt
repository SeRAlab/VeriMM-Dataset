Design a DVFS controller that adjusts the frequency and voltage levels based on the performance requirements. The controller will have a clock input, a reset input, and a request signal that indicates the desired frequency level (0 to 3). The output will be the selected frequency and voltage levels. The frequency and voltage output should correspond to the request input as follows:  
- Request 0: Frequency = 1 GHz, Voltage = 1.0 V  
- Request 1: Frequency = 2 GHz, Voltage = 1.2 V  
- Request 2: Frequency = 3 GHz, Voltage = 1.4 V  
- Request 3: Frequency = 4 GHz, Voltage = 1.6 V
