Implement a module of a 4-bit shift register. The shift register should shift its contents to the right on each positive edge of the clock signal when the shift enable signal is high. When the reset signal is active, the shift register should reset its contents to 0.

Module name:  
    shift_register

Input ports:
    CLK: Clock input
    RST: Active high reset signal
    SHIFT_EN: Shift enable signal

Output ports:
    Q: 4-bit output representing the contents of the shift register

Implementation:
The shift register module uses an always block to update the contents of the register based on the clock signal, reset signal, and shift enable signal.
The always block triggers on the positive edge of the clock signal (posedge CLK) or the positive edge of the reset signal (posedge RST). It checks if the reset signal is active (RST) and sets the register contents (Q) to 0. If the shift enable signal (SHIFT_EN) is high, it shifts the contents of the register to the right by one position.

module shift_register(CLK, RST, SHIFT_EN, Q);
