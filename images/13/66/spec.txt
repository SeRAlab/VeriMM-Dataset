Design a 32-bit Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) that takes two 32-bit inputs (A and B), a 4-bit operation code (opcode), and produces a 32-bit output (result). The ALU should support the following operations: 
- 0000: Addition (A + B)
- 0001: Subtraction (A - B)
- 0010: AND (A & B)
- 0011: OR (A | B)
- 0100: XOR (A ^ B)
- 0101: NOR (~(A | B))
- 0110: NAND (~(A & B))
- 0111: XNOR (~(A ^ B))
The ALU should also include an output for zero flag (ZF) that indicates if the result is zero.
