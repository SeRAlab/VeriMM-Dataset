Design a Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) generator. The module takes a clock signal and a reset input, along with two 4-bit inputs representing the durations for rising and falling edges of the PWM signal. It generates a binary output (`clk_out`) that toggles between high and low states based on the specified `rise` and `fall` durations. The output signal will remain high for the duration defined by the `rise` input and low for the duration defined by the `fall` input, effectively modulating the width of the output pulse according to the input parameters.
module top_module(
		clk,
		reset,
		rise,
		fall,
		clk_out
    );
