Design a 1-bit DRAM cell that can store a single bit of data. The DRAM cell should have the following inputs: `write_enable`, `data_in` (1-bit input), and `refresh` signal. The outputs should be `data_out` (the stored bit) and a `valid` signal indicating if the data is valid. The cell should retain the data when write_enable is low and should be refreshed on a high refresh signal.
