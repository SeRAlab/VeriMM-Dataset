
Create a Verilog module for a Restoring Divider. The divider should take two inputs: a dividend and a divisor, both of 8 bits. The output should be the quotient and remainder, each also 8 bits. The divider should operate in a clocked fashion, starting the division process on a rising edge of a clock signal and when a start signal is asserted. The division should complete after a fixed number of clock cycles.
