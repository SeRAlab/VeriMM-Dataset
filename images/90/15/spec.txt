On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is active, it will set the output Q to LOW and Qbar to HIGH. If J is HIGH and K is LOW, it sets Q to HIGH; if J is LOW and K is HIGH, it sets Q to LOW. When both J and K are HIGH, the flip-flop toggles its output state. This circuit is used for storing binary information and can switch states based on the inputs, making it useful in various digital applications.
