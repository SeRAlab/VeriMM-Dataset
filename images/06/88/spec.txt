Design a Verilog module that implements an SR latch using NAND gates. The SR latch should have inputs S (Set), R (Reset), and outputs Q and Q_not. The behavior should be that when S is high and R is low, Q becomes high. When S is low and R is high, Q becomes low. If both S and R are low, the output retains its state. If both S and R are high, it should be treated as an invalid state for SR latch.
