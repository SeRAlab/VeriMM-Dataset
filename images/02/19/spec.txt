Design a combinational logic 8-bit barrel shifter that can perform left or right shifts based on a control signal. The control signal will determine the direction of the shift, and the number of positions to shift will be provided as a 3-bit input. The module should have the following ports:
- Input: 
  - `data_in` (8 bits)
  - `shift_amt` (3 bits)
  - `direction` (1 bit, 0 for left shift, 1 for right shift)
- Output:
  - `data_out` (8 bits)
