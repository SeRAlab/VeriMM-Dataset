Design a synchronizing circuit that captures a single-bit asynchronous input signal (data_in) and outputs a synchronized version (data_out) with a two-stage synchronization flip-flop. The circuit operates on the rising edge of the clock (clk) and resets to a known state (00) when the reset signal (rst) is asserted. The output data_out provides the second stage of synchronization, effectively mitigating any metastability issues from the asynchronous input.
