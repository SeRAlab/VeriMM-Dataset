Design a 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects one of four input signals (A0, A1, A2, A3) based on the 2-bit selection input (S). When S indicates a specific input (00 for A0, 01 for A1, 10 for A2, and 11 for A3), the corresponding input signal is routed to the output (X). The design utilizes a combination of AND gates to mask the inputs based on the selection lines and OR gates to combine the outputs of the AND gates to produce the final output.
module top_module(
    input A0, A1, A2, A3,
    input [1:0] S,
    output X
);
