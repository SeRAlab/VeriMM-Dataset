
Design a power gating circuit using combinational logic. The circuit has 3 inputs: `A`, `B`, and `C`, and 1 output `Y`. The output `Y` should be high (1) only when `A` is high (1) and at least one of `B` or `C` is high (1). If `A` is low (0), the output `Y` should be low (0) regardless of the values of `B` and `C`.
