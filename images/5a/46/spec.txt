Design a simple ALU that performs an operation based on the exponents of two 32-bit floating-point numbers, `a` and `b`. On each positive edge of the clock, it extracts the exponent bits from both inputs and calculates their difference. If the exponent of `a` is greater than or equal to that of `b`, it outputs the difference in the lower 8 bits of `outp` with the highest bit set to 0; otherwise, it outputs the difference with the highest bit set to 1. Additionally, if the reset signal (`res`) is active (low), the output `outp` is reset to zero.
