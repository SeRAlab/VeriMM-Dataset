Design a Verilog module that implements a simplified version of the AES encryption round. The module should include the following components: SubBytes, ShiftRows, and MixColumns operations. The inputs should be a 128-bit key and a 128-bit block of plaintext. The output should be a 128-bit block of ciphertext after one round of AES. The module should have a clock and a reset signal to control the operation. Assume that the S-box and the MixColumns transformation are predefined.
