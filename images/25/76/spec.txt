
Design a 2/3 convolutional encoder with a constraint length of 3. The encoder takes a 1-bit input and produces two 1-bit outputs. The encoding process uses the following connection: 
- The first output is the modulo-2 sum of the current input and the previous two inputs.
- The second output is the modulo-2 sum of the current input and the previous one input.
The internal states of the encoder should be stored in flip-flops.
