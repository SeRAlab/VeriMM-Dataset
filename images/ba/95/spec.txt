Design a quadrature encoder counter that tracks positional feedback from two quadrature signals, `quadA` and `quadB`. Upon every rising edge of the `clk`, the module updates its internal state by delaying the input signals to detect direction and enable counting based on the edges of the quadrature signals. The count increments or decrements based on the detected direction and is scaled down to the lower 8 bits as the final output, `count`. The module also features an asynchronous reset to initialize the count and signal states.
