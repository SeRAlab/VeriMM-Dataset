Design a 4-bit asynchronous up counter with a reset and enable functionality. Upon receiving a clock pulse, if the reset signal is active, the counter resets to zero. If the reset is not active and the enable signal is asserted, the counter increments by one, wrapping back to zero upon reaching its maximum count of 15 (4'b1111).
module top_module(
  input clock, reset, enable,
  output reg [3:0] count
);
