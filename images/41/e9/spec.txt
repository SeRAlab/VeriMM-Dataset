The testbench systematically applies a series of test cases to the ALU with predefined inputs and operation codes, validating the output against the expected results. Each operation is triggered after a specified delay, and the results are checked to ensure correctness, concluding with a summary of test outcomes. The design purpose is to verify the functional correctness of the ALU implementation across a range of operations.
