Design a D latch with asynchronous reset functionality. The latch captures the input data `d` when the `enable` signal is high. Upon a reset signal (`_reset`) being low, the latch will force the output `q` to low, irrespective of other inputs. The output `q` will hold the last value of `d` until the `enable` signal goes low, effectively maintaining the stored value. The complementary output `_q` is also generated, providing the inverse of `q`.

    input d, enable, _reset,
    output q, _q);
