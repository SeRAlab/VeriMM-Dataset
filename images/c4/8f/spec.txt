
You have been tasked with designing a Verilog module for a full-adder circuit. The module should have three inputs (A, B, and CIN) and two outputs (SUM and COUT). The circuit should perform the following operation: 

SUM = A XOR B XOR CIN
COUT = (A AND B) OR (B AND CIN) OR (A AND CIN)

You should design the module using no more than 200 lines of Verilog code.
