Design a temperature monitoring system that takes a 5-bit binary input representing the temperature in Celsius and outputs a warning signal if the temperature exceeds a predefined threshold (e.g., 25 degrees Celsius). The output should be a single bit that goes high (1) when the temperature crosses the threshold and should stay high until the temperature goes below another threshold (e.g., 23 degrees Celsius). The system should also include a reset signal that can clear the output.
