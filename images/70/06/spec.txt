Design a module that evaluates the combined validity of a selection of items based on their associated values, weights, and volumes. It takes 24 binary inputs (A to X), representing the presence or absence of each item. The module calculates the total value, weight, and volume of the selected items, comparing them against predefined thresholds: a minimum required value (120), and maximum allowable weight and volume (both 60). The output, `valid`, is asserted as true when the combined total value is at least 120, while the total weight and volume do not exceed 60 each. This functionality suggests use in optimization scenarios such as knapsack problems.
