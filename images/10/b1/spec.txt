Design an interrupt controller that handles multiple interrupt requests (IRQs) with priority. The controller should accept 4 interrupt inputs (irq0, irq1, irq2, irq3) and provide a single output (interrupt_out) indicating the highest priority interrupt. The priority is such that irq0 has the highest priority and irq3 has the lowest. If multiple interrupts are asserted simultaneously, the controller should output the interrupt with the highest priority.
