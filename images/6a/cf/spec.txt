The ALU can execute four primary operations determined by the 2-bit op-code (OP): addition, subtraction, logical AND/OR, and bit shifting (both left and right). The output (Y) represents the result of the operation, while COUT indicates a carry-out from addition/subtraction or the most significant bit during shifting. This module provides a flexible and robust means of manipulating binary data within digital circuits.
