Design a 4-bit left shift register with fixed shifting behavior. The circuit takes a 4-bit input and produces a 4-bit output by shifting the input left by 2 positions, filling the two least significant bits with zeros. The two most significant bits of the output retain the original values of the third and fourth bits of the input, while the two least significant bits are set to zero.
