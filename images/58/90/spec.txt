Design a dual 4-bit to 7-segment display driver that takes two 4-bit inputs, in0 and in1, and cycles between displaying them on a common anode 7-segment display. The output 'out' consists of 8 bits representing the segment states for the current digit, while the 'com' output indicates which of the two inputs is currently being displayed. The module utilizes a clock signal to enable time-division multiplexing; it divides the clock signal to ensure each input is displayed in a flashing manner, switching every time the divided clock reaches a specified count. The design translates the binary values of the inputs into the corresponding segment patterns for numbers 0 through 15 to be displayed.
